# Acroud AB (ACROUD) — Q3 2026 Analyst Report
** · Reporting currency: EUR (tables in EUR thousands unless stated; USD in parentheses converted at EUR/USD 1.096 as of 25 Nov 2026) · Results released 25 Nov 2026**
**Market reaction:** Not independently verified: at the time of research, MarketScreener's ACROUD quote page showed SEK 0.13 (~$0.013), -5.8% intraday, with the Q3 report as the latest news item — a 25 Nov closing print could not be obtained before the research tool quota was exhausted [unverified]. Context: the shares closed Q3 at SEK 0.12 (~$0.012) versus SEK 0.15 (~$0.015) a year earlier, implying a market capitalisation of ~SEK 154m (~EUR 13.6m / ~$14.9m) on 1,279m shares.

> A weak quarter that was then made almost irrelevant by Brazil: revenue -20% to EUR 10.4m (~$11.4m) and adj. EBITDA -30% to EUR 1.0m (~$1.1m) on a 52% collapse in SaaS EBITDA, while a Brazilian provisional measure banning fixed-odds betting removes the ~EUR 1.4m (~$1.5m) of Jan–Aug EBITDA — roughly 40-45% of the 9M group total on our arithmetic — and forced the July 2026-28 targets to be suspended. Record NDC volumes (+60%) and a 25.7% Affiliation margin show the operating engine still turns, but with cash at EUR 2.0m (~$2.2m), leverage back to 2.5x, a SEK 10m (~$1.0m) bond amortisation due in December and a failed bondholder consent in September, the equity is now a binary on the Brazilian Congress and on a 2027 refinancing.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Headline: a clear miss against the company's own July trajectory (there is no sell-side consensus for this First North micro-cap).** Revenue EUR 10,391k (~$11.4m), -20% YoY and -20.3% organic, -4% QoQ versus a restated Q2 of EUR 10,813k (~$11.9m); adj. EBITDA EUR 996k (~$1.1m), -30% YoY and -24% QoQ; adj. margin 9.6% (-130bps YoY, -250bps QoQ). Reuters/SquawkNews flashes corroborate revenue EUR 10.391m and EBITDA EUR 996k. Gaming Intelligence's headline of a '10% fall' conflicts with the company's -20%; we use the company figure.
- **Brazil is the whole story.** On 25 Sep 2026 the Brazilian government signed a provisional measure prohibiting the operation, offering, intermediation and advertising of fixed-odds betting (sports and online casino) nationwide; operators had to disable Brazilian access by 6 Oct. Brazil has been one of Acroud's largest markets since the regulated framework started 1 Jan 2025 and generated ~EUR 1.4m (~$1.5m) EBITDA and ~EUR 0.8m (~$0.9m) net income in Jan–Aug 2026 — ~EUR 175k/month, which on our arithmetic is ~40-45% of group adj. EBITDA for the same period.
- **Guidance: the 2026-28 targets were suspended, not revised — there is no new guidance.** The targets adopted 10 Jul 2026 (adj. EBITDA CAGR ~12%, i.e. FY28 adj. EBITDA of ~EUR 5.9m/~$6.4m off FY25's EUR 4.2m/~$4.6m; net debt/adj. EBITDA <1.25x by end-2028) were suspended on 28 Sep 'for as long as the regulatory uncertainty prevails'. Congress may approve, amend, reject or let the measure lapse within up to ~120 days (into late January 2027); management will reinstate targets only after that.
- **SaaS drove the miss.** SaaS revenue EUR 6,919k (~$7.6m), -25% YoY against a record Q3-25 (EUR 9,258k/~$10.1m), with adj. EBITDA EUR 402k (~$0.44m), -52% YoY / -22% QoQ and margin 5.8% (-330bps). Management cites 'a slowdown in a smaller group of higher-margin markets within the Network Model' — markets not named. The slowdown pre-dates the Brazil measure, so it is not all Brazil, but a segment-level Brazil split was not provided.
- **Affiliation EBITDA +4% YoY — but flattered by a cross-period reclass.** Affiliation revenue EUR 3,472k (~$3.8m), -8% YoY / -11.5% QoQ; adj. EBITDA EUR 894k (~$1.0m), +4% YoY at a 25.7% margin (+300bps). The segment footnote discloses EUR 136k (~$0.15m) of April–June revenue moved from SaaS into Q3 Affiliation 'including in the subtotals below EBITDA'. Stripping it (full drop-through assumed, our estimate), underlying Affiliation revenue is ~-12% YoY and EBITDA ~-12% YoY, not +4%.
- **Volume up, monetisation sharply down.** Group NDCs hit a record 72,754 (+60% YoY): Affiliation 40,961 (+58%), SaaS Network 31,793 (+62%, all-time high). Against -20% revenue, revenue per NDC delivered fell ~42% in Affiliation (to ~EUR 85/~$93 from ~EUR 146/~$160) and ~54% in Network (to ~EUR 208/~$228 from ~EUR 454/~$498) — our calculation. Management itself warns NDC value 'varies greatly between regions' and that ~25% of Affiliation delivery historically came from Brazil.
- **Sports-margin pass-through is now near-total.** Revenue-share rose to 89% of Affiliation revenue (79% in Q3-25), CPA fell to 8% (13%), other 3% (8%). Sports-betting affiliate revenue fell 19% to EUR 2,212k (~$2.4m) on 'lower realised operator margins' in the opening weeks of the European football season incl. the Champions League; casino rose 22% to EUR 1,260k (~$1.4m). Acroud is a price-taker on operator hold — no handle, structural margin or promo data exist for an affiliate.
- **Cost base -19%, but mostly mechanical.** Opex ex items affecting comparability EUR 9,395k (~$10.3m), -19% YoY: SaaS opex -23% (network payouts flex with revenue), Affiliation opex -12%. Personnel EUR 1,214k (~$1.3m), -23% reported / -10% adjusted; other external expenses EUR 8,168k (~$9.0m), -20%. Management credits AI-enabled workflows for a 'substantial drop' in Affiliation personnel cost and promises SaaS efficiencies 'into 2027'. There is no quantified savings target, programme cost or target year — unlike a formal cost-transformation plan.
- **Cash conversion collapsed.** CFO EUR 215k (~$0.24m) vs EUR 1,074k (~$1.2m), on lower EBITDA and an EUR 838k (~$0.9m) reduction in operating liabilities (net working-capital outflow EUR 676k/~$0.74m), despite tax paid of only EUR 106k (~$0.12m) vs EUR 575k. FCF (CFO+CFI) ~EUR 212k (~$0.23m); interest paid EUR 437k (~$0.48m) incl. EUR 99k (~$0.11m) final RIAE promissory-note interest. Cash fell to EUR 2,035k (~$2.2m) from EUR 2,298k (~$2.5m) at Q2.
- **Leverage re-rose to 2.5x from 2.2x.** Interest-bearing net debt EUR 10,496k (~$11.5m), flat QoQ (EUR 10,452k) and -9% YoY, but LTM adj. EBITDA fell to ~EUR 4.26m (~$4.7m) by our sum of the last four quarters. Bonds: SEK 65.3m (~EUR 5.8m/~$6.3m) super senior at 10.5% due 31 Dec 2027 and SEK 70.8m (~EUR 6.3m/~$6.9m) restated senior at 10.75% due 30 Jun 2028; nominal SEK 136.1m ≈ EUR 12.0m (~$13.2m); gross debt SEK 145m (~EUR 12.8m/~$14.1m). A SEK 10m (~EUR 0.9m/~$1.0m) senior-bond amortisation falls due in December 2026 — c.43% of period-end cash.
- **A liability-management attempt failed.** On 25 Aug Acroud launched a written procedure under the 2025/28 super senior bonds to amend the intercreditor agreement so it could make repurchase offers for the 2022/28 senior bonds (the two largest holders were supportive); on 9 Sep it withdrew the procedure citing 'diverging views among the broader bondholder base'. The discounted-buyback route to deleveraging is closed for now, and the bondholder base is visibly fragmented ahead of the Dec 2027 maturity.
- **Restatement of Q1 and Q2 2026 (6 Nov).** Revenue and expenses of 'certain affiliate operations' were double-counted: revenue overstated by EUR 1.1m (~$1.2m) in each quarter (EUR 2.2m/~$2.4m for H1), all in Affiliation, with other external and personnel costs overstated by nearly the same amounts; EBITDA moved by only -EUR 22k/-20k, so H1 margin was restated up 120bps to 12.1%. Interim reports are not auditor-reviewed. Together with the Q3 EUR 136k reclass and a EUR 550k (~$0.6m) parent-level impairment of legacy SEO assets (eliminated on consolidation), financial-control quality is a live governance issue.
- **Net loss narrowed only because of FX.** Net loss EUR -225k (~-$0.25m) vs EUR -681k (~-$0.75m), helped by a EUR 262k (~$0.29m) SEK/EUR translation gain on the bond within 'other financial items' (+EUR 243k vs -221k); interest expense EUR 491k (~$0.54m). Adjusted net loss (ex IAC and FX) doubled to EUR -450k (~-$0.49m) from -221k. Basic/diluted EPS rounds to EUR 0.00 (computed ~-EUR 0.0002/~-$0.0002) on 1,279m shares.
- **The 9M picture still reads positively on paper — but the trend inverted in Q3.** 9M revenue EUR 31,686k (~$34.7m), -6%; adj. EBITDA EUR 3,527k (~$3.9m), +3%, margin 11.1% (+90bps); CFO EUR 2,103k (~$2.3m) vs 992k; net loss EUR -678k (~-$0.74m) vs -3,718k (which included a EUR 1,458k bond-extinguishment loss). Q4 will carry the Brazilian shutdown from 6 Oct.
- **Intangibles exceed equity by ~1.5x — the Q4 impairment test matters.** Goodwill EUR 11,687k (~$12.8m) plus other intangibles EUR 13,801k (~$15.1m) = EUR 25.5m (~$27.9m) vs equity EUR 16,724k (~$18.3m; equity ratio 48%). Management states Brazil is 'not expected' to impair the Acroud Media CGU; its model assumes a 9-18% increase in EBITDA margin over five years, 2% terminal growth and an 11.9% post-tax WACC — generous against a 9.6% Q3 margin.
- **Seasonality and events.** Q2/Q3 are structurally Acroud's weakest quarters (Q1/Q4 stronger). The 2026 World Cup knockout rounds (1–19 Jul) fell in Q3 but were not quantified; in Q2 management already conceded WC monetisation was below plan because its European-time-zone customer base was misaligned with the match schedule. Q3's call-out is instead the low operator hold in early-season European football.
- **Ownership, liquidity and valuation context.** Only 722 shareholders; Saxo Bank nominee 36.4%, PMG Group (vendor of the acquired PMG companies) 15.2%, IBKR 9.6%, Sparekassen Danmark 9.1%, Trottholmen 5.4%. At the Q3-end price of SEK 0.12 (~$0.012) market cap is ~SEK 154m (~EUR 13.6m/~$14.9m); EV ~EUR 24m (~$26m), ~5.7x LTM adj. EBITDA on our numbers. ABG Sundal Collier is liquidity provider; FNCA is certified adviser.
- **Management framing vs. what is buried.** The CEO letter leads with Brazil and 'record customer volumes', a 'cost base that continues to fall' and a 'materially repaired' balance sheet. Buried in footnotes: the EUR 136k cross-period segment reclass, the parent SEO impairment, the impairment-test assumptions, the working-capital drain behind the CFO collapse, and the fact that the SaaS 'high-margin markets' slowdown is unexplained geographically.
- **Thesis read-through.** The equity is a levered option on (a) the Brazilian Congress rejecting or amending the measure by late January 2027 and (b) refinancing SEK 65.3m (~$6.3m) of 10.5% super senior paper due 31 Dec 2027. Absent Brazil, we estimate a run-rate adj. EBITDA of ~EUR 2.5-3.0m (~$2.7-3.3m) against ~EUR 12m (~$13.2m) of 10.5-10.75% bonds — leverage drifting to 3.5-4x. Bulls can point to record NDCs and a 24% 9M Affiliation margin; bears to a capital structure that cannot absorb losing its largest market. Next catalysts: Congress decision; Dec 2026 amortisation; Q4 impairment test; FY26 report on 25 Feb 2027.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — iGaming Affiliation (owned comparison/news sites; casino & sports betting)

*Revenue -8% to EUR 3.5m (~$3.8m) on weak sports operator margins; EBITDA +4% at 25.7% margin, but flattered by a EUR 136k Q2 reclass and facing the loss of ~25% of NDC delivery from Brazil.*

### Revenue and mix

**Revenue fell 8% to EUR 3,472k (~$3.8m) on weak sports operator margins; clean of a EUR 136k reclass, the decline is nearer -12%.**

- Revenue -8% year on year and -11.5% quarter on quarter against restated Q2 of EUR 3,923k (~$4.3m); segment is 33% of group revenue versus 29%.
- Casino +22% to EUR 1,260k (~$1.4m); sports betting -19% to EUR 2,212k (~$2.4m); mix moved to 36/64 casino/sports from 27/73.
- Management blames lower realised operator margins in opening weeks of the European football season, a recurring seasonal pattern, but concedes data are too thin for statistical confidence.
- Book is now 89% revenue-share (79% in Q3-25), 8% CPA (13%), 3% other (8%): Acroud is a near-pure price-taker on operator hold.
- Segment includes EUR 136k (~$0.15m) of April–June revenue previously shown in SaaS; clean Q3 is ~EUR 3.34m (~$3.7m), roughly -12% year on year, -15% sequentially.

### NDC delivery and Brazil

**Record 40,961 NDCs (+58%) but revenue per NDC fell 42% to ~EUR 85, and ~25% of delivery historically came from Brazil, now prohibited.**

- NDCs +58% year on year, +4% quarter on quarter (Q1 28,027; Q2 39,239); nine-month delivery ~108k.
- Revenue per NDC ~EUR 85 (~$93) versus ~EUR 146 (~$160) in Q3-25 and ~EUR 100 (~$110) in Q2, implying lower-value players, geographies or partner-shared revenue.
- Approximately 25% of player delivery came from Brazil, subject to prohibition from 6 October; Q4 delivery could mechanically fall by a quarter before paid-media redeployment.
- No market-share, ranking, monthly-active or ARPU data disclosed; Acroud is a long-tail affiliate and NDCs are the only volume metric.

> “varies greatly between regions” — Management

### Channel mix

**SEO rose to 55% of revenue (47%), paid media 37% (47%), social 8% (6%), but the Q2 split is not comparable after restatement.**

- Pre-restatement Q2 report showed paid media at 70% and SEO at 23%; the apparent swing to SEO should not be read as a strategic pivot until restated Q2 mix is published.
- Paid media is cash-upfront, revenue-later under revenue-share, the working-capital profile that depressed group operating cash flow this quarter.
- Parent booked a EUR 550k (~$0.6m) impairment on legacy SEO assets (eliminated on consolidation), an early admission part of the SEO estate no longer earns its carrying value.

### Costs and EBITDA

**Adj. EBITDA +4% to EUR 894k (~$1.0m) at 25.7% margin, but underlying EBITDA ex-reclass is ~EUR 0.76m, about -12% year on year.**

- Segment opex -12% to ~EUR 2.58m (~$2.8m): other external expenses -14% to EUR 1,802k (~$2.0m); personnel -8% to EUR 775k (~$0.85m), though +5% sequentially versus restated EUR 736k.
- Management credits AI-enabled workflows for a 'substantial drop' in personnel cost; 9M segment opex decline of ~13% supports something structural.
- Margin 25.7%, +300bps year on year, flat sequentially; nine-month EBITDA EUR 2,784k (~$3.1m), +33%, at 24.3% margin (+700bps).
- Underlying Q3 EBITDA ~EUR 0.76m (~$0.83m) at ~23% margin assuming full drop-through: respectable, but not the growth management's framing implies.
- D&A of EUR 853k (~$0.93m), mostly amortisation of acquired domains and intangibles, leaves segment EBIT of EUR 41k (~$0.04m) versus EUR 35k loss a year ago.

### Assets and outlook

**No guidance given; Q4 will show whether non-Brazil delivery can hold revenue around EUR 3.0-3.3m at a 22-25% margin (our estimate).**

- Segment assets EUR 21.9m (~$24.0m), -15% year on year, dominated by goodwill and acquired intangibles to be tested in Q4.
- Management says Brazilian developments are not expected to impair the Acroud Media CGU.
- Stated priorities are cost discipline and converting the record NDC base into revenue-share annuities.
- Q4 revenue of EUR 3.0-3.3m (~$3.3-3.6m) at 22-25% margin is what the group's leverage arithmetic now depends on.
- Group Brazil exposure of ~EUR 1.4m (~$1.5m) EBITDA in Jan–Aug is not split by segment; given NDC disclosure we assume the larger part sits here.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — SaaS (Network Model – Matching Visions; Subscription Model – Voonix)

*Revenue -25% to EUR 6.9m (~$7.6m) and adj. EBITDA -52% to EUR 0.4m (~$0.44m) at a 5.8% margin as 'higher-margin markets' slowed — the quarter's real negative, despite record network NDCs.*

### Revenue decline

**Revenue fell 25% to EUR 6,919k (~$7.6m) as unnamed 'higher-margin markets' slowed in the Network Model; the quarter's real negative.**

- Segment is 67% of group revenue (71% a year ago); Network EUR 6,605k (~$7.2m), -26% year on year; Subscription EUR 314k (~$0.34m), -11% year on year, +2% sequentially.

> “the more partnerships we build in the network, the more essential the product itself becomes” — Management

## 5. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- Record customer acquisition: 72,754 NDCs (+60% YoY, +8% QoQ) — Affiliation 40,961 (+58%) and SaaS Network 31,793 (+62%, all-time high); 9M NDCs 190,615 (+14%). Under an 89% revenue-share model this is future cohort revenue, if the players are in markets that stay open. _(mixed)_
- Affiliation margin expansion: adj. EBITDA EUR 894k (~$1.0m) at 25.7% (+300bps YoY); 9M segment EBITDA EUR 2,784k (~$3.1m), +33%, at 24.3% (+700bps) on opex -12% YoY, with AI-enabled workflows cutting personnel cost. Even stripping the EUR 136k (~$0.15m) reclass, underlying margin is ~23%. _(structural)_
- Group opex ex IAC -19% YoY to EUR 9,395k (~$10.3m); personnel -10% adjusted to EUR 1,214k (~$1.3m); management guides to further SaaS efficiencies through Q4 and into 2027. _(structural)_
- Balance-sheet repair since 2025 still shows: net debt EUR 10,496k (~$11.5m), -9% YoY; all non-bond debt (shareholder loan, RIAE promissory note) fully settled; 9M CFO EUR 2,103k (~$2.3m) more than doubled; equity ratio 48% (43%). _(structural)_
- Casino affiliate revenue +22% YoY to EUR 1,260k (~$1.4m) (9M +34%), partly offsetting sports; revenue-share share rose to 89% from 79%, lowering CPA dependence. _(mixed)_
- Reported net loss narrowed 67% to EUR -225k (~-$0.25m), but EUR 262k (~$0.29m) of that is a SEK/EUR translation gain on the bond; adjusted net loss widened to EUR -450k (~-$0.49m). _(one-off)_

**Lowlights**

- Brazil prohibition (signed 25 Sep, sites disabled 6 Oct): ~EUR 1.4m (~$1.5m) of Jan–Aug EBITDA and ~EUR 0.8m (~$0.9m) net income at risk — ~40-45% of group adj. EBITDA for the period on our arithmetic; 2026-28 financial targets suspended; ~25% of Affiliation NDC delivery historically Brazilian. _(structural)_
- SaaS adj. EBITDA -52% YoY to EUR 402k (~$0.44m), margin 5.8% (-330bps), on an unexplained 'slowdown in a smaller group of higher-margin markets'; RGUs -5% to 396; subscription revenue -11% to EUR 314k (~$0.34m). _(structural)_
- Monetisation deteriorating beneath the volume headline: revenue -20% organic while revenue per NDC fell ~42% in Affiliation (~EUR 85/~$93) and ~54% in Network (~EUR 208/~$228), our calculation. _(structural)_
- Cash: CFO EUR 215k (~$0.24m) vs EUR 1,074k (~$1.2m) on a EUR 676k (~$0.74m) working-capital outflow; cash EUR 2,035k (~$2.2m) against a SEK 10m (~EUR 0.9m/~$1.0m) amortisation in December and ~EUR 0.35m (~$0.38m) of quarterly bond coupons. _(watch)_
- Leverage back up to 2.5x (from 2.2x) on falling LTM EBITDA (~EUR 4.26m/~$4.7m); the August written procedure to permit senior-bond repurchases was withdrawn on 9 Sep after 'diverging views' among bondholders — the SEK 65.3m (~$6.3m) super senior tranche matures 31 Dec 2027. _(governance)_
- Restatement of Q1/Q2 2026: revenue overstated EUR 1.1m (~$1.2m) per quarter (EUR 2.2m/~$2.4m H1) via double-counted affiliate operations; plus a EUR 136k (~$0.15m) cross-period/cross-segment reclass in Q3 and a EUR 550k (~$0.6m) parent impairment of legacy SEO assets; interims are not auditor-reviewed. _(governance)_
- Sports-betting affiliate revenue -19% to EUR 2,212k (~$2.4m) on low operator hold in early-season European football; with 89% revenue-share exposure, Acroud now wears operator margin volatility in full, and management admits its seasonality data are 'too limited to draw firm statistical conclusions'. _(mixed)_

## 6. Forecast & Outlook

The driver is entirely the Brazilian exposure (~EUR 1.4m/~$1.5m of EBITDA in Jan–Aug 2026, ~EUR 175k/month), which touches both divisions: management says ~25% of Affiliation NDC delivery historically came from Brazil, and while it did not say so, we think the SaaS Network 'higher-margin markets' slowdown may be partly Brazil-linked.
