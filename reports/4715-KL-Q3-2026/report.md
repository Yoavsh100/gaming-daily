# Genting Malaysia Berhad (4715.KL) — Q3 2026 Analyst Report
** · Reporting currency: MYR (RM) — tables in RM millions, USD equivalents in parentheses at ~RM4.12/US$ · Results released 26 Nov 2026 (after Bursa Malaysia close; quarter ended 30 Sep 2026, FYE 31 Dec)**
**Market reaction:** GENM's own move on 27 Nov 2026 was not captured [unverified]. Parent Genting Bhd fell to a 20-year low on 27 Nov after its RM161.4m (~US$39m) Q3 net loss, indicating a negative group-level reaction.

> A top-line quarter bought with margin. The first full quarter of commercial casino (live-table) operations at Resorts World New York City lifted Q3-26 group revenue 29.1% YoY to RM4.34bn (~US$1.05bn), but RWNYC ramp-up staffing costs, a 22% EBITDA decline at the Resorts World Genting cash cow and higher financing/transaction costs cut group adjusted EBITDA 11.3% to RM743.1m (~US$180m) and attributable profit 66% to RM40.6m (~US$10m). The New York ramp thesis is intact but is landing later and costlier than the Street modelled (US & Bahamas EBITDA RM200.5m vs Maybank's RM300m); the home-market decline is the bigger structural problem.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Revenue delivered the shape, not the margin.** Q3-26 group revenue rose 29.1% YoY to RM4.34bn (~US$1.05bn), which management attributed to the first full-quarter contribution from the initial phase of commercial casino operations at Resorts World New York City (RWNYC). Prior-year base implied by the reported growth rate is ~RM3.36bn (~US$816m).
- **Adjusted EBITDA fell 11.3% to RM743.1m (~US$180m)** against an implied ~RM838m (~US$203m) a year ago; implied group margin compressed c.780bps to ~17.1% from ~24.9% (our arithmetic from reported growth rates — the filing's absolute comparatives were not readable in-session).
- **Bottom line collapsed on cost and financing.** Profit attributable to shareholders was RM40.6m (~US$10m), down 66% from RM119.7m (~US$29m) in Q3-25 and down 14% QoQ from RM47.4m (~US$12m) in Q2-26. PBT fell 69% to RM80.2m (~US$19m). Attributable net margin is below 1% of revenue.
- **The earnings miss sits in New York operating costs, not demand.** US & Bahamas adjusted EBITDA of RM200.5m (~US$49m), +33% YoY, landed ~RM100m (33%) below Maybank's RM300m (~US$73m) estimate and below consensus; the broker attributes the shortfall to higher RWNYC staff costs as the property continues to add gaming capacity.
- **US & Bahamas is now the largest revenue segment.** Segment revenue +161% YoY to RM2.02bn (~US$490m) — 47% of group — but implied segment margin was only ~9.9% versus ~19.5% a year earlier (~RM151m EBITDA on ~RM774m revenue), i.e. the ramp is running with sharply negative operating leverage for now.
- **Home market is the structural worry.** Malaysia (Resorts World Genting) revenue fell 14% to RM1.72bn (~US$417m) and adjusted EBITDA fell 22% to RM486.4m (~US$118m), on weaker gaming activity, lower visitation and higher operating costs. RWG still contributes ~65% of group EBITDA, so its ~RM137m YoY EBITDA decline more than offset the ~RM50m YoY gain from the US.
- **Residual segments (UK & Egypt, Property, Investments & Others)** imply ~RM600m (~US$146m) of revenue and ~RM56m (~US$14m) of adjusted EBITDA by subtraction from group totals; the filing's segment-level split for these was not captured and is marked n/a.
- **Capital structure reset in New York.** The group has put in place a new US$2.0bn secured credit facility at Genting Americas Inc to refinance Genting New York LLC's existing senior secured facility, redeem associate Empire Resorts' US$300.0m 7.75% senior secured notes, and support development and operation of the RWNYC commercial casino. Transaction costs for the facility were booked in Q3-26 (one-off).
- **Financing costs are now a stated drag.** Management cited higher financing costs alongside higher operating costs as reasons for the profit decline — the direction of leverage is up as the RWNYC build-out is debt-funded. Net debt and cash balances at 30 Sep 2026 were not captured [unverified].
- **No numeric guidance; qualitative priorities only.** Management says it is focused on rolling out RWNYC's premium gaming segment and driving revenue growth from full commercial casino operations, with operational and marketing synergies between RWNYC and Resorts World Catskills expected to support long-term growth of the New York operations.
- **Sell-side has trimmed.** Post-results notes trimmed the outlook on costs and lower RWG visitors; Maybank expects earnings to 'improve markedly' in 1H27 as RWNYC table games ramp, but is less constructive on the home market, while still calling the shares deep value.
- **Parent-level stress adds overhang.** Genting Bhd (GENM's controlling shareholder) swung to a RM161.4m (~US$39m) Q3 net loss on revenue +7.3% to RM8.02bn (~US$1.95bn), with its Singapore and Malaysia businesses cited as drags; Genting Bhd shares fell to a 20-year low on 27 Nov.
- **No cost-transformation programme disclosed.** Unlike peers running explicit savings programmes, the captured disclosure contains no quantified cost-mitigation plan for RWG or RWNYC; cost pressure is being absorbed, not offset.
- **No group event effect to quantify.** Q3-26 had no World Cup/major-event driver; the revenue swing is entirely structural (RWNYC licence conversion) and cyclical (RWG demand). Reported vs organic/FX split was not disclosed in captured sources.
- **Dividend: none captured for Q3** [unverified]. GENM has historically paid interim dividends with H1 and final/special with Q4; a Q3 declaration would be unusual but we could not confirm the filing's dividend note.
- **Thesis read-through:** the bull case (RWNYC full-casino economics) is visible in revenue but its margin proof is deferred to 1H27; the bear case (RWG demand/cost erosion, rising leverage, parent overhang) is showing up in the P&L now. Until RWNYC segment margin recovers toward the mid-teens and RWG stabilises, group EBITDA is likely to run below prior-year levels.

Items marked n/a were not independently verified.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — Leisure & Hospitality – Malaysia (Resorts World Genting)

*Revenue -14%, adj. EBITDA -22%: the monopoly cash cow is shrinking on demand and cost at the same time, and it still carries two-thirds of group EBITDA.*

### Headline results

**Revenue fell 14% to RM1.72bn and adjusted EBITDA fell 22% to RM486.4m; the monopoly cash cow is shrinking on demand and cost simultaneously.**

- Revenue RM1.72bn (~US$417m), down 14% year-on-year; adjusted EBITDA RM486.4m (~US$118m), down 22%.
- Implied prior-year base roughly RM2.0bn (~US$485m) revenue and ~RM624m (~US$151m) EBITDA.
- Segment margin compressed about 290bps to ~28.3%.
- Segment remains ~65% of group adjusted EBITDA.

### Group impact

**The ~RM137m EBITDA decline here exceeded the entire ~RM50m gain from the US segment, so the group result was decided at RWG.**

- Malaysia EBITDA fell ~RM137m (~US$33m) year-on-year.
- US segment gain was ~RM50m (~US$12m), smaller than the Malaysia decline.
- The group result was decided at Resorts World Genting, not in Queens.

### Drivers and disclosure

**Management cited weaker gaming activity, lower visitation and higher operating costs, but key operating metrics were not disclosed.**

- VIP versus mass volumes, hold percentage, visitor arrivals, hotel occupancy and gaming/non-gaming split are n/a.
- Cannot determine how much of the 14% revenue fall is volume versus luck.
- Cost commentary continues the 2025–26 pattern; higher labour and indirect-tax costs are background context, not Q3 filing content.
- No quantified cost programme disclosed; operating leverage running the wrong way as 14% revenue decline became 22% EBITDA decline.

### Thesis implications

**As Malaysia's sole licensed casino, RWG's decline is demand and mix, not share loss, which is worse for the thesis.**

- Implies the premium-player and regional-visitor base is softer; the fix is macro/visitation recovery, not a marketing-share battle.
- Post-results broker notes trimmed outlooks specifically on costs and lower visitors.
- Maybank described the home-market outlook as less sunny than New York.
- Management commentary was overwhelmingly about RWNYC; RWG deterioration was explained but not addressed with a plan.

### Q4-26 outlook

**Seasonality should lift RWG sequentially, but year-on-year growth looks unlikely before comparatives ease in 2H27.**

- Year-end holidays should support a sequential lift in Q4-26.
- Want visitor arrivals, hotel occupancy and any statement on cost actions in the Q4 filing.
- Absent those, model Malaysia EBITDA down high-teens year-on-year for FY26 (estimate).
- Treat the segment as the swing factor for group leverage, since it funds the New York capex.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — Leisure & Hospitality – US & Bahamas (RWNYC, Resorts World Catskills via Empire associate, Resorts World Bimini)

*Revenue +161% on the first full quarter of commercial casino operations, but EBITDA +33% to RM200.5m was a third below the Street as table-game staffing front-ran revenue.*

### Headline results

**Revenue rose 161% to RM2.02bn on the first full quarter of commercial casino operations, but EBITDA of RM200.5m was a third below the Street.**

- Revenue RM2.02bn (~US$490m), up 161% year-on-year; adjusted EBITDA RM200.5m (~US$49m), up 33%.
- First full-quarter contribution from the initial phase of commercial casino operations at Resorts World New York City.
- Implied prior-year base ~RM774m (~US$188m) revenue and ~RM151m (~US$37m) EBITDA.
- Margin fell to ~9.9% from ~19.5%, a near-1,000bps compression and the single largest reason group margin fell ~780bps.
- Segment is now 47% of group revenue but only ~27% of group EBITDA.

### Ramp-up costs

**A ~RM100m shortfall versus Maybank's RM300m US EBITDA model, attributed to higher RWNYC staff costs, explains essentially the whole group miss.**

- Maybank modelled RM300m (~US$73m) of US EBITDA on the first full quarter of tables; shortfall ~RM100m (~US$24m).
- Known shape of a VLT-to-full-casino conversion: live tables are labour-intensive; dealer and surveillance headcount hired ahead of premium-segment launch.
- Revenue per table matures over several quarters; the question is duration, not direction.
- RWNYC GGR, table count, slot/ETG count, hold and win per unit not disclosed; must be tracked via New York State Gaming Commission monthly data.

> “partially offset by higher ramp up costs associated with the commercial casino” — Management

### Strategy and financing

**A new US$2.0bn secured credit facility at Genting Americas refinances existing debt and funds RWNYC, with higher financing costs flagged as a profit drag.**

- Priorities are rollout of RWNYC's premium gaming segment and driving revenue from full commercial operations.
- Operational and marketing synergies between RWNYC and Resorts World Catskills expected to support long-term growth.
- US$2.0bn (~RM8.2bn) facility refinances Genting New York LLC's senior secured facility and redeems Empire Resorts' US$300.0m (~RM1.24bn) of 7.75% senior secured notes.
- Facility transaction costs expensed in Q3 (one-off), but higher gross debt is structural.

### Outlook and watch items

**The ramp is real but the Street's margin timing was too aggressive; we model margin recovering toward mid-teens through 1H27.**

- Maybank now expects earnings to 'improve markedly' in 1H27 as table games mature.
- Model US segment margin recovering toward mid-teens through 1H27 (estimate) as revenue outgrows a largely fixed labour base.
- Premium-segment launch is the key catalyst.
- Resorts World Bimini and Catskills contribution not separately disclosed (n/a).
- Watch monthly RWNYC GGR, the redemption and refinancing close, and premium-segment opening timing.

## 6. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- RWNYC's conversion to a full commercial casino is now in the numbers: US & Bahamas revenue +161% YoY to RM2.02bn (~US$490m) in the first full quarter of live-table operations, making it the largest revenue segment (47% of group). _(structural)_
- US & Bahamas adjusted EBITDA still grew 33% YoY to RM200.5m (~US$49m) despite absorbing ramp-up staffing costs — the segment is accretive even in its least efficient phase. _(early)_
- Group revenue +29.1% YoY to RM4.34bn (~US$1.05bn) — the first step-change in the group's revenue base since the Malaysia monopoly matured. _(structural)_
- New US$2.0bn (~RM8.2bn) Genting Americas secured credit facility consolidates the New York capital structure, refinances the GENNY facility and takes out Empire Resorts' 7.75% US$300m notes — should lower the blended coupon on the US debt stack once transaction costs wash through. _(structural)_
- Premium gaming segment rollout at RWNYC is the next catalyst; management is explicitly prioritising it, and Maybank expects earnings to 'improve markedly' in 1H27 as tables mature. _(early)_
- The Q3 earnings hit includes one-off transaction costs for the new facility — part of the 66% attributable-profit decline is non-recurring. _(one-off)_

**Lowlights**

- Resorts World Genting revenue -14% to RM1.72bn (~US$417m) and adjusted EBITDA -22% to RM486.4m (~US$118m) on weaker gaming activity, lower visitors and higher opex — a monopoly asset shrinking on demand and cost simultaneously, with no cost programme disclosed. _(structural)_
- Group adjusted EBITDA -11.3% to RM743.1m (~US$180m) and implied margin c.-780bps to ~17.1%: revenue growth is currently dilutive. _(mixed)_
- US & Bahamas EBITDA of RM200.5m was ~RM100m (33%) below Maybank's RM300m and below consensus on higher RWNYC staff costs — the Street's ramp curve was too steep and estimates are being cut. _(watch)_
- Attributable profit -66% YoY to RM40.6m (~US$10m) and -14% QoQ; PBT -69% to RM80.2m (~US$19m). Higher financing costs are now a stated structural drag as the RWNYC build is debt-funded. _(structural)_
- Controlling shareholder Genting Bhd posted a RM161.4m (~US$39m) Q3 net loss and its shares hit a 20-year low on 27 Nov — group-level sentiment and potential capital-allocation pressure are an overhang. _(governance)_
- Disclosure gaps: no RWNYC GGR/table metrics, no RWG visitor or hold data, no net debt/cash figure captured, no 9M figures captured — management is emphasising the New York narrative while the home-market deterioration and balance-sheet direction are under-explained. _(watch)_

## 7. Forecast & Outlook

Genting Malaysia does not issue numeric guidance, so the 'bridge' is between Street expectations and delivery. The miss was entirely a US & Bahamas cost story: Maybank had RM300m (~US$73m) of segment EBITDA for the first full table-games quarter and got RM200.5m (~US$49m), with higher RWNYC staff costs the stated cause. Malaysia then compounded it, with EBITDA -22% YoY to RM486.4m (~US$118m) on lower visitors, weaker gaming activity and higher opex. Net: group adjusted EBITDA RM743.1m (~US$180m), -11.3% YoY, and attributable profit RM40.6m (~US$10m), -66%. Post-results, brokers trimmed outlooks on costs and lower visitors; Maybank's qualitative guide is for a marked improvement in 1H27 as RWNYC tables ramp, with the home market 'less sunny'. Management's own forward commentary is limited to prioritising the RWNYC premium-gaming rollout and Catskills synergies.

- **Street US & Bahamas EBITDA (Maybank, pre-results):** RM300m (~US$73m) (Actual RM200.5m (~US$49m): -33% on RWNYC staff costs)
- **Malaysia (RWG) adj. EBITDA:** RM486.4m (~US$118m) (-22% YoY (~-RM137m); lower visitors, weaker gaming, higher opex)
- **Group adj. EBITDA:** RM743.1m (~US$180m) (-11.3% YoY; implied margin ~17.1% (c.-780bps))
- **Sell-side outlook post-results:** Trimmed (Costs and lower RWG visitors; 1H27 recovery expected on NY table ramp)

**Q4-26 (estimate):** we would model group revenue of ~RM4.4–4.6bn (~US$1.07–1.12bn) as RWNYC adds capacity and RWG gets its seasonal year-end lift, with group adjusted EBITDA of ~RM750–850m (~US$182–206m) — a US segment margin drifting toward low-double-digits and Malaysia still down year-on-year. **FY26:** group adjusted EBITDA is tracking below FY25 on our arithmetic, because the RWG decline (~RM137m in Q3 alone) outweighs the US ramp until table productivity normalises. **Margin:** the inflection is a 1H27 event — US segment margin recovering toward the mid-teens as revenue outgrows a largely fixed dealer/surveillance base, premium-segment launch the catalyst. **Leverage:** direction is up — the US$2.0bn (~RM8.2bn) Genting Americas facility, Empire note redemption and RWNYC capex all add gross debt while group EBITDA is falling; net debt/EBITDA (not captured) should be assumed to rise into 1H27 before improving. **Swing factors:** (1) RWNYC monthly GGR trajectory and the timing of premium-segment opening; (2) RWG visitor arrivals and whether management launches a cost programme; (3) interest cost after refinancing; (4) parent Genting Bhd's financial stress and any implications for GENM dividends. All figures in this paragraph are analyst estimates, not company guidance.

**What to watch next quarter:**

- RWNYC monthly GGR, table/slot counts and win-per-unit via New York State Gaming Commission data — the only external read on the ramp curve.
- Resorts World Genting visitor arrivals, hotel occupancy and VIP/mass volumes in the Q4-26 filing; any disclosed cost-mitigation measures.
- Net debt and leverage at 31 Dec 2026 after the US$2.0bn Genting Americas facility draws, Empire US$300m 7.75% note redemption and RWNYC capex phasing.
- Premium gaming segment opening timing at RWNYC and any update on the broader RWNYC expansion capex schedule.
- Q4/final dividend decision given the 66% profit decline and parent Genting Bhd's loss and 20-year-low share price.
- UK & Egypt segment EBITDA and any impairment commentary — not disclosed in captured Q3 coverage.
