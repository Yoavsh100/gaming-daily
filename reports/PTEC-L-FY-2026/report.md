# Playtech plc (PTEC.L) — FY 2026 Analyst Report
**LSE · Reporting currency: EUR (tables in €m; ~USD at EUR/USD 1.1214, ECB fix 10 Nov 2026) · Results released 10 Nov 2026 (FY26 trading update, 1 Jul–31 Oct); financial base: H1 2026 results, 10 Sep 2026**
**Market reaction:** 10 Nov 2026 (trading update): shares opened at 377.2p (+2.4%), touched 385p (+4.5%) in early trade, then faded to close at 364.8p, −1.0% on the day versus the 368.4p prior close — the upgrade was sold into on the explicit 2027 warning. Still +28.7% YTD, but ~18% below the 445p close of 17 Sep that followed H1 results (52-week high 456p). For context, the 9 Jul H1 trading update drove an intraday +18% move to 378.6p.

> Playtech's third guidance upgrade of 2026 lifts FY26 adj. EBITDA to ">€300m" (~$336m) — +52% on FY25 and 7.5% above the €279m consensus — on the back of a Hard Rock Digital/PMR windfall in Florida that management itself says is already being competed away. The quality of 2026 is unarguable (H1 FCF €101.0m, net cash, 10% of shares retired in a year), but the same RNS effectively pre-announces a down year: FY27 consensus of €266m sits 11% below the FY26 floor, with PMR wallet-share loss, a Brazilian market shutdown and a full year of 40% UK RGD all landing at once. The shares' intraday +4.5% fading to −1.0% at the close is the right read: peak-year earnings, not a re-rating.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Scope note — FY26 actuals are not yet published.** Playtech reports half-yearly; the FY26 results are due ~March 2027. This note is built on the 10 Nov 2026 trading update (covering 1 Jul–31 Oct 2026, which contains guidance only, no P&L figures) layered on the reviewed H1 2026 results (10 Sep 2026). H2-25 comparatives are derived as FY25 less H1-25 and are flagged as such.
- **Third upgrade of the year: FY26 adj. EBITDA now '>€300m' (~$336m).** The path: FY25 results (26 Mar) left consensus at €205–225m; 9 Jul update set '≥€270m'; 10 Sep H1 results held '>€270m'; 10 Nov lifts to '>€300m' — 7.5% above the €279m mean (range €271–285m, 7 analysts) and the top end of the €250–300m medium-term range set in early 2025, reached ~two years early.
- **The upgrade is almost entirely the Americas, specifically Hard Rock Digital's PMR product in Florida.** The RNS cites PMR revenues that 'continued to perform strongly in H2' (vs the H1 guidance of normalisation to a 'more sustainable level'), plus Mexico and Colombia post-World Cup tailwinds. Implied H2-26 adj. EBITDA is >€137.5m (~$154m): −15% HoH but +30% YoY vs derived H2-25 €105.4m, and ~€30m above the >€107.5m implied by the prior ≥€270m guide.
- **But the same RNS pre-warns on 2027 and declines to guide.** Three named headwinds: (1) third-party PMR suppliers in Florida are 'now expected to have a more material impact on Playtech's wallet share into 2027'; (2) Brazil — a three-month 2026 EBITDA hit from MP 1.394 is absorbed within >€300m, but 'a more significant impact' in 2027 if the shutdown holds; (3) UK Remote Gaming Duty 21%→40% (Apr-26) annualises, with 'increased taxation across other European markets'. Management points to FY27 consensus of €266m (~$298m), −11% vs the FY26 floor — an unusually explicit way of endorsing a down year.
- **H1-26 was a step change, not a beat on a soft base.** Revenue €425.1m (~$477m), +10% YoY (+13% HoH); adj. EBITDA €162.5m (~$182m), +77% YoY and +54% HoH; group adj. EBITDA margin 38.2% (+1,450bps YoY, +1,020bps HoH). Reported EBITDA €86.8m (~$97.3m) vs €12.9m; reported PAT €98.1m (~$110m) vs a €78.1m loss, helped by €80.7m of unrealised fair-value gains (HRD stake, Wplay option).
- **B2B is the engine: revenue +14% (17% underlying) to €394.8m (~$443m), adj. EBITDA +75% to €128.1m (~$144m), margin 32.4% (+1,340bps).** The margin bridge is 14% revenue growth against a 3% cost reduction (B2B costs €266.7m): R&D −5%, G&A −6%, S&M flat at 2.6% of revenue, operations −1%. Pure operating leverage on a high-margin incremental revenue stream (Florida PMR) rather than a structural cost programme.
- **US & Canada revenue +161% (+176% cc) to €56.9m (~$63.8m); US by licensee location +232% to €50.1m.** Six regulated iGaming states after Connecticut; multi-state launches with Fanatics (NJ/MI/PA/WV), FanDuel (WV/CT casino & Live, iPoker in MI/NJ/PA), DraftKings (CT Live), Bet365 (MI Live), Ember Casino (ex-Delaware North) on PAM+ in NJ. HRD live in NJ, MI and FL; PMR is Florida-only 'for the time being'.
- **Latin America +14% reported / +29% underlying to €99.9m (~$112m); Colombia revenue >+100%.** Colombia benefited from the 19% deposit VAT being suspended (1 Jan) and replaced by a 16% GGR consumption tax (Mar-26) — but the outgoing Petro administration filed a bill in July to reinstate the 19% VAT from 2027. Mexico B2B revenue €70.3m (+6%); Caliente remains the largest customer at 14.1% of group revenue.
- **Europe ex-UK +2% (+10% ex-prior-year hardware) to €104.5m; UK −8% (−5% cc) to €59.0m; RoW +23% to €8.1m.** UK decline reflects RGD plus one operator insourcing SSBTs and contractual changes with another. Regulated revenue is 83% of B2B (81%); unregulated €66.4m (+2%) — Alberta moved to regulated from 13 Jul 2026.
- **SaaS and Live are the structural positives.** SaaS +20% to €69.0m (~$77.4m), now 17% of B2B across ~2,000 brands/~80 licensees (Brazil, Greece, Mexico, Spain, UK). Live revenue +8% (+12% regulated) with adj. EBITDA margin +550bps to 26.6% after table optimisation (c.500→c.480 tables); 'EGR Live Casino Supplier of the Year 2026'; AI 'Virtual Host' launched July.
- **Investment income €34.2m (~$38.4m), +73%, is 21% of group adj. EBITDA.** Caliente Interactive (30.8%) share of income €30.1m (~$33.8m) vs €20.3m for three months in H1-25 — i.e. run-rate roughly flat, with June World Cup marketing a drag and Q2-25 having had exceptional sports results. HRD dividends €4.4m (~$4.9m), +110%; HRD fair value marked to €246.7m (~$277m) from €178.8m, >3x the ~€80m 2023 cost.
- **Cash conversion was the headline quality signal: FCF €101.0m (~$113m) vs €6.6m, already above the €70–100m medium-term range in a single half.** Driven by EBITDA plus a €35.6m net Caliente dividend (H2-25: €31.3m). Capex + capitalised development €42.1m; adj. cash tax rate 15.1% (Caliente dividend is tax-exempt). Statutory operating cash flow was a lower €55.1m after €27.2m Snaitech CGT and €36.3m incentive-plan payments.
- **Balance sheet: net cash €39.2m (~$44.0m) vs €28.5m at Dec-25; adj. gross cash €338.1m (~$379m) against the €300m 5.875% 2028 bond; €225m RCF undrawn.** Net cash/LTM adj. EBITDA ≈ −0.15x. The investment portfolio (Caliente €706.3m, HRD €246.7m, Wplay option €89.8m, LSports €56.5m) is carried at €1,134.1m (~$1.27bn) — larger than the ~£1.05bn market cap.
- **Capital return: 10% of share capital bought back for ~€100m since Sep-25 (€24.6m/1.8% in H1-26; €77m/8.3% in H2-25), all into the EBT.** CFO on the call: intention is to 'return what we see as structural surplus capital'; the board 'regularly discusses' dividends and buybacks; no immediate M&A. No new programme was announced with the Nov update — a notable omission given the cash build.
- **No formal cost-transformation programme; efficiencies are organic.** Restructuring costs fell to €1.6m (H1-25: €5.0m); R&D −5% from 'cost optimisation and business restructuring'; Live table rationalisation; AI deployment. Adjusting items remain heavy: €15.9m share-based pay, €13.4m incentive arrangements, €7.0m professional fees, €19.3m NorthStar/loan provisions — a €75.7m gap between reported and adjusted EBITDA.
- **Governance/legal watch items.** (i) NorthStar (Ontario): full €28.9m (CAD 46.9m) ECL provision on Playtech's loan guarantee after an OSC cease-trade order and auditor resignation. (ii) Evolution/Black Cube: NJ judge denied (without prejudice, 5 Jun 26) Evolution's motion to add Playtech plc; UPEPA hearing scheduled Nov-26; no claim served. (iii) Sun Bingo under strategic review since FY25 results; RGD at 40% 'materially' deteriorates its long-term profitability.
- **Brazil is the binary.** MP 1.394 (25 Sep 26) banned fixed-odds betting and online casino; sites offline from 6 Oct; licences extinguished 25 Oct; the congressional deadline of 23 Nov was extended 60 days (Act 118, 11 Nov). Playtech's 'major strategic partnership' expected to sign late-2026 is now, per the H1 call, targeted to conclude and launch 'around the beginning of next year'; H1 Brazil investment and a São Paulo Live studio are sunk.
- **Medium-term targets to be reset after year-end.** Management said on the H1 call the Brazil opportunity and PMR 'did not exist when we agreed the targets' and the €250–300m / €70–100m ranges will be revisited post-FY26. Expect new targets alongside FY26 results in March 2027 — the key re-rating catalyst, or the moment the 2027 air-pocket is quantified.
- **Thesis read-through: 2026 is a peak-quality, peak-cyclicality year.** The bull case (B2B pure-play, 83% regulated, net cash, €1.1bn investment book, 10% share count shrink) is intact; the bear case (one product/one customer/one state drove the upgrade, Caliente = 14% of revenue and ~€66m of cash income, Brazil/UK/Colombia tax risk) is now explicitly validated by management's own FY27 framing. The shares' reversal on upgrade day says the market is already looking through 2026.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — B2B (platform, content, Live, SaaS, services)

*H1 B2B adj. EBITDA +75% to €128.1m on a 32% margin as Americas revenue rose ~60% underlying; the Nov update says Florida PMR stayed strong through October, but that is the revenue line now being competed away into 2027.*

### H1 revenue and EBITDA

**H1 2026 B2B adj. EBITDA rose 75% to €128.1m on a 32.4% margin as revenue grew 14% to €394.8m.**

- Revenue €394.8m (~$443m), +14% reported, +17% underlying excluding €10.0m legacy Caliente 'additional services fee' in Q1-25; +16% half-on-half versus derived H2-25 €340.7m.
- Regulated revenue +16% (21% underlying) to €328.4m (~$368m); regulated mix 83% from 81%.
- Unregulated revenue flat at €66.4m (+2%) ahead of Alberta's reclassification to regulated from 13 July.
- Adj. EBITDA +75% to €128.1m (~$144m); margin +1,340bps to 32.4% (H2-25 derived: 20.0%).
- Margin arithmetic is simple: revenue +€47m, costs −€7.6m.

### Cost base

**B2B costs fell 3% to €266.7m; this is operating leverage on high-margin incremental revenue, not a structurally reset cost base.**

- R&D −5% to €58.6m on cost optimisation and restructuring; capitalised development steady at 27% of R&D.
- G&A −6% to €45.0m, lapping non-recurring legal/advisory costs in 2025.
- Sales & marketing flat at €10.4m, 2.6% of revenue; operations −1% to €152.7m as 2025's hardware-heavy launch rolled off, partly replaced by Americas investment.
- No named cost programme; the H2 implied margin will tell us how much sticks.

### US and Canada

**US and Canada revenue rose 161% to €56.9m, driven by Florida PMR for Hard Rock Bet, which is now being competed away into 2027.**

- Revenue +161% (+176% constant currency) to €56.9m (~$63.8m), +117% half-on-half from derived €26.2m; US by licensee location €15.1m to €50.1m (+232%).
- Driver is Games powered by Past Motor Racing (PMR) sports-betting product for Hard Rock Bet/Seminole in Florida, where Playtech was first to market.
- H1 call guided HRD contribution to 'trend toward a lower but more sustainable level'; November RNS said third-party impact 'now expected to be more material into 2027'.
- Read together: normalisation slower than guided in H2-26 (hence upgrade), sharper in 2027 (hence warning). PMR is Florida-only 'for the time being'.
- Footprint widened to six regulated iGaming states via Connecticut; launches for Fanatics, FanDuel, DraftKings, Bet365, Ember Casino; Canada added Ontario launches including Superbet. US market-share data not disclosed.

> “continued to perform strongly in H2” — Management

### Latin America

**Latin America revenue was €99.9m, +29% underlying, but Colombia tax and Brazil's market shutdown make 2027 a swing factor.**

- Revenue €99.9m (~$112m), +14% reported, +7% constant currency, +29% underlying, +35% half-on-half; Mexico +6% to €70.3m.
- Caliente is a 30.8% equity-accounted stake plus a software licence; $140m fixed fees recognised straight-line over eight years (€7.5m in H1; €26.0m deferred). Largest customer at 14.1% of group revenue (15.1%).
- Colombia more than doubled on Wplay; 19% deposit VAT suspended 1 January, replaced by 16% GGR consumption tax in March. Bill submitted in July to reinstate 19% VAT from 2027; new tax reform due H2-26.
- Brazil: São Paulo Live studio completed; 'major strategic partnership' now targeted 'around the beginning of next year' after election-year concerns.
- MP 1.394 took Brazil's regulated market offline (6 October) and extinguished licences (25 October); three months' lost EBITDA absorbed within >€300m guide, 2027 hit unquantified; Congress review extended 60 days.

### Europe, UK, Rest of World

**Europe ex-UK grew 2% to €104.5m while the UK fell 8% to €59.0m on Remote Gaming Duty rising to 40%.**

- Europe ex-UK €104.5m (~$117m), +2%, or +10% excluding non-recurring hardware sales in H1-25; flat half-on-half versus €105.4m. Spain and Poland (Totalizator) called out.
- UK −8% (−5% constant currency) to €59.0m (~$66.2m), −8% half-on-half, on RGD moving from 21% to 40% in April 2026, one operator insourcing self-service terminals and contractual changes with another.
- UK RGD will have 'a full year of impact' in 2027; November 2025 Budget set 25% remote betting duty from April 2027, squeezing operator marketing and revenue-share pools.
- Rest of World +23% to €8.1m on South Africa (Hollywoodbets, Betway, Tsogo Sun) and December 2025 LottoStar launch.
- By licensee location, Rest of Europe €141.4m (+2%) and UK €60.3m (−7%).

### Product mix

**Mix is shifting toward recurring, lower-touch revenue: SaaS +20% to €69.0m, now 17% of B2B, and Live margin up 550bps to 26.6%.**

- SaaS €69.0m (~$77.4m), +20%, serving ~2,000 brands across ~80 licensees; Brazil, Greece, Mexico, Spain, UK strongest; reaches operators outside PAM+ and dilutes concentration.
- Live revenue +8% (12% in regulated markets); tables cut to c.480 from c.500 lifted Live adj. EBITDA margin 550bps to 26.6%; AI 'Virtual Host' launched July.
- Licensee fees +18% to €248.0m, fixed-fee +27% to €36.1m, cost-based +7% to €35.7m; legacy Caliente services fee halved to €5.1m.
- Playtech Protect reached 41 brands across 16 jurisdictions (from 28).
- Operator KPIs not applicable; quality metrics are regulated mix (83%), SaaS share (17%), S&M intensity (2.6%), customer concentration (14.1%).

### Segment outlook

**H2 B2B margin implied in the mid-to-high 20s rather than 32%; anything above 25% in FY27 needs Brazil re-opening or PMR beyond Florida.**

- H2 adj. EBITDA to be lower than H1 on HRD normalisation, Brazil investment ahead of signing and a full half of UK RGD.
- November update implies group H2 of >€137.5m; on ~€34m flat investment income, H2 B2B+B2C operating EBITDA around €100–105m [our estimate].
- Mid-to-high 20s margin is still well above 2025's 20–21% and the right base for 2027 before subtracting PMR share loss, Brazil and UK duty.

## 6. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- FY26 adj. EBITDA guide raised to >€300m (~$336m) — the third upgrade of 2026, 7.5% above the €279m consensus and +52% on FY25 — with H1 already at €162.5m (+77%) and management citing continued H2 strength in Florida PMR and post-World Cup Mexico/Colombia. _(mixed)_
- B2B operating leverage: revenue +14% (17% underlying) with costs −3% drove the B2B margin to 32.4% (+1,340bps YoY, +1,240bps HoH); Live margin +550bps to 26.6% on table optimisation; S&M only 2.6% of revenue. _(mixed)_
- Cash conversion: FCF €101.0m (~$113m) in one half vs the €70–100m annual medium-term target, net cash €39.2m after a €24.6m buyback and €63.5m of Snaitech CGT/incentive payments; 10% of share capital retired for ~€100m since Sep-25. _(structural)_
- Regulated mix rising (83% of B2B; 84% of group) with SaaS +20% to €69.0m (17% of B2B, ~2,000 brands) — the recurring, lower-concentration revenue the multiple should ultimately be built on. _(structural)_
- US footprint broadened beyond HRD: six iGaming states, multi-state launches with Fanatics, FanDuel, DraftKings, Bet365 and Ember Casino on PAM+; US licensee-location revenue +232% to €50.1m. _(structural)_
- Investment book marked at €1,134m (~$1.27bn), above the ~£1.05bn market cap: HRD stake €246.7m (>3x cost), Caliente €706.3m paying €35.6m net cash dividends in H1 and ~€66m annualised. _(structural)_
- Colombia revenue >+100% after the 19% deposit VAT was replaced by a 16% GGR consumption tax; Wplay option marked up to €89.8m. _(early)_

**Lowlights**

- The upgrade is one product, one customer, one state: Florida PMR with Hard Rock. Management now says third-party PMR suppliers will have a 'more material impact on wallet share into 2027' — the RNS reads as a peak-year marker, and FY27 consensus (€266m) sits 11% below the FY26 floor. _(structural)_
- Brazil shutdown (MP 1.394): regulated sites offline since 6 Oct, licences extinguished 25 Oct, congressional review extended 60 days. A three-month 2026 hit is absorbed; 2027 impact is 'more significant' and unquantified, and the long-trailed 'major strategic partnership' has slipped to early-2027 at best after a year of sunk investment. _(watch)_
- UK: RGD 21%→40% from Apr-26 annualises in 2027, remote betting duty rises to 25% from Apr-27; UK B2B revenue already −8% and Sun Bingo's long-term profitability 'materially' impaired, strategic review unresolved. _(structural)_
- Concentration: Caliente is 14.1% of group revenue plus €30.1m of equity income and €35.6m of cash dividends; Caliente plus HRD likely account for well over a third of group adj. EBITDA [our estimate — HRD B2B revenue not disclosed]. _(structural)_
- Adjusting items remain heavy: €75.7m gap between reported (€86.8m) and adjusted (€162.5m) EBITDA, including €15.9m share-based pay, €13.4m incentive arrangements, €7.0m professional fees and a €28.9m full provision on the NorthStar loan guarantee after an OSC cease-trade order. _(governance)_
- No new capital-return programme accompanied the November upgrade despite the cash build; the Evolution/Black Cube litigation (UPEPA hearing Nov-26) and Colombia's proposed VAT reinstatement from 2027 are open tails. _(watch)_
- Shares reversed from +4.5% intraday to −1.0% at the close on upgrade day and are ~18% below the mid-September high — the market is pricing 2027, not 2026. _(mixed)_

## 7. Forecast & Outlook

Guidance has moved three times in 2026: from a post-FY25 consensus of €219m (range €205–225m) to '≥€270m' on 9 July (H1 >€155m), held at '>€270m' with H1 results on 10 September, and to '>€300m' on 10 November against a €279m consensus (€271–285m). Every step was driven by the B2B Americas line — specifically Hard Rock Digital's PMR product in Florida normalising more slowly than guided — with Mexico and Colombia post-World Cup volumes as the secondary contributor. Brazil's three-month shutdown is absorbed inside the new floor. The November RNS pointedly gives no FY27 guidance but cites FY27 consensus of €266m and lists three headwinds (PMR competition, Brazil, UK RGD annualisation), which we read as management steering the Street toward a double-digit EBITDA decline next year.

- **Prior FY26 guide (9 Jul / 10 Sep):** ≥€270m (~$303m) (implied H2 ≥€107.5m)
- **Florida PMR / HRD slower normalisation + US iGaming state expansion:** c.+€20–25m [est.] (H2 B2B outperformance)
- **Mexico & Colombia post-World Cup volumes, less Brazil 3-month shutdown:** c.+€5–10m net [est.] (LatAm B2B + Caliente share of income)
- **New FY26 guide (10 Nov):** >€300m (~$336m) (implied H2 >€137.5m; +52% vs FY25)

**H2-26 / FY26.** We model FY26 adj. EBITDA of c.€303–308m (~$340–345m) [estimate], i.e. H2 of c.€140–145m: operating EBITDA c.€105–110m (B2B margin high-20s on H2 B2B revenue of c.€380–400m, down from 32% as HRD normalises and UK duty bites) plus investment income c.€35m. FY26 revenue c.€820–840m (~$920–940m) [estimate; not guided]. FCF should land c.€150–170m for the year given H1 €101m and a second Caliente dividend, taking year-end net cash to c.€100–130m before any further buyback — ample room for a renewed programme alongside FY26 results. **FY27.** The honest base case is a decline: FY27 consensus €266m implies −11% vs the FY26 floor, and we think the risk is skewed slightly below that if Brazil stays shut all year (Playtech's Brazil B2B revenue is not disclosed, but the lost GaleraBet/SaaS/Live contribution plus the delayed partnership is a multi-year growth hole) and PMR wallet share halves. Offsets: full-year Connecticut/Alberta, SaaS compounding at ~20%, HAPPYBET costs to zero, Colombia if VAT is not reinstated, and continued Caliente growth. **Margin direction:** B2B margin should settle in the mid-20s in FY27 versus ~29–30% for FY26 [estimates]; group adj. margin high-20s to ~30%. **Leverage:** net cash rising; with the €300m 5.875% bond due June 2028, expect refinancing discussion at a smaller quantum and a formal capital-allocation framework with the March results. The medium-term targets (€250–300m / €70–100m) will be re-based then; a credible new range would need to start from the FY27 trough, not the FY26 peak.

**What to watch next quarter:**

- Brazil: whether Congress converts, amends or lets MP 1.394 lapse within the extended 60-day window; any Supreme Court ruling; timing/terms of the 'major strategic partnership' and whether it is still live.
- Florida PMR: the pace at which Hard Rock onboards competing PMR suppliers and Playtech's residual wallet share; any evidence PMR can be licensed in other states or to other operators.
- FY26 results (c. March 2027): H2 B2B margin, Brazil revenue disclosure, re-based medium-term targets, and a new buyback/dividend framework given net cash >€100m [est.].
- UK: full-year 40% RGD impact on UK B2B (−8% in H1) and the Sun Bingo strategic-review outcome; 25% remote betting duty from April 2027.
- Colombia tax: fate of the Petro bill to reinstate 19% deposit VAT from 2027 versus the 16% GGR consumption tax; Wplay option value.
- Caliente Interactive: H2 post-World Cup volume conversion, dividend cadence (€35.6m H1), and customer concentration at 14% of revenue.
- Evolution/Black Cube litigation (UPEPA hearing Nov-26) and NorthStar recovery; the €300m 2028 bond refinancing plan.
