## 1. Executive Summary

Redeye described the print as below its forecasts on a soft sports-win margin and higher direct costs; we could not locate a formal consensus [unverified].
- **Luck masked ~€6.5m of earnings.** Management quantifies the unusually low sports-win margin as a €6.5m (~$7.1m) hit to both revenue and EBITDA versus a normal margin; because Q3-25 was a record-low quarter (-€10m), the YoY effect is nil. Normalised, Q3 EBITDA bsi would have been ~€28m at a ~32% margin. Q2-26 enjoyed a ~€3m tailwind, so the QoQ swing from luck alone is ~€9.5m — most of the €6.0m QoQ revenue decline. On the call management conceded margins have been below normal in four of the last five quarters.
- **Revenue bridge: ~€8m of underlying growth absorbed ~€3m of tax/regulatory drag.** Underlying growth ~€8m (incl. ~€1m FX tailwind) from talent-led media, sponsorship, prediction markets, Playbook and HLTV; less ~€2m from the UK Remote Gaming Duty rise (21%→40% from 1 Apr 2026) and ~€1m from Brazil. Net: +€4.9m.

At ~0.5x book and ~SEK 3.3bn market cap the equity is pricing Brazil as permanent and NA as unproven; the FY26 report on 24 Feb 2027 (impairment decision, any 2027 framework) is the next catalyst.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — North America

*Revenue +28% and EBITDA tripled to a 39% margin on sponsorship, prediction-market CPA and maturing revenue-share cohorts — now 47% of group EBITDA, but Q4-25's derived 56% margin is the comp to beat.*

### Revenue growth

**North American revenue rose 28% to €26.6m (~$29.2m), up 10% sequentially and 24% year-to-date to €76.7m (~$84.1m).**

- Trailing-twelve-month revenue is €108m (~$118m), up 19% reported and 26% in constant currency after a ~€5.5m FX translation headwind from the weaker dollar.
- Sponsorship more than doubled to €9.2m (34% of regional revenue versus 21%) on Playmaker HQ, the Roommates Show and the new Yahoo Sports distribution partnership.
- CPA rose 39% to €5.5m on prediction-market partners whose deals are structured on upfront CPA terms; CPM grew 28% to €2.9m.
- Revenue share fell 18% to €5.8m as prior-year hybrid agreements with upfront CPA components converted to pure revenue share.
- Subscriptions slipped 9% to €2.9m, though management flagged a strong September rebound in Action Network's products with the NFL season start.

### EBITDA and costs

**EBITDA before special items reached €10.3m (~$11.4m) versus €3.3m, lifting the margin to 38.9% from 16.0%, a 2,290bps expansion.**

- Margin was 25.9% in Q2 and roughly 31% in Q1 (derived).
- Regional costs fell 7% to €16.2m, or 61% of revenue versus 84%, on maturing revenue-share cohorts, prediction-market and Playbook contributions, and premium distribution leverage.
- Reconciliation wrinkle for IR: Publishing reports costs up 16% 'driven entirely by higher direct costs associated with the expansion of North American activities', yet NA segment costs fell 7%.
- Either allocations differ materially or talent-led content costs are partly capitalised (9M intangible additions €32.6m versus €1.8m), flattering the 39% EBITDA margin relative to EBITA.

### Volumes and products

**CPA-based NDC share rose on North American prediction-market demand, with NA CPA rising every quarter this year, though we see the stream as still CPA-weighted.**

- NA CPA: Q1 €5.2m, Q2 €5.1m, Q3 €5.5m (derived); volume disclosure is not regionalised.
- Playbook won an Affiliate Product Innovation award and integrated Kalshi and then Novig.
- Prediction Pro launched 19 October (after quarter-end) with Kalshi and Polymarket pricing, forecasting tools and a 2026 Midterms hub, standalone or as an Action Pro add-on.
- The Roommates Show's outdoor live podcast sold more than 10,000 tickets in New York.
- CPA is less recurring than revenue share and more exposed to partner marketing budgets and state-level pushback on sports event contracts.

> “already” — Søgaard

### Q4 outlook

**No regional guidance, but Q4-25 delivered €31.3m revenue and €17.5m EBITDA bsi (56% margin, derived), so 39% is neither ceiling nor peak comp.**

- North America is now 32% of group revenue and 47% of EBITDA bsi; the group result is effectively this region offsetting Europe.
- Swing factors: NFL-season subscription and revenue-share conversion, direct-sold sponsorship substituting for programmatic at higher yield, and prediction-market CPA pricing as operator competition intensifies.
- FX: EUR/USD around 1.10 versus ~1.16 a year ago continues to cost translation growth.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — Europe & Rest of World

*Flat revenue, EBITDA down a third and margin -970bps to 20% as UK duty, Brazil and a €6.5m sports-margin shortfall meet +12% cost growth; the Brazil hole opens fully in Q4.*

### Revenue mix

**Revenue fell 1.5% to €56.5m (~$62.0m), down 13% sequentially and only 0.7% higher year-to-date at €181.9m (~$199.6m).**

- Region is now 68% of group revenue versus 73% a year ago.
- Revenue share fell 5% to €29.7m, absorbing the €6.5m sports-win-margin shortfall, ~€2m UK Remote Gaming Duty effect (21% to 40% from 1 April) and ~€1m Brazil impact.
- CPA edged up 2% to €14.9m; sponsorship rose 29% to €6.7m on HLTV's Esports World Cup traffic and European talent-led formats.
- Subscriptions doubled to €1.5m on Mindway AI; CPM collapsed 35% to €3.5m as FUTBIN monetisation softened ahead of EA FC 27 and direct sales cannibalised programmatic.

### Profitability

**EBITDA before special items fell 33% to €11.6m (~$12.7m), taking the margin to 20.4% from 30.1% a year ago and 31.9% in Q2.**

- Regional costs rose 12% to €45.0m, 80% of revenue versus 70%, reflecting the group's €4m increase in direct costs around the World Cup, talent-led media and Paid Media.
- Of the €5.7m EBITDA decline, roughly €3m is UK duty and Brazil; the balance is cost growth against a flat top line.
- Sports margin was neutral year-on-year but, had it been normal, the regional margin would have been closer to 29%.
- Structural damage from tax and regulation is running at €3-4m a quarter, with luck doubling the optical hit this quarter.

### Brazil

**Brazil revenue is now expected at ~€30m (~$32.9m) for 2026 versus ~€45m planned, with ~€15m (~$16.5m) disappearing in Q4 after the betting ban.**

- Revenue peaked above €70m in 2024 and fell to €49m in 2025 after the 1 January regulation and new tax regime.
- Provisional Measure 1,394/2026 of 25 September banned fixed-odds betting, online casino and related marketing; operators required to cease by 6 October. Q3 impact ~€1m plus softer NDC intake.
- Management plans on zero Brazilian betting and casino revenue, has started cost adjustments, and declined to quantify the residual cost base (Nordea's analyst referenced ~€10m).
- Brazil-related domains and customer accounts carry €50-70m of value to be impaired if the MP becomes law; Congress granted a 60-day extension on 9 November, deliberation deadline 4 March 2027.
- The CEO's letter is unusually blunt in calling the ban counter-productive.

> “fully visible in Q4” — Management

### UK duty

**The UK is the second regulatory drag, with operators passing the duty increase through via tighter CPA and revenue-share terms.**

- Paid Media's UK casino book is the most exposed.

### Disclosure and Q4 setup

- No split of UK & Ireland, the Nordics, Southern Europe or LatAm ex-Brazil, a material gap given regulatory flux.
- Counter-Strike event promotion phasing cut Esports costs 36%; HLTV Brain and FUTBIN's official FC 27 status are genuine content assets.

- A rejection or lapse would restore a free option on ~€30m-plus of annual revenue that management is explicitly not planning for.

## 5. Segment deep-dive — Business-model segments: Publishing, Paid Media, Esports

*Publishing (+16%) carries the group; Paid Media (-12% revenue, -31% EBITDA) is where UK tax and Brazil bite; Esports' 73% margin is event-timing.*

### Publishing

**Publishing grew revenue 16% to €53.9m (~$59.1m), 65% of the group, with EBITDA bsi up 19% to €13.4m (~$14.7m) at a 24.8% margin.**

- Organic growth 16% against -11% a year ago; sponsorship rose 79% to €12.3m; CPA nearly doubled to €7.0m on prediction-market acquisition.
- Subscriptions rose 12% to €4.4m; revenue share slipped 5% to €24.3m on hybrid conversion; CPM fell 12% to €5.4m as direct sales displaced programmatic.
- Costs rose 16% to €40.5m, attributed entirely to direct costs of North American expansion; most group support-function costs are booked here.
- Margin up 50bps year-on-year but down from 29.3% in Q2 and well short of the 32% FY25 margin, mostly due to the sports-margin shortfall.
- Publishing contributed 61% of group EBITDA bsi versus 54%.

### Paid Media

**Paid Media revenue fell 12% to €24.2m (~$26.6m) and EBITDA bsi fell 31% to €4.9m (~$5.3m), with margin down 560bps to 20.1%.**

- Segment is 29% of group versus 35%; CPA down 10% to €13.4m, revenue share down 15% to €10.8m; runs at ~80% cost-to-revenue.
- Management cites the UK casino taxation increase, Brazil's late-quarter developments and the sports margin, which hurts Paid Media's revenue-share book.
- Costs cut 6% to €19.4m through campaign ROI discipline; Q2 margin was 26.2%; year-to-date revenue +1% and EBITDA -3%.
- Most exposed segment to the Brazil marketing ban and UK operator term renegotiation; clearest evidence the legacy affiliate model is under structural price pressure.

> “maintaining solid profitability” — Management

### Esports

**Esports revenue grew 14% to €5.0m (~$5.5m) and EBITDA bsi jumped 58% to €3.7m (~$4.0m) at a 73% margin driven by event-cost timing.**

- Sponsorship rose 32% to €3.5m on HLTV's record Esports World Cup traffic; revenue share doubled to €0.5m; CPM fell 33% to €1.0m on FUTBIN headwinds ahead of EA FC 27.
- Costs dropped 36% to €1.3m on Counter-Strike event promotional phasing; margin was 53% a year ago and 63% in Q2.
- Year-to-date margin is an unchanged 66%, the better guide; Q4 will carry the deferred event costs.
- Esports is 17% of group EBITDA bsi on 6% of revenue; FC 27 partnership and HLTV Brain launch are real engagement assets.

### Mix shift

**Recurring revenue fell to 56% of the total from 64%, while CPA and sponsorship rose to 43% from 36%; we see the annuity shrinking.**

- Management frames this as diversification and improving advertising yield.
- Revenue share fell 8%, and Brazil will cut it further, while direct-sold advertising, contract-based, cyclical and budget-sensitive, fills the gap.

### Reporting points

**The FY26 annual report will merge sponsorship and CPM into one 'Advertising' line, making the programmatic decline invisible; EBITA and EBIT bsi give a fuller read.**

- 9M intangible additions were €32.6m (versus €1.8m) as media-partnership and development projects are capitalised.
- Resulting amortisation (€11.6m in Q3, +54%) sits below EBITDA bsi; EBITA €20.4m (+7%) and EBIT bsi €8.7m (-24%) better reflect talent-led media economics than the 26% EBITDA margin.

## 6. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- **North America margin step-change.** EBITDA bsi €10.3m (~$11.4m) vs €3.3m, margin 39% vs 16% (+2,290bps), on sponsorship +112%, prediction-market CPA +39% and maturing pure revenue-share cohorts; regional costs -7% YoY. TTM revenue €108m (+26% cc). NA is now 47% of group EBITDA bsi. _(structural)_
- **Direct-sold advertising is scaling.** Group sponsorship +66% to €15.9m (~$17.4m), 18% of revenue vs 12%; Publishing sponsorship +79%, Esports +32%. Yield improvement from direct sales is replacing programmatic (CPM -16%) at higher margin. _(structural)_
- **Underlying growth absorbed ~€3m of tax/regulatory drag and €6.5m of bad luck.** ~€8m of underlying revenue growth; at a normal sports-win margin Q3 EBITDA bsi would have been ~€28m (~$31m) at ~32% margin rather than €21.9m at 26%. _(mixed)_
- **Balance sheet de-risked ahead of the Brazil hit.** NIBD/EBITDA bsi 2.29x (2.51x); €319m (~$350m) club facility extended to Oct-2029 with €80m accordion, €55m undrawn, €78m capital reserves; swaps fixed at 2.64-2.66%. No refinancing event before the Brazil outcome is known. _(structural)_
- **Cost base already flexed.** Average FTE 1,277 vs 1,535 in Q2 (-17% QoQ) after July efficiency measures; staff costs -3% YoY; total costs -€1m QoQ with only €2.1m of special items. Further Brazil-related adjustments post quarter-end. _(early)_
- **Prediction markets moving from pilot to P&L.** Kalshi and Novig live in Playbook (which won an Affiliate Product Innovation award), Prediction Pro launched 19 Oct with Kalshi/Polymarket pricing and a Midterms hub; Publishing CPA +93% is largely this. Yahoo Sports distribution partnership adds reach. _(early)_
- **Esports: HLTV sponsorship +32%, EBITDA bsi +58% at a 73% margin, FUTBIN official EA FC 27 partner.** Margin is flattered by Counter-Strike event-cost phasing (9M margin flat at 66%), but revenue growth of 14% is clean. _(one-off)_

**Lowlights**

- **Brazil: ~€15m (~$16.5m) of Q4 revenue gone, ~€30m FY26 vs €45m planned, zero assumed thereafter.** 2027-28 targets withdrawn, buybacks discontinued, and a €50-70m (~$55-77m) non-cash impairment follows if MP 1,394/2026 becomes law (Congress deadline 4 Mar 2027). Brazil was >€70m of revenue in 2024. _(structural)_
- **Europe & RoW EBITDA bsi -33% to €11.6m (~$12.7m), margin -970bps to 20.4%.** Costs +12% on revenue -1.5%; UK duty (~€2m/qtr) and Brazil are permanent, and Q4 faces a derived comp of ~€63m revenue / ~€19m EBITDA. _(structural)_
- **Paid Media revenue -12%, EBITDA bsi -31%, margin 20.1% (-560bps).** UK casino tax pass-through and Brazil's marketing ban hit the performance-marketing model first; this is the segment most exposed to both. _(structural)_
- **The annuity is shrinking.** Revenue share -8% to €35.5m (~$39.0m); recurring revenue 56% of total vs 64%; NDCs -3% YoY and -27% QoQ; Value of Deposits -5% QoQ with management flagging a Brazil drag on VoD in coming quarters. _(watch)_
- **Earnings quality below EBITDA.** D&A +37% to €13.2m as capitalised talent/dev projects (9M additions €32.6m vs €1.8m) amortise; EBIT bsi -24%; ETR 54.5%; net profit €1.2m (~$1.3m), EPS €0.02; our FCF estimate €8.1m (~$8.9m) vs €13.9m. €34m of media-partnership liabilities sit outside NIBD. _(governance)_
- **Leadership churn and a withdrawn framework.** Co-CEO structure unwound after ~one year; CFO moves to COO and a new CFO installed on the same day guidance beyond 2026 is absent; the interim report is still signed by both co-CEOs. Shares -9% to a 52-week low. _(governance)_
- **Disclosure is moving the wrong way.** Sponsorship and CPM to be merged into 'Advertising' from FY26; no regional split inside Europe & RoW despite Brazil/UK flux; Publishing's '+16% costs on NA expansion' does not reconcile with NA geographic costs -7%. _(governance)_

## 7. Forecast & Outlook

FY26 guidance is unchanged from the 27 September revision (regulatory release 85/2026), which cut organic revenue growth to 3-8% from 7-12% and EBITDA bsi growth to -7%/+3% from +8%/+18% (both constant currency), withdrew the 2027-28 targets (which had included a 35-40% EBITDA bsi margin ambition for 2027) and discontinued the €40m FY26 buyback after €23m. The entire downgrade is Brazil — i.e. Europe & RoW, split across Publishing and Paid Media — with FY26 Brazil revenue now ~€30m (~$32.9m) versus ~€45m and a ~€15m hit concentrated in Q4; the UK duty rise (~€2m/quarter) was already in the original plan. The net-debt/EBITDA target of <3x is retained. With 9M organic growth at 8% cc and EBITDA bsi +9% cc, the guidance implies Q4 revenue of €85-102m versus €94.3m in Q4-25 and Q4 EBITDA bsi of €24-34m versus €36.9m — a year-on-year profit decline in every scenario, which is what the equity is reacting to.

- **FY26 organic revenue growth (cc):** +3% to +8% (was +7% to +12% (cut 27 Sep, Brazil); 9M running at +8%)
- **FY26 EBITDA bsi growth (cc):** -7% to +3% (≈€95-105m / ~$104-115m) (was +8% to +18%; 9M running at +9%; implies Q4 €24-34m vs €36.9m)
- **Brazil FY26 revenue:** ~€30m (~$32.9m) (was ~€45m (~$49.4m); Q4 hit ~€15m; zero assumed from 2027; €50-70m impairment risk)
- **Capital returns / 2027-28 targets:** Buybacks discontinued; targets withdrawn (was €40m FY26 buyback (€23m done) and 35-40% 2027 EBITDA bsi margin ambition)
- **Leverage target:** <3.0x NIBD/EBITDA bsi (2.29x now) (unchanged; adjusted for €34m media-partnership payables ~2.6x)

**Q4-26E (our estimate):** revenue ~€90-93m (~$99-102m), -1% to -4% YoY, as the ~€15m Brazil loss is partly offset by NFL-driven North America, Esports deferred activity and some sports-margin normalisation; EBITDA bsi ~€27-30m (~$30-33m), a 29-32% margin versus 39% in Q4-25, assuming Brazil drops through at ~70% before cost actions. That lands **FY26E revenue ~€349-352m (+4-5% organic cc) and EBITDA bsi ~€101-104m (-1% to +2%), ~29% margin** — low-to-mid guidance on revenue, upper half on EBITDA given 9M is already €74m. **2027E:** the arithmetic starts with a ~€30m revenue hole (~9% of the base) at 60-70% drop-through (-€18-21m EBITDA pre-mitigation), offset by removal of a ~€10m Brazil cost base and by North America compounding at 15-20% on a €108m TTM base (+€16-22m revenue at ~40% incremental margin, +€6-9m EBITDA); net, we see **2027E EBITDA bsi roughly flat to -10% versus FY26E (~€90-105m) before any sports-margin normalisation (worth +€6-7m if Q3-type shortfalls do not recur) and before any Brazil reopening option**. **Leverage:** with buybacks halted and FCF (our definition) of ~€35-40m a year, NIBD should drift to ~€230-240m by end-2027, keeping NIBD/EBITDA at ~2.2-2.6x on that EBITDA range; the 3x ceiling is only threatened if EBITDA bsi falls below ~€80m. The €50-70m impairment is non-cash, outside the EBITDA-based metric, and would cut equity to ~€570-590m. **Margin direction** is down into 2027 at group level (Europe & RoW drag) but up in North America; the stock debate is whether NA's 39% is a run-rate or a seasonal/capitalisation-assisted peak — Q4-25's derived 56% NA margin suggests Q4 can be strong, but Q1-27 will be the first clean read.

**What to watch next quarter:**

- Brazil MP 1,394/2026: Congress conversion vote (deadline 4 Mar 2027 per call) and the impairment decision at FY26 results on 24 Feb 2027 — a lapse/rejection restores a ~€30m+ revenue option management is not modelling.
- Q4 sports-win margin: a fifth sub-normal quarter in six would raise questions about whether 'normal' has moved as parlay/SGP mix shifts in partners' books.
- Value of Deposits and NDC trajectory ex-Brazil in Q4/Q1 — the only external read on the revenue-share annuity.
- North America Q4 against a derived 56% margin / €31.3m revenue comp; NFL subscription conversion; prediction-market CPA pricing as operators compete; state-level pushback on sports event contracts.
- UK operator CPA/revenue-share terms after the 40% RGD — Paid Media's margin (20%) is the early-warning indicator.
- Capitalised intangibles run-rate (~€10-11m/quarter) versus amortisation, and the EBITA/EBIT bsi trend — the honest measure of talent-led media economics.
- Whether a 2027 framework is reinstated at FY26 results, the Brazil cost base actually removed, and execution under the sole-CEO/new-CFO structure.
- The new 'Advertising' line from FY26 — insist on continued sponsorship vs CPM disclosure.
