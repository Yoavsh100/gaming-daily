# BetMakers Technology Group (BET.AX) — Q1 FY2027 Analyst Report
** · Reporting currency: AUD (A$), unaudited; USD equivalents in parentheses at AUD/USD 0.704 (29 Oct 2026). Tables in A$ millions unless stated. · Results released 29 Oct 2026**
**Market reaction:** Shares −2.3% to A$0.21 (~$0.15) on 29 Oct 2026 per Stocklight (secondary source); the stock has held a A$0.21–0.22 band since the 10 Aug SID, ~12.5% below Tabcorp's A$0.24 cash offer. 52-week range A$0.135–0.25.

> A clean quarter on the metrics that matter for a B2B supplier: record Adj. EBITDA of A$5.9m (~$4.1m) at a 23.8% margin on revenue of A$24.7m (~$17.4m), +16.3% YoY in constant currency (+11.8% reported), with opex down 2.4% YoY and the 70% gross-margin / 25% EBITDA-margin long-term goals now one quarter away. But the equity is a merger-arb instrument, not a growth stock: Tabcorp's A$0.24 cash scheme caps the upside, the shares sit ~12% below the offer at A$0.21, and the beat mostly sharpens the question of whether the board recommended a sale at the bottom of the margin curve.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Record quarter with no consensus to beat.** Q1 FY27 (quarter to 30 Sep 2026) revenue A$24.7m (~$17.4m), +16.3% YoY constant currency and +2.1% QoQ; Adj. EBITDA A$5.9m (~$4.1m), +176.1% CC, a record; margin 23.8%. There is no sell-side consensus for this ASX micro-cap, so the 'beat' is against the company's own trajectory (Q4 FY26: 18.5% margin) and its long-term targets.
- **Headline growth is constant-currency; reported growth is lower.** Q1 FY26 as originally reported was A$22.1m revenue / A$2.4m Adj. EBITDA (11.0% margin, 64.7% GM). Restating the PCP at Q1 FY27 average rates (AUD/USD 0.7060, AUD/EUR 0.6134, AUD/GBP 0.5250) cut it to A$21.2m / A$2.1m. On a reported basis revenue grew ~11.8% and Adj. EBITDA ~145%. Still strong, but roughly 450bps of the headline revenue growth rate is FX restatement from a stronger AUD.
- **Operating leverage: >100% drop-through.** Incremental revenue of A$3.45m converted into A$3.75m of incremental Adj. EBITDA. Total opex fell to A$11.2m (~$7.9m) from A$11.4m (−2.4%) despite absorbing GT Vegas (ex-LVDC) operating costs that were not in the PCP; overheads −11.4% to A$2.6m, staff costs flat at A$8.5m net of capitalisation.
- **Capitalisation flatters the margin.** Capitalised staff costs rose 43% to A$2.03m (~$1.4m), 8.2% of revenue, from A$1.42m. Expensing them would give Adj. EBITDA of ~A$3.9m and a ~15.6% margin (our calculation). The A$0.6m YoY increase in capitalisation alone explains ~16% of the A$3.75m EBITDA improvement.
- **Gross margin 69.1% (+520bps YoY, +60bps QoQ).** COGS was flat at A$7.6m on 16% more revenue, so gross profit rose 25.6% to A$17.1m (~$12.0m). Management attributes this to digital revenues carrying high incremental margin plus cloud/cost efficiencies in Global Tote. The 70% long-term goal is effectively reached already (Q4 FY26 was 69.8% ex A$0.3m of prior-quarter COGS).
- **No guidance change — long-term goals reaffirmed, no FY27 numbers.** The targets set on 28 Aug 2025 (10%+ annual revenue growth, 70%+ gross margin, 25%+ Adj. EBITDA margin over 3–5 years) are restated unchanged. Q1 delivered 16.3% / 69.1% / 23.8%. The only forward comment is the CEO's FY26-results remark that 1H FY27 should continue to demonstrate the upgraded technology and product suite.
- **Division driver: Global Betting Services (digital).** The quarterly report does not split the two reportable segments; growth is attributed to 'expansion of the digital customer base', i.e. Apollo-driven GBS. FY26 context: GBS revenue A$43.3m (~$30.5m), +25.5%; Global Tote A$49.3m (~$34.7m), −2.6% reported / +1.1% CC with churn acknowledged. The mix shift to digital is the margin engine; Tote is ex-growth.
- **Event engagement quantified only qualitatively.** Apollo recorded 'record levels of wagering activity' in the quarter and, post quarter-end on 17 Oct 2026, its highest-ever traffic on Caulfield Cup / The Everest Day with performance maintained. No handle, actives or ARPU are disclosed (B2B supplier). The bulk of the Spring Carnival (Cox Plate, Melbourne Cup) falls in Q2 FY27.
- **Cash: positive OCF of A$2.8m (~$2.0m) after the annual US$2.0m Monmouth Park minimum guarantee.** Receipts from customers A$25.9m (~$18.2m). OCF compares with −A$1.2m in Q1 FY26 and A$3.2m in Q4 FY26 (which had no MG payment). Unrestricted cash A$16.0m (~$11.3m), +A$0.4m QoQ; total cash A$39.3m (~$27.7m) including A$23.4m (~$16.5m) of restricted customer funds (Global Tote Hub/ADW). Zero borrowings, no facilities.
- **Free cash flow is thin once development spend is counted.** Payments for intellectual property A$2.25m plus A$0.21m PP&E (BetLine US betting terminals) leave FCF of ~A$0.4m (~$0.3m, our calculation), excluding the +A$1.24m customer-funds movement booked in investing. 'Consistent free cash flow generation' remains a stated aspiration, not an achievement.
- **Tabcorp scheme on track for Q3 FY27 implementation.** SID signed 10 Aug 2026: A$0.24 (~$0.17) cash per share, with an election for up to 25% of total consideration in Tabcorp scrip priced at the higher of A$1.00 or 5-day VWAP; implied EV ~A$267m (~$188m), equity ~A$283m (~$199m) fully diluted; 45.5% premium to the pre-announcement close. Conditions: ACCC clearance, gaming/racing regulatory consents, Independent Expert's report, shareholder and court approvals; A$2.83m reciprocal break fees. Board unanimous; directors hold ~10%. Scheme booklet due late CY26, meeting targeted for Dec 2026.
- **The offer looks increasingly light against the new run-rate.** EV A$267m equates to ~11.4x Q1 FY27 annualised Adj. EBITDA (A$23.5m, ~$16.5m; company's own annualisation, seasonality-unadjusted) versus ~19x the LTM Jun-26 EBITDA of A$14.0m at signing. Tabcorp's cited 6.1x multiple includes the full A$30m (~$21m) synergy run-rate. The IER's conclusion is now a live swing factor; the market prices the shares at A$0.21, a ~12.5% discount to the offer.
- **LTIP Tranche 2 vests — 41.9m performance rights.** The EBITDA limb was met: six-month EBITDA to 30 Sep 2026 of A$10.4m (A$20.8m run-rate vs A$20.0m hurdle) and 12-month EBITDA of A$17.1m (vs A$15.0m). The six-month figure equals Q4 FY26 + Q1 FY27 Adjusted EBITDA (A$4.5m + A$5.9m), i.e. the test is effectively on the non-IFRS adjusted measure. ~3.7% dilution on ~1.12bn shares; worth ~A$10.1m (~$7.1m) at the offer price. This follows 4.825m FY23 Tranche 3 rights vesting in August (2.5m to Executive Chair Matt Davey).
- **Cost transformation: the heavy lifting is done; AI is the next lever.** No new program. FY25 restructuring took FY26 opex to A$49.4m (53.3% of revenue vs 61.7% in FY25); Q1 FY27 annualises at ~A$44.7m. Management is embedding AI tools across engineering, product and operations for productivity. Under Tabcorp ownership, A$30m of run-rate cost synergies by end of Year 2 are targeted, mostly from Tabcorp's side (data centres, corporate applications, legacy-platform replacement).
- **Product pipeline.** Parimutuel-powered 'reveal games' (commercial agreements with Bragg Gaming Group and Dreamstreak signed in Q4 FY26) targeted for go-to-market in Q3 FY27 — a new content vertical. BetLine terminals for US on-track/in-venue use are being built. New Apollo customers in FY26 included the CrownBet relaunch (Q3) and Stake.com and Dafabet.com.au (Q4).
- **Non-IFRS adjustments are small but will grow.** Add-backs of A$0.5m (deal costs A$0.4m, bad debts A$0.1m, severance A$0.1m) take EBITDA to A$5.4m (~$3.8m), +166%. Scheme-related costs (booklet, IER, advisers) will escalate in Q2/Q3 FY27; they are excluded from Adj. EBITDA but will hit cash.
- **Below-the-line is not disclosed quarterly.** The 4C format stops at EBITDA; no net income or EPS. FY26 NLAT was A$(5.2)m (~$(3.7)m) after D&A of A$15.5m and a A$1.5m tax benefit. With D&A running ~A$3.9m/quarter, Q1 FY27 EBITDA of A$5.4m implies the first positive EBIT quarter (~A$1.5m, our unaudited estimate).
- **Thesis read-through.** Operationally BetMakers is now a ~24%-margin, net-cash, double-digit-growth racing-technology asset — the turnaround management promised. But with the SID signed, equity value is capped at A$0.24 absent a superior proposal; the quarter raises the bar for the Independent Expert, de-risks Tabcorp's EPS-accretion case, and makes the ~12.5% scheme spread the only trade that matters for holders.
- **Share price.** −2.3% to A$0.21 (~$0.15) on 29 Oct 2026 per Stocklight (secondary source); the stock has traded in a A$0.21–0.22 band since the SID. 52-week range A$0.135–0.25.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — Global Betting Services (Apollo digital platform, fixed odds, content & data)

*The growth engine: digital customer expansion delivered essentially all of the quarter's +A$3.5m revenue uplift and the mix-driven gross-margin gain, though the quarterly filing gives no segment split.*

### Segment disclosure and growth attribution

**Digital customer expansion is the stated driver of 16.3% constant-currency revenue growth to A$24.7m, though the Q1 FY27 report gives no segment split.**

- 4C format stops at group EBITDA; the two reportable segments are only disclosed at half-year and full-year, so split cannot be verified until H1 FY27 accounts in February 2027.
- FY26 GBS revenue rose 25.5% to A$43.3m (~$30.5m) from A$34.5m, roughly 47% of group, while Global Tote was flat to down.
- If GBS sustained mid-20s growth in Q1 it would account for the whole ~A$3.5m YoY increase with Tote roughly flat, consistent with FY26.
- By product, FY26 revenue was 37.1% digital and 14.8% content and data, against 48.1% tote products.

> “continues to be an expansion of the digital customer base” — Management

### Volumes and customer base

**Apollo recorded record wagering activity and its highest-ever traffic on 17 October 2026, but no handle, actives, ARPU or margin unit economics are disclosed.**

- Highest-ever traffic on Caulfield Cup / The Everest Day with platform performance maintained; this is the only event quantification offered.
- Revenue model is hybrid/variable and success-based rather than per-seat SaaS, so platform revenue scales with partner turnover; Q2 FY27 (Cox Plate, Melbourne Cup) is the seasonal peak.
- Customer base added CrownBet relaunch on Apollo in Q3 FY26 (exclusive five-year deal) and Stake.com and Dafabet.com.au in Q4 FY26; pipeline described as strong.
- Marketing spend is immaterial at A$38k of advertising in the 4C because distribution is through partner integrations.

### US operations

**The US leg is visible mainly through cash, with the US$2.0m Monmouth Park minimum guarantee paid in the quarter and no profit-level disclosure.**

- Annual Monmouth Park minimum guarantee for New Jersey fixed-odds racing was US$2.0m (A$2.0m) versus A$2.1m a year earlier; A$2.8m operating cash inflow is stated after it.
- This is effectively a rights cost the company must fund regardless of NJ handle.
- GT Vegas (Las Vegas Dissemination Company, acquired February 2026) added operating costs with no prior-period comparison.
- A$0.21m of PP&E went into new BetLine betting terminals and other US on-track and in-venue hardware.
- We cannot see whether US businesses are contributing or consuming EBITDA; we treat NJ fixed-odds economics as unproven until handle disclosure.

### Gross margin and capitalisation

**Gross margin rose 520bps to 69.1% with COGS flat at A$7.6m, but rising capitalised development costs somewhat flatter the reported leverage.**

- Management states digital revenues continue to deliver incremental gross margin; Apollo carries near-zero marginal cost per bet.
- Capitalised staff costs rose 43% to A$2.03m (8.2% of revenue); the CFO said this reflected investment in new products, including emerging gaming and vision technology for international markets.
- On our arithmetic the A$0.6m YoY rise in capitalisation is worth about 2.5 points of the 13.8-point margin expansion; leverage is real but somewhat flattered.

### Pipeline and Tabcorp risk

**Reveal games with Bragg Gaming Group and Dreamstreak are guided to Q3 FY27 go-to-market, but Tabcorp ownership raises an unaddressed counterparty concentration risk.**

- Parimutuel-powered reveal games would be a new, casino-adjacent content vertical; agreements were announced with the Q4 FY26 report.
- Continued Apollo and Global Tote feature development and AI tooling across engineering.
- Under the Tabcorp scheme Apollo is the asset being bought; Tabcorp intends to replace legacy platforms with BetMakers' products and run the group as a global B2B engine.
- Third-party operators competing with TAB, the customers driving growth, must decide whether to keep core trading and pricing on a platform owned by their largest competitor.
- Management does not address this; it is a Year-2 question for Tabcorp but bears on durability of the current growth rate.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — Global Tote (tote products, Global Tote Hub pool connectivity, ADW operations)

*The larger but ex-growth half: FY26 revenue −2.6% reported (+1.1% CC) with churn; its Q1 contribution was cost efficiency and gross-margin support, not top-line growth.*

### Revenue run-rate

**No Q1 figures were given; FY26 revenue of A$49.3m fell 2.6% reported (+1.1% constant currency) with churn, making this the larger but ex-growth segment.**

- FY26 revenue A$49.3m (~$34.7m) versus A$50.6m in FY25; management acknowledged customer churn and FX headwinds.
- Tote products were 48.1% of FY26 group revenue but contributed none of the growth; roughly a ~A$12m-a-quarter business growing at low single digits at best.
- Every Q1 growth reference is to the digital customer base; Global Tote appears only in feature development and 'cloud and cost efficiencies' context.

### Cost efficiency

**Cloud migration and lower hosting costs are one of two drivers of the 520bps gross-margin gain to 69.1%, a durable structural improvement.**

- Group COGS was flat at A$7.6m on 16% higher revenue.
- Management explicitly credits Global Tote's migration to cloud infrastructure alongside digital mix.
- For a tote-hosting business where cost is largely infrastructure and pool-connectivity, this is structural rather than one-off.
- This is how a flat-revenue segment still contributes toward the 70% group gross-margin goal.

### Customer funds and liquidity

**Customer funds on deposit explain A$23.4m of restricted cash within A$39.3m total; relevant liquidity is unrestricted cash of A$16.0m with no debt.**

- Total cash A$39.3m (~$27.7m) includes A$23.4m (~$16.5m) restricted cash held by Global Tote Hub and ADW operations.
- The 4C's +A$1.24m 'other investing' inflow is simply the movement in customer balances.
- CFO noted the statutory cash-flow statement is distorted by customer-funds movements and prefers the 4C operating-cash-flow definition; we agree.
- We strip restricted cash out entirely: unrestricted cash of A$16.0m (~$11.3m), no debt.

### Currency and outlook

**AUD appreciation of roughly 7–8% hit this segment's foreign-currency revenue, so reported Q1 FY27 Tote revenue was more likely down than flat.**

- Business bills in USD, GBP and EUR; Q1 FY26 comparative restated from A$22.1m to A$21.2m at this quarter's average rates (AUD/USD 0.7060).
- The translation hit is largely on this segment; H1 FY27 accounts will confirm.
- Under the Tabcorp scheme the segment is positioned as the global B2B growth engine for international pool connectivity.
- FY26 track record is of a business needing new pool partners simply to hold revenue; churn in the base is the number to watch in February.

## 5. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- Fifth consecutive quarter of Adj. EBITDA margin expansion — 10.0% → 11.8% → 14.7% → 18.5% → 23.8% — with Q1 FY27 Adj. EBITDA of A$5.9m (~$4.1m) a record and the 25% long-term goal one quarter away. Annualised run-rate A$23.5m (~$16.5m) vs FY26 actual A$14.1m. _(structural)_
- Gross margin 69.1% (+520bps YoY, +60bps QoQ): COGS flat at A$7.6m on 16% more revenue, driven by digital mix and Global Tote cloud/cost efficiencies. The 70% goal is effectively met (Q4 FY26 was 69.8% ex prior-quarter COGS). _(structural)_
- Opex down 2.4% YoY to A$11.2m (~$7.9m) and down 7.7% QoQ, while absorbing GT Vegas operating costs absent from the PCP; overheads −11.4% to A$2.6m. Incremental revenue of A$3.45m converted to A$3.75m of Adj. EBITDA. _(structural)_
- Positive operating cash flow of A$2.8m (~$2.0m) after the US$2.0m Monmouth Park minimum guarantee; unrestricted cash +A$0.4m QoQ to A$16.0m (~$11.3m) with zero debt and no facilities. _(structural)_
- Apollo delivered record wagering activity in the quarter and its highest-ever traffic on Caulfield Cup / Everest Day (17 Oct) without degradation — a credible operational read-across into the Q2 FY27 Spring Carnival peak and into Tabcorp's platform-replacement thesis. _(early)_
- The quarter re-rates the asset against the scheme: Tabcorp's A$267m EV is ~11.4x Q1 FY27 annualised Adj. EBITDA versus ~19x LTM EBITDA at signing — a stronger hand for holders and the Independent Expert, even if the offer price is fixed. _(mixed)_

**Lowlights**

- The +16.3% headline is constant currency. Against the originally reported Q1 FY26 (A$22.1m revenue, A$2.4m Adj. EBITDA), growth is ~+11.8% and ~+145% respectively; ~450bps of the revenue growth rate is AUD-strength restatement of the comparative. _(mixed)_
- Capitalised staff costs +43% YoY to A$2.03m (8.2% of revenue). Adj. EBITDA ex-capitalisation would be ~A$3.9m at ~15.6% margin (our calc); A$0.6m of the A$3.75m YoY EBITDA improvement is simply more capitalisation. _(watch)_
- No segment disclosure quarterly. Global Tote — ~half of FY26 revenue — was −2.6% reported with churn acknowledged; the group's growth and margin story rests entirely on digital (GBS), whose Q1 contribution we cannot verify until February. _(structural)_
- LTIP Tranche 2 (41.9m performance rights, ~A$10.1m at the offer price) vests on an EBITDA test that, by the company's own numbers, equals Q4 FY26 + Q1 FY27 Adjusted EBITDA — a non-IFRS measure with add-backs — eleven weeks after the board recommended a sale at A$0.24. Combined with the 4.8m FY23 T3 rights (2.5m to the Executive Chair) vested in August, insider economics around the scheme deserve scrutiny in the scheme booklet. _(governance)_
- Free cash flow is only ~A$0.4m after A$2.25m of capitalised IP and A$0.21m of PP&E; the +A$1.24m 'investing' inflow is customer-funds movement, not cash generation. Consistent FCF remains an aspiration. _(watch)_
- Deal costs of A$0.4m were added back; scheme-booklet, IER and adviser costs will rise in Q2/Q3 FY27 and hit cash while staying outside Adj. EBITDA. _(one-off)_
- The shares closed at A$0.21, ~12.5% below the A$0.24 cash offer, a wide spread for a deal targeted to complete in Q3 FY27 — the market is pricing meaningful ACCC/regulatory or timing risk that management's 'on track' language does not address. _(watch)_

## 6. Forecast & Outlook

There is no numeric guidance to bridge. BetMakers reaffirmed, unchanged, the long-term goals set on 28 Aug 2025 — 10%+ annual revenue growth, 70%+ gross margin and a 25%+ Adj. EBITDA margin over three to five years — and the only forward statement is the CEO's FY26-results comment that 1H FY27 should continue to demonstrate the strength of the upgraded technology and product suite. The division driving delivery against those goals is Global Betting Services (Apollo/digital); Global Tote is a cost-efficiency and gross-margin contributor, not a growth driver. The parallel 'guidance' now comes from Tabcorp: A$30m of run-rate cost synergies by the end of Year 2, EPS accretion from Year 2 and double-digit accretion from Year 3, with scheme implementation targeted for Q3 FY27 subject to ACCC clearance, regulatory consents, the Independent Expert, and shareholder and court approvals.

- **Long-term revenue growth goal:** 10%+ p.a. (unchanged) (Q1 FY27 delivered +16.3% CC / +11.8% reported)
- **Long-term gross margin goal:** 70%+ (unchanged) (Q1 FY27 delivered 69.1%, +520bps YoY)
- **Long-term Adj. EBITDA margin goal:** 25%+ (unchanged) (Q1 FY27 delivered 23.8%, +1,380bps YoY)
- **Tabcorp scheme:** A$0.24 cash (≤25% scrip); Q3 FY27 completion (unchanged) (ACCC, regulatory consents, IER, shareholder & court approvals outstanding; booklet late CY26)

**Our estimates (not company guidance).** Q2 FY27 (Oct–Dec) is the Spring Carnival quarter and Apollo's record 17 Oct traffic suggests volume-linked digital revenue is running hot. Against a Q2 FY26 base of ~A$24.0m (derived: H1 FY26 A$46.1m less Q1 FY26 A$22.1m as reported) we model revenue of **A$26–27m (~$18.3–19.0m), +8–12% reported**, with constant-currency growth a few points higher if the AUD holds above 0.70. With opex held near A$11.5m and gross margin at 69–70%, that implies **Adj. EBITDA of A$6.0–6.5m (~$4.2–4.6m) at a 23–24% margin** — a 25%+ print is possible if capitalisation stays at ~A$2m. H1 FY27 would then be revenue ~A$51m (+10–11%) and Adj. EBITDA ~A$12m versus A$6.0m. For FY27 we pencil **revenue A$102–106m (+10–14%) and Adj. EBITDA A$23–26m (22.5–24.5% margin)**; reveal-games revenue from Q3 is upside we do not include. **Leverage** stays net cash: with OCF of ~A$3–4m a quarter in non-MG quarters against ~A$2.5m of capitalised development and rising scheme costs, unrestricted cash should drift up by ~A$1–2m a quarter to ~A$18–20m by June 2027 — unless the scheme completes first, in which case the balance sheet is Tabcorp's problem. **Swing factors:** AUD strength (reported growth will keep trailing CC), Global Tote churn, NJ fixed-odds economics under the US$2.0m MG, capitalisation policy, and above all ACCC timing. The quarter does not change the equity outcome — A$0.24 absent a superior proposal — but it raises the bar for the Independent Expert and lowers the probability that Tabcorp's bid is the last word if the ACCC process drags into the Spring Carnival print.

**What to watch next quarter:**

- ACCC clearance and the scheme booklet / Independent Expert's report (late CY26), scheme meeting (targeted Dec 2026) and Q3 FY27 implementation — the ~12.5% spread is the only equity trade.
- Q2 FY27 Appendix 4C (late Jan 2027): Spring Carnival digital volumes, gross margin crossing 70%, Adj. EBITDA margin vs the 25% goal, and opex discipline with GT Vegas fully in the base.
- Capitalised staff costs (A$2.03m, 8.2% of revenue) and free cash flow after IP spend — the quality-of-earnings test for the margin story.
- H1 FY27 accounts (Feb 2027): first segment split since FY26 — Global Tote revenue and churn vs GBS growth; statutory EBIT/NLAT after ~A$3.9m/qtr D&A.
- Reveal-games go-to-market in Q3 FY27 (Bragg Gaming, Dreamstreak) and any early revenue disclosure.
- FX: AUD/USD above 0.70 compresses reported growth from USD/GBP/EUR tote and US revenue; watch the CC vs reported gap.
