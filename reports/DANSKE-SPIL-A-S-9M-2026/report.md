# Danske Spil A/S () — 9M 2026 Analyst Report
** · Reporting currency: DKK (tables in DKK millions; ~US$ millions in parentheses at USD/DKK 6.76) · Results released 12 Nov 2026**
**Market reaction:** Not applicable — Danske Spil A/S is unlisted (owned by the Danish State 81%, DIF 9.5% and DGI 9.5% per AR 2025; CVR 64 01 17 15) with no traded equity or bonds, so there is no market reaction to measure.

> A flat nine-month print that hides a softer third quarter: 9M-26 gross gaming revenue (BSI) of DKK 3,841m (~$568m, +0.5% YoY) and net profit of DKK 1,547m (~$229m, +1.5%) look steady, but the derived Q3 standalone shows BSI down 1.4% to DKK 1,255m (~$186m) and net profit down 4.2% to DKK 503m (~$74m) as the Eurojackpot/Lotto jackpot cycle turned against the lottery monopoly. The liberalised Danske Licens Spil unit (Q3 BSI +7.5%, 9M EBIT +24%) and cost take-out are doing the heavy lifting; FY26 guidance (BSI DKK 5.2–5.3bn, net profit DKK 1.95–2.05bn) is held for a third time and the Elite Gaming disposal tidies the portfolio at negligible earnings cost.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Flat headline, weaker quarter.** 9M-26 BSI DKK 3,841m (~$568m), +0.5% YoY (+DKK 21m); net profit DKK 1,547m (~$229m), +1.5% (+DKK 23m). Our derived Q3 standalone (9M less H1) is BSI DKK 1,255m (~$186m), −1.4% YoY and −9.2% QoQ, and net profit DKK 503m (~$74m), −4.2% YoY — Q2’s rebound (+8.7% BSI / +19% NI) has not carried into Q3, and Q1 was also down (−5.6% / −10%).
- **Mix is the whole story.** Lotteries (Danske Lotteri Spil + Klasselotteri) 9M BSI −2.1% to DKK 2,398m (~$355m); liberalised Danske Licens Spil (Oddset sportsbook, online casino, poker, bingo) +6.6% to DKK 1,238m (~$183m); Elite Gaming slots −2.9% to DKK 204m (~$30m). Danske Licens Spil is now 32.2% of group BSI (9M-25: 30.4%).
- **The division that drove the quarter was the lottery.** Danske Lotteri Spil Q3 BSI fell 5.9% to DKK 717m (~$106m, −DKK 45m) on fewer Eurojackpot high pools, smaller Lotto pools and a drop in Quick scratch sales in shops; that single line is larger than the entire group decline (−DKK 18m). Danske Licens Spil (+7.5% to DKK 403m, ~$60m) cushioned it.
- **Profit growth is cost-driven, not revenue-driven.** 9M EBIT DKK 2,004m (~$296m), +2.2%, margin 52.2% (+90bps) on flat BSI. ‘Other costs incl. D&A’ fell DKK 55m to DKK 902m (~$133m): other external costs −5.6% to DKK 505m (~$75m, marketing cuts), staff −4.5% to DKK 278m (~$41m), D&A −9.9% to DKK 118m (~$17m). Partly offset by state duties +DKK 17m (+3.7%) and direct game-related costs +DKK 13m.
- **No consensus to beat — guidance is the benchmark.** Unlisted, no sell-side estimates. 9M BSI equals 73.9% of the DKK 5,200m low end (9M-25 was 74.1% of the FY25 outturn of DKK 5,158m), i.e. tracking the bottom of the range; 9M net profit is 79.3% of the DKK 1,950m low end (9M-25: 75.9% of FY25’s DKK 2,008m), i.e. profit is tracking comfortably.
- **Guidance unchanged for the third time.** FY26 BSI DKK 5,200–5,300m (~$769–784m) and net profit DKK 1,950–2,050m (~$288–303m), first set 13 Mar 2026 and reiterated in June, September and now November; explicitly stated before any effect of the conditional Elite Gaming sale. Implied Q4-26: BSI DKK 1,359–1,459m (~$201–216m), +1.6% to +9.0% vs Q4-25’s DKK 1,338m; net profit DKK 403–503m (~$60–74m), −17% to +4% vs Q4-25’s DKK 484m.
- **Event/engagement statistics: none disclosed.** The group reports no handle, actives, ARPU or event data. Management does not mention the June–July 2026 World Cup; Danske Licens Spil growth is attributed to online casino and the GG Poker-powered poker platform launched in Feb 2026. Market context (Spillemyndigheden, GDP-adjusted): Danish betting GGR −42.7% in August and +64.6% in September; online casino +12.5% and +17.9%.
- **No formal cost-transformation programme.** Management cites ongoing marketing reductions and lower depreciation (FY24 impairments rolled off: D&A DKK 337m in 2024 → DKK 190m in 2025). 9M capex additions DKK 127m (~$19m) vs DKK 139m; average FTE 480 vs 472 (+1.7%) despite staff costs −4.5%.
- **Portfolio: Elite Gaming exit (17 Sep 2026).** Agreement to sell the land-based slot operator (c.6,000 machines) to 87KDSL A/S, owner of Dan-Automater Struer and ultimately Erhvervsinvest — the PE fund that sold Danske Spil its 50.1% in 2012. Price undisclosed; approved at an EGM on 16 Sep; conditional on Danish Competition and Consumer Authority (KFST) clearance; effect to be booked on completion. FY25 BSI DKK 279m (~$41m), net profit DKK 10m (~$1.5m); segment assets DKK 72m (~$11m) at 30 Sep. Our pro-forma: removes ~5% of BSI, <1% of EBIT, lifts group EBIT margin ~260bps (FY25 50.1% → 52.7%).
- **Capital position: net cash, no leverage.** Cash DKK 1,399m (~$207m) + securities DKK 832m (~$123m) = DKK 2,231m (~$330m), +2.5% YoY; the only interest-bearing liabilities are leases (~DKK 18m at FY25). Equity DKK 1,851m (~$274m), equity ratio 58.8%. Note the Q3 cash level is seasonally inflated ahead of the Q4/Q2 statutory distributions.
- **Cash flow: strong conversion, slight working-capital drag.** 9M CFO DKK 1,860m (~$275m), −3.4% YoY, 120% of net profit (9M-25: 126%); investing outflow DKK 180m (~$27m); FCF (CFO−CFI) DKK 1,680m (~$249m) vs DKK 1,704m. Financing outflow DKK 1,141m (~$169m) is statutory lottery-profit distributions and State dividend — there are no buybacks by construction.
- **Financial items are MTM noise.** 9M net financial items −DKK 16m (~$2m) vs −DKK 5m: Q1 −25m (unrealised securities losses), Q2 +26m, Q3 −17m on a DKK 832m (~$123m) securities portfolio. Effective tax rate 22.2% (Danish statutory 22%).
- **Duty and regulatory drift.** State duties +3.7% to DKK 474m (~$70m), 12.3% of BSI vs 12.0%, mechanically rising as mix shifts toward the 28%-of-GGR-taxed Danske Licens Spil. Spilpakke 1 (broad political agreement, Oct 2025) tightens online-casino/sports marketing, bans revenue-share affiliate payments and adds youth protections; management welcomes it as levelling a field on which it already self-restricts (no whistle-to-whistle TV ads, no stadium live odds, no affiliates).
- **Retail footprint is shrinking fast.** Dealers 3,464 at 30 Sep vs 3,622 at 30 Jun and 3,759 a year ago (−4.4% QoQ, −7.8% YoY, −248 YTD from 3,712). Online was 70% of group BSI in 2025 (64% in 2023). Dealer commission −7.8% to DKK 247m (~$37m) is the offsetting benefit; Quick scratch in shops is the casualty.
- **Direct cost creep in the liberalised business.** ‘Other game-related costs’ +16.6% to DKK 239m (~$35m) for 9M and +30% YoY in Q3 to DKK 86m (~$13m) — growing 2–4x faster than Danske Licens Spil BSI, consistent with content/platform/poker-network fees. Gross profit from games therefore slipped 0.3% to DKK 2,881m (~$426m) for 9M and 3.8% in Q3.
- **Management, governance, disclosure.** CEO Nikolas Lyhne-Knudsen (since Oct 2020) and CFO Søren Skovdal Rasmussen; Chair Lars Krarup. Quarterly report is unaudited, IFRS recognition/measurement, approved 12 Nov 2026. A new subsidiary, Aurora Alea A/S, is consolidated with no revenue and DKK 1m of assets; purpose undisclosed. Owners: Danish State 81%, DIF 9.5%, DGI 9.5% (AR 2025). No earnings call or transcript exists.
- **‘Shareholder return’ is the social contribution.** FY25 total contribution DKK 3,148m (~$466m) incl. DKK 1,787m (~$264m) to the udlodningsmidler (sport, culture, associations). 9M-26 lottery + Klasselotteri EBIT of DKK 1,763m (~$261m) underpins a similar 2026 distribution; the figure is not disclosed quarterly.
- **Thesis read-through for the sector.** Danske Spil is a clean read on (i) Danish regulated online casino demand, still growing double digits at market level; (ii) the lottery jackpot cycle, where a strong 2025 Eurojackpot/holiday-product comp (holiday products +24% in 2025) makes Q4 the swing quarter; and (iii) the regulatory tightening coming with Spilpakke 1. The incumbent’s casino-led +7.5% against a +12–18% online-casino market says international operators are still taking share; that window narrows once marketing rules bite.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — Danske Lotteri Spil (lotteries & scratch — statutory monopoly)

*Q3 BSI −5.9% to DKK 717m (~$106m) as the jackpot cycle turned; 9M EBIT held flat at DKK 1,617m (~$239m) on cost take-out, margin 73.7% (+130bps).*

### Revenue and jackpot cycle

**Q3 BSI fell 5.9% to DKK 717m (~$106m) and 9M BSI fell 2.1% to DKK 2,193m (~$324m) as the jackpot cycle turned.**

- Sole licensed provider of number games and scratch cards in Denmark; lottery share effectively 100%, competition is share of wallet versus casino and betting.
- Quarterly path volatile: Q1 −10.1% (DKK 659m vs 733m), Q2 +9.8% (DKK 817m vs 744m), Q3 −5.9% (DKK 717m vs 762m), Q3 down 12.2% on Q2.
- Management cites fewer Eurojackpot high pools, smaller Lotto pools and lower Quick shop sales, partly offset by Vikinglotto.
- H1 had Eurojackpot ahead on a record May draw (358,000 tickets, DKK 750m pool); Q3 compared poorly with strong Q3-25 when 9M-25 BSI ran +3.0%.

### Profitability

**9M EBIT held flat at DKK 1,617m (~$239m, −0.2%) with margin 73.7% (+130bps) as the cost base absorbed the revenue shortfall.**

- Q3 EBIT DKK 534m (~$79m), −3.8% YoY and −13.3% QoQ, at 74.5% margin (+170bps YoY, −90bps QoQ).
- Levers are group-wide: dealer commission fell 7.8% to DKK 247m (~$37m) as sales migrate online, marketing cut, depreciation lower.
- Segment generated 80.7% of group EBIT in nine months (82.7% a year ago); DKK 1,644m (~$243m) distributed from this segment in 2025 after DKK 20m to Greenland and reserves.

### Jackpot sensitivity and products

**Volume, price and luck cannot be decomposed; 2025 had four DKK 750m Eurojackpot draws versus 13 in 2024, costing roughly 2% of segment BSI.**

- No handle or payout-ratio reporting; 18 high-pool draws in 2025 versus 27, with fewer still indicated in 2026.
- Holiday portfolio (Det Store Jule Lotto, Påske Lotto, Quick Christmas calendar) grew 24% in 2025, a demanding Q4 comparison.
- Lotto PLUS exceeded 210,000 subscribers, Spil Sammen moved to subscription, free Klub Lotto launched October 2025 passed 100,000 members.
- 24 new online Quick games launched in 2025; roughly 1,400 new terminals being installed in 2026, 9M-26 capex DKK 51m (~$8m) vs DKK 73m.

### Retail attrition

**The retail channel is the structural drag: group dealer count fell to 3,464 at 30 September, −7.8% YoY and −248 since year-end.**

- Dealers were 3,622 at 30 June and 3,759 a year earlier (−4.4% QoQ); Quick shop-sales decline is the visible symptom.
- Online accounted for 70% of group BSI in 2025 (64% in 2023, 67% in 2024).
- Lower commission is a mechanical margin benefit, but Quick and terminal-based games are most exposed; terminal roll-out is as much defensive as growth capex.
- Segment assets DKK 1,134m (~$168m) versus DKK 1,362m, reflecting timing of distributions.

### Outlook

**No segment guidance; we treat it as a stable ~DKK 3.0bn (~$444m) BSI, ~73–74% margin annuity with jackpot luck driving quarterly noise.**

- Group guidance implies Q4-26 BSI of DKK 1,359–1,459m (~$201–216m); Q4-25 lottery BSI roughly DKK 772m (~$114m, derived).
- Lottery needs only a low-single-digit increase to reach bottom of range, achievable with normal holiday seasonality.
- Exposed to a weak Eurojackpot roll cycle against last year's record Jule Lotto and Quick Christmas sales.
- Retail attrition is the slow-burn risk.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — Danske Licens Spil (Oddset sportsbook, Danske Spil Casino, Tivoli Casino, poker, bingo — liberalised market)

*Q3 BSI +7.5% to DKK 403m (~$60m), 9M +6.6% to DKK 1,238m (~$183m); 9M EBIT +24% to DKK 230m (~$34m), margin 18.6% (+270bps) — growing, but slower than the Danish online-casino market.*

### Market share

**Self-described market leader, but our regulator-based estimate puts share at roughly 24% of liberalised online verticals, down from roughly 27% in 2024.**

- 2025 annual report concedes share and revenue under pressure, above all in sports betting, from large international operators with bigger budgets.
- Spillemyndigheden: 2025 online casino GGR DKK 4,309m (~$637m, +12.1%), betting roughly DKK 2.2bn (~$325m, flat).
- FY25 segment BSI DKK 1,584m (~$234m); share figures are our estimates, not company data, but direction matches management's language.

### Revenue growth

**9M BSI rose 6.6% to DKK 1,238m (~$183m), the best in several years, though +7.5% in Q3 implies continued slower share erosion.**

- Cadence: Q1 +3.5% (DKK 410m vs 396m), Q2 +9.0% (DKK 425m vs 390m), Q3 +7.5% (DKK 403m vs 375m); 5.2% QoQ decline is normal seasonality.
- Growth attributed to online casino and the new GG Poker-based platform launched February 2026, offering the world's largest poker liquidity and WSOP qualification.
- World Cup not mentioned; plausible given self-imposed restrictions on sports marketing and deliberately low bonuses.
- Online-casino market grew 17.9% in September, 12.5% in August, over 20% in July; betting swung from −42.7% in August to +64.6% in September.

### Costs and EBIT

**9M EBIT rose 24.3% to DKK 230m (~$34m) at 18.6% margin (+270bps), driven by lower marketing and depreciation rather than gross margin.**

- No handle, sports margin, promotional spend, actives, ARPU, sports/casino split or marketing kroner disclosed.
- Unit bears 28%-of-GGR duty (FY25 DKK 451m on DKK 1,584m BSI); group duties rose 3.7% to DKK 474m (~$70m), 12.3% of BSI from 12.0%.
- Other game-related costs up 16.6% to DKK 239m (~$35m) for 9M and up 30% YoY in Q3 to DKK 86m (~$13m), two to four times revenue growth.
- Q3 EBIT DKK 73m (~$11m), +10.6%, at 18.1% (+50bps YoY, −50bps QoQ); FY25 margin 15.6%, net profit DKK 171m.

### Product and responsibility

**2025 rebuilt the digital estate across casino, poker, bingo and sportsbook, while responsibility measures act as a deliberate commercial handicap.**

- New Casino web platform, Casino app extended with personalised messaging, in-house 'Danmarks Vikinger III' topped the game hierarchy, Tivoli Casino+ loyalty scheme.
- Record DKK 707k bingo jackpot October 2025; wider bet-builder and player-props, in-house Danish odds, horse racing consolidated into Oddset after Dantoto closed December 2025.
- Highest-concern players down 69% versus 2021 to 0.24% of customers, with AI monitoring and low deposit limits that management says depress BSI.
- Segment assets DKK 1,060m (~$157m) vs DKK 1,097m; 9M capex DKK 39m (~$6m) vs DKK 32m.

### Outlook and regulation

**We assume mid-single-digit BSI growth into Q4 (roughly DKK 440–450m, ~$65–67m), full-year margin around 18%, and slow casino share loss until marketing rules bite.**

- No segment guidance; Q4-25 BSI roughly DKK 423m (~$63m, derived) was 2025's strongest quarter on football and Christmas casino activity.
- Spilpakke 1 (October 2025 agreement) curbs aggressive marketing, bans revenue-share affiliates and tightens youth protection; implementation expected 2027.
- Management frames it as levelling a playing field; a less promotional market would mechanically favour the incumbent brand.

## 7. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- Danske Licens Spil is compounding again: Q3 BSI +7.5% to DKK 403m (~$60m), 9M +6.6% to DKK 1,238m (~$183m), 9M EBIT +24% to DKK 230m (~$34m) with the margin up 270bps to 18.6% — online casino and the Feb-2026 GG Poker platform are working, and operating leverage from lower marketing/D&A is real. _(structural)_
- Cost discipline is delivering margin on flat revenue: 9M EBIT +2.2% to DKK 2,004m (~$296m) and margin +90bps to 52.2% while BSI grew 0.5%; ‘other costs incl. D&A’ fell DKK 55m (−5.7%) to DKK 902m (~$133m) with staff costs down 4.5% despite eight more FTEs. _(structural)_
- Lottery margin resilience: Danske Lotteri Spil 9M EBIT DKK 1,617m (~$239m) was flat (−0.2%) on BSI −2.1%, margin 73.7% (+130bps), as dealer commission (−7.8% to DKK 247m, ~$37m) shrinks with the online mix (70% of BSI in 2025). _(structural)_
- Klasselotteri converted a 2.4% BSI decline into +6.6% EBIT (DKK 146m, ~$22m; margin 71.2%, +600bps) — a reminder that the class-lottery annuity is almost all drop-through. _(mixed)_
- Portfolio simplification: the Elite Gaming sale (undisclosed price; EGM-approved 16 Sep; KFST pending) removes ~5% of BSI but <1% of EBIT and is ~260bps accretive to group EBIT margin on FY25 numbers (our calc.), while exiting a structurally declining land-based machine market. _(structural)_
- Balance sheet needs no interpretation: cash + securities DKK 2,231m (~$330m), zero bank debt, 9M CFO DKK 1,860m (~$275m) = 120% of net profit, FCF DKK 1,680m (~$249m), DKK 1,141m (~$169m) already distributed to the State and sport/culture funds. _(structural)_
- Guidance held for the third time with profit tracking comfortably: 9M net profit is 79.3% of the DKK 1,950m low end versus 75.9% at the same point of 2025; the implied Q4 range (DKK 403–503m, ~$60–74m) brackets Q4-25’s DKK 484m. _(watch)_

**Lowlights**

- The 9M headline flatters: derived Q3 BSI fell 1.4% YoY to DKK 1,255m (~$186m) and net profit 4.2% to DKK 503m (~$74m); Q3 net margin 40.1% (−110bps YoY, −300bps QoQ). Two of three quarters in 2026 have now shown YoY declines in both revenue and profit. _(mixed)_
- Jackpot dependence: Danske Lotteri Spil Q3 BSI −5.9% to DKK 717m (~$106m) on fewer Eurojackpot high pools, smaller Lotto pools and weaker Quick retail — the −DKK 45m swing exceeded the whole group’s decline. Q4 laps record 2025 Jule Lotto and Quick Christmas sales (holiday products +24% in 2025). _(one-off)_
- Direct cost creep: ‘other game-related costs’ +30% YoY in Q3 to DKK 86m (~$13m) and +16.6% for 9M to DKK 239m (~$35m), growing 2–4x faster than the liberalised unit’s BSI — content/platform/poker-network fees are eating into incremental margin; gross profit from games fell 3.8% in Q3. _(structural)_
- Retail attrition is accelerating: dealers 3,464, −158 in the quarter alone, −7.8% YoY and −248 since year-end; Quick scratch and terminal-based number games are the exposed products and the ~1,400-terminal roll-out is defensive capex. _(structural)_
- Still losing share in the liberalised verticals: +7.5% against a Danish online-casino market growing 12–18% (and >20% in July) implies international operators continue to take share; our estimate of Danske Licens Spil’s share of online casino + betting is ~24% (FY25) vs ~27% (FY24). _(structural)_
- Disclosure is thin for a DKK 5bn operator: no sports/casino split, no handle, net or structural margin, promo intensity, actives, ARPU or marketing spend; no earnings call; Elite Gaming price undisclosed; the purpose of the new Aurora Alea A/S subsidiary is unexplained; quarterly reports are unaudited and give cumulative figures only. _(governance)_
- Regulatory and fiscal drift: state duties +3.7% to DKK 474m (~$70m) and rising as a share of BSI (12.3% vs 12.0%) as mix shifts to the 28%-taxed unit; Spilpakke 1 marketing/affiliate/bonus restrictions arrive in 2027 and, while levelling the field, cap the growth ceiling of the only growing division. _(watch)_

## 8. Forecast & Outlook

There is no guidance change to bridge: FY26 BSI of DKK 5,200–5,300m (~$769–784m) and net profit of DKK 1,950–2,050m (~$288–303m) were set with the FY25 annual report on 13 March 2026 and have been reiterated in the Q1 (June), H1 (September) and now Q3 (12 November) reports, explicitly before any effect of the conditional Elite Gaming sale. The moving parts are divisional, not group-level: the lottery (Danske Lotteri Spil) is the division pulling BSI toward the bottom of the range (9M −2.1%, Q3 −5.9% on the jackpot cycle), the liberalised Danske Licens Spil is the division pulling it up (9M +6.6%, Q3 +7.5%, EBIT +24%), and cost take-out (other costs incl. D&A −DKK 55m) is what keeps net profit tracking the upper half of its range despite flat revenue. Arithmetically, 9M BSI is 73.9% of the low end (versus 74.1% of the FY25 outturn at the same point last year), so the revenue range requires Q4 BSI of DKK 1,359–1,459m (~$201–216m), +1.6% to +9.0% on Q4-25’s DKK 1,338m, while the profit range requires only DKK 403–503m (~$60–74m), −17% to +4% on Q4-25’s DKK 484m.

- **FY26 BSI guidance:** DKK 5,200–5,300m (~$769–784m) (Unchanged (set 13 Mar 2026; reiterated Jun, Sep, Nov). 9M = 73.9% of low end.)
- **FY26 net profit guidance:** DKK 1,950–2,050m (~$288–303m) (Unchanged; excludes Elite Gaming disposal. 9M = 79.3% of low end.)
- **Implied Q4-26 BSI:** DKK 1,359–1,459m (~$201–216m) (+1.6% to +9.0% vs Q4-25 DKK 1,338m (~$198m) — lottery jackpot cycle is the swing)
- **Implied Q4-26 net profit:** DKK 403–503m (~$60–74m) (−17% to +4% vs Q4-25 DKK 484m (~$72m) — conservative)

Our independent view (all figures estimates): **FY26 BSI of roughly DKK 5,200–5,240m (~$769–775m), i.e. at or marginally below the bottom of guidance**, built from Q4 lottery BSI of ~DKK 785m (+1–2% on a jackpot cycle we assume merely normal against a record holiday-product comp), Danske Licens Spil ~DKK 440–450m (+4–6%, lapping the strongest quarter of 2025 at ~DKK 423m), Klasselotteri ~DKK 72m and Elite Gaming ~DKK 68m if still consolidated. **Net profit of DKK 2,000–2,040m (~$296–302m), in the upper half of the range**, as Q4 duty and direct-cost inflation (duties ~12.5% of BSI, game-related costs +15–20%) is more than covered by lower marketing and D&A; that implies a full-year EBIT margin of ~51–52% (FY25: 50.1%) and a Q4 net profit of ~DKK 455–495m. **Leverage stays nil**: with ~DKK 1.7bn (~$251m) of cash and securities at year-end after Q4 distributions (FY25 year-end: DKK 1,672m), the group remains net cash, and the only balance-sheet event is the Elite Gaming completion, where we would expect a small gain or loss against DKK 72m of segment assets rather than anything material. For 2027 the structural set-up is a lottery annuity (~DKK 3.0bn BSI at ~73% EBIT margin) whose growth is a function of Eurojackpot luck and holiday products, a liberalised unit growing mid-to-high single digits with a sub-20% margin, and a regulatory regime (Spilpakke 1) that should narrow the marketing gap with international competitors; the risks skew to the lottery comp in Q4 and to direct-cost inflation in casino and poker. Note that mix shift toward Danske Licens Spil is structurally dilutive to group margin (18% vs 73%) even as it is the only source of top-line growth.

**What to watch next quarter:**

- Q4 lottery jackpot cycle: number of Eurojackpot high/max-pool (DKK 750m) draws and Lotto rollovers versus a record Q4-25 for Det Store Jule Lotto and Quick’s Christmas calendar — the single biggest swing factor for hitting the DKK 5,200m BSI floor.
- KFST clearance and completion of the Elite Gaming sale: timing (Q4-26 vs Q1-27), disclosed consideration, gain/loss versus DKK 72m (~$11m) of segment assets, and whether FY26 is restated on a continuing-operations basis.
- Danske Licens Spil versus the market: Spillemyndigheden’s October–December online-casino and betting GGR prints (Sep: +17.9% / +64.6%) against the unit’s +7.5% — any widening of the gap signals faster share loss; watch for the sports/casino split in the FY26 annual report (March 2027).
- Direct cost trajectory: ‘other game-related costs’ (+30% in Q3) relative to Danske Licens Spil BSI — if the ratio keeps rising, the 18–19% segment margin is at risk once marketing cuts are exhausted.
- Spilpakke 1 implementation detail for 2027 (marketing, bonus and affiliate rules, any duty changes) and the group’s ability to re-weight marketing within the new limits.
- Retail network: dealer count (3,464, −7.8% YoY) and the ~1,400-terminal roll-out; continued attrition would pressure Quick and terminal number games beyond the commission savings.
- Financial items on the DKK 832m securities portfolio (Q1 −25m, Q2 +26m, Q3 −17m) and the FY26 contribution to the udlodningsmidler (FY25: DKK 1,787m, ~$264m) in the March 2027 annual report.
