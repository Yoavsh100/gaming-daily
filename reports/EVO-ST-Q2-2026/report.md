# Evolution AB (EVO.ST) — Q2 2026 Analyst Report
**Nasdaq Stockholm · Reporting currency: EUR (tables in EUR millions unless stated; Evolution reports in EUR and lists in SEK on Nasdaq Stockholm) · Results released 17 Jul 2026**
**Market reaction:** Shares closed +0.3% at SEK 691.40 on 17 Jul 2026 (prior close SEK 689.60) after indicating c.-1.2% (SEK 681.4) pre-open; a muted reaction consistent with an in-line print (Yahoo Finance close; Investing.com pre-market). For context, the stock subsequently traded at SEK ~800-810 in early October 2026 ahead of the 23 Oct Q3 report (press/data snippets, secondary).

> An in-line, marginally light quarter that confirms the new shape of Evolution: a ~66%-margin cash machine whose growth now comes from the Americas and RNG, while Europe stabilises and Asia (37% of end-player revenue) stays hostage to content piracy. Net revenue fell 1.2% to EUR 517.8m (+2.4% constant currency) and EBITDA fell 1.2% to EUR 341.0m at a flat 65.9% margin; EPS still rose 4.7% because the EUR 2bn buyback and a financial-items swing did the work. Until Asia and Europe both grow, the equity case is capital return plus product optionality rather than compounding growth.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Marginally light vs consensus, effectively in line.** Q2-26 net revenue EUR 517.8m (-1.2% YoY, +0.9% QoQ) vs consensus EUR 520.0-522m (Gambling Insider / FactSet via Finwire); EBITDA EUR 341.0m vs EUR 342-343.5m (Infront/FactSet); EPS EUR 1.27 vs EUR 1.28. Swedish wires headlined it 'in line with expectations'. Shares closed +0.3% at SEK 691.40.
- **Margin held exactly where guided.** EBITDA margin 65.9% (65.9% Q2-25; 65.4% Q1-26). Total opex EUR 220.0m, +1.0% YoY but -0.2% QoQ: personnel -1.4% YoY to EUR 121.8m (23.5% of revenue), other opex -0.8% to EUR 55.0m (10.6%), D&A +11.1% to EUR 43.2m (8.3% of revenue vs 7.4%). The D&A step-up is why EBIT margin fell 90bps YoY to 57.5% while EBITDA margin was flat.
- **Guidance: no revenue guidance; FY26 EBITDA margin target of ~66% reiterated without qualification** ('The EBITDA margin for the full year remains, no question about that'). H1-26 delivered 65.6%, so the arithmetic requires ~66.4% in both H2 quarters (our calc, assuming H2 revenue EUR 1.04-1.06bn) - i.e. a repeat of Q3-25/Q4-25 levels (66.4% adj.), helped by Q4 seasonality.
- **Product mix is the story: Live -3.6% YoY to EUR 437.3m, RNG +14.1% to EUR 80.5m.** RNG posted its first double-digit growth in roughly three years (since early 2023) and its fourth consecutive sequential increase; management described it as broad-based, not one market or one title, but explicitly declined to guide that the pace holds.
- **Regional picture (end-player IP basis): Americas at all-time highs, Europe back to sequential growth, Asia down again.** Europe EUR 173.0m (+3.5% QoQ, -4.0% YoY, first QoQ growth after several quarters of decline); Asia EUR 190.5m (-3.7% QoQ, -8.9% YoY, cybercrime); North America EUR 81.0m (+2.9% QoQ, +9.5% YoY); LatAm EUR 47.5m (+1.5% QoQ, +26.3% YoY); Other/Africa EUR 25.8m (+14.2% QoQ, +10.3% YoY).
- **Regulated share rose to 49% (44% Q2-25, 48% Q1-26).** Regulated-market revenue EUR 256.1m (+10.4% YoY, +3.8% QoQ) vs non-regulated EUR 261.7m (-10.5% YoY, -1.7% QoQ). The regulated and grey books are now the same size; the former is growing double digits, the latter shrinking double digits, which is why group growth is a wash.
- **FX headwind eased but still masks underlying growth.** Constant-currency revenue growth +2.4% vs -1.2% reported = c.3.6pp (~EUR 19m) FX drag, down from c.8.3pp in Q1 (+6.8% cc vs -1.5% reported). CFO would not call the easing sustainable. The weak USD/LatAm currencies flatter the Americas' local-currency momentum relative to EUR-reported figures.
- **Cash generation strong and working capital is now a tailwind.** OCF EUR 293.0m (+30.0% YoY) despite EUR 86.0m tax paid (EUR 65.7m); OCF after capex EUR 258.4m (+34.3%); LTM cash conversion 86% (company). Accounts receivable released EUR 32.8m in the quarter (AR EUR 407.2m vs EUR 447.0m at YE25) after the CFO's collections push. Capex EUR 34.7m (intangibles EUR 16.0m, PPE EUR 18.7m), stable as % of revenue.
- **Balance sheet: EUR 1,153.5m cash, zero financial debt, EUR 90.2m lease liabilities.** Net cash after leases EUR 1,063m (~0.8x LTM adj. EBITDA of EUR 1,354.7m). Equity EUR 4,234.4m (77.4% equity/assets). The EUR 104m bond portfolio matured and was repaid into cash. A EUR 300m revolving credit facility was arranged alongside the buyback (press reports, 18 May).
- **Capital return: EUR 2bn buyback replaces the dividend.** Programme announced 18 May 2026 (management: largest ever on Nasdaq Stockholm). 5,141,528 shares (2.58% of share capital) bought for EUR 303.2m between 19 May and 30 June, implying ~EUR 59/share; 15% of the programme done in six weeks. No dividend paid in H1-26 vs EUR 572.5m (EUR 2.80/share) in H1-25. CFO says Q2 was 'maxed out' at the 25%-of-ADV constraint, so Q3/Q4 pace depends on market volume and price.
- **EPS growth is financially engineered, not operational.** Profit for the period +1.3% to EUR 251.4m; EPS +4.7% to EUR 1.27 on a 3.3% lower average share count (197.2m vs 203.9m) and a EUR 15.0m positive swing in net financial items (+EUR 3.1m vs -EUR 11.9m). ETR 16.4% (15.7%) on Pillar II top-up tax. Period-end shares 194.1m (-4.6% YoY).
- **UKGC licence review closed with a GBP 4.75m settlement (15 Jul).** Related mainly to Evolution content reachable on six unlicensed UK-facing sites via two operators, in breach of its terms of supply; the 18-19-month review found no broader pattern of unlicensed access. CEO called the amount 'a bit high in relation to the actual issue'; no change to UK operations now or planned. The regulator's own public statement followed on 23 Jul. Management also said it could not replicate the Investigate Europe allegation of UK-IP access via Soft2Bet.
- **Galaxy Gaming: deal dead after two years.** The outside date of the USD 85m (USD 3.20/share) agreement fell on 17 Jul; Evolution gave notice of termination on 21 Jul and, per press reports, owes a ~USD 5.2m termination fee. Management calls it immaterial to US plans; we agree on size, but it closes the land-based table-game adjacency and the two years of administrative effort yielded nothing.
- **No formal cost-transformation programme; cost control is studio-mix optimisation.** Headcount 22,880 (+2.8% YoY, flat QoQ); average FTE 16,534 (+3.6%). Second studios opened in Michigan (also home to the Ezugi brand) and Argentina (studio acquired from a competitor for EUR 6.0m, goodwill EUR 5.8m); Vilnius, Lithuania to open later in 2026. Management says each new studio improves 'distribution and cost mix' after a period of 'very unfavorable' mix; the proof is personnel cost falling YoY while FTEs rise.
- **Event effects: none.** Asked about the 2026 World Cup, the CFO said Evolution does not see a clear activity boost from World Cups or Olympics ('maybe the time zone'); nothing is expected in H2 either. The only engagement datapoint is the game-rounds index at an all-time high (an analyst put it ~12% above Q4-25; management did not dispute), which the CEO frames as faster sessions with smaller bets - volume up, monetisation per round down.
- **Product roadmap is back-end loaded.** Q2 saw the first Hasbro titles (Monopoly Roulette; Monopoly Roll'em post-period), Monopoly Live in NJ/DE/MI/CT, a localised Ice Fishing Brasil from the Sao Paulo studio, and Ice Fishing itself now 'one of the biggest games in the whole industry'. Disco Balls (Funky Time spin-off) is the major Q3 release. CEO conceded the roadmap 'had been a little bit tilted towards the end all along'.
- **Asia remains the swing factor and management is not quantifying it.** Revenue has oscillated EUR 189-210m per quarter since the cybercrime (table copying and restreaming) hit in Q3-25. Management: 'cat and mouse game, the volatility will remain', underlying demand intact, regulated Philippines growing nicely, competitive landscape unchanged, no intra-quarter comment. Q3-25 (EUR 189.1m) is an easy comp but the direction within the range is unknowable from outside.
- **What management emphasised vs buried.** Emphasised: the buyback ('largest ever'), RNG double-digit, Americas all-time highs, UKGC closure, cash flow. Buried: Live -3.6% got two sentences; no sizing of the Asia cyber impact; no comment on UK RGD impact beyond 'UK is not really a huge market for Evolution'; Galaxy 'will not comment further'; the CEO's own admission that Live growth should be better.
- **Other legal/structural items.** The New Jersey defamation suit against Calcagni & Kanefsky, Black Cube and Playtech proceeds through 2026. H1-26 earn-out payments of EUR 25.9m (NLC EUR 11.1m, BTG EUR 14.3m, Arcadia EUR 0.4m). Gross tax balances remain unusually large (current tax receivables EUR 550.1m vs current tax liabilities EUR 911.1m) and are not explained in the interim report.
- **Thesis read-through.** Eight consecutive quarters of revenue growth between -3.7% and +3.9% confirm Evolution has become an ex-growth compounder at the group level, with the mix migrating toward regulated Americas/RNG at the expense of grey Asia and taxed Europe. The 66% margin, ~EUR 1.1bn annual free cash flow and EUR 2bn buyback underpin EPS mid-single-digit growth even with a flat top line; re-rating requires Asia to stop falling and Europe's Q2 uptick to persist. Next catalyst: Q3 report 23 Oct 2026.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — Live Casino (84% of revenue)

*Live revenue EUR 437.3m, -3.6% YoY / +0.6% QoQ: a four-quarter plateau at EUR 432-439m, with the YoY decline 'mostly' Europe and Asia piracy capping the upside.*

### Live revenue plateau

**Live revenue EUR 437.3m, -3.6% YoY / +0.6% QoQ, a four-quarter plateau at EUR 432-439m rather than a decline.**

- H1 Live EUR 872.2m (-3.3%); trailing-twelve-month Live EUR 1,742.4m, 1.7% below prior year.
- Live is 84.5% of group revenue versus 86.5% a year ago.
- Management attributed the YoY decline 'mostly' to Europe; no product-level constant-currency figure given.
- With group +2.4% constant currency and RNG +14%, Live was probably around flat in local currencies (our inference, unverified).
- Sequence since Q3-25: EUR 431.7m, 438.6m, 434.9m, 437.3m. Market share, handle, actives and ARPU not disclosed; we do not estimate them.

> “I think we can always do better. I expect more when it comes to live growth.” — CEO

### Content and game rounds

**The content engine works where pointed, but product contribution to revenue is a Q4 and 2027 story, not Q3.**

- Europe game-show category 'did really good'; demand for localised native-speaking tables rising; Ice Fishing is one of the biggest games in the industry per the CEO.
- Hasbro delivered Monopoly Roulette (June) and Monopoly Roll'em (just after quarter end) with 'very strong' initial response; Monopoly Live launched in New Jersey, Delaware, Michigan and Connecticut.
- Ice Fishing Brasil streams from the expanded Sao Paulo studio; major Q3 release is Disco Balls, a Funky Time spin-off.
- Game-rounds index hit an all-time high (roughly 12% above Q4-25 per an analyst, unchallenged) while revenue was flat; sessions faster with smaller bets.
- We read this as a mix shift toward game shows and lower-stake formats: good for retention and reach, dilutive to revenue per round.

> “had been a little bit tilted towards the end all along” — CEO

### Studios and costs

**Capacity is being added in lower-cost locations; personnel expense fell 1.4% to EUR 121.8m but D&A rose 11.1%, cutting EBIT margin 90bps to 57.5%.**

- Second Michigan studio opened, hosting state game shows and the Ezugi brand; second Argentine studio (acquired Q1 for EUR 6.0m, EUR 5.8m goodwill) relaunched around quarter end.
- Vilnius, Lithuania scheduled for later in 2026; Alberta added in July following Canada's newest provincial regulation.
- Headcount 22,880 (+2.8% YoY, flat QoQ); average FTEs 16,534 (+3.6%); personnel expense 23.5% of revenue.
- D&A EUR 43.2m (8.3% of revenue vs 7.4%) is the only cost line growing faster than revenue; EBITDA margin held at 65.9%.
- Management says each new studio is 'optimizing the distribution and cost mix' after a 'very unfavorable cost mix'.

### Asia piracy

**Asia Live, EUR 190.5m, fell 3.7% QoQ and 8.9% YoY on piracy, with no sizing, no intra-quarter comment and no direction given.**

- CEO: pirates copy and redistribute the product 'just like they would do with ... a handbag'.
- Management: 'the volatility is still here. We are not in a stable situation'.
- Positives: underlying regional demand has growth potential, regulated Philippine market 'growing nicely', regulatory picture stable, competitive landscape unchanged.
- We treat Asia Live as a EUR 185-200m per quarter range-bound line with downside skew until two consecutive quarters say otherwise.

> “they take our product, make a copy, and redistribute it ... just like they would do with ... a handbag” — CEO

### Outlook

**No Live-specific guidance; soft comps make flat-to-low-single-digit H2 Live growth achievable without an Asia recovery; we pencil EUR 438-442m for Q3 (estimate).**

- Group targets growth faster than the global online casino market and, per the Q2 slide deck, in line with the market in each region in Live and RNG.
- Q3-25 Live EUR 431.7m was the first cyber-hit quarter; Q4-25 EUR 438.6m is similarly undemanding.
- Structural question of whether Live can grow in Europe at 50% channelisation and rising duties is unanswered by this quarter.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — RNG (16% of revenue)

*RNG EUR 80.5m, +14.1% YoY and a fourth straight sequential rise - first double-digit growth since early 2023 - but it is too small to offset Live.*

### RNG growth

**RNG revenue EUR 80.5m, +14.1% YoY and +3.0% QoQ, the fourth straight sequential rise and first double-digit growth since early 2023.**

- H1 RNG EUR 158.7m (+11.1%); trailing twelve months EUR 309.8m (+5.4%).
- Sequence since Q2-25 trough: EUR 70.6m, 75.5m, 75.7m, 78.2m, 80.5m.
- RNG share of group revenue up to 15.5% from 13.5%.
- Berenberg dated the last double-digit print to the start of 2023.

> “this quarter is the quarter of double-digit growth for RNG, it took a while, we're here now, we're proud of that because we stated that we were going to do that.” — Management

### Growth drivers

**Growth is broad-based across the RNG portfolio rather than from one market or release, per the CEO.**

- North American slots 'performs well at the moment' but 'we shouldn't overvalue North America ... still a small market in comparison to others'.
- Growth is 'from the total situation in RNG, not any specific market' and not any single release.
- Interpretation: One Stop Shop distribution and the multi-studio portfolio (NetEnt, Red Tiger, Big Time Gaming, Nolimit City, Sneaky Slots) are broadening the base.
- Q2 Hasbro launches are Live formats; RNG Hasbro titles still to come.
- Earn-out cash of EUR 25.9m paid in H1 (NLC EUR 11.1m, BTG EUR 14.3m, Arcadia EUR 0.4m).

### Guidance and comps

**Management refused to guide that double-digit growth holds; harder comps mean we model EUR 82-84m for Q3-26 (+9-11% YoY, estimate).**

- Q3-25 was EUR 75.5m and Q4-25 EUR 75.7m, so holding +10% requires EUR 83-84m in Q3 and Q4.
- That implies roughly EUR 1.5m sequential growth per quarter, below the H1 run-rate of EUR 2.3-2.5m per quarter.

> “I won't guide on that, but now we took a good step forward” — CEO

### Investment relevance

**At 15.5% of group, 14% RNG growth adds roughly EUR 10m per quarter, about 2 percentage points to group growth, and cannot offset Live's EUR 16m YoY decline.**

- RNG profitability is not disclosed; we make no assumption on it.
- Structural signal is positive: a three-year drag is now a consistent contributor.
- RNG is predominantly regulated-market revenue, where the group mix is heading.

## 5. Segment deep-dive — Regional mix (group revenue by end-player IP; customer-location basis also shown)

*Americas at all-time highs (+9.5% / +26.3% YoY), Europe +3.5% QoQ but -4.0% YoY, Asia -8.9% YoY on piracy; regulated share up to 49%.*

### Europe

**Europe EUR 173.0m (33% of end-player revenue), +3.5% QoQ after several quarters of decline but -4.0% YoY; CEO declined to call an inflection.**

- Prior three quarters: EUR 182.2m, 177.6m, 167.1m. Drivers: strong game shows, native-speaking tables, 'increasing activity in regulated markets'.
- Channelisation 'very weak in a number of jurisdictions'; duties pushing channelisation toward 50% in the UK and Netherlands 'is not good'.
- On UK Remote Gaming Duty increase from April: UK 'is not really a huge market for Evolution. Even with an impact, it's not that big.'
- By customer location Europe fell 10.2% YoY to EUR 344.7m while Asia-located customers jumped from EUR 1.3m to EUR 17.7m, indicating re-domiciling; player-IP series (-4.0%) is the better read.
- UKGC settlement (GBP 4.75m, 15 Jul) changes nothing operationally; ring-fencing already in place, no further restrictions agreed.

> “I wish that I had a clear answer” — CEO

### Asia

**Asia EUR 190.5m (37% of end-player revenue), -3.7% QoQ and -8.9% YoY on piracy; this remains the single largest risk to revenue and the multiple.**

- Quarterly path since the cybercrime problem: EUR 209.1m (Q2-25), 189.1m, 193.6m, 197.8m, 190.5m.
- Management calls unauthorised restreaming a 'cat and mouse game' it is 'certain that we will eventually win' but will not time; no sizing or post-quarter comment.
- Positives: underlying demand has growth potential, regulated Philippines growing nicely, regulation stable, competition unchanged.
- With group regulated share at 49%, the bulk of Asia is grey-market revenue; investors are asked to take direction on trust.

> “I don't guide on quarter-on-quarter growth for any region” — CEO

### North America

**North America hit a record EUR 81.0m (16% of revenue), +2.9% QoQ and +9.5% YoY by player IP, +10.8% by customer location.**

- Monopoly Live launched in New Jersey, Delaware, Michigan and Connecticut; second Michigan studio hosts state game shows and Ezugi; Alberta live in July.
- RNG slots singled out as performing well in the region.
- Galaxy Gaming termination (notice 21 Jul; ~USD 5.2m fee per press reports) removes the land-based adjacency; USD 85m equity value is immaterial next to EUR 1.15bn cash.

### Latin America and other

**Latin America set a record EUR 47.5m (9% of revenue), +1.5% QoQ and +26.3% YoY, still the fastest-growing region despite decelerating from +29.3% in Q1.**

- Drivers: regulated Brazilian market, localised Ice Fishing Brasil and the second Argentine studio; FX headwind is material in EUR terms.
- Other regions, mainly Africa, grew 14.2% QoQ and 10.3% YoY to EUR 25.8m, with a 'strong position' in premium Live and rising RNG activity.

### Regulated mix and FX

**Regulated revenue rose 10.4% YoY to EUR 256.1m (49% vs 44%) while non-regulated fell 10.5% to EUR 261.7m, which is why group revenue is flat.**

- The two books are now the same size and moving in opposite directions at similar speed.
- Constant-currency growth of 2.4% versus -1.2% reported implies c.3.6pp (~EUR 19m) FX drag, down from c.8.3pp in Q1; CFO would not call the easing sustainable.
- 2026 World Cup produced no discernible activity boost ('maybe the time zone'); none expected in H2.
- Mobile accounted for 75% of operators' GGR (73%).

## 6. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- RNG +14.1% YoY to EUR 80.5m, first double-digit growth since early 2023 and a fourth consecutive sequential rise; management says it is broad-based rather than one market or title. _(structural)_
- Europe returned to sequential growth (+3.5% QoQ to EUR 173.0m) after several quarters of decline, driven by game shows and native-speaking tables; regulated-market activity rising. _(early)_
- North America (+9.5% YoY, EUR 81.0m) and LatAm (+26.3% YoY, EUR 47.5m) both at all-time highs; Monopoly Live in four US states, Alberta added in July, Brazil localisation and a second Argentine studio. _(structural)_
- Regulated share of revenue 49% (44% a year ago); regulated revenue +10.4% YoY. The quality of the revenue base keeps improving even as the headline is flat. _(structural)_
- Cost discipline is real: total opex -0.2% QoQ, personnel -1.4% YoY despite +3.6% FTEs, EBITDA margin 65.9% held and FY26 ~66% target reiterated without hedging. _(structural)_
- Cash: OCF +30% to EUR 293.0m, AR released EUR 32.8m, EUR 1.15bn cash with zero financial debt; EUR 303m (5.1m shares, 2.6% of capital) bought back in six weeks under the EUR 2bn programme, which also removes the dividend-vs-buyback overhang. _(structural)_
- UKGC review closed with a GBP 4.75m settlement, no broader pattern of unlicensed access found and no new operating restrictions; a 19-month overhang removed at low cost. _(one-off)_

**Lowlights**

- Asia (37% of end-player revenue) -3.7% QoQ and -8.9% YoY on content piracy; management will not size the impact, comment intra-quarter or call a direction, and conceded 'we are not in a stable situation'. _(structural)_
- Live, 84% of the group, fell 3.6% YoY to EUR 437.3m and has been flat at EUR 432-439m for four quarters; the CEO himself said 'I expect more when it comes to live growth'. _(structural)_
- Europe still -4.0% YoY with channelisation near 50% in named markets (UK, Netherlands) and the UK RGD increase live from April; management explicitly refused to call Q2 an inflection point. _(structural)_
- Slight miss on every line (revenue EUR 517.8m vs EUR 520-522m consensus; EBITDA EUR 341.0m vs EUR 342-343.5m; EPS EUR 1.27 vs 1.28); eighth consecutive quarter of growth between -3.7% and +3.9%. _(mixed)_
- EPS growth (+4.7%) is entirely from a 3.3% lower share count and a EUR 15m financial-items swing; profit grew 1.3% and EBIT fell 2.8% as D&A rose 11% (EBIT margin -90bps to 57.5%). _(watch)_
- Galaxy Gaming terminated after two years and a ~USD 5.2m fee: immaterial in size but a visible failure of execution on US regulatory approvals that consumed 'significant time, effort and resources'. _(one-off)_
- Governance/compliance noise persists: UKGC public statement (23 Jul), Investigate Europe allegations of UK-IP access via Soft2Bet (management could not replicate), ongoing NJ litigation vs Playtech/Black Cube, and unexplained gross tax balances (EUR 550m receivable / EUR 911m liability). _(governance)_

## 7. Forecast & Outlook

Evolution does not guide revenue; its only formal 2026 guidance is an EBITDA margin of about 66% for the full year, set with the Q4-25 report and reiterated unconditionally on 17 July ('The EBITDA margin for the full year remains, no question about that'). Nothing changed, so there is no division-driven bridge to reconcile; the moving parts are instead (i) H1-26 printed 65.6%, 40bps below the target, so H2 must average ~66.4% (our calc, H2 revenue EUR 1.04-1.06bn), which is what Q3-25 and Q4-25 delivered (66.4% adj. each) with Q4 seasonality helping; (ii) the CFO was explicit that costs will rise if revenue rises ('we will then maintain the 66% margin target'), so margin upside is capped by design; (iii) the product roadmap and the Vilnius studio are back-end loaded, adding modest H2 cost; and (iv) 'about 66%' gives management ~30-40bps of latitude either way.

- **FY26 EBITDA margin guidance:** ~66% (unchanged) (Reiterated 17 Jul; set at Q4-25 report)
- **H1-26 delivered:** 65.6% (-20bps YoY; Q2 65.9%, Q1 65.4%)
- **Implied H2-26 margin to hit 66%:** ~66.4% (+50bps vs Q2; matches Q3-25/Q4-25 levels (our calc))
- **Q3-26E net revenue (our estimate):** EUR 520-525m (+2.5-3.5% YoY vs EUR 507.1m (cyber-hit comp))

Our Q3-26 base case (all estimates): net revenue **EUR ~522m** (Live EUR ~440m roughly +2% YoY against the first cyber-hit comp of EUR 431.7m; RNG EUR ~82-83m, +9-10%), EBITDA **EUR ~345m at ~66.0%**, profit ~EUR 255m and EPS ~EUR 1.32-1.34 on an average share count of ~192m if Q3 buybacks run at EUR 250-300m. The YoY revenue comparison turns positive almost mechanically because Q3-25 absorbed the initial Asia hit and Q4-25 (EUR 514.2m) is also soft, so a return to reported growth in H2 says little about underlying momentum - watch sequential Asia (range EUR 185-200m) and whether Europe holds above EUR 170m. For FY26 we see revenue **EUR ~2.08-2.10bn (+1% YoY)**, EBITDA **EUR ~1.37-1.39bn at ~66%** and EPS **EUR ~5.2-5.3**, flat-to-up on FY25's reported EUR 5.24 (which included a EUR 51.7m earn-out reversal) with the share count doing the work. Leverage direction: net cash falls from EUR 1.15bn as buybacks (~EUR 1.0-1.2bn annualised at the Q2 pace, volume-constrained at 25% of ADV) run slightly ahead of ~EUR 1.0-1.1bn of post-tax, post-capex cash generation; we expect year-end cash of EUR ~0.9-1.0bn with the EUR 300m RCF undrawn, i.e. the balance sheet stays net cash through the programme. Swing factors, in order: Asia piracy (+/- EUR 10-15m per quarter on revenue, near-100% drop-through), European channelisation and duty (UK RGD, Netherlands), FX (USD, BRL, ARS vs EUR; c.3.6pp drag in Q2), the H2 launch cadence (Disco Balls, further Hasbro titles) and Q4 seasonality. The risk to our numbers is asymmetric to the downside on revenue (Asia) and symmetric on margin (management has shown it will flex costs).

**What to watch next quarter:**

- Asia revenue in the Q3 report (23 Oct 2026): a print below EUR 185m would mean the piracy drag is worsening; above EUR 195m would validate management's 'we will eventually win' framing.
- Whether Europe holds sequential growth (>EUR 173m) through the UK RGD step-up and ahead of the Vilnius studio launch; any management willingness to call an inflection.
- RNG staying double-digit against EUR 75.5m / 75.7m comps in Q3/Q4 (needs EUR 83-84m).
- Buyback cadence and average price: Q2 was EUR 303m at ~EUR 59/share at the 25%-of-ADV cap; a higher share price mechanically slows share-count shrinkage per euro.
- H2 EBITDA margin >=66.4% with D&A still growing ~11%; EBIT margin trajectory (57.5%) matters more for EPS than EBITDA.
- Compliance tail: the UKGC public statement's implications, the Investigate Europe/Soft2Bet allegations, the NJ litigation vs Playtech/Black Cube, and any explanation of the EUR 550m / EUR 911m gross tax balances.
- Post-period shareholder developments (press reports of founder share sales and of a proposal to cancel repurchased shares) - noted but unverified at the time of writing.
