# Bragg Gaming Group () — Q3 2026 Analyst Report
** · Reporting currency: EUR (tables in millions; USD shown at EUR/USD 1.1355, the company's Q3-26 rate) · Results released 13 Nov 2026**
**Market reaction:** Nasdaq/TSX-listed BRAG fell on 13 Nov 2026: same-day snapshots ranged from -6.2% (Motley Fool) to -9.9% (Investing.com, which headlined an 8.9% drop); the official closing level could not be verified [unverified]. Casino.org puts the shares ~76% below their January-2026 peak. For scale, the 30 Sep share price used in the company's warrant valuation was USD 1.25 (CAD 1.70), implying a market capitalisation of ~USD 39m on 31.1m shares.

> A shrinking-but-cleaner quarter: revenue fell 23% to EUR 20.6m (~$23.4m) as the Dutch platform migrations removed EUR 6.2m (~$7.1m) on their own, while the mix shift away from aggregated third-party content lifted gross margin 155bps to 56%. Adj. EBITDA still dropped 33% to EUR 3.0m (~$3.4m) because EUR 1.6m (~$1.8m) of cost savings covered only half of the EUR 3.1m (~$3.5m) gross-profit loss, and cash is down to EUR 2.5m (~$2.8m) with capitalised development running ahead of operating cash flow. With the CEO replaced a week before the print and no guidance for FY26 or FY27, the case now rests on the interim CEO's cash discipline rather than growth.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Headline miss on a shrinking base.** Q3-26 revenue EUR 20.6m (~$23.4m), -23.0% YoY and -9.9% QoQ; gross profit EUR 11.6m (~$13.2m), -20.8%; adj. EBITDA EUR 3.0m (~$3.4m), -32.5%; net loss EUR 4.3m (~$4.9m) vs EUR 2.3m (~$2.7m). Yahoo Finance reports the print missed consensus on both revenue and EPS [consensus figures unverified].
- **The entire YoY decline is the Netherlands.** Revenue from Dutch-contracted customers fell to EUR 0.1m (~$0.1m) from EUR 6.4m (~$7.5m), a EUR 6.2m drop versus a EUR 6.2m group decline. BetCity (EUR 4.1m in Q3-25, zero now) explains two-thirds; the remaining ~EUR 2.2m is other migrations off the Dutch PAM. There is no longer any >10% customer.
- **Normalized revenue shows the first sequential uptick.** Ex-BetCity and ex-Drayton, revenue was EUR 20.0m (~$22.7m): -11.9% YoY but +3.7% QoQ from EUR 19.3m (~$21.9m) in Q2-26, after EUR 21.0m in Q1-26. Management is leaning on this as evidence that the like-for-like base has bottomed; one quarter does not make a trend.
- **Mix shift is the real structural positive.** Low-margin third-party aggregated content fell to 39% of revenue from 52%; proprietary content rose to 19% from 16% and exclusive content to 23% from 14%. Cost of revenue fell to 43.8% of revenue from 45.3%, lifting gross margin to 56.2% (+155bps YoY, +~450bps vs 52% in Q2-26).
- **But gross profit fell faster than costs.** Gross profit dropped EUR 3.1m (~$3.5m) YoY while operating cost savings delivered were ~EUR 1.6m (~$1.8m); adj. EBITDA margin compressed 204bps to 14.5% (company rounds to 15% vs 17%). Unadjusted EBITDA was only EUR 1.1m (~$1.3m), -72%, with EUR 1.9m (~$2.1m) of add-backs equal to 62% of adj. EBITDA.
- **Cost-transformation programme: two rounds, 27% of headcount.** The 8 Jan 2026 and 9 Jul 2026 (-19%) restructurings have cut the global workforce ~27% YTD (to ~390 per call coverage) and target ~EUR 10.5m (~$11.9m) of annualised cash savings, up from EUR 4.5m (~$5.1m) flagged at end-2025. Salaries and subcontractors fell 30% YoY on an underlying basis (reported EUR 5.7m vs EUR 6.8m includes EUR 1.1m (~$1.2m) of termination costs treated as exceptional). July savings phase in through Q4-26 and into 2027.
- **No guidance, old or new.** The release contains no numeric FY26 guidance and management declined to give 2027 guidance on the call, saying it needs time to set realistic expectations after the leadership change; the only outlook statement is that operating conditions are unchanged other than Brazil. We model Q4-26E revenue EUR 18.5-19.5m (~$21-22m) and adj. EBITDA EUR 2.5-3.5m (~$2.8-4.0m) (estimates, see Forecast).
- **Brazil is gone from Q4.** A presidential provisional measure signed 25 Sep 2026 suspended licensed online betting/casino; bragg ceased supply on 6 Oct. Brazil-contracted revenue was EUR 2.0m (~$2.3m) in Q3 (9.6% of group) and EUR 7.4m (~$8.4m) for 9M. It is predominantly low-margin aggregated content, so the gross-profit and cash hit is far smaller than the revenue hit; management says gross margin improved in the first month after cessation. EUR 0.6m (~$0.7m) of Brazil-related impairments (IP, certification prepayments, a Brazil-focused associate) were booked below operating loss.
- **North American proprietary content growth decelerated sharply.** Revenue from proprietary content deployed in Canada and the US grew 7% YoY in Q3 versus 44% in Q2 (per call coverage) and 21% for 9M. US-contracted revenue in the geographic note fell 25% to EUR 2.3m (~$2.6m), though that cut is by contracting jurisdiction, not end-user location.
- **Drayton International closed 22 Jul 2026, all in paper.** 4.5m shares (USD 9.0m headline; EUR 6.6m (~$7.5m) fair value at close) plus 180k shares for deal costs; EUR 0.7m (~$0.7m) of acquisition costs expensed. Drayton contributed EUR 0.65m (~$0.7m) revenue and EUR 0.3m (~$0.4m) PBT in ten weeks; pro forma 9M revenue would have been EUR 2.4m (~$2.8m). It brings five game studios, three technology platforms and an ADW horse-racing wagering entry point; goodwill EUR 3.7m (~$4.1m), NCI EUR 1.0m (~$1.1m).
- **Related-party angle on the deal.** Note 16 shows EUR 2.9m (~$3.2m) of the Drayton consideration shares (43% of the EUR 6.6m) and EUR 0.45m (~$0.5m) of the concurrent private placement went to related parties. Chair Matt Davey described himself on the call as having joined the board this year as a significant investor. Share count is 31.1m vs 25.6m at 1 Jan 2026 (+22%).
- **Cash is the binding constraint.** Cash fell to EUR 2.5m (~$2.8m) from EUR 6.7m (~$7.6m) at Dec-25. 9M operating cash flow was EUR 6.0m (~$6.8m), -37% YoY, against EUR 10.4m (~$11.8m) of capitalised development (+9% YoY while revenue fell 12%), EUR 1.0m (~$1.2m) of lease repayments and EUR 1.8m (~$2.1m) of tax paid. Our FCF definition (OCF less capex less leases) was -EUR 5.6m (~$6.3m) for 9M vs -EUR 1.3m (~$1.5m) a year ago.
- **Balance sheet: low debt, thin liquidity.** CAD 5.2m (EUR 3.3m / ~$3.7m) is drawn on a USD 6m BMO revolver (no Q3 drawdowns); net debt ex-leases is EUR 0.8m (~$0.9m), EUR 3.9m (~$4.5m) including EUR 3.1m of leases. Covenants (Total Funded Debt/EBITDA <=2.5x; FCCR >=1.25x) were met at 30 Sep. Annualised 9M adj. EBITDA of ~EUR 14m puts net debt/EBITDA at ~0.1x (estimate), but headroom on the revolver is only ~USD 2m (estimate).
- **CFO sets his own yardstick.** Bressler: capitalised development spend 'has been running ahead of our operational cash generation' and closing that gap is the measure he expects to be held to over the next several quarters. That is the right metric; it also implies a lower content-release cadence.
- **Leadership transition a week before results.** On 6 Nov 2026 Morten Tonnesen (COO since Mar-26) became interim CEO and founder-CEO Matevz Mazij moved to Strategic Advisor. Tonnesen's stated priorities: (1) cash - balance investment with operating cash generation, be more selective in what is built; (2) proprietary content - 'most profitable product' with the 'clearest right to win', with Drayton adding studio capacity; (3) focus/simplification; and operator delivery with AI-driven efficiency. The Chair said 2027 planning is already under way.
- **Quality-of-earnings flags.** Q3 exceptional costs were EUR 2.0m (~$2.3m), of which EUR 1.1m is termination costs - the remaining ~EUR 0.9m is not itemised. 9M exceptional and transaction costs total EUR 4.4m (~$5.0m). D&A of EUR 5.0m (~$5.7m) is 24% of revenue, mostly amortisation of past capitalised development, so adj. EBITDA materially overstates cash earnings.
- **Receivables concentration has emerged.** Three customers each exceed 10% of trade receivables (none at Dec-25); gross receivables of EUR 18.2m (~$20.6m) equate to ~76 days of Q3 revenue (estimate). Post-suspension collectability of Brazilian balances is a watch item; the ECL provision was cut to EUR 0.9m from EUR 1.5m.
- **Equity is mostly intangible.** Goodwill plus intangibles of EUR 65.5m (~$74.4m) are 69% of total assets against a market capitalisation of ~USD 39m at the 30 Sep share price of USD 1.25 (per the warrant valuation inputs). No triggering event was identified for goodwill in 9M; that judgement will be tested at the FY26 year-end.
- **Share price.** BRAG fell on the day - snapshots ranged from -6.2% (Motley Fool) to -9.9% (Investing.com, which headlined an 8.9% decline); closing level unverified. Casino.org puts the stock ~76% below its January-2026 peak.
- **Thesis read-through.** The product story (own content, higher margin, lighter aggregation) is coherent and visible in the gross margin, but the company is still shrinking faster than it is cutting, and adj. EBITDA of ~EUR 14m annualised roughly equals annual capitalised development, leaving true cash earnings near zero. The equity is an option on (i) capex discipline turning adj. EBITDA into cash, (ii) North American proprietary growth re-accelerating with Drayton, and (iii) a credible 2027 plan under a permanent CEO.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — B2B iGaming - sole reportable segment: product mix (content vs platform)

*Aggregation is being deliberately shrunk, own and exclusive content is now 42% of revenue and gross margin is 56%, but the platform book has lost its Dutch anchor and cost take-out lags the gross-profit loss.*

### Product mix shift

**Aggregation is being deliberately shrunk: third-party content fell to 39% of revenue from 52%, while own and exclusive content reached 42%.**

- Proprietary (bragg studios) content rose to 19% from 16%; exclusive content rose to 23% from 14%.
- Implied third-party content roughly EUR 8.0m (~$9.1m), down ~42% YoY; proprietary ~EUR 3.9m (~$4.5m), down high-single digits.
- Implied exclusive content ~EUR 4.7m (~$5.4m), up ~26%; platform/PAM/turnkey-and-other ~EUR 3.9m (~$4.5m), down ~19%; tolerance ~EUR 0.1-0.15m per line.
- Global proprietary content looks flat-to-down despite North American proprietary revenue growing 7%, pointing to proprietary losses in the Netherlands and Brazil.

### Gross margin

**Gross margin rose to 56.2%, up 155bps YoY and ~450bps from Q2-26's 52%, the quarter's one unambiguous positive, but gross profit fell EUR 3.1m.**

- Cost of revenue fell 25.7% to EUR 9.0m (~$10.3m), 43.8% of revenue versus 45.3%, on higher proprietary content and lower aggregated volumes.
- Gross profit fell EUR 3.1m (~$3.5m) to EUR 11.6m (~$13.2m); mix did ~1.5 points of work but volume took 21% of gross profit dollars.
- Gross profit, not margin, pays the salaries.
- Brazil's exit should push the ratio higher in Q4; management said gross margin improved in the first month after supply ceased.
- We estimate Q4 gross margin lands in the 58-60% range.

### Operating costs

**The cost base is coming down but not fast enough: ~EUR 1.6m (~$1.8m) savings delivered against a EUR 3.1m gross-profit decline.**

- Salaries and subcontractors EUR 5.7m (~$6.4m) vs EUR 6.8m (~$7.9m), -16% reported, ~30% underlying excluding EUR 1.1m (~$1.2m) termination costs.
- IT and hosting EUR 1.4m (~$1.6m), -5%; professional fees +14% to EUR 1.4m (~$1.5m); sales and marketing EUR 35k, 0.2% of revenue vs 0.8%.
- D&A EUR 5.0m (~$5.7m) is 24% of revenue, EUR 4.5m of it amortisation of intangibles, overwhelmingly past capitalised game and platform development.
- Total SG&A fell only 5.2% to EUR 15.0m (~$17.1m), rising to 73% of revenue from 59%.
- The savings-vs-gross-profit gap is the whole story of the 204bps adj. EBITDA margin compression to 14.5%.

### Restructuring

**January and July programmes removed ~27% of the workforce, targeting ~EUR 10.5m (~$11.9m) annualised savings against Q3 cash operating costs of ~EUR 8.6m.**

- The July round alone was a 19% cut; Q3 cash operating costs of ~EUR 8.6m (~$9.8m) implied by gross profit less adj. EBITDA.
- Delivered savings of EUR 1.6m a quarter annualise to ~EUR 6.4m, so roughly EUR 1m a quarter of further benefit should phase in through Q4 and 2027.
- Exceptional costs were EUR 2.0m (~$2.3m) in Q3 and EUR 3.7m (~$4.2m) for 9M; only EUR 1.1m termination costs itemised.
- About EUR 0.9m of Q3 add-backs is unexplained; we would want MD&A detail before giving full credit to the 15% adjusted margin.

### Drayton acquisition

**Drayton, the only growth lever management can point to, contributed EUR 0.65m (~$0.7m) revenue and EUR 0.31m (~$0.4m) PBT in ten weeks.**

- Consolidated from 22 July; PBT margin well above the group's.
- Pro forma nine months would have added EUR 2.4m (~$2.8m) revenue but only EUR 0.2m PBT, so the Q3 margin may flatter.
- Brings five game studios (Boomerang, Rise, Dream Streak, Neotopia stakes and US LLCs), three technology platforms and an advance-deposit-wagering entry into US horse racing.
- Thesis is proprietary studio capacity for North America, where proprietary content revenue grew 7% in Q3 and 21% for 9M.
- Deceleration from 44% in Q2 was raised by analysts and is the number to watch in Q4.

> “paid their own way from day one” — CFO

### Guidance and outlook

**No segment guidance; management's focus on proprietary content and selectivity implies fewer releases and a slower content pipeline in 2027.**

- Management reiterated proprietary content is its most profitable product with the clearest right to win, and more development capacity will be allocated to it.
- CFO committed to bring capitalised development (EUR 10.4m / ~$11.8m for 9M, +9% YoY) back below operating cash generation.
- Sportsbook-style metrics (handle, hold, promo intensity, actives, ARPU) are not applicable to this B2B model and not disclosed.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — Geographic mix - revenue by contracted-customer jurisdiction (Note 19)

*The Netherlands has gone from a quarter of revenue to nil, Brazil (9.6% of Q3) drops out from 6 October, and the growth pockets - Czech Republic, Isle of Man, Malta, Belgium, Croatia - are too small to offset.*

### Netherlands

**Netherlands revenue fell to EUR 0.1m (~$0.1m) from EUR 6.4m (~$7.5m) in Q3-25; the BetCity roll-off is complete and the base resets from Q4-26.**

- Note 19 cuts revenue by contracting-customer jurisdiction, not end-player; management warns the split is not correlated with the end-user base.
- 9M-26 Netherlands revenue EUR 8.5m (~$9.6m) versus EUR 15.4m (~$17.5m).
- BetCity, the >10% customer migrating to its Entain-owned platform, contributed EUR 4.1m (~$4.9m) in Q3-25, EUR 4.7m in Q1-26, EUR 3.6m in Q2-26 and nothing in Q3-26.
- A further ~EUR 2.2m of Dutch revenue disappeared as other customers left the Dutch PAM.
- The single >10% customer generated EUR 8.3m (~$9.4m) in 9M-26 and nil in Q3.

### Brazil

**Brazil-contracted revenue of EUR 2.0m (~$2.3m), 9.6% of Q3, falls to zero in Q4 after the 25 September provisional measure.**

- Q3 was only 5% lower YoY, but 9M was EUR 7.4m (~$8.4m), -20%, reflecting third-party content providers shifting to direct operator integrations.
- Operators required to stop on 6 October.
- Cash consequence is downplayed by management and we think correctly: the book was mostly aggregated content on which bragg keeps a thin spread.
- Impairment was EUR 0.6m (~$0.7m): EUR 0.12m IP, EUR 0.05m certification prepaids and EUR 0.41m for a Brazil-focused associate.
- Three customers now exceed 10% of trade receivables where none did at December; we treat any Brazilian balances as at risk until collected.

### United States and offshore

**US-contracted revenue fell 25% YoY to EUR 2.3m (~$2.6m), sitting awkwardly with 7% growth in North American proprietary content.**

- 9M US revenue EUR 6.8m (~$7.7m), -9%.
- Reconciliation likely a mix of lower third-party content to US operators and North American contracts booked through non-US entities; the company has not reconciled the two and we flag the gap.
- Curacao, a proxy for grey-market and offshore aggregation, fell 39% to EUR 1.6m (~$1.8m); Marshall Islands fell 26% to EUR 1.2m (~$1.3m).
- Both declines are consistent with the stated retreat from low-margin aggregation volume.

### Growth pockets

**Offsets are real but small; everything outside the Netherlands was down roughly EUR 0.1m YoY, so the ex-Dutch, ex-Brazil business is approximately flat.**

- Malta, the largest line at 25% of revenue, grew 12% to EUR 5.1m (~$5.8m) (9M -2%).
- Czech Republic more than doubled to EUR 1.2m (~$1.4m) and Isle of Man doubled to EUR 1.1m (~$1.3m), both up ~66% for 9M, pointing to newer regulated-market PAM/content wins.
- Belgium +9% to EUR 1.4m (~$1.5m) and Croatia +7% to EUR 1.2m (~$1.3m).
- Other rose 12% to EUR 3.6m (~$4.1m), where Drayton's EUR 0.65m first appears.
- Flat ex-Dutch, ex-Brazil matches the +3.7% QoQ in normalized revenue, the best that can be said for the top line.

### Guidance and assets

**No regional guidance beyond the Brazil cessation; the non-current asset split moved sharply and warrants a question to IR.**

- United States non-current assets EUR 42.7m (~$48.5m) from EUR 61.7m; Rest of World EUR 28.0m (~$31.7m) from EUR 6.0m.
- Reads as a reallocation of goodwill/intangibles rather than any disposal.
- Goodwill impairment testing is done at the cash-generating-unit level and the FY26 test will be conducted against a market capitalisation of roughly USD 39m.

## 5. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- Gross margin 56.2%, +155bps YoY and ~450bps above Q2-26, as third-party aggregated content fell to 39% of revenue from 52% and proprietary plus exclusive content rose to 42% from 30%; Brazil's exit should push Q4 higher still (management: margin improved in the first month post-cessation). _(structural)_
- Normalized revenue (ex-BetCity/Drayton) EUR 20.0m (~$22.7m) rose 3.7% QoQ - the first sequential increase this year - and revenue ex-Netherlands/Brazil was ~flat YoY at EUR 18.5m (~$21.0m), suggesting the like-for-like base has stopped shrinking. _(early)_
- Exclusive content ~+26% YoY to ~EUR 4.7m (~$5.4m, estimate) and North American proprietary content +7% in Q3 / +21% for 9M; Czech Republic (+131%) and Isle of Man (+99%) contracts are scaling. _(structural)_
- Cost programme is tangible: workforce -27% YTD, salaries & subcontractors -30% underlying, ~EUR 1.6m (~$1.8m) of savings in Q3 with ~EUR 10.5m (~$11.9m) annualised targeted (vs EUR 4.5m at end-25) and the July round still phasing in through Q4-26/2027. _(structural)_
- Drayton bought for shares only (no cash out), contributed EUR 0.65m (~$0.7m) revenue and EUR 0.31m (~$0.4m) PBT in ten weeks, and adds five studios, three platforms and a US ADW licence path - the only new growth lever on the table. _(mixed)_
- Debt is modest: EUR 3.3m (~$3.7m) drawn on a USD 6m BMO revolver, net debt EUR 0.8m (~$0.9m) ex-leases (~0.1x annualised adj. EBITDA, estimate), covenants (TFD/EBITDA <=2.5x, FCCR >=1.25x) met. _(watch)_

**Lowlights**

- Revenue -23.0% to EUR 20.6m (~$23.4m) and normalized -11.9%; Dutch-contracted revenue collapsed to EUR 0.1m (~$0.1m) from EUR 6.4m (~$7.5m), accounting for the entire YoY decline, and Yahoo Finance reports a miss on both revenue and EPS [consensus unverified]. _(structural)_
- Adj. EBITDA -32.5% to EUR 3.0m (~$3.4m), margin -204bps to 14.5%: the EUR 3.1m (~$3.5m) gross-profit loss was only half covered by EUR 1.6m of savings. Unadjusted EBITDA was EUR 1.1m (~$1.3m), -72%, with EUR 1.9m (~$2.1m) of add-backs (62% of adj. EBITDA), ~EUR 0.9m of which is un-itemised 'exceptional' cost. _(mixed)_
- Cash down to EUR 2.5m (~$2.8m) from EUR 6.7m (~$7.6m) at Dec-25: 9M OCF EUR 6.0m (~$6.8m), -37%, versus capitalised development of EUR 10.4m (~$11.8m), +9% while revenue fell 12%; our FCF (OCF - capex - leases) was -EUR 5.6m (~$6.3m). The CFO concedes capitalised spend is running ahead of operating cash generation. _(structural)_
- Brazil suspension removes ~EUR 2.0m (~$2.3m) per quarter (9.6% of Q3 revenue) from 6 October with EUR 0.6m (~$0.7m) of impairments taken; three customers now exceed 10% of receivables (none at Dec-25), raising collectability questions. _(one-off)_
- Founder-CEO Matevz Mazij moved to Strategic Advisor on 6 November, a week before results; interim CEO is the COO of eight months; no numeric FY26 guidance in the release and 2027 guidance explicitly deferred. _(governance)_
- Related parties received EUR 2.9m (~$3.2m) of the EUR 6.6m (~$7.5m) Drayton consideration shares (43%) and EUR 0.45m (~$0.5m) of the private placement (Note 16); the share count is up 22% YTD to 31.1m with 1.73m warrants outstanding (USD 2.16 and CAD 9.28 strikes). _(governance)_
- North American proprietary growth decelerated to +7% from +44% in Q2, US-contracted revenue fell 25% and net loss widened to EUR 4.3m (~$4.9m), EPS EUR (0.15) (~$(0.17)) vs EUR (0.09); D&A at 24% of revenue means adj. EBITDA overstates cash earnings. _(watch)_

## 6. Forecast & Outlook

There is no guidance bridge to reconcile because there is no guidance: the 13 Nov release carries no numeric FY26 outlook (the forward-looking-statement boilerplate references the company's 'ability to forecast and provide guidance for the combined business'), and on the call management declined to give 2027 guidance, saying it needs time after the leadership change to set realistic expectations; whether a formal FY26 range was issued and withdrawn earlier in 2026 could not be verified [unverified]. The only forward statements are qualitative: operating conditions are unchanged other than the Brazil suspension; the July cost programme continues to phase in; capitalised development will be brought below operating cash generation; and capacity is being re-pointed at proprietary content. The moving parts into Q4 are therefore (i) the loss of ~EUR 1.9m (~$2.2m) of Brazil revenue from 6 October at low gross margin, (ii) a first full quarter of Drayton (~EUR 0.8m / ~$0.9m run-rate vs EUR 0.65m in Q3), (iii) a clean Dutch comparison base from here, and (iv) a further ~EUR 0.4-0.6m (~$0.5-0.7m) of quarterly opex savings as the July programme lands.

- **Company guidance (FY26 / FY27):** None issued (FY27 guidance deferred; no numeric FY26 range in release)
- **Q4-26E revenue (our estimate):** EUR 18.5-19.5m (~$21-22m) (Q3 EUR 20.6m less Brazil ~EUR 1.9m, plus Drayton ~EUR 0.15m, underlying flat to +2% QoQ)
- **Q4-26E adj. EBITDA (our estimate):** EUR 2.5-3.5m (~$2.8-4.0m), 14-18% margin (GM 58-60% on Brazil exit; cash opex ~EUR 8.0-8.2m vs ~EUR 8.6m in Q3)
- **FY26E (our estimate):** Revenue ~EUR 88m (~$100m); adj. EBITDA ~EUR 13.5m (~$15.3m) (9M actual EUR 69.2m / EUR 10.5m plus Q4E midpoint)

**Revenue:** we expect Q4-26 revenue of roughly EUR 19m (~$21.6m), -8% QoQ, with the entire sequential decline being Brazil; the ex-Netherlands, ex-Brazil base of ~EUR 18.5m (~$21.0m) has been flat for a year and we see no catalyst for it to inflect before the Drayton studios and the Czech/Isle of Man wins compound in H1-27. FY26E lands near EUR 88m (~$100m), roughly -12% YoY on our arithmetic (9M actual EUR 69.2m plus Q4E), and the FY27 starting run-rate is ~EUR 76m (~$86m) before any growth. **Margin:** gross margin should print 58-60% in Q4 as the Brazil aggregation book drops out, and cash opex should fall toward EUR 8.0m (~$9.1m) a quarter, giving adj. EBITDA of EUR 2.5-3.5m (~$2.8-4.0m); the bigger lever is the remaining ~EUR 4m (~$4.5m) of annualised savings still to phase in, which in a flat-revenue 2027 would lift adj. EBITDA margin toward the high-teens. **Cash and leverage:** this is the swing factor. At the 9M run-rate of ~EUR 3.5m (~$4.0m) a quarter of capitalised development, Q4 free cash flow is roughly -EUR 1.5m to -EUR 2.0m (~$1.7-2.3m) after restructuring cash, leases and interest, which would take cash below EUR 1m (~$1.1m) by year-end absent a further revolver draw (headroom ~USD 2m, estimate); if the CFO cuts capitalised development to ~EUR 2m a quarter, FCF turns to roughly break-even. Net debt stays below 0.5x adj. EBITDA on any scenario, so this is a liquidity question, not a solvency one, but the covenant denominator (LTM EBITDA) is falling and the equity cushion is largely goodwill and capitalised software. **Our stance:** the mix story is credible and the cost programme is real, but until capitalised development is demonstrably below operating cash flow for two consecutive quarters, adj. EBITDA is not a cash number for this company. All figures above are our estimates, not company guidance.

**What to watch next quarter:**

- Q4-26 cash balance and revolver utilisation (USD 6m BMO facility, CAD 5.2m drawn) - the first hard test of the CFO's 'capex below operating cash flow' commitment; capitalised development additions vs the ~EUR 3.5m/quarter 9M run-rate.
- Permanent CEO appointment and the 2027 plan/guidance the Chair says is under construction; any change to the proprietary-content release cadence as capacity is 'more selective'.
- North American proprietary content growth (+7% in Q3 vs +44% in Q2) and first full-quarter Drayton contribution (~EUR 0.8m revenue run-rate, PBT margin); evidence that Drayton studios are shipping into Ontario/US operators.
- Brazil: receivable collection from suspended operators (three customers now >10% of receivables), any legal reversal of the provisional measure, and whether Q4 gross margin prints 58-60% as implied.
- Replacement of the Dutch PAM revenue: PAM/turnkey pipeline in Czech Republic, Isle of Man, Belgium and Croatia, and whether platform/other revenue (~EUR 3.9m/quarter, estimate) stabilises.
- Year-end goodwill/intangible impairment test (EUR 65.5m / ~$74m of carrying value, 69% of assets) against a ~USD 39m market capitalisation; quantum of un-itemised 'exceptional' costs in the FY26 MD&A.
- Covenant headroom (Total Funded Debt/EBITDA <=2.5x, FCCR >=1.25x) as LTM adj. EBITDA declines; any further equity issuance given related-party participation in 2026 deals.
