# Gaming Innovation Group (GIGSEK) — Q3 2026 Analyst Report
** · Reporting currency: EUR (tables in € millions; ~USD at EUR/USD 1.115 shown in parentheses) · Results released 28 Oct 2026**
**Market reaction:** Shares (GIG SDB, Nasdaq First North Premier Growth Market) rose 19.6% to SEK 3.23 on 28 Oct 2026 on 320,793 SDRs traded (next.io cited >22% intraday), from SEK 2.70 the prior close; the stock had traded at SEK 1.38 after the August Q2/deal announcement and remained ~31% down year-to-date. The CEO bought 294,704 shares between 28 and 30 Oct.

> A weak core quarter wrapped around a transformational deal. Q3-26 revenue fell 12% YoY to €8.5m (~$9.5m) — the third consecutive YoY decline, now accelerating — and adj. EBITDA fell a third to €0.8m (~$0.9m) at a 9% margin, while the net loss widened 50% to €4.5m (~$5.0m). The substance is the 30 Sep completion of 80% of 888AFRICA (€16.4m/~$18.3m), which lifts the 2027 outlook to €85-90m revenue and €17-19m adj. EBITDA; the shares rose ~20%, but the bridge to that number runs through unaudited acquired earnings, a €10.3m deferred payment against €8.0m of (partly acquired) cash, and a CFO who left five days after the print.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Core miss, deal headline.** Q3-26 revenue €8.5m (~$9.5m), -12% YoY and -3% QoQ, against a tough +31% comp in Q3-25; adj. EBITDA €0.8m (~$0.9m), -33% YoY, flat QoQ, margin 9% (-300bps). This is the third straight YoY revenue decline (Q1 -1%, Q2 -5%, Q3 -12%) and the rate of decline is accelerating. No sell-side consensus was available; Redeye and DNB Carnegie had both cut fair values ahead of/after Q2.
- **Revenue mix: recurring held, non-recurring collapsed.** Recurring revenue €7.1m (~$7.9m) vs €7.0m (+1%), now 84% of revenue vs 72%; the €1.2m YoY revenue drop is client exits (incl. the Richmond Atlantic insolvency) and €0.3m lower set-up fees, i.e. non-recurring revenue roughly halved to ~€1.4m (derived).
- **Costs down 12%, not enough.** Opex ex-exceptionals and SBC €7.0m (~$7.8m) vs €8.0m, personnel-led (-€1m); >25% headcount reduction since 1 Jan, exits from the Philippines and the white-label business. Programme: €4.5m annualised delivered plus €6.0m initiated in June = >€10m annualised savings. Management says revenue now runs above operating costs and the core should break even in Q4-26.
- **Below the line deteriorated.** Reported EBITDA €0.4m (-64%); operating loss €(4.3)m vs €(3.5)m; finance expense €0.1m and tax €0.1m; loss after tax €(4.5)m vs €(3.0)m. Implied D&A/impairment ~€4.7m per quarter (derived) — ~55% of revenue — reflects capitalised development and acquired intangibles. 9M-26: revenue €26.3m (~$29.3m, -6%), adj. EBITDA €1.8m (-31%), operating loss €(16.2)m, loss after tax €(16.9)m vs €(11.6)m.
- **Guidance bridge — nominally 'reiterated', floor nudged up, but the core was cut in August.** FY26 combined guidance now €45-48m revenue / €5.5-7.0m adj. EBITDA (~$50.2-53.5m / ~$6.1-7.8m) vs €44-48m / €5-7m at Q2 (26 Aug) and €44-48m / €10-13m standalone at Q1 (May). Since the combined range embeds ~€10-13m of 888AFRICA Q4 revenue, the standalone core has been cut to roughly €33-37m revenue and ~€3-4m EBITDA — a halving of the May EBITDA guide masked by the deal. The division driving the uplift is 888AFRICA; the division driving the underlying cut is the core B2B platform.
- **Implied Q4 arithmetic is demanding.** Guidance implies combined Q4 revenue €18.7-21.7m and adj. EBITDA €3.7-5.2m. On the Q2 call Carter pointed to ~€8.5m revenue / ~€2.2m EBITDA for the core in Q4; the balance (~€10-13m revenue, ~€1.5-3.0m EBITDA) must come from 888AFRICA. Core EBITDA stepping from €0.8m to €2.2m in one quarter is the single biggest swing factor in FY26.
- **2027 outlook introduced: €85-90m revenue, €17-19m adj. EBITDA** (first full year of 888AFRICA). Against the August standalone indication (€36-37m / €8-9m), this implies 888AFRICA contributing ~€48-54m revenue and ~€8-11m adj. EBITDA (derived) — i.e. ~15-20% margin vs 13% in Q2-26. Note Carter's August verbal framing was ~€18-20m EBITDA (per casino.org), so the formal range is ~€1m lighter at both ends.
- **888AFRICA terms.** 80% stake from Virtual Emerging Entertainment Ltd for €16.4m (~$18.3m): €6.1m (~$6.8m) paid at completion (30 Sep 2026), €10.3m (~$11.5m) in instalments within 10 months; no further contingent payments; management retains 20%; GiG is not obliged to keep the 888 brand. Target metrics (management-sourced, unaudited): ~$50m (~€45m) annualised NGR run-rate, Q2-26 NGR +30% YoY / +17% QoQ, 13% adj. EBITDA margin, >$1m net cash generated in Q2. Implied ~3.5x run-rate adj. EBITDA for 100% (derived).
- **Several outlets state the deal 'completed in August'; the company's release says 30 September 2026.** We use the company date — it matters because it means zero P&L contribution in Q3 and only balance-sheet consolidation (cash) at quarter-end.
- **Funding: €8.5m raised 26 Aug** — €2.5m (~$2.8m) directed SDR issue at SEK 1.725 (~9% dilution; SDRs to 176.9m) taken by the MJ and ZJ Foundations and the CEO, plus €6.0m (~$6.7m) two-year convertible loans at 15% p.a. (lenders: Carter, Myrild AS, Nalavio Ltd), 25% convertible every six months at a 10% discount to 10-day VWAP. Cash interest ~€0.9m (~$1.0m) p.a.; conversion mechanics are structurally dilutive.
- **Liquidity is the bear case.** Group cash €8.0m (~$8.9m) at 30 Sep 'reflecting the consolidation of 888AFRICA' — i.e. it includes the target's cash; the core's own cash is probably ~€4-5m (our estimate: €3.5m + €8.5m raised − €6.1m upfront − Q3 burn). Against that sit €10.3m of deferred consideration due by ~Jul-27, ~€0.9m p.a. of convertible coupon and a core that has not yet printed a cash-positive quarter. Net debt/leverage not disclosed in the sources read [unverified].
- **Commercial momentum is real but small.** One new partner signed and one extension (Luckydays three-year platform renewal); three brand launches in Q3, all in Alberta with existing partners (market opened July; LuckyDays live on day one, 15 Jul). Post-quarter: PowerPlay went live in Alberta (8 Oct, first SportX deployment in the province) and River Cree Resort & Casino signed as the fifth Alberta brand (Oct). CoreX certified for Spain (20 Jul), where partners hold ~9% combined share.
- **Governance: CFO out five days after results.** Phil Richards (CFO since Jul-24) left effective 2 Nov 2026 to become CFO of Chaos; a search is under way with interim arrangements. Offsetting signal: CEO Richard Carter bought 294,704 shares on 28-30 Oct, after 1.5m shares in September (holding ~5.1m before the latest purchase), and he is both an equity subscriber and a convertible lender.
- **Share reaction: +19.6% to SEK 3.23** on 28 Oct (320,793 SDRs traded; next.io cited >22% intraday), from SEK 2.70, versus SEK 1.38 after the August Q2/deal announcement; still ~-31% YTD. Market cap roughly €50m, EV ~€55m before deferred consideration (MarketScreener).
- **Strategic read-through: B2B pure-play becomes a B2C-led group.** Management now describes 888AFRICA as the 'high-growth second engine'; in reality on 2027 numbers it would be ~55-60% of revenue and more than half of EBITDA. Points to underwrite: channel conflict with African B2B prospects, 20% minority leakage on the best asset, FX (MZN/AOA/TZS alongside existing ARS exposure), and whether a sub-scale platform business with ~€36m revenue can sustain 23-25% margins as implied for 2027.
- **Disclosure gaps.** The headline release gives no product (platform vs sportsbook) or geographic split, no EPS, and no balance-sheet/cash-flow detail; 888AFRICA figures are management-sourced; secondary coverage shows 9M-25 adj. EBITDA as €2.8m vs the company's €2.6m (we use the company figure). The full interim PDF could not be opened within this exercise.
- **Thesis.** The equity is now a levered bet that 888AFRICA's growth and cash conversion fund the €10.3m deferred payment while the core reaches breakeven; if the 2027 guide is delivered, the stock trades at roughly 3-4x EV/EBITDA on attributable earnings (our rough estimate incl. deferred consideration and minorities). If the core Q4 step-up slips, a further raise — likely via the discounted convertibles — is the base case.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — Core B2B platform & sportsbook (GiG standalone)

*Recurring base held at €7.1m but non-recurring halved; opex -12% could not offset a -12% top line, leaving adj. EBITDA at €0.8m/9% for a second quarter and a Q4 breakeven promise doing the heavy lifting.*

### Revenue and mix

**Revenue fell 12% YoY to €8.5m (~$9.5m) as client exits and lower set-up fees offset a flat recurring line of €7.1m.**

- Revenue down 3% QoQ from €8.8m (~$9.8m), lapping a quarter in which revenue had grown 31%.
- Decline attributed chiefly to the Richmond Atlantic insolvency (which triggered a €3m bad-debt provision in Q2) and €0.3m (~$0.3m) lower set-up fees.
- Recurring revenue €7.1m (~$7.9m) versus €7.0m; 84% of revenue against 72% a year ago.
- Non-recurring revenue roughly halved to ~€1.4m (~$1.6m) from ~€2.7m (~$3.0m).
- Flat recurring after Ontario and Alberta go-lives implies churn (Richmond Atlantic, Philippines exit, white-label wind-down) is absorbing essentially all new-business ramp.
- No product or geographic split is disclosed, so analysis is at segment level.

### Costs and profitability

**Opex fell 12% to €7.0m but adj. EBITDA was still €0.8m (~$0.9m), down 33% YoY, at a 9% margin.**

- Opex excluding exceptionals and SBC fell to €7.0m (~$7.8m) from €8.0m (~$8.9m), almost entirely personnel (-€1m) after January redundancies; headcount down more than 25% since 1 January.
- Implied cost of sales ~€0.7m (~$0.8m, ~8% of revenue).
- Adj. EBITDA flat QoQ; margin 9% (12% in Q3-25, 9% in Q2-26); 9M margin 7% versus 9%.
- Reported EBITDA €0.4m (~$0.4m, -64%); €0.4m of adjustments versus €0.1m a year ago, so the adjustment load is rising.
- ~€4.7m D&A and impairment took operating loss to €(4.3)m (~$(4.8)m) from €(3.5)m; loss after tax €(4.5)m (~$(5.0)m), 50% wider YoY, narrower than Q2's €(7.2)m.
- D&A above half of revenue, legacy of capitalised development and Sportnco intangibles, will keep IFRS earnings deeply negative even if EBITDA turns.

> “protecting delivery” — CFO

### Alberta and commercial

**The quarter was about Alberta: three brand launches with existing partners, with PowerPlay and River Cree following post-quarter, but none yet visible in recurring revenue.**

- Alberta regulated market opened in July; GiG secured AGLC registration and took LuckyDays live on day one (15 July).
- Three brand launches in the quarter, all Alberta, all existing partners; one new partner signed and one extension (LuckyDays' three-year platform renewal).
- Post-quarter: PowerPlay live in Alberta on 8 October, first SportX sportsbook in the province; River Cree Resort & Casino signed mid-October as fifth Alberta brand on full end-to-end stack.
- CoreX certified for Spain on 20 July, where partners generate an estimated 9% combined market share.
- Alberta launches with existing partners carry little or no set-up fee.

> “contracted pipeline of go-lives” — Management

### Guidance

**Standalone breakeven is promised for Q4-26, but guidance arithmetic needs ~€2.2m quarterly EBITDA; we would underwrite €1.3-1.7m until printed.**

- Standalone expected to break even in Q4-26 and be cash generative by year-end; Carter says incremental revenue flows through to cash.
- FY26 combined adj. EBITDA €5.5-7.0m less 9M €1.8m implies €3.7-5.2m in Q4; Carter pointed to ~€8.5m revenue and ~€2.2m EBITDA for the core.
- Stepping from €0.8m to ~€2.2m on flat revenue requires the full >€10m annualised savings (~€2.5m per quarter) in run-rate with no offsetting churn.
- August 2027 standalone outlook (€36-37m revenue, €8-9m adj. EBITDA, €3-4m cash flow) not withdrawn but folded into combined number; implies 23-25% margin versus a never-exceeded 12%.

### Our read

**The core is run for cash rather than growth, the right call, but the equity story now rests on three unproven things.**

- Alberta/Ontario go-lives converting to recurring revenue in H1-27.
- No further client failures of Richmond Atlantic scale.
- The Q4 cost run-rate holding.
- Lack of product/geographic split hides whether SportX is gaining share or Spain and Canada offset legacy Malta-licensed attrition; that opacity is a discount factor in its own right.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — 888AFRICA (B2C — Mozambique, Angola, Tanzania; 80% owned, consolidated from 30 Sep 2026)

*The 'second engine' is already the bigger one: ~$50m NGR run-rate, +30% YoY, 13% margin and cash-generative — bought at ~3.5x run-rate EBITDA, but with €10.3m still to pay and every number so far management-sourced.*

### Deal terms

**GiG bought 80% of 888AFRICA on 30 September 2026 for €16.4m (~$18.3m), with €10.3m still to pay and no Q3 contribution.**

- Agreement in principle 25-26 August, binding SPA 28 September, completion 30 September; incumbent management retains 20%, described by Carter as highly aligned.
- €6.1m (~$6.8m) paid at completion; €10.3m (~$11.5m) in instalments within ten months; no contingent payments; no obligation to keep the 888 brand.
- Only visible Q3 effect is group cash rising to €8.0m (~$8.9m) from €3.5m, attributed to consolidating the target.
- Several outlets say the deal closed 'in August'; the company's own date is 30 September.

### Asset and valuation

**Management-sourced numbers show ~$50m NGR run-rate, +30% YoY, 13% margin, implying ~3.5x run-rate EBITDA paid.**

- Market-leading in Mozambique, licensed in Angola and Tanzania, launched late 2022; Q2-26 NGR up 30% YoY and 17% QoQ (one source says 19%); >$1m (~€0.9m) net cash generated in the quarter.
- 'Total revenue' cited rising 32% from €11.3m in Q4-25 to €14.8m in Q2-26 is probably gross pre-bonus; we would not annualise it.
- $50m NGR at 13% margin gives ~$6.5m (~€5.8m) run-rate adj. EBITDA; 100% price €20.5m implies ~3.5x EBITDA and ~0.45x NGR, cheap versus European B2C, reflecting jurisdictional, FX and counterparty risk.
- Ambition: top-three African online operator by revenue and profit within five years, including M&A beyond three current markets.

### Forward contribution

**Guidance implies 888AFRICA delivering ~€48-54m revenue and ~€8-11m adj. EBITDA in 2027, with margin expansion the stretch.**

- Q4-26 implied contribution ~€10-13m (~$11-14.5m) revenue and ~€1.5-3.0m adj. EBITDA (combined €18.7-21.7m / €3.7-5.2m less core ~€8.5m / ~€2.2m).
- 2027 group guidance €85-90m revenue, €17-19m adj. EBITDA less standalone €36-37m / €8-9m leaves ~€48-54m (~$54-60m) and ~€8-11m (~$9-12m).
- Implies ~10-20% growth on $50m run-rate, conservative versus +30%, and margin to ~17-20% from 13%, dependent on cost actions and platform synergy.
- Twenty percent accrues to the minority; attributable 2027 adj. EBITDA ~€6.5-9m.

### Funding and FX

**Funding is the pressure point: the €10.3m deferred balance leaves little room for a weak quarter on either side.**

- €8.5m raise (equity at SEK 1.725 plus €6m of 15% two-year convertibles, 25% convertible each half-year at 10% discount to VWAP) covered €6.1m upfront with ~€2.4m spare.
- Deferred €10.3m must come from 888AFRICA cash (perhaps €3-5m over ten months), the core turning cash positive, and group cash of €8.0m, of which only ~€4-5m sits in the core.
- Convertible structure means marginal equity is raised at a discount to an already-reset share price.
- FX: metical, kwanza and shilling earnings consolidated into euro, on top of existing Argentine peso exposure in B2B.

### Our read

**Price is attractive if numbers are real, but the market is capitalising unaudited earnings; Q4-26 is the first real datapoint.**

- Early evidence supportive: cash generation, management rollover, CEO co-investing; regulatory footing in Mozambique must hold.
- First consolidated quarter (Q4-26, reported February 2027) will show NGR, promo intensity, margin, cash conversion and instalment schedule.
- Watch for channel conflict: GiG sells its platform to African operators and now owns one.

## 5. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- **Recurring revenue held at €7.1m (~$7.9m), +1% YoY, now 84% of the mix** despite the Richmond Atlantic insolvency and exits from the Philippines/white-label — the base the 2027 core guide is built on did not shrink. _(structural)_
- **Opex ex-exceptionals -12% YoY to €7.0m (~$7.8m); >€10m (~$11.2m) annualised savings enacted (€4.5m delivered + €6.0m initiated in June); headcount >25% lower since 1 Jan.** Core guided to breakeven in Q4-26 and cash generative by year-end. _(structural)_
- **888AFRICA closed at ~3.5x run-rate EBITDA (derived) for a #1 operator in Mozambique growing NGR 30% YoY, 13% margin, >$1m quarterly net cash**; management rolled 20%, no earn-outs, no brand-licence dependency on 888. _(early)_
- **FY27 outlook of €85-90m revenue / €17-19m adj. EBITDA (~$94.8-100.4m / ~$19.0-21.2m)** roughly doubles revenue and triples EBITDA versus FY26 guidance; FY26 floor nudged up to €45m / €5.5m. _(early)_
- **Alberta footprint built in one quarter: three launches in Q3, five partners by mid-October (LuckyDays day-one, Casino Time, PowerPlay incl. first SportX deployment, River Cree) plus Luckydays 3-year renewal and CoreX Spain certification** — a contracted pipeline for 2027 recurring revenue. _(structural)_
- **Group cash €8.0m (~$8.9m) vs €3.5m at June and €4.7m a year ago**, and the shares +19.6% on the day — the market gave credit for the strategic reset. _(mixed)_
- **Insider alignment: CEO bought 294,704 shares on 28-30 Oct after 1.5m in September, and personally funded both the equity placing and the convertible** alongside the two largest holders. _(structural)_

**Lowlights**

- **Revenue -12% YoY to €8.5m (~$9.5m), -3% QoQ — third consecutive YoY decline and accelerating (Q1 -1%, Q2 -5%, Q3 -12%)**; non-recurring revenue roughly halved and client churn is absorbing all new-business ramp. _(structural)_
- **Adj. EBITDA €0.8m (~$0.9m), -33% YoY, 9% margin for a second quarter; implied standalone FY26 EBITDA has gone from €10-13m (May) to ~€3-4m (derived) — a halving masked by presenting 'combined' guidance.** _(structural)_
- **Loss after tax -50% to €(4.5)m (~$(5.0)m); 9M €(16.9)m (~$(18.8)m) vs €(11.6)m; ~€4.7m/quarter of D&A keeps IFRS earnings deeply negative regardless of EBITDA progress.** _(structural)_
- **CFO Phil Richards left five days after the print (effective 2 Nov) with a search only just initiated**, at the moment the group must integrate a B2C business, pay €10.3m of deferred consideration and manage a convertible. _(governance)_
- **Funding gap: €10.3m (~$11.5m) due within 10 months plus ~€0.9m p.a. of 15% coupon, against €8.0m of group cash of which only ~€4-5m (est.) is the core's; the convertibles convert at a 10% discount to VWAP** — further dilution is the base case if Q4 slips. _(watch)_
- **Guidance arithmetic requires combined Q4 adj. EBITDA of €3.7-5.2m (~$4.1-5.8m) vs €0.8m delivered by the core in Q3, including a core step-up to ~€2.2m** — the biggest single execution risk in FY26. _(watch)_
- **Disclosure thin and 888AFRICA numbers unaudited: no product/geographic split, no EPS or balance-sheet detail in the headline release, secondary sources disagree on the deal's completion date and 9M-25 EBITDA; strategic pivot from B2B pure-play to B2C creates channel conflict and 20% minority leakage.** _(mixed)_

## 6. Forecast & Outlook

The guidance bridge has three steps. In May (Q1), standalone FY26 guidance was €44-48m revenue and €10-13m adj. EBITDA. In August (Q2), alongside the 888AFRICA announcement, this became a combined €44-48m / €5-7m assuming a full Q4 contribution from the target — which, because ~€10-13m of Q4 revenue now comes from 888AFRICA, amounted to cutting the core to ~€33-37m revenue and ~€3-4m EBITDA. In October (Q3) the combined range was 'reiterated' with the floor raised to €45m / €5.5m (~$50.2m / ~$6.1m), reflecting deal certainty and 888AFRICA's growth rather than any improvement in the core. A formal FY27 range of €85-90m / €17-19m (~$94.8-100.4m / ~$19.0-21.2m) was added; against the August standalone indication (€36-37m / €8-9m) it implies ~€48-54m / ~€8-11m from 888AFRICA, and sits ~€1m below the €18-20m EBITDA Carter had floated verbally in August.

- **FY26 revenue (combined):** €45-48m (~$50.2-53.5m) (from €44-48m (Aug); €44-48m standalone (May))
- **FY26 adj. EBITDA (combined):** €5.5-7.0m (~$6.1-7.8m) (from €5-7m (Aug); €10-13m standalone (May))
- **FY27 revenue (first full year of 888AFRICA):** €85-90m (~$94.8-100.4m) (new; vs €36-37m standalone indication (Aug))
- **FY27 adj. EBITDA:** €17-19m (~$19.0-21.2m) (new; vs €8-9m standalone (Aug); ~€18-20m verbal (Aug call))

**Q4-26 (our estimate):** combined revenue ~€19-21m (~$21-23m) — core ~€8.3-8.7m on flat recurring revenue and minimal set-up fees, plus 888AFRICA ~€10.5-12m at the $50m run-rate with modest growth; combined adj. EBITDA ~€3.0-4.0m (~$3.3-4.5m), assuming core €1.3-1.7m (not the ~€2.2m indicated) and 888AFRICA €1.5-2.3m at 13-19%. That puts **FY26 revenue at ~€45-47m, inside the range, but adj. EBITDA at ~€4.8-5.8m — at or just below the €5.5m floor**; the risk is skewed to the low end because the core's cost run-rate has to drop a further ~€1m per quarter with no churn offset. **FY27:** we would model revenue of ~€82-88m (core flat at ~€35-36m as Alberta/Spain go-lives offset attrition; 888AFRICA ~€47-52m on 10-15% NGR growth, i.e. we assume growth decelerates from +30%) and adj. EBITDA of ~€13-16m (~$14.5-17.8m) versus guidance of €17-19m — our gap is the core margin, where we assume ~17-20% rather than the 23-25% implied, and 888AFRICA at ~16-18%. Attributable adj. EBITDA after the 20% minority is ~€11-14m. **Leverage/liquidity direction: worse before better.** Net debt rises mechanically as the €10.3m deferred consideration is paid out of cash through Jul-27 while €6m of 15% convertibles sit on the balance sheet; we expect at least partial conversion (dilutive at a 10% VWAP discount) and would not rule out a further equity raise in H1-27 if Q4 core EBITDA prints below ~€1.5m. **IFRS earnings** stay negative through 2027 on ~€18-19m of annual D&A plus acquisition amortisation and purchase-price allocation on 888AFRICA. **Valuation sense-check (rough):** at ~€50m market cap and ~€55m EV before deferred consideration, adding €10.3m of deferred payments and adjusting for minorities gives ~4-5x our FY27E attributable EBITDA and ~3.5-4x on guidance — undemanding if delivered, but the delivery risk is concentrated in a business the market has not yet seen a consolidated quarter from. **Swing factors:** the Q4 core cost run-rate; 888AFRICA NGR growth and promo intensity in its first consolidated quarter; MZN/AOA/TZS FX; any further client insolvency; timing of Alberta go-lives (River Cree) converting to recurring revenue; the convertible conversion schedule; and the CFO appointment.

**What to watch next quarter:**

- Q4-26 print (Feb 2027): core adj. EBITDA vs the ~€2.2m indication and whether the core is cash-positive; first consolidated 888AFRICA quarter — NGR, margin, net cash generated, and the GGR-vs-NGR revenue basis.
- Deferred consideration: cadence of the €10.3m instalments through ~Jul-27 and the cash source (888AFRICA dividends/upstreaming vs core cash vs new financing); any conversion notices on the €6m 15% convertibles and resulting dilution.
- CFO succession: permanent appointment and whether interim arrangements affect the FY26 audit and first consolidated accounts for 888AFRICA (PPA, goodwill, NCI).
- Core commercial conversion: River Cree and further Alberta go-lives, Spain CoreX migrations, and whether recurring revenue moves above €7.1m in Q4/Q1; any further client exits or bad-debt provisions after Richmond Atlantic.
- Disclosure improvements: product (platform/sportsbook) and geographic revenue split, EPS, cash-flow statement and net debt in the Q4 report — and clarification of the FY27 standalone vs 888AFRICA split.
- African regulatory and FX developments in Mozambique, Angola and Tanzania; evidence of channel conflict with GiG's B2B African prospects.
- Share-count creep: SDRs at 176.9m post-placing, plus LTIP issuance and convertible conversions.
