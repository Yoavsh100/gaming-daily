# Entain plc (ENT.L) — Q3 2026 Analyst Report
**LSE · Reporting currency: GBP (£m; USD equivalents at GBP/USD 1.32 shown as ~$). BetMGM reported in USD. · Results released 15 Oct 2026**
**Market reaction:** Shares -2.6% to 432.6p (~$5.71) shortly after the open and -3.4% to 429.3p (~$5.67) by 12:01 BST on 15 Oct 2026 (implied prior close ~444p / ~$5.86); the Investing.com ticker showed -3.4% intraday. Closing level not verified at time of writing. The stock is -42% year-to-date (from ~744p in January); Deutsche Bank had already cut its target to 872p after the 28 Sep Brazil update.

> An "as guided" quarter with the UK doing all the heavy lifting: Q3 group NGR +2% cc, online +3% (+5% ex-Brazil) and UK&I online +14% on +15% volumes, against a ~4pp drag from customer-friendly football and a -36% Brazil print that precedes a full Q4 wipe-out. FY26 guidance — online NGR +4-6% cc and underlying EBITDA at the lower end of £910-960m (~$1.20-1.27bn) — was reiterated three weeks after being cut to the floor, so the ~3% share-price fall reflects quality-of-growth questions (sports NGR -3%, International online -3%, ex-Brazil International only +1%, a "softer Q4" nod from the CFO) rather than any new estimate change. BetMGM's $86m adjusted EBITDA (+112%) and $68m of cash to parents is the genuine positive surprise.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **In line, not better:** Q3 group NGR +2% cc (continuing operations, ex-CEE), online +3%, retail -2% — versus Q2's +7%/+8%/+3% and Q3-25's +5%/+6%/+3%. Entain's Q3 trading updates disclose growth rates only; there is no quarterly P&L, so profit visibility rests entirely on the reiterated guidance and the H1-26 base (NGR £2,545.3m / ~$3.36bn; underlying EBITDA £479.3m / ~$633m).
- **Guidance reiterated — but it was already cut to the floor on 28 Sep:** FY26 online NGR +4-6% cc (ex-Brazil at the top end of 5-7%); underlying EBITDA exc. parent fees at the lower end of £910-960m (~$1.20-1.27bn); online EBITDA margin at the lower end of 21-22%. The 13 Aug (H1) guide was online NGR +5-7% and 'comfortable' with the £934m (~$1.23bn) consensus midpoint. The ~£24m (~$32m) step-down is entirely the International division, specifically Brazil.
- **Brazil is the swing division:** Q3 Brazil NGR -36% (Q3-25: -11%; H1-26: -25%) as management pulled back marketing in an 'intensely competitive, highly promotional' market; then on 25 Sep a provisional executive measure withdrew every operator's online licence with immediate effect. Congress has 120 days (plus recess) to ratify. Brazil was expected to be ~5% of FY26 online NGR with modest EBITDA; guidance assumes the ban holds for the rest of 2026. Management expects the market to 'reopen in some form' but will re-enter conservatively.
- **Sports results cost ~4pp of online growth:** customer-friendly football in August and September more than offset July's strong World Cup finish; management puts the Q3 sports-margin headwind at c.1-2pp versus plan, equivalent to ~4% of online NGR growth, while the YoY margin move was only -0.2pp (group) because Q3-25 was itself a weak comparator. Note that online sports wagers were -1% and group wagers only +1% — the volume line in sports was flat before any margin effect, which the release does not dwell on.
- **Gaming is the engine:** online gaming NGR +8% (UK&I +17%, International +1%); 9M online gaming +9%. Gaming is carrying the group while sports NGR is -3% — a mix shift that is margin-positive pre-tax but concentrates growth in the line most exposed to the UK's 40% Remote Gaming Duty (from 1 Apr 2026).
- **UK&I beat expectations:** NGR +8% cc (online +14% on volumes +15%; retail +1%, +3% LFL), with management citing continued market-share gains in both channels. UK&I online has run at +13-15% in every disclosed quarter since Q3-25 (Q3-25 +15%, Q2-26 +14%, Q3-26 +14%; H1-26 +13%) despite a 19-point RGD step-up. UK&I is ~46% of continuing NGR (H1: £1,176.8m / ~$1.55bn).
- **International is weak beyond Brazil:** NGR -4% (online -3%, retail -12%); ex-Brazil online only +1% (9M +2%). Italy -6% (online -5%, retail -8%) on +9% volumes but a -3.1pp sports margin; Australia online +1% (H1 +13%) lapping tough comps; New Zealand, Spain and Canada kept double-digit online growth. International online volumes were -3% — the first negative volume print in the current run.
- **World Cup read-across (group event stats):** the expanded Men's World Cup drove Q2 online NGR +8% cc and first-time depositors double the 2022 tournament; July's strong sports margin was the tail of that, and BetMGM also cited 'successful World Cup' engagement. The event is now fully in the base and Q4 faces the NFL/football calendar without it.
- **BetMGM delivered the quarter's upside surprise:** Q3 net revenue $710m (+7%), iGaming $483m (+6%), online sports $216m (+7%) on handle $3.24bn (+3%) and GGR hold 10.7% (+55bps); contribution $175m (+32%, 24.6% margin); adjusted EBITDA $86m (+112%, 12.1% margin); 9M adjusted EBITDA $185m (+24%). FY26 guidance reiterated at the lower end ($2.9bn NR / $300m adj. EBITDA), which on our arithmetic implies Q4 of at least ~$784m revenue and ~$115m adj. EBITDA.
- **BetMGM cash is now real for Entain:** $68m paid to parents in Q3 ($18m parent fees + $50m distributions), so ~$34m (~£26m) to Entain. Against that, Entain's equity-accounted H1 share of BetMGM profit fell to £19.7m (~$26m) from £33.6m (~$44m) as parent fees moved above the JV line — more cash, less reported JV profit; Q3 should reverse the profit trend given the EBITDA doubling (Entain's 50% of Q3 adj. EBITDA ≈ $43m / ~£33m).
- **Margins are under tax pressure (H1 base):** underlying EBITDA exc. parent fees £472.6m (~$624m), -3%; online EBITDA margin 21.4% (-3.2pp), UK&I online EBITDA margin 23.1% vs 28.6%; group contribution margin -2.9pp from RGD. The lower-end FY26 guide implies H2 underlying EBITDA exc. parent fees of only ~£437m (~$577m) vs £473m in H1 — two quarters of 40% RGD plus Brazil, partly offset by World Cup marketing roll-off and Brazil marketing savings (our arithmetic).
- **No named cost programme, but mitigation is quantified:** management expects to offset ~25% of the FY26 UK online tax impact; operating costs were held to +3% in H1 (inflation, pay and employer NI, offset by efficiencies); press reports put ~400 Entain customer-care roles at risk. Expect the FY results in March to re-express the cost/cash framework alongside the CEE deconsolidation.
- **Balance sheet:** 30 Jun adjusted net debt £3,598.9m (~$4.75bn), reported leverage 3.1x (flat YoY; look-through incl. 50% BetMGM/DPA was 3.6x at FY25), available liquidity £938.6m (~$1.24bn) with £135m (~$178m) drawn on the RCF. H1 operating cash flow £348.8m (~$460m), net capex £138.3m (~$183m), 'adjusted cashflow' £77.4m (~$102m) vs £151m (~$199m) for all of FY25. Interim DPS 10.3p (~$0.14), +5%, paid 28 Sep.
- **CEE exit on track:** sale of an initial 20% of Entain CEE (SuperSport/STS) to EMMA Capital for €425m (~$476m / ~£361m), implying EV €2.1bn (~$2.35bn, c.10x EBITDA), is expected to close in Q4-26; Entain drops to 47.5% and CEE (H1 NGR £268.9m / ~$355m, EBITDA £94.7m / ~$125m) is deconsolidated. Q3 CEE NGR flat (online +4%, retail -22%). Proceeds from the full exit are earmarked to take reported leverage below 3x, excess to shareholders.
- **£500m (~$660m) 2028 cash target survives — for now:** reiterated, including the 47.5% CEE stake, but CFO Michael Snape said the cash-generation outlook will be 'articulated differently' at FY results. With CEE deconsolidated, BetMGM distributions ramping and UK duty rising, expect a re-basing rather than a simple reaffirmation.
- **UK tax is the macro overhang:** RGD 21%→40% from 1 Apr 2026; remote betting duty to 25% from Apr 2027 (sports is untouched until then); Budget speculation of machine gaming duty 20%→40%. Management cited 630 industry shop closures since last year and third-party estimates of ~1,500 further closures / ~16,000 jobs if MGD doubles. On the H1 run-rate, UK&I retail gaming NGR is ~£525m (~$693m) annualised, so a 20pt MGD rise is mechanically ~£100m (~$130m) before mitigation (our estimate).
- **'Somewhat softer' Q4 flagged by the CFO** after a strong YTD ex-Brazil (+8%); our arithmetic: FY online +4-6% on 9M +5% requires Q4 of roughly +1% to +9% including a ~4-5pp Brazil hole, so the bottom of the range (~+4%) is the realistic landing, with sports margin the swing (Q3-25 reference: a 1-2pp margin miss cost c.£20m / ~$26m of EBITDA in a quarter).
- **Management and strategy:** CEO Stella David and CFO Michael Snape (joined Mar 2026) framed Q3 as the tenth consecutive quarter of online growth. Other call points: three New Zealand licences secured (iGaming growth option), Italy/Eurobet refresh under a new team (AS Roma partnership), BetMGM Alberta launch tracking above Ontario, and a continued shift of BetMGM investment toward iGaming/multi-product states and 'premium mass' sports customers.
- **Share reaction:** -2.6% to 432.6p (~$5.71) at the open and -3.4% to 429.3p (~$5.67) by midday on 15 Oct (implied prior close ~444p); the stock is -42% YTD and Deutsche Bank had already cut its target to 872p after the 28 Sep Brazil update.
- **Thesis read-through:** the equity case is UK share gains + BetMGM cash + CEE monetisation versus UK tax + Brazil + 3x-plus leverage. Q3 strengthens the first two and confirms the third, but International ex-Brazil at +1% and flat sports volumes mean the 'high-quality growth' claim is, for now, a UK&I-only statement. The FY26 number is de-risked at the floor; FY27 (25% GBD, MGD, CEE out) is where estimates remain unsettled.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — UK & Ireland Online

*+14% NGR on +15% volumes with gaming +17% — share gains are real, but every incremental pound now lands at a 5-7pp lower margin after the 40% RGD.*

### Q3 growth

**UK&I online NGR rose 14% cc on volumes +15%, with gaming +17% and sports +3%; the sports slowdown is luck, not demand.**

- Q2-26 was +14% and Q3-25 +15%; management attributes growth to continued market-share gains in both sports and gaming.
- Gaming NGR accelerated to +17% (Q2 +13%; 9M +14%).
- Sports NGR +3% on wagers +6%; sports margin fell 0.4pp YoY against a Q3-25 comparator flattered by +0.8pp.
- Stripping out margin, sports volume growth of roughly 6-7% is consistent with the first half.
- RNS calls the region 'ahead of expectations'; 9M run-rate of +13% sits comfortably above the group's 4-6% online guide.

### Quality of growth

**Running +13-15% every quarter since Q3-25 while digesting the RGD rise from 21% to 40% supports share being taken, not bought.**

- Drivers cited: native-app upgrade, BetBuilder expansion ahead of the World Cup, gaming 'coin economy' and AI-driven bonus optimisation.
- Entain argues the higher tax regime will squeeze weaker operators; the Q3 volume number is the first hard evidence rather than a hope.
- Management does not disclose UK actives or ARPU, so the volume/value split is not verifiable from the release.

> “well positioned to capture opportunities as the market adjusts” — Management

### H1 economics

**H1 underlying EBITDA fell 8% to £148.0m (~$195m) with margin 23.1% versus 28.6%, a 5.5pp compression with one quarter of 40% RGD.**

- H1 NGR £640.4m (~$845m, +13%); gross profit +1% to £366.4m (~$484m); gross margin 57.2%, -7.3pp as RGD absorbed most growth.
- Marketing +15% to £123.3m (~$163m), 19.3% of NGR (+0.3pp, World Cup phasing).
- Contribution fell 5% to £243.1m (~$321m); contribution margin down 7.5pp to 38.0%; operating costs flat at £95.1m (~$126m).
- Management expects to mitigate roughly 25% of the FY26 tax impact; press reports of ~400 customer-care roles at risk indicate the source.
- Q3 growth mix is gaming-led (+17%), so it carries lower incremental margin than the headline suggests.

### 2027 tax outlook

**Remote betting duty rises to 25% from April 2027; FY26 online EBITDA margin guide (lower end of 21-22% vs 25.7% in FY25) prices the growth.**

- Budget speculation on machine gaming duty is largely a retail issue.
- The lobbying line was the most prominent item in the CEO statement, which tells you where they see the risk.
- Our read: volume momentum is genuine and best-in-class among UK incumbents; the top line delivered, the bottom line is set by HM Treasury.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — UK & Ireland Retail

*+1% NGR (+3% LFL), ~21% EBITDA margin and stable volumes — a cash cow whose FY27 earnings hinge on the Budget's machine gaming duty decision.*

### Q3 trading

**Retail NGR rose 1% cc (+3% like-for-like excluding closures), a steady quarter with stable volumes through a still-pruned estate.**

- Gaming +1%, sports +1% on wagers +1%, sports margin +0.1pp YoY.
- Q2 was flattered by results: +4% NGR with sports +7% on a +1.3pp margin.
- 9M: NGR +1%, wagers +4%, margin -0.1pp; management says the estate continues to outperform the market in sports and gaming.

### H1 financials

**H1 underlying EBITDA was £114.1m (~$151m, +2%) at a flat 21.3% margin, roughly 24% of continuing-ops EBITDA from ~21% of NGR.**

- NGR £536.4m (~$708m, +2%); gross profit £387.6m (~$512m); contribution £386.9m (~$511m) at 72.1% margin (+0.2pp).
- Operating costs +2% on shop colleague pay and employer NI increase (annualised end of Q1), partly offset by closures and efficiencies.
- Underlying operating profit +7% to £71.6m (~$95m) as depreciation fell.
- Gaming (machines) £263.1m (~$347m) and sports £264.5m (~$349m) are each about half of NGR.

### Machine gaming duty risk

**A rise in machine gaming duty from 20% to 40% would cost roughly £100m (~$130m) a year before mitigation, ~45% of annualised EBITDA.**

- Management cited 630 industry shop closures since last year and analysis pointing to ~1,500 additional closures and ~16,000 jobs if the rate doubles.
- Shop customers do not simply migrate online, but Entain's estate and brands would be comparatively well placed to gain share from disruption.
- UK&I retail gaming NGR is ~£525m (~$693m) annualised; our estimate, and machine gross win may differ from gaming NGR.
- Mitigation plans exist but are undisclosed.

### Our view

**Retail holds volumes and generates ~£230m (~$300m) annual EBITDA, but the market will not pay for it until the Budget is known.**

- LFL growth at low single digits is what the estate should deliver.
- Omnichannel linkage with online is a genuine UK differentiator and would become more valuable if competitor shops close.
- Near-term earnings swing from MGD is larger than anything the operating line can deliver.

## 5. Segment deep-dive — International (Online & Retail; incl. CEE discontinued)

*NGR -4% with online -3% and volumes -3%; ex-Brazil online only +1% — Brazil's ban explains the guidance cut, but Italy/Australia explain why the segment isn't growing without it.*

### Q3 overview

**International NGR fell 4% cc with online -3% and retail -12%; ex-Brazil online was only +1%, so the business is barely growing.**

- Q2-26 was +5% and Q3-25 +1%; online volumes -3% is the first negative volume print in the current run.
- Online gaming NGR +1%, sports NGR -5% on wagers -2%; online sports margin +0.2pp YoY.
- Ex-Brazil 9M online +2%; 'disciplined' language should be read as a pull-back of Brazilian marketing, not demand recovery elsewhere.
- 9M segment: NGR +1%, online +2%, retail -5%, below the group's 4-6% online guide and far below the UK.

### Brazil

## 6. Segment deep-dive — BetMGM (50/50 JV with MGM Resorts; equity-accounted)

*Adj. EBITDA $86m (+112%) and $68m of cash to parents on net revenue +7% — a cost-discipline and hold story on a 3% smaller active base.*

### Q3 results

**Net revenue was $710m (+7%) and adjusted EBITDA more than doubled to $86m (+112%), a cost-discipline story rather than top-line leverage.**

- 9M net revenue $2,116m (+5%); iGaming $483m (+6%), online sports $216m (+7%), retail/other $11m.
- EBITDA margin 12.1% versus 6.1%; 9M $185m (+24%), already above the $150m earned in 9M-25.
- Contribution +32% to $175m (24.6% margin, +4.7pp).
- Q3 revenue was flat on the H1 quarterly run-rate (~$703m); the EBITDA step-up from ~$50m H1 quarterly average is the real change.

### iGaming

**iGaming remains the franchise with 20% GGR share in active markets, actives +2%, NGR per active +4% and Q3 contribution above $135m.**

- Game of Thrones launch across the US, Ontario and Alberta was described as the most successful franchise launch in BetMGM's history.
- Borgata brand refresh lifted average daily actives 28%; progressive SideBet jackpot in MI/WV and live-streamed slots in NJ/PA differentiate from aggregators.
- Alberta July launch, the first newly regulated iGaming market since 2022, is tracking above Ontario on per-player metrics.
- Entain supplies the platform and exclusive studio content, an endorsement of the Entain technology stack; H1 iGaming contribution was $280m.

### Online sports

**Handle was $3.24bn (+3%) with growth hold-driven; promotions rose to ~4.0% of handle from ~3.7% while actives fell to 878k (-3%).**

- GGR hold 10.7% (+55bps), NGR hold 6.7% (+30bps); Nevada handle +8%.
- Handle per active +13% and NGR per active +18% imply sports actives down high-single digits; 9M actives 909k (-5%).
- GGR share 8% in OSB and 14% blended.
- Management said acquisition environment has 'improved a lot' since the World Cup, with CPAs down and payback periods the best since the turn of the year.
- Macquarie (per NEXT.io) flagged acquisition returns at their best in several quarters and stable share despite lower OSB marketing.

### Cash to Entain

**BetMGM paid $68m to parents in Q3 ($18m fees, $50m distributions), so Entain's half is ~$34m (~£26m).**

- Parent fees started in 2026 and flow to Entain as revenue/EBITDA, £6.7m (~$8.8m) in H1.
- Adjusted EBITDA less capex ($4m in Q3) is a reasonable proxy for cash to parents.
- Entain's H1 share of BetMGM profit was £19.7m (~$26m) versus £33.6m (~$44m) in H1-25, reflecting parent fees and a more competitive market.
- Q3 should mark a sharp improvement; Entain's 50% of Q3 adjusted EBITDA is ~$43m (~£33m).

### Guidance

**Guidance was reiterated at the lower end of $2.9-3.1bn revenue and $300-350m EBITDA, requiring Q4 of ~$784m and ~$115m respectively.**

- A seasonally strong NFL/NBA quarter makes the revenue hurdle reasonable; the EBITDA hurdle is demanding but achievable if the Q3 cost base holds.
- The $500m adjusted EBITDA ambition is now 'in the coming years'; delivery may extend beyond 2027 if prediction-market disruption and competitive intensity persist.
- MGM reports on 28 October and will give the first read on Q4 trading.

### Our view

**This is the best-quality number in the Entain print, but a margin story on a shrinking active base with hold- and promo-dependent OSB.**

- The iGaming moat (20% share, exclusive content, omnichannel via MGM Rewards and Nevada) is intact and why the JV is worth more than its P&L.
- OSB is being managed for cash, the right call at 8% share but it caps the growth multiple.
- Prediction markets remain the unquantified wildcard.

## 7. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- UK&I online +14% cc on +15% volumes with gaming +17% — a fourth consecutive double-digit quarter while absorbing the 21%→40% RGD step-up; management cites continued market-share gains and the release calls it 'ahead of expectations'. _(structural)_
- BetMGM adjusted EBITDA $86m (+112%), contribution margin +4.7pp to 24.6%, 9M adj. EBITDA $185m (+24%), and $68m of cash to parents in the quarter (~$34m / ~£26m to Entain) — the JV is now a cash contributor, not a funding call. _(structural)_
- Online gaming NGR +8% group-wide (9M +9%), with New Zealand, Spain and Canada all double-digit and three new NZ licences adding an iGaming option — the growth base beyond the UK is narrow but real. _(structural)_
- The ~4pp sports-margin drag is luck, not demand: group sports margin was only -0.2pp YoY against a weak Q3-25 comp, and ex-Brazil online volumes were +7%; normalised online growth would have been ~+7%. _(one-off)_
- Guidance held after the Brazil shock: ex-Brazil FY26 online NGR expected at the top end of 5-7% (9M +8%), and the EBITDA range (£910-960m / ~$1.20-1.27bn) survived because Brazil carried little profit and its marketing is now switched off. _(mixed)_
- CEE 20% sale (€425m / ~$476m, EV €2.1bn / ~$2.35bn, c.10x EBITDA) on track to close in Q4 — a value crystallisation at a multiple well above Entain's own, with proceeds directed at deleveraging. _(one-off)_
- UK retail LFL +3%, H1 retail EBITDA +6% at a 21% margin, and UK&I retail contribution margin 72% — the estate remains a reliable cash generator ahead of any duty change. _(structural)_

**Lowlights**

- Brazil NGR -36% in Q3, then zero from 25 Sep under a provisional ban: ~5% of planned FY26 online NGR gone, guidance assumes no reopening in 2026, and management will re-enter only conservatively if Congress amends or rejects the measure. _(structural)_
- International online ex-Brazil grew only +1% (9M +2%), with online volumes -3%: Italy -6% (online -5%), Australia decelerated to +1% from +13% in H1 — the growth story outside the UK is Spain/NZ/Canada and little else. _(mixed)_
- Sports volumes are flat before any margin effect — online sports wagers -1%, group +1% — and sports NGR -3%; the gaming-led mix (+8%) concentrates growth in the most heavily taxed product line. _(watch)_
- EBITDA guidance is now at the floor: lower end of £910-960m (~$1.20bn) vs the £934m (~$1.23bn) consensus management was 'comfortable' with on 13 Aug; H2 implied ~£437m (~$577m) vs £473m in H1 as two quarters of 40% RGD bite. UK&I online EBITDA margin already 23.1% vs 28.6%. _(structural)_
- UK tax escalation is open-ended: remote betting duty to 25% from Apr 2027 and Budget speculation on machine gaming duty 20%→40% (mechanically ~£100m / ~$130m p.a. on UK retail gaming NGR before mitigation — our estimate); the CEO statement led with a plea to government, which tells you the scale of the exposure. _(governance)_
- Leverage: reported 3.1x (look-through 3.6x at FY25) with net debt £3.6bn (~$4.75bn); CEE deconsolidation lifts pro-forma reported leverage toward ~3.4-3.5x on the lower-end EBITDA guide (our estimate), and the £500m (~$660m) 2028 cash target is to be 'articulated differently' at FY results — read: re-based. _(watch)_
- BetMGM growth is price, not volume: average monthly actives -3% (9M -5%), OSB revenue +7% driven by +55bps GGR hold, promo intensity up ~25-30bps of handle, and the $500m EBITDA ambition has slipped from 2027 to 'the coming years'. _(mixed)_
- Disclosure: no quarterly P&L, no group actives/ARPU, no Q3 marketing or margin data — a 1-2pp sports-margin miss cost c.£20m (~$26m) of EBITDA in Q3-25 by management's own account, and investors cannot see the Q3-26 equivalent until March. _(watch)_

## 8. Forecast & Outlook

The guidance bridge is three-stage, and the division is International/Brazil at every step. At FY25 results (5 Mar 2026) Entain guided FY26 online NGR +5-7% cc and an online EBITDA margin of 23-24% (incl. CEE). With the CEE exit (25 Jun) the group re-based to continuing operations: underlying EBITDA exc. parent fees £910-960m (~$1.20-1.27bn) and online margin 21-22% (CEE out, ~25% UK-tax mitigation in); at H1 (13 Aug) it said it was 'comfortable' with the £934m (~$1.23bn) consensus midpoint. On 28 Sep, three days after Brazil's provisional online ban, online NGR growth was cut to 4-6% (ex-Brazil at the top end of 5-7%) and both EBITDA and online margin were steered to the lower end of their ranges, Brazil having been ~5% of expected online NGR with modest EBITDA. The 15 Oct update reiterated the 28 Sep guide without change, so the Q3 print carries no new estimate revision — the ~£24m (~$32m) cut versus August consensus was already taken. BetMGM's $2.9-3.1bn / $300-350m ranges were likewise reiterated at the lower end.

- **FY26 online NGR growth (cc):** +4-6% (ex-Brazil: top end of 5-7%) (was +5-7% (5 Mar / 13 Aug); cut 28 Sep on Brazil ban; reiterated 15 Oct — International/Brazil)
- **FY26 underlying EBITDA exc. parent fees:** lower end of £910-960m (~$1,201-1,267m) (was midpoint / £934m (~$1,233m) consensus (13 Aug); ~-£24m (~-$32m), Brazil-driven)
- **FY26 online underlying EBITDA margin:** lower end of 21-22% (was 21-22% (25 Jun, ex-CEE); 23-24% incl. CEE at 5 Mar)
- **BetMGM FY26 (100% JV):** NR $2.9-3.1bn; adj. EBITDA $300-350m — lower end (unchanged; 9M $2,116m / $185m → Q4 ≥ ~$784m / ~$115m implied)

**Q4-26 (estimate):** we expect group online NGR growth of roughly -1% to +2% cc including Brazil, and +4-6% ex-Brazil. 9M is +5% with Brazil still in the base; the Q4-25 base carries a full quarter of Brazil (~4-5% of online NGR by our estimate, hence a ~4-5pp drag), and the CFO explicitly flagged a 'somewhat softer' Q4 as Australia laps its reinvigoration and UK comps toughen. **FY26 online NGR therefore lands at ~4% cc — the bottom of the 4-6% range — with ex-Brazil at ~7%** (9M +8% ex-Brazil × ~0.74 weight + Q4 ~+4-5% × ~0.26 ≈ 7.0%). A normal-margin Q4 is the main swing: a 1-2pp sports-margin miss cost c.£20m (~$26m) of EBITDA in Q3-25 by management's account. **FY26 underlying EBITDA exc. parent fees: we model ~£905-920m (~$1.19-1.21bn)**, i.e. the lower end — H2 implied at the floor is ~£437m (~$577m) vs H1 £473m (~-8% HoH), reflecting two quarters of 40% RGD (vs one in H1) partly offset by World Cup marketing roll-off, Brazil marketing savings and the ~25% tax mitigation; the H1 online margin of 21.4% should settle at ~21% for the year. **Margin:** group underlying EBITDA margin ~18% on ~£5.0bn (~$6.6bn) of continuing NGR (FY25 continuing base is our estimate), vs 18.8% in H1 and ~20.6% in H1-25. **Leverage — direction down on look-through, optically flat-to-up on reported:** reported 3.1x at June; the CEE 20% proceeds (~£361m / ~$476m in Q4) take net debt to ~£3.24bn (~$4.3bn), but deconsolidation removes ~£180-190m (~$240-250m) of annual CEE EBITDA from the denominator, so pro-forma reported leverage ex-CEE is ~3.4-3.5x on the lower-end guide (estimate); the sub-3x target depends on the remaining 47.5% exit. BetMGM distributions (~$25m/quarter to Entain at the Q3 rate) plus parent fees (~$9m/quarter) are the incremental cash positives; the interim dividend (10.3p / ~$0.14, ~£66m / ~$87m) and ~£108m (~$142m) of half-year interest are the fixed calls. **FY27:** the 25% remote betting duty from April 2027 and any MGD change are not in guidance; we expect the £500m (~$660m) 2028 cash target to be re-expressed at FY results in March — most likely on an ex-CEE, including-BetMGM-distributions basis — and FY27 consensus to drift lower on UK duty until the Budget is known.

**What to watch next quarter:**

- UK Autumn Budget: machine gaming duty (20%→40% speculation), any further RGD/GBD changes, and Entain's quantified mitigation and shop-closure plan.
- Brazil: the Congressional vote within the 120-day window (runs to late January 2027), reopening terms, and whether Entain re-enters — guidance assumes no 2026 revenue.
- Q4 sports margins through the football/NFL calendar — the single largest swing factor for landing at £910m; management's Q3-25 sensitivity was c.£20m (~$26m) of EBITDA per 1-2pp miss in a quarter.
- CEE 20% completion in Q4, the restated continuing-ops base and leverage definition at FY results, and timing/pricing of the remaining 47.5% exit.
- MGM Resorts Q3 results (28 Oct): BetMGM Q4 run-rate, actives trend, Alberta ramp, prediction-market exposure and the $115m Q4 adj. EBITDA hurdle.
- International ex-Brazil online volumes turning positive; Australia's lap of H2-25; Italy sports margin normalisation and the Eurobet relaunch.
- FY results (March 2027): the re-based cash-flow target, FY27 guidance incorporating GBD at 25%, and UK&I online EBITDA margin trajectory after a full year at 40% RGD.
- Consensus: FY26 EBITDA needs to settle at ~£910m (~$1.2bn); watch for FY27 cuts on tax rather than on Q3 trading.
