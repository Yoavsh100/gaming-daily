Aristocrat Leisure Limited

ALL.AX · Released 12 Nov 2026 · Currency AUD (A$) — company reporting currency; tables in A$ millions with ~US$ equivalents at AUD/USD 0.6841 (RBA 4pm fix, 12 Nov 2026). Segment KPIs for North America, Product Madness and Interactive are shown in US$ as the company reports them. · Data confidence: HIGH

Market reaction: Shares opened at A$65.80, fell as much as ~7% intraday to A$61.18 (~US$41.85) as the CEO succession announcement overshadowed the result (-6.2% at A$61.78 at 12:08pm AEDT per Capital Brief), then recovered to close at A$65.02 (~US$44.48), -1.3% on the day (12 Nov 2026). The following day JP Morgan raised its FY27 adjusted EBITDA forecast by ~4%.

The one-line verdict

A clean, Gaming-led FY26 (year to 30 Sep 2026): normalised NPATA A$1,578.5m (~US$1,080m) +1.8% reported / +9.7% constant currency and EPSA 259.3c (~US$1.77) +13% cc, built on ~6,500 net North American Gaming Operations adds to 81,678 units at a 39% share and A$1.93bn (~US$1.32bn) returned to shareholders. The quality fades into the second half — H2 NPATA A$784.5m (~US$537m) was -4% YoY reported (≈+4% cc, our estimate, vs +16% cc in H1), ~A$45m of Light & Wonder settlement recovery sits inside the "normalised" corporate line, Interactive margins fell 280bps and D&D ran ahead of guidance. The ~7% intraday sell-off (close -1.3% at A$65.02 / ~US$44.48) was about CEO Trevor Croker's April-2027 retirement and the unquantified "NPATA growth in cc" FY27 outlook, not the numbers.

1Executive summary

  • Headline: solid on earnings, soft on reported revenue, no clear beat. Normalised revenue A$6,092.4m (~US$4,168m) -3.2% reported but +3.8% constant currency (cc); EBITA A$2,236.1m (~US$1,530m) +0.1% / +7.9% cc; NPATA A$1,578.5m (~US$1,080m) +1.8% / +9.7% cc; EPSA 259.3c (~US$1.77) +4.9% / +13.0% cc. A stronger A$ (FY average 0.6933 vs 0.6414) stripped A$441m from revenue and A$123m from NPATA. JP Morgan said Gaming Ops net adds and fee-per-day beat its forecasts; a public consensus NPATA print could not be verified [unverified].
  • Statutory: continuing PAT A$1,364.4m (~US$933m) +15.2%; total profit A$1,371.4m (~US$938m) -16.4% because FY25 carried A$456.2m from the discontinued Plarium disposal. Statutory diluted EPS 225.3c (~US$1.54) vs 261.5c; continuing diluted EPS 224.1c (~US$1.53) vs 188.7c. Statutory ETR jumped to 35.1% (28.5%) on a A$152.1m deferred-tax charge relating to historical group restructuring (treated as a significant item).
  • Normalisation adds back A$79.0m net after tax, and one more A$45m hides inside 'normalised'. Significant items: L&W litigation settlement A$138.7m pre-tax (A$100.3m post) as a gain; Big Fish/Plarium gains A$68.9m; acquisition costs (A$62.9m); White Label exit costs (A$29.8m); the DTA charge (A$152.1m). Separately, ~A$45m (US$30m) of the A$184m (~US$126m) total L&W settlement was booked in the normalised corporate line as legal-cost recovery — strip it out and normalised EBITA was -1.9% reported (~+6% cc), not +0.1% / +7.9%. FY27 laps this in H1.
  • Guidance scorecard: FY26 delivered on every stated metric bar D&D; FY27 is directionally identical and unquantified. May-26 guidance was 'NPATA growth in cc' (delivered +9.7%), NA Gaming Ops net adds at the 'upper end' of 4,000–5,000 (delivered ~6,500), fee-per-day to rise (US$53.93, +1.3%; H2 +3% sequential), UA 18–21% of Product Madness revenue (19%), ETR ~27% (27.4%), D&D mid-single-digit cc growth (missed: +9% cc to A$810.4m / ~US$554m, 'ahead of guidance' on new iLottery contracts and acquisitions). FY27: 'NPATA growth on a constant-currency basis', with Gaming revenue/share growth, PM share gains with rising DTC, and Interactive 'accelerating' toward the FY29 US$1bn revenue target. The call transcript refers to slide 17 as 'EBITA growth'; we treat the ASX release wording (NPATA) as authoritative but flag the inconsistency.
  • Which division drove it: Gaming, unambiguously. Gaming segment profit A$2,208.2m (~US$1,511m) +2.2% / +9.8% cc at a 54.9% margin (+30bps) is now 70% of segment profit (68% FY25). Product Madness profit A$752.2m (~US$515m) -6.5% / +1.1% cc (FY25 included Big Fish social-casual, sold Oct-25); Interactive profit A$177.6m (~US$121m) -13.2% / -5.8% cc. Total segment profit A$3,138.0m (~US$2,147m) -1.0% / +6.6% cc.
  • H2 decelerated sharply (our derivation, FY26 less HY26). H2 revenue A$3,064.2m (~US$2,096m) -6.1% YoY (+1.2% HoH); H2 EBITA A$1,118.7m (~US$765m) -5.4% YoY with margin 36.5% vs 36.9% in H1; H2 NPATA A$784.5m (~US$537m) -4.1% YoY and -1.2% HoH. Implied cc growth in H2 was only ≈+1% revenue / ≈+4% NPATA (est.) versus +6% / +16% in H1 — the Big Fish disposal (~US$100m of FY25 revenue), the White Label exit and tougher Gaming comps all land in the second half.
  • North American Gaming Operations is the engine. Installed base +6,453 units (+8.6%) to 81,678 — Class III premium 51,590 (+13.8%), Class II 30,088 (+0.7%); market share +2.7pts to 39% (Eilers 2Q26); fee-per-day US$53.93 (+1.3% FY, +3% H2 sequential). Adds were back-half weighted (~2,000 H1 / ~4,400 H2), so the exit run-rate (81,678 × US$53.93 × 365 ≈ US$1.61bn annualised vs ≈US$1.53bn on our FY26 average-base estimate) carries ~5% revenue momentum into FY27 before any new placements. Aristocrat held 17 of Eilers' top-25 premium leased games and 1.4x house-average performance.
  • Outright sales were strong in every region. NA units 26,923 (+8.5%) at ASP US$21,772 (+4.9%), with adjacencies (Georgia COAM, Illinois VGT) 6,762 units (+4.9%, double-digit in both states); ANZ 11,007 units (+13.2%), ASP A$25,649 (~US$17,546) +7.5%, ship share 45% (+2pts); RoW ex-ANZ units -3.0% (LatAm) but ASP US$23,824 +8.0% (Asia mix). Rest of World profit A$379.0m (~US$259m) +10.3%, margin 44.9% (+270bps). Gaming operations revenue A$2,206.2m (~US$1,509m) -0.4% reported (FX-masked ~high-single-digit US$ growth); outright sales & other A$1,814.5m (~US$1,241m) +4.0%.
  • Product Madness: share gains in a shrinking market, but growth stalled in H2. Social Casino revenue US$1,076.8m +2.2%, bookings US$1,071.3m +1.6%, profit US$521.4m +7.0%, margin 48.4% (+210bps) against a Social Casino Slots market down ~11%; share +2pts to 24% (call: +290bps). DTC reached 27% of revenue (30% in H2; 16% in FY25) and is the margin lever; UA rose to 19% of revenue (17%). DAU -8% to 1.2m, ABPDAU US$2.36 (+8%). H2 social casino revenue was flat YoY in US$ (US$535.1m vs US$536.2m) after +4.7% in H1.
  • Interactive: right mix, wrong margin. Total revenue (incl. 50% NPI JV share) US$457.2m +3.4%: Content US$108.6m +16.9% (Lightning Link High Stakes/Tiki Fire; iSlots share 8%), iLottery US$179.6m +16.8% (Michigan and Massachusetts live in July; 69% US gross-wager share), Platforms US$169.0m -13.6% (White Label exit, CXS contract timing slipping into FY27, early-stage Awager/Gaming Analytics). Profit US$122.6m -6.2%, margin 26.8% (-280bps); H2 margin 25.7%. Management: ex-White Label, revenue growth was 'almost 10%'. The FY29 US$1bn target now needs a ~22% CAGR.
  • Cost programme: A$100m (~US$68m) of annualised savings 'during FY27' from the One Aristocrat operating model and AI-enabled productivity; management explicitly said a portion will be reinvested in strategic priorities, so full drop-through should not be modelled. Underlying corporate costs were 'marginally higher' (inflation and redundancy payments offset by productivity savings) once the L&W recovery is excluded.
  • Cash: generous returns, weaker conversion, rising leverage. Operating cash flow A$1,889.1m (~US$1,292m) -2.3% on a A$265m working-capital build (Gaming receivables, inventory +16% for The Baron/Reign cabinets, payables timing; NWC/revenue 8.6% vs 5.8%). FCF (OCF less net capex A$436.8m) ≈A$1,452m (~US$994m), -3.8%. Returned A$1,930m (~US$1,320m): dividends A$603.4m (~US$413m) plus buyback A$1,326.7m (~US$908m; 24.5m shares at an average A$54.09 / ~US$37.00). Net debt rose A$712m to A$1,134.9m (~US$776m), 0.4x Bank EBITDA (0.2x); cash A$373.7m (~US$256m) -71%; liquidity ~A$1.5bn (~US$1.0bn).
  • Balance sheet reset in April-26: new unsecured US$850m Term Loan A (Apr-2031, US$845m drawn) and US$1.0bn RCF (Apr-2030, US$206m drawn), ~95% of floating exposure swapped; interest cover 31.5x; ratings BBB (S&P), Baa3 positive (Moody's), BBB (Fitch). Net interest fell A$29m to A$72.7m (~US$50m).
  • Buyback has ~A$0.6bn (~US$410m) of headroom under the A$2.5bn authorisation running to 12 May 2027 (A$1.9bn completed at 30 Sep); shares on issue -4.0% to 592.3m and WANOS -2.9% explain the gap between NPATA +1.8% and EPSA +4.9% reported. Final DPS 51c (~US$0.35), unfranked, payable 8 Dec; FY DPS 101c (~US$0.69), 38% NPATA payout; DRP suspended.
  • Management transition is the share-price story. CEO Trevor Croker (since Mar-2017) retires April 2027; Dafne Guisard — Chief Commercial Officer EMEA since Feb-26 and head of Product Madness since May-26, i.e. less than 15 months inside the group — is CEO-elect and relocates from London to Las Vegas, subject to regulatory pre-approvals. The Board called it 'an evolution, not a reset' after an internal and external search. Other changes: Dylan Slaney (CEO Interactive, Nov-25), Bob Serr (CTO, Feb-26), Michael Rumbolz (ex-Everi) joined the Board Aug-26, Pat Ramsey retired Feb-26, Bill Lance became Lead US Director.
  • M&A and legal. Acquired Awager (live slot streaming) for A$112.4m (~US$77m, Nov-25) and Gaming Analytics (AI player analytics) for A$83.8m (~US$57m, Jan-26) — A$138.7m of goodwill, both early-stage and loss-making, a drag on Interactive 'this year, maybe next year as well'. L&W litigation (Dragon Train) settled Jan-26 for A$184m (~US$126m) cash; a High Court patent win and an Ainsworth licensing agreement were cited on the call; the NeoGames legal provision was released.
  • Regulatory and risk disclosures to note: social casino scrutiny 'in at least one US state and Australia' (age assurance, consumer protection); prediction markets flagged as a new regulatory/competitive risk to regulated and tribal customers; tariffs and memory/semiconductor pricing on the supply chain; UAE market opening targeted for 2027; withdrawal from selected jurisdictions as part of de-risking.
  • Thesis read-through. The land-based franchise is compounding (Gaming cc profit +10%, ops base +9%, share 39%) and funds an aggressive buyback, but the two 'growth' engines are not yet pulling their weight — PM is a share-gain story in an -11% market and Interactive margins are going the wrong way while D&D runs ahead of guidance. FY27 is a year of annualising placements and cost-out under a new CEO; the stock de-rated intraday on succession, not on the numbers.
  • Share price: opened A$65.80, traded as low as A$61.18 (~-7%) as succession overshadowed the result, closed A$65.02 (~US$44.48), -1.3% on the day.

3Segment deep-dive: Aristocrat Gaming (land-based: North America + Rest of World)

Gaming Ops base +8.6% to 81,678 units at a 39% share and outright sales up in every region drove profit +10% cc at a 55% margin; H2 cc growth roughly halved versus H1 and FPD growth was only +1.3% for the year.

Segment results

Revenue A$4,020.7m (+8.7% cc) and profit A$2,208.2m (+9.8% cc) at a 54.9% margin; H2 cc growth roughly halved versus H1.

  • Revenue A$4,020.7m (~US$2,751m), +1.5% reported, +8.7% cc; profit A$2,208.2m (~US$1,511m), +2.2% / +9.8% cc, margin 54.9% (+30bps).
  • Segment is now 66% of group revenue and 70% of segment profit (63% / 68% a year ago).
  • Gaming operations revenue A$2,206.2m (~US$1,509m), -0.4% reported; units +8.6% and FPD +1.3% imply high-single-digit US$ growth. Outright sales +4.0% to A$1,814.5m.
  • H2 revenue A$2,060.1m (~US$1,409m), +5.1% HoH, -1.4% YoY; H2 profit A$1,144.9m (~US$783m), +7.7% HoH, +1.4% YoY; H2 margin 55.6% (+160bps YoY).
  • Implied H2 cc revenue growth roughly 6% versus about 12% in H1 (estimates); the comp is getting harder.

North America ops

Installed base +6,453 units (+8.6%) to 81,678 and share up 2.7pts to 39%, but FPD only +1.3% for the year.

  • Revenue +9.1% to US$2,201.1m; profit +8.7% to US$1,267.1m; margin 57.6%, down 20bps on outright-sales mix substantially offset by operating leverage.
  • Base: Class III premium 51,590 (+13.8%), Class II 30,088 (+0.7%); versus May guidance of 4,000–5,000 at the 'upper end'; roughly 2,000 added in H1, 4,400 in H2.
  • Fee per day US$53.93, +1.3% for the year, H2 up 3% sequentially; base-weighted dilution from the placement wave is visible.
  • 17 of Eilers' top-25 premium leased games, running 1.4x house average; MONOPOLY Big Board Bucks Slot of the Year for an eighth consecutive year.
  • Class III premium is now 63% of the base — right on yield, but where competitor response (Light & Wonder post-settlement, IGT/Everi) will be aimed.
“a very good outcome”Management

North America outright sales

Outright units +8.5% to 26,923 at ASP US$21,772 (+4.9%); exit base annualises to roughly US$1.61bn of ops revenue.

  • Driven by Spooky Link on The Baron Portrait and the August launch of Reign Single; adjacencies 6,762 units (+4.9%) with double-digit growth in Georgia COAM and Illinois VGT.
  • Broker commentary: H1 units roughly 13,100 with ship share 31% (+260bps), implying ~13,800 units in H2.
  • Group inventories A$305.7m vs A$264.1m, plus Gaming receivables and payables timing, explain most of the A$265m group working-capital outflow.
  • FY27 named launches: MONOPOLY High Roller, MONOPOLY Double Wild Jackpots and Dragon Link ('early next year'); management declined to guide net adds.
  • Exit base annualises to roughly US$1.61bn versus ≈US$1.53bn on the FY26 average base — about 5% revenue momentum before FY27 placements.
“one of the strongest pipelines ever planned”Management

Rest of World

RoW revenue +3.7% to A$844.2m and profit +10.3% to A$379.0m, margin up 270bps to 44.9% on volume and ASP.

  • ANZ sold 11,007 units (+13.2%) at ASP A$25,649 (~US$17,546), +7.5% on cabinet mix; ship share 45% (+2pts).
  • RoW ex-ANZ units -3.0% to 6,731 on weaker Latin America; ASP +8.0% to US$23,824 on Asian cabinet mix; RoW Class III units +6.4% to 17,738.
  • Positioning for the UAE market opening in 2027; supplier-of-the-year awards in APAC and EMEA.
  • Risks: prediction markets outside state and tribal frameworks threatening tribal capex, tariff and memory/semiconductor cost pressure, withdrawal from selected jurisdictions.

FY27 guidance

Guidance is qualitative; we estimate cc revenue growth of 6–8% at a ~55% margin with 3,500–4,500 net adds.

  • Management guides 'continued revenue and market share growth' supported by back-half-weighted placements and product cadence.
  • Ops revenue up high-single-digit on annualisation plus a smaller placement year (3,500–4,500 net adds vs FY26's 6,500).
  • Outright sales flat to +3% against a +8.5% unit comp; ANZ holding share at 45%.
  • Swing factors: FPD dilution from a second placement wave, Dragon Link relaunch timing, and operator capital budgets staying 'supportive' if prediction markets erode tribal cash flows.

Customer economics

  • On our arithmetic each percentage point of DTC mix is worth around 0.3pt of margin before UA reinvestment.

Content +17% and iLottery +17% are real, but the White Label exit, soft CXS and loss-making acquisitions cut profit 6% in US$ and the margin 280bps to 26.8%; the FY29 US$1bn target now needs a ~22% CAGR.

Total revenue US$457.2m (+3.4%) but total profit US$122.6m (-6.2%) at a 26.8% margin, down 280bps; trajectory flat-to-down within the year.

  • A$ terms: revenue A$509.6m (~US$349m), -5.0% reported, +0.8% cc; profit A$177.6m (~US$121m), -13.2% / -5.8% cc; A$ margin down 320bps to 34.9%.
  • H2: revenue A$247.6m (~US$169m), -9.3% YoY, -5.5% HoH; profit A$82.4m (~US$56m), -9.5% YoY, -13.4% HoH; margin 33.3% (flat YoY, -300bps HoH).

  • H2 total revenue US$226.9m up just 0.4% YoY (H1 +6.5%); H2 profit US$58.3m down 0.9% at a 25.7% margin.

Content and iLottery

Content revenue +16.9% to US$108.6m and iLottery +16.8% to US$179.6m on market access and new state launches.

  • Lightning Link High Stakes entered regulated North American online RMG in July and Lightning Link Tiki Fire in September, alongside Buffalo, Buffalo Gold Collection and Mo Mummy ports.

  • Interactive's growth lines are real: Content +16.9% to US$108.6m (iSlots share 8%, Lightning Link brand launched online) and iLottery +16.8% to US$179.6m (Michigan and Massachusetts live, 69% US gross-wager share); ex-White Label, segment revenue grew 'almost 10%'. structural

7Forecast & outlook

Guidance bridge. There is no numeric guidance to bridge, only direction. The FY27 outlook published on 12 Nov 2026 is the same sentence rolled forward — 'NPATA growth over the full year to 30 September 2027 on a constant currency basis' — with three stated drivers: continued revenue and share growth at Aristocrat Gaming (the division that carried FY26 and that management expects to carry FY27 given back-half-weighted placements and the Dragon Link/MONOPOLY launch slate), continued share growth with rising DTC at Product Madness, and 'accelerating performance' at Interactive toward the FY29 US$1bn total-revenue target. The A$100m (~US$68m) cost programme was reiterated for delivery during FY27 with partial reinvestment; the buyback (A$0.6bn remaining to 12 May 2027) and a ~27–28% ETR were implied rather than restated. Independent view. Our FY27 estimates (constant currency). Gaming revenue +6–8% with a ~55% margin: ops revenue up high-single-digit on annualisation of the 6,453 FY26 adds (exit base 81,678 × US$53.93 × 365 ≈ US$1.61bn vs ≈US$1.53bn on our FY26 average-base estimate, i.e. ≈+5% before placements) plus a smaller placement year (we assume 3,500–4,500 net adds), and outright sales flat to +3% against an +8.5% unit comp, helped by Dragon Link, the MONOPOLY titles and UAE. Product Madness social casino revenue flat to +3% in US$ with a 49–50% margin as DTC passes 30% (≈0.3pt of margin per point of DTC mix, our estimate) and UA stays at 18–21%. Interactive total revenue +10–14% (US$500–520m) as the White Label exit annualises in H1 and Michigan/Massachusetts iLottery contribute a full year, with the margin recovering to 27–29%. Group D&D +mid-to-high single digit cc (A$860–880m); A$100m of savings with roughly half reinvested (est.); net interest A$80–90m on higher average net debt; ETR 27–28%. That yields normalised NPATA of ≈A$1.69–1.74bn (~US$1.16–1.19bn), +7–10% cc, and EPSA +10–13% cc on a ~3% lower share count — comfortably 'growth', but H1 FY27 will look pedestrian because it laps the A$45m L&W recovery and H1 FY26's +16% cc NPATA. Margin: EBITA margin flat to +50bps (36.7–37.2%) — the corporate-line lap (~-200bps) offsets cost-out and Gaming leverage. Reported vs cc: spot AUD/USD 0.684 versus an FY26 average of 0.693 is a ~1% tailwind if sustained (sensitivity A$25m NPATA per US cent), so reported growth should converge with cc after two years of translation drag. Leverage: with FCF ≈A$1.5bn, dividends ≈A$0.62bn and the remaining A$0.6bn buyback (we expect renewal at the May-27 expiry), net debt/EBITDA drifts to 0.5–0.7x — immaterial for an investment-grade issuer but a change of direction from the net-cash years. Next period (H1 FY27, to 31 Mar 2027): we look for NA Gaming Ops net adds of 1,500–2,500 with FPD flat-to-up, H1 cc revenue growth of 3–5% and NPATA roughly flat-to-+5% cc against the inflated H1 FY26 base; a materially better print would require Dragon Link landing early and Interactive margins already recovering.

  • H1 FY27 NA Gaming Ops net adds and fee-per-day — whether Dragon Link ('early next year'), MONOPOLY High Roller and MONOPOLY Double Wild Jackpots sustain FPD or dilute it, and the competitive response from Light & Wonder post-settlement and the IGT/Everi combination.
  • Social casino bookings after a flat H2 in US$: DAU trend, DTC >30%, UA within 18–21%, and any regulatory action in the flagged US state or Australia (age assurance, platform steering rules).

  • Exogenous risks disclosed in the filing: prediction markets eroding tribal operators' economics and capex, tariff and memory/semiconductor cost inflation on cabinet margins, UAE market opening timing in 2027 and adjacencies (Georgia COAM, Illinois VGT) growth.
