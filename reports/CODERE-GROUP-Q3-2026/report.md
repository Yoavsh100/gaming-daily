# Codere Group () — Q3 2026 Analyst Report
** · Reporting currency: EUR (tables in € millions; ~USD shown at EUR/USD 1.105) · Results released 12 Nov 2026**
**Market reaction:** n/a for the group — Codere's equity is unlisted (Codere New Topco S.A., Luxembourg; held by former creditors since the late-2024 debt-for-equity restructuring). The listed subsidiary Codere Online (Nasdaq: CDRO) barely moved on 12 Nov 2026: secondary reports conflict (Investing.com described the stock as edging up; a Motley Fool transcript page shows −1.6%) — [unverified].

> A profitability quarter, not a growth quarter: group gross win rose just 6% to €344m (~$380m) but adj. EBITDA jumped 39% to €65.4m (~$72m), lifting margin ~450bps to 19.0%, with Retail (+32% to €54.0m on Mexico cost actions, Argentina and Spain) and Online (group-basis €11.3m vs €6.2m) both contributing and LTM retail EBITDA rising for an eighth straight quarter to €210.7m (~$233m). Public disclosure remains thin — no P&L below EBITDA, no net debt, no country split — and the €11.3m "online" EBITDA the group books is 2.7x the €4.2m Codere Online reports standalone, a definitional gap any buyer in the reported ~€2bn (~$2.2bn) sale process will price.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **EBITDA-led beat on a modest top line.** Q3-26 gaming revenue (gross win: stakes less prizes, pre-gaming-tax) €344m (~$380m), +6% YoY (+5% constant currency); adj. EBITDA €65.4m (~$72.3m), +39% (+35% cc); margin ~19.0% vs ~14.5% derived for Q3-25 (+~450bps). There is no sell-side consensus (equity is unlisted, audience is bondholders/sale bidders), so the yardstick is the company's own FY26 framework from April (~+5% gross win, >+8% adj. EBITDA) — Q3 ran far ahead on profit.
- **Retail drove ~70% of the increment.** Retail adj. EBITDA +32% to €54.0m (~$59.7m), i.e. ~€13m of the ~€18m YoY group increase; group-basis online adj. EBITDA €11.3m (~$12.5m) vs €6.2m added ~€5m. Management attributes the profit step-up 'principally' to operational improvements implemented in Mexico, plus positive Argentina, Spain and online; no country financials are given in the public release.
- **Retail volume is flat; this is a margin story.** Deriving retail gross win as group less Codere Online (€344.0m − €67.1m = ~€276.9m, ~$306m) implies only ~+1.5% YoY and ~−2% QoQ, so retail margin expanded ~450bps to ~19.5% of gross win — cost-out and mix, not demand.
- **Eight consecutive quarters of LTM retail improvement.** LTM retail adj. EBITDA €210.7m (~$233m), +23% YoY (implies ~€171m a year earlier); the annualised Q3 run-rate (4×€54m = €216m) is still above the LTM figure, so the trend has not yet plateaued.
- **FX helped at the margin, not decisively.** Reported +6%/+39% vs constant-currency +5%/+35%: roughly 1pt of revenue growth and 4pts of EBITDA growth came from currency (MXN strength; ARS under IAS 29 hyperinflation accounting adds noise).
- **Online: 30% gaming-win growth, Mexico +39%.** Codere Online (Nasdaq: CDRO, majority-owned) gaming win €67.1m (~$74.1m): Spain €24.8m (+13%), Mexico €37.2m (+39%; +26% cc), Other €5.2m (+86%); IFRS revenue €62.6m (~$69.2m), +31%; standalone adj. EBITDA €4.2m (~$4.6m), +45%; Q3 net income €5.5m vs €1.4m; 9M net income €11.1m (~$12.3m) vs a €1.7m loss.
- **Online EBITDA definition gap — flag it.** The group books 'online' adj. EBITDA of €11.3m vs CDRO's standalone €4.2m (and €6.2m vs €2.9m in Q3-25). The gap (~€7m) is persistent and most plausibly reflects intercompany fees paid by Codere Online to group entities that eliminate on consolidation (plus codere.it in Italy sitting outside CDRO), but the release does not reconcile it. It matters for CDRO minorities (standalone) and for a group buyer (consolidated).
- **Q3 online was flattered by the World Cup tail and FX.** The CFO said Q3 benefited from the final stages of the World Cup in July and favourable exchange rates, and that against a strong Q4-25 (gaming win €60.7m, +15%) growth 'in the coming months' will be more in line with underlying trends — i.e. single digits.
- **Codere Online guidance reiterated, not raised.** FY26 gaming win €255–265m (~$282–293m) and adj. EBITDA €20–25m (~$22–28m), unchanged since the July raise (from €235–245m / €15–20m). 9M gaming win €201.0m implies Q4 of €54–64m; management points to the upper part of the range, i.e. ~€62–64m (+2–5% YoY). 9M adj. EBITDA €16.1m implies Q4 of €3.9–8.9m vs €6.7m in Q4-25.
- **No formal group guidance update.** The April FY25 release framed FY26 as ~+5% gross win and >+8% adj. EBITDA (>€243m vs €225.1m). With Q2 +14% and Q3 +39%, the group is tracking well above that; the Q1-26 group print was not retrieved for this note [gap].
- **Mexico tax is the structural headwind the cost actions are masking.** CDRO's Mexico adj. EBITDA fell 15% to €1.7m (~$1.9m) despite +39% gaming win, on higher gaming taxes and marketing (timing of provisions). The same fiscal regime applies to the group's 67 Mexican halls and 12,366 machines — the retail 'operational improvements' are offsetting a tax step-up rather than pure margin expansion.
- **Monetisation, not actives, is driving Mexico online.** CDRO Mexico average monthly actives −3% YoY to 85.6k while gaming win +39% → derived ARPU ~€145/month (~$160), +43%. Spain is healthier: actives +5% to 52.8k, spend per active +7%, adj. EBITDA +24% to €7.2m (29% margin).
- **Colombia normalisation is a one-off.** 'Other' online gaming win +86% to €5.2m largely because Colombia's 19% VAT on deposits lapsed; Panama also cited as good. Actives in Other +37% to 28.4k.
- **Liquidity solid, leverage opaque.** Cash €136.9m (~$151m): €71.3m retail, €65.6m at Codere Online (no financial debt; ~€61m available; 9M cash flow €16.4m). The release gives no net debt; the last company marker is ~1.1x adj. EBITDA at FY25 after the late-2024 debt-for-equity restructuring cut debt from ~€1.4bn to under €200m (secondary).
- **Sale process is the real event.** Since March 2026 Jefferies and Macquarie have been mandated with a reported ~€2bn (~$2.2bn) ask; recent trade reports point to a different/piecemeal approach (break-up). On an LTM adj. EBITDA we estimate at ≥€250m (FY25 €225.1m + Q2/Q3 increments; Q1 unknown), €2bn is ~8x — rich for LatAm retail, cheap for online comps; the Q3 print helps the vendor.
- **Market position.** Blask Index places Codere as the 4th-largest online operator in both Spain and Mexico and 40th in Argentina (secondary); in Spain retail the company claims #1 in retail sports betting and #2 in B-type AWP machines. Footprint (FY25): 43,252 gaming terminals, 128 halls, 18,076 bingo seats, 6,252 betting terminals, 7,665 bars, 4 racetracks, 10,302 employees.
- **What management emphasises vs buries.** Emphasised: 39% EBITDA growth, eight-quarter retail streak, cash. Buried/absent: retail gross win by country, Mexico tax impact, net debt, capex, net income, and the group-vs-standalone online EBITDA reconciliation.
- **Capital allocation.** No group dividend or buyback disclosed; equity holders are former creditors with an exit incentive, so cash build and EBITDA momentum are being marshalled for the sale rather than returned.
- **Thesis read-through.** For bondholders and bidders, the quarter de-risks the equity story (margin, cash, no leverage pressure). The bear case is unchanged: low structural retail growth, Argentina macro/FX, Mexico taxation, Italian concession costs, and a 39% EBITDA jump that cannot be audited from the public release.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — Retail (land-based: Spain, Italy, Argentina, Mexico, Panama, Colombia, Uruguay)

*Retail adj. EBITDA +32% to €54.0m (~$59.7m) on roughly flat gross win — Mexico cost actions, Argentina and Spain do the work; country detail is withheld from the public release.*

### Retail EBITDA and margin

**Retail adj. EBITDA rose 32% to €54.0m (~$59.7m) on roughly flat gross win, lifting retail margin ~450bps to ~19.5%.**

- Prior-year base derived at ~€40.9m; retail gross win estimated at ~€276.9m (~$306m), group €344.0m less Codere Online's €67.1m.
- Retail gross win only ~1.5% higher YoY and ~2% lower than Q2's ~€283m.
- Margin ~19.5% of gross win vs ~15.0% a year ago, essentially all of the group's margin expansion.
- LTM retail EBITDA €210.7m (~$233m), +23% YoY, eighth consecutive quarter of LTM improvement; Q3 annualised run-rate ~€216m suggests cost base still being worked.
- Management attributes the step-up 'principally' to Mexico operational improvements, with Argentina and Spain positive; Italy, Panama, Colombia, Uruguay unmentioned, read as flat-to-down or immaterial.

### Mexico

**Mexico is the swing factor and the least transparent; cost actions appear to more than offset a 2026 gaming-tax step-up.**

- Footprint: 67 gaming halls, 12,366 machines, 302 bingo seats, 357 table seats, 35 sports-betting points plus the Las Américas complex in Mexico City.
- 'Operational improvements' are not quantified; usual levers are hall rationalisation, labour, machine-fleet optimisation and lease renegotiation.
- Mexico's gaming-tax burden stepped up in 2026; Codere Online explicitly cited higher Mexican gaming taxes this quarter, so unit economics did not improve by the full amount.
- MXN strength contributed to the ~4pt gap between reported (+39%) and constant-currency (+35%) group EBITDA growth, mostly from Mexico.
- We would want Mexican retail gross win in local currency before crediting the entire improvement to self-help.

### Argentina

**Argentina was again cited as positive, though IAS 29 hyperinflation accounting inflates nominal lines and adds a monetary-position effect.**

- 13 halls, 6,772 machines, 11,692 bingo seats; largest private gaming-hall operator in Buenos Aires Province, with online in the City via Codere Online.
- Group constant-currency framing shows FX a net tailwind at group level despite the peso.
- In FY25 Argentina was, with Spain, a 'main value-generation driver', supported by continued investment in gaming floors and equipment.
- Q4 swing risks: the peso crawling band and real-wage dynamics for the hall customer.

### Spain, Italy, smaller markets

**Spain delivered another positive quarter; Italy is unreferenced, while Panama, Colombia and Uruguay are small and unmentioned.**

- Spain: 8,852 machines, 1,064 bingo seats, 5,360 betting terminals across 5,856 bars, 759 arcades, 22 betting shops, 3 halls; #1 retail sports betting, #2 B-type AWP operator.
- Spain's positive quarter follows FY25's operational efficiencies and fleet optimisation.
- Italy: 10 halls, 7,660 machines, 5,018 bingo seats, 1,809 bars, Codere Network concession, codere.it launched 2025; concession-renewal and online-licence fees remain cost items to watch.
- Panama (12 halls, 2,214 machines, 480 table seats, Hipódromo Presidente Remón), Colombia (17 halls, 3,226 machines), Uruguay (6 halls, two racetracks, Hotel Casino Carrasco).

### Cash, valuation, guidance

**Retail is the valuation anchor at ~84% of LTM group EBITDA, but disclosure cannot separate tax, mix, volume and cost effects.**

- Retail cash €71.3m (~$78.8m) at 30 September; group net debt not disclosed, last marker ~1.1x adj. EBITDA at FY25.
- We treat the +32% as a blend of structural cost-out and favourable FX/comparison effects until the gated bondholder report confirms the country split.
- LTM trend is credible and the Q3 margin is good.
- No retail-specific guidance; group FY26 framework (~+5% gross win, >+8% adj. EBITDA) was not updated.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — Online (Codere Online, Nasdaq: CDRO — Spain, Mexico, Colombia, Panama, City of Buenos Aires)

*Gaming win +30% to €67.1m (~$74m) with Mexico +39% on ARPU rather than actives; standalone adj. EBITDA €4.2m (+45%) versus €11.3m booked by the group; Q4 guided to single-digit growth.*

### Group online results

**Gaming win rose 30% to €67.1m (~$74.1m) and standalone adj. EBITDA 45% to €4.2m, though margin fell sequentially to 6.3%.**

- Growth accelerated from +27% in Q2 and +13% in Q1, helped by World Cup final stages in July and favourable FX in the base; IFRS revenue €62.6m (~$69.2m), +31%, €4.5m gap mainly Mexican VAT and Colombian partner recognition.
- Sequentially gaming win fell 3.3% from Q2's record €69.4m: Spain −10% QoQ, Mexico +3% QoQ.
- Adj. EBITDA €4.2m (~$4.6m) vs €2.9m, 6.3% margin (+70bps YoY) but down from 8.4% in Q2 and 9.4% in Q1; IFRS EBITDA €2.9m vs €3.2m, so adjustments (LTIP, IAS 29) carry the growth.
- Nine months: gaming win €201.0m (+23%), revenue €187.3m (+22%), adj. EBITDA €16.1m vs €7.1m (margin 8.0% vs 4.3%), net income €11.1m (~$12.3m) vs €1.7m loss; Q3 net income €5.5m vs €1.4m, flattered by €3.4m warrant gain (€2.3m 9M).
- Cash €65.6m (~$72.5m, ~€61m available), no financial debt, up €3.0m QoQ; 9M cash flow €16.4m.

### Mexico

**Mexico gaming win grew 39% to €37.2m entirely on ARPU, while adj. EBITDA fell 15% to €1.7m on taxes and marketing.**

- Gaming win €37.2m (~$41.1m), +39% reported, +26% constant currency; revenue €32.9m (+37%).
- Monthly actives fell 3% to 85.6k; derived ARPU ~€145/month (~$160) vs ~€101, +43%.
- Monetisation-led growth likely combines rising casino share (~64% of NGR in FY25), VIP concentration and lower promo intensity — sustainable but lower quality and more exposed to a few accounts.
- Adj. EBITDA €1.7m (~$1.9m), 4.6% margin vs 7.5%, attributed to higher gaming taxes and marketing including timing of certain provisions and expenses.
- Galilea Montijo ambassador signing (October) and '99 Royale' launch signal elevated Q4 marketing; LTM revenue ~€123m, ~€8.7m adj. EBITDA at Q1-26; Blask ranks Codere #4.

### Spain

**Spain remains the cash engine: gaming win +13% to €24.8m and adj. EBITDA +24% to €7.2m at a 29.0% margin.**

- Gaming win and revenue both €24.8m (~$27.4m); actives +5% to 52.8k, spend per active +7% (derived ARPU ~€157/month, ~$173, vs ~€146).
- Adj. EBITDA €7.2m (~$8.0m), margin up ~260bps to 29.0% of gaming win.
- −10% QoQ is normalisation from Q2's World Cup-inflated €27.6m.
- Nine-month gaming win €77.9m (+18%) on actives 55.4k (+10%); LTM revenue ~€94m with ~€26m adj. EBITDA at Q1-26; Blask ranks the brand #4.
- Sportsbook handle, net margin, structural margin and promo % of handle not disclosed — n/a.

### Other markets

**Other markets gaming win rose 86% to €5.2m, mostly because Colombia's 19% deposit VAT lapsed; corporate drag flat at −€4.7m.**

- Gaming win €5.2m (~$5.7m) vs €2.8m; Panama also called out as good; actives +37% to 28.4k at ~€61/month ARPU.
- Nine-month Other gaming win €15.3m (+39%).
- Derived 'Other markets plus undistributed B2B/HQ opex' line −€4.7m vs −€4.9m, shrinking as a share of gaming win.
- Colombian licence renewed November 2025 through November 2030.

### Group reconciliation

**Grupo Codere books online adj. EBITDA of €11.3m against CDRO's €4.2m, a persistent ~€7m gap the release does not explain.**

- Q3-25 comparison: €6.2m group vs €2.9m CDRO.
- Most plausibly intra-group fees (platform, brand/licence, shared services) that eliminate on consolidation, plus codere.it in Italy sitting outside CDRO.
- Standalone is what CDRO minorities own; consolidated is what a buyer of the group gets.

### Guidance

**FY26 guidance of €255–265m gaming win and €20–25m adj. EBITDA reiterated, not raised, with Q4 growth slowing to single digits.**

- Gaming win €255–265m (~$282–293m), adj. EBITDA €20–25m (~$22–28m); management points to upper part of gaming-win range.
- Q4 faces a strong Q4-25 base (€60.7m, +15%).
- Implied Q4 gaming win €54–64m (upper end ~€62–64m, +2–5% YoY) and Q4 adj. EBITDA €3.9–8.9m vs €6.7m a year ago.
- Marketing spend and its % of revenue not disclosed in accessible materials — n/a.

## 5. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- Retail LTM adj. EBITDA up for an eighth straight quarter to €210.7m (~$233m), +23% YoY — the Q3 +32% is the continuation of a trend, not a one-off. _(structural)_
- Group adj. EBITDA margin ~19.0%, +~450bps YoY and +270bps QoQ, on retail gross win that is roughly flat — cost-out in Mexico plus Argentina and Spain efficiency is converting a low-growth top line into 39% profit growth. _(structural)_
- Constant-currency EBITDA growth of +35% confirms FX contributed only ~4pts; the underlying improvement is real. _(mixed)_
- Online gaming win +30% with Spain adj. EBITDA +24% to €7.2m (~$8.0m) at a 29% margin; CDRO 9M adj. EBITDA more than doubled to €16.1m (~$17.8m) and 9M net income swung to €11.1m (~$12.3m). _(structural)_
- Balance sheet: €136.9m (~$151m) group cash, a debt-free Codere Online adding ~€3m of cash per quarter, and FY25 leverage of ~1.1x — the restructured group is funding capex and the sale process from strength. _(structural)_
- Colombia's 19% deposit VAT lapsing drove Other-markets gaming win +86% and the July World Cup tail lifted Spain/Mexico — helpful, but non-recurring into Q4. _(one-off)_
- Mexico online ARPU +43% to ~€145/month (~$160): if retention holds, Mexican EBITDA inflects sharply once marketing normalises. _(early)_

**Lowlights**

- Disclosure is minimal: a two-page release with no P&L below EBITDA, no net debt, no capex/FCF and no country split; the detailed quarterly report sits behind the bondholder login. The 39% cannot be audited from public information. _(governance)_
- Online EBITDA definitional gap: the group books €11.3m vs CDRO's standalone €4.2m (2.7x), persistently and without reconciliation — a valuation issue for the ~€2bn sale and for CDRO minorities. _(governance)_
- Mexico taxation: CDRO Mexico adj. EBITDA −15% to €1.7m despite +39% gaming win (margin 4.6% vs 7.5%) on higher gaming taxes and marketing; the same regime weighs on 67 retail halls, so part of the retail cost-out is merely offsetting a structural tax step-up. _(structural)_
- Mexico online actives −3% YoY: growth is entirely monetisation, raising concentration and sustainability questions. _(watch)_
- Q4 deceleration is pre-announced: CDRO guides to single-digit growth against a strong Q4-25 (€60.7m, +15%) and FX could reverse; implied Q4 adj. EBITDA of €3.9–8.9m straddles last year's €6.7m. _(watch)_
- Retail volume ~+1.5% derived — the group is a margin story in a structurally low-growth retail estate, with Argentina (IAS 29, peso) adding reporting noise. _(structural)_
- Sale process overhang: ~€2bn (~$2.2bn) ask, reports of a break-up approach, and an owner base of former creditors with an exit incentive; a failed or piecemeal process would leave the group with a stranded strategy. _(watch)_

## 6. Forecast & Outlook

There was no formal group guidance change in the Q3 release. The reference framework remains the April FY25 outlook — FY26 gross win growth of ~5% and adj. EBITDA growth of >8% (i.e. >€243m / ~$269m vs €225.1m). With Q2 +14% and Q3 +39% EBITDA growth, the group is tracking well above that envelope, and the driver is Retail (Mexico cost actions, Argentina, Spain), with Online adding ~€5m YoY in Q3 on the group's basis. The listed subsidiary Codere Online reiterated FY26 gaming win of €255–265m and adj. EBITDA of €20–25m (raised in July from €235–245m / €15–20m) and explicitly flagged a single-digit Q4.

- **Group FY26 adj. EBITDA framework (set Apr-26, not updated):** >€243m (~$269m) (>+8% vs FY25 €225.1m; Q2/Q3 actuals running +14% / +39%)
- **Group Q3-26 adj. EBITDA actual:** €65.4m (~$72.3m) (+39% YoY (+€18.3m): Retail ≈ +€13m, Online ≈ +€5m)
- **Codere Online FY26 gaming-win guide:** €255–265m (~$282–293m) (Reiterated; Q4 implied €54–64m vs €60.7m Q4-25; management points to upper end)
- **Codere Online FY26 adj. EBITDA guide:** €20–25m (~$22–28m) (Reiterated; Q4 implied €3.9–8.9m vs €6.7m Q4-25)

Our independent take, all figures **estimates** given the missing Q1-26 group print: FY25 adj. EBITDA of €225.1m plus the Q2/Q3 YoY increments (+€7.1m, +€18.3m) already puts the LTM run-rate at **≥€250m (~$276m)** before Q1-26 and Q4-26. If Q4 holds Q3's ~+35% cc momentum against a seasonally strong Q4-25 base, **FY26 adj. EBITDA lands ~€255–270m (+13–20%)**; even a flat Q4 YoY implies ~€245–250m, i.e. above the >8% framework either way. We model **FY26 gross win ~€1.40–1.42bn (~$1.55–1.57bn), +3–5%**, at the low end of the ~5% framework given flat retail volumes and a single-digit online Q4 — so FY26 group margin should print ~18% vs 16.6% in FY25. **Leverage direction is down**: with €136.9m cash against gross debt last reported under €200m and positive free cash flow, net leverage should finish 2026 well below 1x (we estimate <0.5x), ahead of any sale. For Codere Online we expect Q4 gaming win ~€62–64m (+2–5%) and adj. EBITDA ~€5–7m, landing FY26 at ~€263m / ~€21–23m — within guidance but with margin compressing sequentially on Mexican marketing. Swing factors: MXN/ARS, Mexico's gaming-tax regime, Argentine consumer demand, Italian concession costs, and whether the sale proceeds whole or in parts.

**What to watch next quarter:**

- Country split in the bondholder report / FY26 results: Mexico retail gross win in MXN and margin — is the +32% self-help or FX/tax timing?
- Sale-process milestones (binding bids, whole vs break-up) and the implied multiple against ≥€250m LTM adj. EBITDA; any pre-sale refinancing of residual notes and first disclosure of net debt since FY25.
- Codere Online Q4: gaming win ≥€62m needed for the upper end of guidance; Mexico adj. EBITDA margin recovery vs stepped-up marketing (Montijo campaign, 99 Royale) and taxes; Mexico actives turning positive.
- A published reconciliation of group-basis online adj. EBITDA (€11.3m) to CDRO standalone (€4.2m).
- Argentina: IAS 29 effects, peso band and Buenos Aires Province bingo-hall licence terms; Italy: AWP/VLT and bingo concession renewals and codere.it ramp.
