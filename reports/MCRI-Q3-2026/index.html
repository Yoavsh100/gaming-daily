Q3 2026 · results · analyst report

Monarch Casino & Resort

MCRI · Released 27 Oct 2026 (after market close) · Currency USD (tables in $ millions unless stated) · Data confidence: MEDIUM

Market reaction: Shares gapped ~7% lower at the 28-Oct open ($107.31 vs a $115.28 prior close), traded below the $112.27 200-day average, and recovered through the session to close ~2.6% down at ~$112.3 (closing level per secondary sources).

The one-line verdict

A record but decelerating quarter: net revenue +2.4% to $146.3m and adj. EBITDA +2.1% to $56.0m were both Q3 records, yet both missed the Street (~$148m revenue; EPS $1.76 vs ~$1.86) and growth slowed sharply from H1's +6.4%/+10.3%. The 38.3% margin held, net cash rose to $137.4m with zero debt and $42m went back to holders in the quarter — but with organic growth converging on low single digits, the equity now hinges on Q4 seasonality, the pending ~$75m PCL appeal, and whether the Farahis finally deploy the balance sheet beyond buybacks.

1Executive summary

  • Record print, Street miss. Q3-26 net revenue +2.4% YoY to a record $146.3m vs ~$148.0m consensus (-1.2%); GAAP diluted EPS $1.76 (+4.1%) vs a $1.84–1.91 consensus range (MarketBeat $1.86; Zacks $1.91; S&P $1.84); adj. EBITDA $56.0m (+2.1%) was flagged by secondary sources as marginally below estimates. Shares gapped ~7% lower at the open and closed ~2.6% down.
  • Sharp deceleration versus H1. Implied H1-26 net revenue was +6.4% and adj. EBITDA +10.3%; Q3 slowed to +2.4%/+2.1%. Hotel went from +13.2% (H1) to +0.9%, casino from +5.8% to +3.4%, F&B from +4.3% to +1.3% — the Atlantis room-count/convention tailwind of H1 has been lapped and 2025's +3.6% Q3 comp was itself a record.
  • No formal guidance, so the 'bridge' is consensus-to-actual. Monarch does not guide. The ~$1.7m revenue shortfall sits in near-flat hotel and F&B; on EPS the effective tax rate (24.0% vs 18.5%) cost ~$2.3m / ~$0.13 — at the prior-year ETR EPS would have been ~$1.89, i.e. above consensus. There is no divisional attribution because the company reports a single segment.
  • Margin held near record. Adj. EBITDA margin 38.3% (-11bps YoY, +115bps QoQ); casino opex ratio flat at 35.8% of casino revenue; F&B 69.6% (-33bps, higher revenue per cover); hotel 31.3% (-10bps, higher ADR, lower cost per occupied room). SG&A was the only line to de-lever: +5.4% to $29.0m, 19.8% of revenue (+56bps) on legal and employee costs.
  • EPS quality: a D&A tailwind and a tax headwind. D&A fell 23% to $10.8m (-$3.2m), lifting GAAP operating income +8.1% to $41.3m (28.2% margin, +149bps) — a non-cash roll-off, not operating leverage. Pre-tax +8.7%, but net income only +1.4% as the ETR normalised; 9M ETR is flat at 21.3%, so the Q3 distortion is timing of option-exercise tax benefits.
  • Buybacks did the per-share work. Diluted shares -3.1% YoY to 18.15m turned +1.4% net income into +4.1% EPS. Q3 repurchases: 300,859 shares for $37.0m (~$123 average — above the ~$112 post-print price); 9M: ~482k shares for $54.9m (~$114 avg). 670,644 shares (~3.8% of 17.64m outstanding, ~$75m) remain under the 3.0m-share authorisation.
  • Dividend maintained. $0.30/share declared (record 1 Dec, payable 15 Dec 2026), part of the $1.20 annual programme (~1.1% yield); $5.3m paid in Q3, $16.0m in 9M.
  • Balance sheet: $137.4m net cash, revolver undrawn. Cash rose $40.9m YTD even after $70.9m of buybacks and dividends; 9M operating cash flow $130.0m (+2.7%), capex $19.6m (-35%), FCF (OCF less capex) $110.4m (+14.7%), ~$6.06 per diluted share. Capital returned = 64% of 9M FCF.
  • Capex is running at ~0.6x D&A — and includes a land purchase. Q3 capex $9.7m covered maintenance, new slot product at both properties and land with a building adjacent to Atlantis (balance-sheet land +$2.2m YTD to $36.9m). No project announced; it reads as a master-plan option on the Reno site. Annualised D&A ~$43m vs ~$26m annualised capex flatters FCF and will need to normalise.
  • PCL litigation remains the overhang. A $74,627,657 judgment for PCL Construction (Denver District Court, Case 2019cv33368) over the Black Hawk build is under appeal at the Colorado Court of Appeals; PCL has post-judgment motions for 'further release'. Post-judgment interest of $1.1m was accrued in Q3 ($3.3m 9M, ~$4.5m/yr run-rate) and excluded from adj. EBITDA. Construction payables of $47.5m are already carried on the balance sheet.
  • Adj. EBITDA add-backs are growing. Non-D&A/tax/interest add-backs were $3.9m in Q3 vs $2.6m: stock-based comp $2.6m (+26%, 1.8% of revenue), PCL judgment interest $1.1m, litigation fees $0.2m. We treat the PCL interest as a real economic cost; 'clean' EBITDA growth is closer to +0.5%.
  • Cost base (9M, CODM view): labor is the only lever being pulled. Labor $120.6m (+2.5%; 28.4% of revenue, -70bps), cost of sales $34.2m (+3.2%), gaming tax & licence $63.2m (+5.1%; 14.8% of revenue), other opex $56.2m (+6.0%). Tax & licence and other opex are growing faster than revenue; the gaming-tax drift is consistent with mix shifting toward Colorado's graduated (up to 20%) regime from Nevada's 6.75%.
  • Segment disclosure is thin. Atlantis (Reno) and Monarch Black Hawk are two operating segments aggregated into one reportable segment under ASC 280; the CEO is the CODM and allocates on consolidated net income. No property-level revenue, EBITDA, handle, hold, occupancy or ADR is disclosed. The Q1-26 release claimed YoY market-share gains at both properties; the Q3 release is silent on share — a notable omission.
  • M&A rhetoric unchanged, action absent. Management 'continues to diligently evaluate potential M&A transactions' — the same language for several quarters. With $137m cash, an undrawn facility and ~$210m annualised EBITDA, there is capacity for a meaningful acquisition; insiders (~26%) have not used it. Capital allocation remains the central debate.
  • Explicitly anti-iGaming. $181k of 9M lobbying expense to oppose iGaming expansion (added back) confirms Monarch is a land-based defender; Colorado/Nevada online casino legislation is a structural thesis risk the company is actively fighting rather than hedging.
  • Related-party footprint persists. Parking-lot and driveway leases with Biggest Little Investments (Farahi-family partnership) adjacent to Atlantis; weighted remaining lease term 15.3 years, $1.2m cash lease payments in 9M. Governance point rather than a financial one.
  • Valuation framing (estimates). At ~$112: market cap ~$2.0bn, EV ~$1.84bn, ~8.8x annualised 9M adj. EBITDA, ~7% FCF yield on annualised 9M FCF (a capex-light year). The multiple already discounts low-single-digit growth; re-rating requires a capital-deployment catalyst or PCL resolution.
  • Thesis read-through. Q3 confirms a mature, debt-free, ~38%-margin two-asset operator whose organic growth has converged to GDP-like rates. The bull case rests on cash conversion plus buybacks (EPS +21% 9M) and optionality on M&A/Atlantis land; the bear case is that the record-margin era is fully priced, growth has rolled over, and a ~$75m legal cash call sits unresolved.

3Segment deep-dive: Consolidated casino resorts — revenue streams (Casino / F&B / Hotel / Other)

Casino carried the quarter (+3.4%) while hotel and F&B growth stalled as the Atlantis room-count comp normalised; departmental cost ratios were stable to slightly better.

Net revenue and mix

Net revenue rose 2.4% to $146.3m with all four lines growing slowly, a clear step-down from implied H1 growth of +6.4%.

  • Net revenue +2.4% year-on-year and +2.5% on the derived Q2-26 base of $142.6m.
  • Casino +3.4% to $82.8m (56.6% of mix, +53bps); F&B +1.3% to $34.3m; hotel +0.9% to $22.7m; other +2.0% to $6.5m.
  • Implied H1: net revenue +6.4%, casino +5.8%, F&B +4.3%, hotel +13.2%; hotel explains most of the change as Atlantis room additions and group business were lapped.
  • Q3-25 comparison was itself a record (+3.6%).
  • No slot handle, table drop, hold, occupancy or ADR figures; only that ADR rose and revenue per cover improved, so casino win-rate versus volume is unknown.

Casino department

Casino departmental profit was $53.2m (+3.3%) at a 64.2% margin on an unchanged 35.8% cost ratio; no market-share claim was made this quarter.

  • Nine-month casino revenue +4.9% to $244.1m with cost ratio 62bps better at 35.8%.
  • Gaming tax and licence +5.1% in 9M against revenue +4.9%, consistent with growth skewing to Black Hawk (Colorado tax tops out at 20% versus Nevada's 6.75%) — analyst inference.
  • Q3 capex included new gaming equipment at both properties, the only product-refresh datapoint.
  • Q1-26 release claimed share gains at both Atlantis and Monarch Black Hawk; the Q3 release makes no market-share claim, which we read as share at best flat.
  • Colorado Division of Gaming Black Hawk AGP and Nevada GCB Washoe County data are the checks; not accessed for this note.

F&B and hotel

F&B profit rose 2.4% to $10.4m and hotel profit 1.0% to $15.6m, but the hotel growth phase from room additions looks over for now.

  • F&B +1.3% to $34.3m, cost ratio improved 33bps to 69.6% on higher revenue per cover; 30.4% margin. 9M F&B +3.2% with cost ratio 26bps worse at 71.7%.
  • Hotel $22.7m (+0.9%), cost ratio 31.3% (-10bps) on higher ADR and lower cost per occupied room; 68.7% margin.
  • 9M hotel +8.4% to $63.2m, cost ratio 119bps better at 33.0%, departmental profit +10.4%.
  • 1,333 rooms (817 Atlantis, 516 Black Hawk); without occupancy or RevPAR disclosure we cannot tell if +0.9% was rate-led with softer occupancy or a tough comp.

SG&A, EBITDA and EPS

Adj. EBITDA of $56.0m held a 38.3% margin, and EPS of $1.76 (+4.1%) would have been ~$1.89 at last year's tax rate — the key point on the 'miss'.

  • SG&A +5.4% to $29.0m (19.8% of revenue, +56bps) on legal and employee costs; ~$0.5m of the $1.5m rise is stock-based compensation ($2.6m, +26%), which is added back.
  • PCL-related legal costs sit in 'other operating items', so the SG&A legal increase is incremental to the litigation line.
  • Adj. EBITDA margin 38.3%, 11bps below last year's Q3 record and 115bps above Q2.
  • D&A fell $3.2m (-23%) to $10.8m, lifting operating income 8.1% to $41.3m (28.2% margin, +149bps).
  • ETR jumped to 24.0% from 18.5% (prior-year option-exercise benefits); net income +1.4% to $32.0m; 3.1% lower share count gave EPS $1.76. At prior ETR, net income ~$34.3m, EPS ~$1.89, above consensus.

Q4 outlook

No segment guidance; on seasonality we pencil Q4-26E net revenue ~$133–138m and adj. EBITDA ~$47–50m (35–36% margin).

  • Adj. EBITDA margin ran 35.9% in Q1-26, 37.2% in Q2 and 38.3% in Q3, so Q4 should step down on revenue and margin.
  • Winter weather in Black Hawk is the usual swing factor.
  • These are our estimates, not company guidance; the Q4-25 base is not disclosed in documents reviewed.

4Segment deep-dive: Cost base, cash flow & capital allocation (single reportable segment — CODM expense view)

Labor leverage and a $3.2m D&A step-down did the heavy lifting; 9M FCF of $110m (+15%) funded $71m of capital returns while cash still rose $41m — but add-backs are growing and the PCL judgment is unresolved.

Cost base

Labor was the only major line leveraged (28.4% of revenue, -70bps); other opex and gaming taxes grew faster than revenue, so margins depend on wage discipline.

  • 9M labor +2.5% to $120.6m; cost of sales +3.2% to $34.2m (8.0%, -15bps); gaming tax and licence +5.1% to $63.2m (14.8%, flat); other opex +6.0% to $56.2m (13.2%, +12bps).
  • Total expenses including tax and interest +2.0% to $333.3m against revenue +5.0%; 9M net margin 21.7% from 19.4%.
  • No formal cost-transformation programme with a dollar target or target year; prior efficiency narrative was marketing efficiency and technology.
  • Two properties in tight labor markets make wage discipline the whole margin story.
“maintaining operational efficiency”Management

EBITDA add-backs

Q3 add-backs grew to $3.9m from $2.6m; treating PCL interest as recurring, 'clean' EBITDA grew closer to +0.5% than reported +2.1%.

  • Add-backs: stock-based compensation $2.6m (+26%, 1.8% of revenue vs 1.5%), $1.1m accrued PCL post-judgment interest, $0.2m construction-litigation fees, trivial iGaming lobbying.
  • PCL interest is ~$4.5m annualised while the appeal is pending.
  • D&A decline (-$3.2m Q3, -$8.9m 9M) reflects expansion-era furniture and equipment reaching end of useful lives (analyst inference); a GAAP tailwind for several quarters.
  • 9M capex of $19.6m runs at ~0.6x D&A of $31.9m, flattering free cash flow until the refresh cycle returns.

Cash flow and balance sheet

9M FCF of $110.4m (+14.7%, ~$6.06 per diluted share) funded $71m of capital returns while cash still rose $40.9m to $137.4m.

  • 9M operating cash flow $130.0m, +2.7% against adj. EBITDA +7.3%; working capital absorbed cash (accrued expenses -$4.8m, receivables -$1.9m, inventories -$1.1m, prepaids -$1.3m) and cash taxes rose.
  • Capex $19.6m vs $30.4m.
  • Financing: $54.9m buybacks, $16.0m dividends, $7.4m payroll taxes on net option exercises, offset by $11.3m option proceeds.
  • Lease liabilities $12.6m (BLI partnership parking-lot and driveway leases, 15.3-year term, 4.34% incremental borrowing rate).
  • Total assets $736.1m, equity $569.5m; no debt, credit facility undrawn.

Capital returns and valuation

Q3 buyback of 300,859 shares for $37.0m averaged ~$123, above the ~$112 post-print price; 670,644 shares (~3.8%) remain authorised.

  • 9M average ~$114 on ~482k shares; shares outstanding -1.0% YTD to 17.64m as the gross 2.7% buyback was partly offset by option exercises.
  • Remaining authorisation ~$75m at current prices, roughly two quarters at the Q3 pace; $0.30 quarterly dividend maintained.
  • At ~$112, market cap ~$2.0bn, EV ~$1.84bn: ~8.8x annualised 9M adj. EBITDA and ~7% FCF yield (our estimates, flattered by light capex).
  • With debt capacity in the high hundreds of millions, the absence of a deal is a choice rather than a constraint.

5Segment deep-dive: Property view — Atlantis Casino Resort Spa (Reno, NV) and Monarch Casino Resort Spa Black Hawk (CO)

Two assets, one disclosure line: no property P&L, no market-share claim this quarter, and the live items are the Black Hawk litigation and the Atlantis land purchase.

Segment disclosure and footprint

Two operating segments are aggregated into one reportable segment, with no property P&L; Q3 revenue of $146.3m spans ~2,300 slots and ~67 tables.

  • Aggregated under ASC 280-10-50-11; CEO as CODM allocates on consolidated net income; $25.1m goodwill all at Black Hawk, no interim impairment indicators.
  • Atlantis: 817 rooms, ~61,000 sq ft casino, ~1,200 slots and video poker, ~30 tables, race and sportsbook, keno, poker, eight food outlets, 30,000 sq ft spa, ~52,000 sq ft convention space.
  • Black Hawk: 516 rooms, ~60,000 sq ft casino, ~1,100 slots, 37 tables, poker, keno, sportsbook, four restaurants, rooftop spa and pool, ~1,500 parking spaces.
  • Per-unit win is not disclosed.

Market share

The Q3 release drops the Q1-26 claim of share gains at both properties; we suspect the Atlantis room tailwind explains most of the deceleration.

  • Black Hawk has taken share at the upper end of the Denver regional market on luxury positioning; Reno described by management as 'extremely competitive'.
  • Hotel growth collapsed from +13.2% in H1 to +0.9%; attribution to Atlantis rather than Black Hawk is inference without property disclosure.
  • Colorado Black Hawk adjusted gross proceeds and Nevada Washoe County win are external checks; not accessed, share recorded as n/a.

Atlantis land purchase

Q3 capex included adjacent land with a building at Atlantis, a pattern resembling how the Black Hawk expansion began; investors should ask for a capital plan.

  • Balance-sheet land +$2.2m YTD to $36.9m; construction in progress +$3.2m to $14.7m; plus new gaming equipment.
  • Monarch already owns separate adjacent parcels and leases parking-lot and driveway land from Biggest Little Investments, the Farahi-family partnership owning the adjacent shopping centre.
  • Nothing announced; assembling contiguous land while running capex at ~0.6x D&A suggests a Reno master plan as the obvious use of the $137m cash pile.
  • Ask for a capital plan before the next 10-K.

Black Hawk overhangs

The PCL judgment of $74,627,657 under appeal is the overhang, with $1.1m Q3 interest accrued ($3.3m 9M) excluded from adj. EBITDA.

  • PCL has filed post-judgment motions for further release of funds.
  • Colorado's graduated gaming tax reaches 20% against Nevada's 6.75% top rate, consistent with group tax and licence growing faster than revenue.
  • $181k of 9M lobbying spend to oppose iGaming expansion shows defence of the land-based franchise rather than a digital option.

Guidance and disclosure gaps

No property-level guidance; property EBITDA, slot handle and hold, occupancy, ADR and quarterly share are not provided.

  • Management commits to 'technology and other strategic capital investments that enhance both properties'.
  • The Q3 earnings call transcript was not accessible, so any property colour given on the call should be checked against the replay.
