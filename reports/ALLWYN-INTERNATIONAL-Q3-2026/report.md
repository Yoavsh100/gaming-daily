## 1. Executive Summary

- **Headline: revenue +19%, adj. EBITDA +6%, margin -410bps.** Q3-26 net revenue €1,220m (~$1.34bn) vs €1,023m, but only +4% on the company's comparable basis (PrizePicks in both periods, LottoItalia amortisation adjusted); adj. EBITDA €396m (~$436m) vs €374m, +2% comparable; margin 32.5% vs 36.6%. Investing.com cites revenue consensus of ~€1.23bn, so the top line was in line; management did not publish a quarterly EBITDA consensus, only a FY range.
- **Operating EBITDA +13% to €341m (~$375m) as add-backs fall away.** EBITDA adjustments dropped to €55m (~$61m) from €73m (Q1-26 peak ~€107m): UK transition costs now nil, PrizePicks transaction/retention €9m, IWG acquisition accounting €3m — but the 'Allwyn brand initiative' is now €32m/quarter and the single largest add-back, which looks recurring in all but name.
- **Guidance: revenue reaffirmed, margin target retired.** FY26 net revenue growth of 'mid-to-high 20%s' before c.€60m (~$66m) of Continental Europe one-offs is unchanged. However, the '~37% adj. EBITDA margin' language in the Q1 and Q2 releases has been replaced by 'within the range of consensus expectations' (company-compiled €1,769–1,891m / ~$1.95–2.08bn as of 13 Nov). At the midpoint (€1,830m) on €5.14bn revenue (+25%) that is ~35.6% — a ~140bps walk-down. Hitting 37% would need Q4 adj. EBITDA of ~€605m (+22% YoY), above the top of the consensus range.
- **North America is the division that moved the margin.** PrizePicks net revenue +29% cFX (+24% reported) to €183m (~$201m), NA adj. EBITDA +26% pro forma to €43m (~$47m), but at a 17.8% margin; consolidating an 18%-margin DFS business against a 44%-margin European lottery base is mechanically dilutive. Add CE -270bps (LottoItalia) and corporate & eliminations at -€28m vs -€16m (+75%; corporate costs +€6m, rest eliminations) and the 410bps is fully explained.
- **Comparable growth of 4% sits below the 'high single-digit' medium-term framing** that Barclays challenged on the call; the CFO declined to re-steer, citing record Q3-25 jackpots (Tzoker in Greece/Cyprus at an all-time high in Aug-25, EuroMillions in Austria/UK) and 'exceptionally customer-friendly' sports outcomes in August–September. Strong July margins (World Cup latter stages) were more than offset. Customer-friendly results continued into October and are 'factored in' to the outlook.
- **Product mix: lottery down, iGaming and DFS up.** Lottery €513m (~$564m) -7%; sports betting €130m (~$143m) +1% (stakes up, win margin down); iGaming €151m (~$166m) +26%; DFS incl. prediction markets €183m (~$201m); VLTs & casinos €142m (~$156m) +2%; non-gaming €101m (~$111m) +20%. NGR €1,119m (~$1.23bn) +19%. Online NGR €530m (~$583m) +55%; online now 47% of NGR (37% LY, 45% comparable) and 54% of online-eligible NGR.
- **Continental Europe (62% of net revenue): +3% against a +10% GGR comp.** Net revenue €754m (~$829m); Austria +10%, Czech Republic +4%, Greece & Cyprus broadly flat. Adj. EBITDA €328m (~$361m) -3%, flat excluding the €9m LottoItalia amortisation step-up; margin 43.5% (-270bps). Online NGR +8% to €226m (~$249m), 32% of segment NGR. Eurojackpot online (Greece, Aug) reached one-in-three online players in month one.

EBITDA doubled to €18m (~$20m) via the licence cost-recovery mechanism (live since Q2), CAPEX down to €10m from €38m, transformation adjustments now nil. Powerball (July) has 1.3m+ players; new leadership team being installed. CFO: 'not going to say that we're happy with the performance'.
- **Betano (36.75% equity-accounted): growth intact, Brazil shut.** Total revenue €795m (~$875m) +17% cFX / +21% reported; Allwyn's share of net income €35m (~$39m) +3% — profit growth lagged revenue on customer-friendly sports results. Brazil, Betano's largest market, closed on 25 Sep 2026 under a provisional measure; four new country launches planned for early 2027 (Spain confirmed; CEO of Allwyn Digital said five). Betano paid €150m (~$165m) post-quarter (Allwyn ~€55m / ~$61m), taking 2026 dividends to Allwyn to ~€258m (~$284m), +40% vs €184m; CFO expects 'material' dividends in 2027 'in any scenario'.

Adj. EBITDA less CAPEX +14% to €345m (~$380m) as CAPEX fell 29% to €51m (4.2% of net revenue vs 7%). IFRS net cash from operations €88m vs €213m.

- **Technology and content in-sourcing accelerates.** Post-quarter: perpetual global licence to Kambi's sportsbook source code for €50m (~$55m) over three years to 2029 (~half in 2027, capitalised) plus 'low tens of millions' of incremental annual opex from 2027; sportsbook is 13% of NGR and management flagged US sportsbook optionality. Remaining 30% of IWG acquired for USD 122m (€107m), payable Q1-27. Next Lotto (Germany) to 64.53% in October, consolidated from Q4. Australian casinos sold for €63m (~$69m), €3m gain. Brussels casino concession lost from July 2027 (<1% of CE adj. EBITDA).
- **Below the line is noisy and flattered.** Adj. profit attributable €141m (~$155m) +5%; adj. EPS €0.18 (~$0.20) vs OPAP's historical €0.36 — not comparable (765.7m vs 358.6m weighted shares). Reported EPS €0.03 (~$0.03); IFRS profit €37m after €82m of PPA amortisation (D&A €158m reported vs €76m adjusted) — CFO says assume the Q3 run-rate until the PPA is finalised. Adj. tax rate 20% vs 35% on a deferred-tax benefit lifted adj. PAT +25%; NCI €45m vs €15m (PrizePicks minorities).
- **Licence calendar is the next structural watch-point.** Austria's exclusive lottery licence extended from Sep-27 to end-2028; tender expected 2027, €40m (~$44m) fee on award for a 15-year term, bid coordinated with 30%+ partner ÖBAG. Greece legacy-games licence due 2030 ('110% attention'; Allwyn is Athens-listed).
- **Governance and reporting.** Allwyn AG (Euronext Athens: ALWN) was formed on 24 Mar 2026 from the OPAP/Allwyn International combination; KKCG holds 79.28%, free float 20.72%. Petra Ehmann proposed as INED to lift board independence to 50%. All Q3 figures are preliminary, unaudited and on a 'look-through' non-IFRS basis; IFRS statements follow 26 Nov 2026.
- **Share price: a relief bounce off the low.** ALWN +0.7% intraday to €9.63 (~$10.6) vs €9.56 prior close (range €9.52–9.71), one day after a 52-week low of €9.43; -50% from the €19.03 January high and -21.5% over the past month following the Brazil ban.
- **Thesis read-through.** Diversification is delivering top-line resilience, but the earnings algorithm is migrating to a lower-margin, more acquisitive model (DFS, in-sourced tech, minority buy-ins) while the two cash engines that fund the €1/share floor — Betano and the UK — face regulatory and execution risk respectively. FY26 margin is heading to ~35.5%, leverage is stuck around 3.5x, and the equity is pricing the Brazil downside; the Q4 lottery seasonality and PrizePicks' NFL/NBA quarter are the near-term support.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — Continental Europe (Austria, Czech Republic, Greece & Cyprus, Italy, Germany, Slovakia)

*+3% net revenue against a +10% GGR comp; EBITDA flat ex-LottoItalia; iGaming +26% and online +8% carry a -8% lottery line.*

### Revenue and GGR

**Net revenue grew 3% to €754m (~$829m) against a +10% GGR comp; Continental Europe remains the earnings engine at 62% of group revenue.**

- Adj. EBITDA €328m (~$361m) is 83% of group; net revenue +3% reported and constant currency.
- Comp included segment GGR +10% and a record Tzoker jackpot in Greece/Cyprus in August 2025.
- Austria led with +10%, Czech Republic +4%, Greece & Cyprus broadly stable.
- GGR €1,161m (~$1.28bn), +1%; gaming taxes and good-cause contributions €462m (~$508m) absorbed 40% of GGR (+2%).
- Non-gaming revenue +38% to €55m (~$61m); NGR only +1% at €699m (~$769m). Sequentially +3.1% versus Q2's €731m.

### Product mix

**iGaming +26% to €151m (~$166m) carried a -8% lottery line; sports betting +1% year-on-year but -10% quarter-on-quarter on weak margins.**

- iGaming +2.7% quarter-on-quarter; sports betting net revenue €130m (~$143m).
- Stakes higher but win margins materially lower: strong July World Cup final margins offset by customer-friendly August and September; Q3-25 comp also weak.
- Lottery net revenue -8% to €276m (~$304m) on Tzoker jackpot cycle; VLTs & casinos +2% at €142m (~$156m).
- CFO: Czech Republic and Austria run roughly €450m iGaming against ~€30m sports revenue versus a 'good proposition' norm of 50/50.
- This is the real strategic rationale for the Kambi deal and implies sportsbook is a long way from scale in these markets.

### Digital and innovation

**Online NGR rose 8% to €226m (~$249m), 32% of segment NGR versus 30% last year and 41% of online-eligible NGR.**

- Eurojackpot online launched in Greece in August, reaching one in three online players in its first month.
- Greece's first Eurojackpot jackpot winner (€38m) drawn this week, which should help Q4 awareness.
- Smaller innovations continue: Keno upgrade in Czech Republic, gold-prize scratchcard in Austria.
- Next Lotto (German online lottery reseller) moved to 64.53% controlling stake in October for ~€10m (~$11m) after €3m contribution lifted interest to 46.29%; consolidates from Q4.

### Profitability and costs

**Adj. EBITDA fell 3% to €328m, 43.5% margin versus 46.2%; entire decline is the €11m fall in equity-method profit, mainly LottoItalia amortisation.**

- Margin -270bps year-on-year but +340bps quarter-on-quarter on seasonality and Q2 tax drag.
- Share of profit of equity-method investees €8m vs €19m; €9m is higher LottoItalia (32.5% owned) licence-fee amortisation under licence renewed 30 November 2025, ~€36m annual drag.
- CFO stressed LottoItalia will keep distributing ~100% of free cash flow, about €100m a year net to Allwyn; ex-LottoItalia aggregate EBITDA was flat.
- Marketing +21% to €80m (~$88m), 10.6% of net revenue versus 9.1%; agents' commissions and materials +3%, personnel/other -2%.
- No country-level EBITDA breakout; Greece & Cyprus most hit by margins. CAPEX €15m (~$17m), -17%; adj. EBITDA less CAPEX €313m (~$344m), -2%.

### Portfolio and regulation

**Australian casinos sold for AUD 103m with €3m gain; Austrian lottery licence extended to end-2028 with tender expected in 2027.**

- Australian casino sale in August for AUD 103m (€63m / ~$69m); Brussels casino concession awarded to another operator from July 2027 (less than 1% of segment EBITDA in 2025).
- Austrian tender carries ~€40m (~$44m) fee on award for 15-year term, bid alongside 30%+ partner ÖBAG; Greece's legacy-games licence falls due in 2030.
- FY26 carries c.€60m (~$66m) of previously flagged one-off revenue impacts (c.-2%).
- Our read: mid-single-digit NGR grower with lottery cycling tough jackpot comps into seasonally strong Q4.
- Margin ambition should be reset to the low-40s rather than mid-40s given LottoItalia amortisation and rising marketing intensity.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — North America (PrizePicks, Allwyn LS Group/Illinois, IWG) — presented on 100% basis

*PrizePicks re-accelerated to +29% cFX with EBITDA +26% at a 17.8% margin; Packs adds a non-sports vertical; Q3 is the seasonal trough ahead of the NFL/NBA quarter.*

### Revenue re-acceleration

**Net revenue rose 21% cFX to €242m (~$266m), with PrizePicks +29% cFX to €183m (~$201m), a sharp re-acceleration from Q2's +3%.**

- Reported growth 20% segment, 24% PrizePicks; Q2 had been depressed by sports outcomes.
- Customer activity (DFS entry fees plus real cash in prediction-market contracts, not notional volume) grew more than 30% year-on-year; no material sports-outcome effect.
- World Cup boost in July offset by later NFL start.
- Segment GGR €190m (~$209m), +30% cFX; non-gaming revenue €59m (~$65m), +7%. Illinois fee and allowance are pass-throughs depressing margin ratio, not absolute EBITDA.
- Sequentially net revenue fell 18% from Q2's €294m on the DFS calendar.

### EBITDA and costs

**Adj. EBITDA rose 26% to €43m (~$47m) at a 17.8% margin, driven by operating leverage on marketing; Q3 is structurally the DFS low season.**

- Margin +100bps year-on-year but -1,760bps versus Q2's €104m / 35.4% on seasonality; pro forma prior year €34m.
- Marketing services +14% to €75m (~$83m), 31% of net revenue versus 33%; materials/consumables +12% to €74m; personnel/other -2% at €65m.
- Operating EBITDA €30m versus €5m as adjustments halved to €13m (PrizePicks transaction/retention €9m, IWG put-option €3m, litigation €1m) from €29m.
- CFO conceded a 'significant part' of Q1–Q3 non-marketing cost dynamics were acquisition-related one-offs.
- CAPEX €13m (~$14m), +44%; adj. EBITDA less CAPEX €30m (~$33m), +20%. Nine-month: €775m (~$853m) revenue, €222m (~$244m) adj. EBITDA, 28.6% margin.

> “differentiated customer acquisition and retention model” — Management

### Products and trading

**DFS remains the biggest line; Packs collectible cards launched in October add a year-round non-sports vertical, with first contribution in Q4.**

## 5. Segment deep-dive — United Kingdom (Allwyn UK — The National Lottery)

## 7. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- PrizePicks re-accelerated: net revenue +29% cFX to €183m (~$201m) from +3% in Q2, customer activity +30%+, NA adj. EBITDA +26% pro forma with marketing only +14% (31% of revenue); October set a record for activity and November MAU is up double digits — the H1 'PrizePicks has stalled' worry is answered. _(structural)_
- Operating EBITDA +13% to €341m (~$375m) as EBITDA add-backs halved from the Q1 peak to €55m, UK transition costs went to nil and CAPEX normalised to 4.2% of revenue; adj. EBITDA less CAPEX +14% to €345m (~$380m), +11% comparable. _(mixed)_
- Digital mix shift continues: online NGR +55% to €530m (~$583m), 47% of NGR (54% of online-eligible); CE online +8%; iGaming +26%; Eurojackpot online reached one-in-three Greek online players in month one. _(structural)_
- Capital returns delivered and defended: €1.19/share (~$1.31) in 2026 (€1.00 distributions + €150m buyback), and the €1.00 minimum explicitly reaffirmed for 2027 even in a Brazil-shut scenario, backed by Betano dividends +40% to ~€258m (~$284m) and ~€100m/yr from LottoItalia. _(structural)_
- UK economics inflected: adj. EBITDA doubled to €18m (~$20m), adj. EBITDA less CAPEX +€37m YoY to +€8m as the ~£450m transformation ended and cost recovery kicked in; Powerball already has 1.3m+ players. _(early)_
- Kambi perpetual source-code licence for €50m (~$55m) is a cheap route to owning the sportsbook stack behind 13% of NGR, with cross-sell into a €450m Czech/Austrian iGaming base and a US sportsbook option; IWG 100% and Next Lotto control tidy the structure. _(early)_
- Liquidity is not the issue: €1.94bn (~$2.1bn) cash plus €215m Austrian money-market assets, €936m (~$1.0bn) undrawn, no material maturity before 2029, and the 2032 TLB repriced 50bps tighter in July. _(structural)_

**Lowlights**

- The '~37% adj. EBITDA margin' FY26 target in the Q1 and Q2 releases has been replaced by 'within consensus' (€1,769–1,891m); the midpoint implies ~35.6% on mid-20s revenue growth, and 9M margin is 35.3%. This is a guidance cut in all but name and was not called out as such. _(structural)_
- Comparable net revenue growth of 4% and comparable adj. EBITDA of +2% are below the high-single-digit medium-term framing; lottery -7%, sports betting +1% on weak win margins that persisted into October, and management would not re-steer 2027. _(mixed)_
- UK demand is still deteriorating: GGR -11% cFX, online NGR -9%, 'players continuing to adjust' to the new platform nine months after launch; the CFO is 'not happy' and gave no recovery timeline — the EBITDA doubling is a cost-recovery mechanic, not trading. _(structural)_
- Brazil: Betano's largest market closed from 25 Sep with no visibility on reopening; Betano's share of profit (+3%) already lags revenue (+17%), Chile is a further regulatory risk, and 2027 Betano dividends are the swing factor for leverage and the €1/share floor. _(watch)_
- Free cash flow -61% to €84m (~$92m) on a smaller working-capital inflow and €138m of net finance costs paid; net debt €6.64bn (~$7.3bn), 3.5x and 'above target', with €107m IWG, ~€25m Kambi and €40m Austria outflows still to come and ~€36m/yr of LottoItalia amortisation now structural. _(structural)_
- Corporate & eliminations swelled 75% to -€28m and the 'Allwyn brand initiative' add-back is running at €32m a quarter (€130m annualised) — a recurring consumer-brand investment being excluded from adjusted EBITDA. _(governance)_
- Earnings quality: adj. EPS €0.18 (~$0.20) is shown against OPAP's €0.36 on a share count that more than doubled; a deferred-tax benefit cut the adjusted tax rate to 20% from 35% and flattered adj. PAT (+25%); reported EPS is €0.03 after €82m of PPA amortisation on a still-unfinished purchase-price allocation. _(governance)_

## 8. Forecast & Outlook

Revenue guidance is unchanged — FY26 consolidated net revenue growth of 'mid-to-high 20%s' on the FY25 look-through base of €4,112m (~$4.5bn), before c.€60m (~$66m) of one-off impacts in Continental Europe — which implies €5.14–5.30bn and a Q4 of €1.47–1.63bn, i.e. +31–46% on Q4-25's €1,119m with PrizePicks' strongest quarter (NFL/NBA plus the first Packs contribution) and Next Lotto consolidation doing the heavy lifting. The EBITDA framing has changed: Q1 and Q2 said 'adj. EBITDA margin of ~37%'; Q3 says 'within the range of consensus expectations' of €1,769–1,891m (~$1.95–2.08bn, company-compiled 13 Nov). With 9M adj. EBITDA at €1,297m (~$1.43bn), that range implies Q4 of €472–594m against €497m last year, and a FY margin of 34.4–36.8% — the midpoint (€1,830m, ~35.6%) is ~140bps below the old target. The division that moved it is North America (PrizePicks consolidated at an 18–35% seasonal margin against a 44% CE base), compounded by LottoItalia amortisation (€9m/quarter in CE), corporate costs/eliminations (+€12m YoY) and October's customer-friendly sports results, with Betano's Brazil closure feeding the share-of-profit line from Q4.

- **FY26 net revenue growth (before c.€60m CE one-offs):** Mid-to-high 20%s (unchanged) → €5.14–5.30bn (~$5.7–5.8bn) (Reaffirmed; our estimate ~€5.15bn (+25%), lower half)
- **FY26 adj. EBITDA margin → absolute range:** '~37%' (Q1/Q2) → 'within consensus' €1,769–1,891m (~$1.95–2.08bn) (De facto cut: midpoint ≈ 35.6%; 37% would need Q4 ≈ €605m (+22% YoY))
- **Implied Q4-26 adj. EBITDA:** €472–594m vs Q4-25 €497m; our estimate ~€530m (+7%) (Driven by PrizePicks Q4 seasonality, Packs, Q4 lottery strength vs October sports margins and Brazil)
- **Net debt / EBITDA:** 3.5x at Q3 (€6,641m / €1,876m pro forma LTM) (Flat-to-slightly-down by YE26E; €107m IWG + ~€25m Kambi + €40m Austria in 2027 slow deleveraging)

Our FY26 estimate is net revenue of ~€5.15bn (~$5.7bn), +25%, i.e. the lower half of guidance, built on Q4 of ~€1.48bn: CE +2–3% with Q4 lottery seasonality but a still-tough Tzoker/EuroMillions comp, UK -4–5% as online stabilises on Powerball and Christmas scratchcards, and a PrizePicks quarter in the €330–380m range with Packs incremental. We model FY26 adj. EBITDA of ~€1.83bn (~$2.0bn), a **~35.5% margin, roughly the consensus midpoint and 150bps below the retired 37% target**, which requires Q4 adj. EBITDA of ~€530m (+7% YoY) — PrizePicks' seasonal margin recovery and ~€20m of UK improvement against ~€9m of LottoItalia amortisation, a ~€10m higher corporate run-rate, weaker October sports margins and a Betano share-of-profit line likely down year-on-year with Brazil dark. Q4 is also the first quarter with Next Lotto consolidated (small). Cash: Q4 should be the strongest working-capital quarter and includes ~€55m from Betano, against €35m of residual buyback (October) and €149m of interim distribution cash (November); we expect **year-end net debt/EBITDA of ~3.4–3.5x**, with 2027 deleveraging dependent on Betano's payout — in a Brazil-stays-shut case we would not assume leverage falls below ~3.2x by end-2027 after the €1.00/share (~€760m) floor, IWG, Kambi and Austria. For 2027, the arithmetic that matters is PrizePicks' growth (and the earn-out), the UK's online recovery, and whether 'material' Betano dividends mean €150m or €250m to Allwyn; medium-term comparable growth of 4% needs to re-accelerate to the high single digits for the margin debate to become secondary. All figures above are our estimates.

**What to watch next quarter:**

- Brazil: whether the 25 Sep provisional measure lapses, is converted to law or amended; Betano's Q4 revenue run-rate ex-Brazil and the size/timing of its first 2027 dividend (Allwyn guided 'material in any scenario').
- Q4 PrizePicks: first Packs revenue contribution, marketing spend around NFL/NBA (Q3 was 31% of revenue), MAU/activity growth and any state-level DFS/prediction-market enforcement; the implied earn-out trajectory on the reported $1.6bn + up to $1bn consideration.
- UK: Q4 National Lottery GGR and online NGR trend (Q3: -11% cFX / -9%) with a full Powerball quarter and £10 scratchcard pilot; confirmation of the permanent UK CEO and any revised sales/good-causes plan.
- FY26 landing versus the €1,769–1,891m consensus range and the FY27 margin framework — will management reinstate a margin target, and what is the run-rate of the €32m/quarter brand add-back and corporate costs?
- Capital allocation sequencing: €1.00/share 2027 distribution timetable, any further buyback, IWG $122m (Q1-27), Kambi ~€25m (2027) and the Austrian licence tender (€40m fee on award; 15-year term).
- 26 Nov IFRS statements: finalised purchase-price allocation and PPA amortisation run-rate (€82m in Q3), NCI line, and reported leverage/covenant headroom disclosure.
- Continental Europe sports margins in Q4 (October already customer-friendly), Tzoker jackpot cycle into year-end, and Eurojackpot online traction in Greece after the first €38m winner.
