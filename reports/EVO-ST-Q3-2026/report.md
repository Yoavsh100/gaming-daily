# Evolution AB (EVO.ST) — Q3 2026 Analyst Report
**Nasdaq Stockholm · Reporting currency: EUR (tables in millions; ~USD at EUR/USD 1.12 as of 23 Oct 2026) · Results released 23 Oct 2026**
**Market reaction:** Shares opened ~1.6% lower at SEK 798.8 on the modest revenue/EPS miss, were +1.3% at SEK 822.20 by midday, and closed +3.6% at SEK 841.40 (prev. close SEK 812.00; ~623k shares traded) as the unchanged ~66% margin target and the cleared UKGC/Galaxy overhangs outweighed Brazil. Options had implied a ±5.6% move. (SEK/USD on the day not verified; USD equivalent n/a.)

> Evolution's Q3-26 delivered its first year-on-year revenue growth in five quarters (+3.7% to EUR 526.1m, ~$589m; est. +4.0% at constant currency) and held the adjusted EBITDA margin at 65.8%, but the quality is mixed: the ~30% of revenue in the Americas and Africa grew ~18% while the ~70% in Europe (-4.7%, share loss conceded) and Asia (+1.6%, cyber-crime unresolved) shrank ~1.5%. EUR 12.1m (~$13.6m) of one-offs pushed profit for the period down 3.6%, EPS growth is entirely buyback-driven, and Brazil's 5 October ban removes a growth engine from Q4. Stabilisation, not re-acceleration — with the FY26 ~66% margin target unchanged.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Growth is back, barely:** Q3 net revenue EUR 526.1m (~$589m), +3.7% YoY and +1.6% QoQ, est. +4.0% at constant currency — the first YoY increase after four consecutive declines (-2.4%, -3.7%, -1.5%, -1.2%). Marginal miss vs consensus (~EUR 529m, ~$592m; Investing.com), the fifth straight quarter at or below the Street on revenue.
- **Margin held — on an adjusted basis:** Adj. EBITDA EUR 345.9m (~$387m), +2.7% YoY, margin 65.8% (-60bps YoY, -10bps QoQ), ~1% above FactSet EBITDA consensus of ~EUR 343m. Reported EBITDA EUR 333.8m (~$374m), -0.9%, margin 63.5% (-290bps) after EUR 12.1m (~$13.6m) of one-offs excluded from the adjusted line.
- **The one-offs clear two overhangs:** EUR 5.6m (~$6.3m; GBP 4.75m) settlement with the UK Gambling Commission closing the licence review of Evolution Malta Holding opened in Dec 2024 (announced 15 Jul, fully paid), and EUR 6.5m (~$7.3m) of termination and legal costs after walking away from the $85m Galaxy Gaming acquisition in July (incl. a $5.2m break fee). Sell-side explicitly congratulated management on removing both.
- **Bottom line down, per-share up:** Operating profit EUR 290.3m (~$325m), -2.1% (margin 55.2%, -330bps); profit for the period EUR 243.3m (~$273m), -3.6%. Basic EPS EUR 1.31 (~$1.47) +4.6% (diluted EUR 1.29, ~$1.44, +2.4% vs consensus ~1.33) purely because average shares fell 7.8% to 186.0m from 201.8m.
- **Guidance unchanged — and it is only a margin target:** FY26 EBITDA margin 'in line with 2025' (~66%; FY25 adjusted was 66.1%), reiterated on the call despite the Brazil studio closure; 9M-26 stands at 65.7%, so Q4 needs ~66.5-67% to land on 66.0%. No revenue guidance; the qualitative ambition is to grow at least in line with the market in every region for both Live and RNG, with 110+ game releases in 2026.
- **RNG, not Live, drove the beat on growth:** RNG +10.1% to EUR 83.1m (~$93m), second consecutive double-digit quarter (Q2 +14.0%), contributing EUR 7.6m of the EUR 19.0m YoY revenue increase (40%) from <16% of the base. Live +2.6% to EUR 443.0m (~$496m) after -3.6% in Q2; 9M Live still -1.4% at EUR 1,315.2m (~$1,473m).
- **Geographic divergence by player IP:** North America +13.6% to a record EUR 84.3m (~$94m); Latin America +23.1% to a record EUR 49.0m (~$55m); Africa & other +23.7% to ~EUR 27.0m (~$30m), also a record. Europe -4.7% to EUR 173.7m (~$195m); Asia +1.6% to EUR 192.1m (~$215m). All five regions grew QoQ for the first time since 2024 (Europe +0.4%, Asia +0.8%, NA +4.1%, LatAm +3.2%, Africa ~+4.7%).

Remedy offered: 'a few stable quarters' of customer work — no pricing or product lever named.

Asked if cyber-crime (video piracy, unauthorised redistribution) is solved: 'No'; 'no quick solution'. Regulated markets 49% of revenue (EUR 258.5m, ~$290m).
- **Brazil: the post-period shock.** Provisional Measure 1.394/2026 suspended licensed online betting and casino; the São Paulo studio (opened 2025) closed on 5 Oct. Lula won the 25 Oct run-off (51.76%) having campaigned on the ban; resolution rests with the STF (constitutionality) and Congress. Brazil is 'well below half' of LatAm IP revenue → gross exposure <EUR 24.5m (~$27m)/quarter, <4.7% of group; market is not ring-fenced so some play may leak to offshore-licensed operators. CEO: 'flabbergasted', a 'total negative', studio can reopen 'on short notice'; Q4 'more eventful'. Staff retained for now, so cost persists without revenue.
- **Activity vs revenue divergence:** game rounds index 435, +22.5% YoY / +11.3% QoQ against +3.7% revenue growth. Management calls the index 'directional' and blames bet levels, market mix and regional traction; we read it as mix toward lower-value rounds, with Asia the likeliest source.

- **Product & AI:** Monopoly Roll'em and Disco Balls launched (Live), three branded titles in the quarter; Game Night before year-end; Ice Fishing (RNG) for North America in Q4; 2027 roadmap 'spectacular' with the largest, most complex games yet. On AI dealers: 'a possibility… it's like an RNG live game, it's a complement. I don't think that it will take over live.'
- **Litigation:** CEO confirmed 'we are in a full lawsuit with them and that will continue' and declined to comment on settlement speculation [counterparty not confirmed in the sources read].
- **Market reaction and sell-side:** shares opened ~1.6% lower (SEK 798.8) on the revenue/EPS miss, then reversed to close +3.6% at SEK 841.40 (prev. SEK 812.00). Citi trimmed forecasts (TP unchanged); JP Morgan cut its TP to SEK 645; Pareto raised to SEK 820 (hold); BNP Paribas raised to SEK 890.
- **Thesis read-through:** this is a stabilisation print, not a re-acceleration. Ex-Americas/Africa, revenue is still shrinking (~-1.5%); profit for the period is down while EPS is up only via the share count; the 66% margin is defended by other-opex restraint while headcount grows 8%. Q4 — with Brazil out, Asia unresolved and a ~67% margin needed to hit the target exactly — is the real test of whether Q3 was a turn or a pause.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — Live Casino

*Back to +2.6% growth after four quarters of decline, but it is an Americas/Africa story — Europe is ceding share, Asia is flat, and Brazil goes dark in Q4.*

### Return to growth

**Live returned to growth after four quarters of decline, net revenues up 2.6% to EUR 443.0m (~$496m), but the band has barely moved.**

- Up 1.3% sequentially, against a -3.6% print in Q2; nine-month run-rate still 1.4% lower at EUR 1,315.2m (~$1,473m).
- Live is 84.2% of group revenue, down 90bps year on year as RNG outgrows it.
- Five-quarter series EUR 431.7m, 438.6m, 434.9m, 437.3m, 443.0m (~$484m-$496m): oscillating in a EUR 430-445m band for over a year, only now edging above it.
- No segment EBITDA disclosed; group adjusted margin of 65.8% (-60bps YoY) is the only read-across and effectively the Live margin.

> “uneventful” — Management

### Americas and Africa

**Live's recovery is an Americas-and-Africa story, with North America, Latin America and Africa all at records.**

- North America +13.6% to record EUR 84.3m (~$94m), +4.1% QoQ, helped by second Michigan studio and Monopoly Live roll-out; New Jersey studio next in pipeline.
- Latin America +23.1% to record EUR 49.0m (~$55m), with Argentine studio acquired in Q1 for EUR 6.0m (~$6.7m) adding capacity.
- Africa & other +23.7% to roughly EUR 27.0m (~$30m); CEO cautioned the region is small and volatile.

> “hardcore focused business as usual” — CEO

### Europe

**Europe fell 4.7% to EUR 173.7m (~$195m), with the CEO effectively acknowledging market-share loss in the most mature, regulated region.**

- +0.4% QoQ after +3.5% QoQ in Q2.
- Management pointed to regulatory change and tax as external drags.
- Second Lithuanian studio opened in the quarter; native-language tables remain the tactical response.

> “it's not exciting and we should do better” — CEO

### Asia

**Asia, largest region by IP at EUR 192.1m (~$215m), grew just 1.6% YoY and 0.8% QoQ as cyber-crime remains unresolved.**

- Management explicit that the cyber-crime problem affecting video distribution and unauthorised product use is 'not solved'.

> “no quick solution” — Management

### Product and capacity

**Headcount grew 8.1% YoY to 23,408, more than double revenue growth, so Live unit economics rely on restraint elsewhere, not operating leverage.**

- Launched Monopoly Roll'em and Disco Balls among three new branded titles; 2026 releases to exceed 110 games; Game Night due before year-end.
- 2027 roadmap described as 'spectacular', containing some of the largest and most complex games ever built.
- Studio count rose to 27 with one opening in the quarter (third this year); headcount +2.3% QoQ; other opex ex one-offs +0.7% YoY.
- Game rounds index jumped to 435 (+22.5% YoY, +11.3% QoQ) while revenue grew 3.7%; management says correlation holds only 'over a very long time period'.
- We read the gap as mix shift toward lower-value rounds, likely from weakly monetised markets or unauthorised use.

### Brazil

**Provisional Measure 1.394/2026 suspended licensed online betting; São Paulo studio closed 5 October, capping exposure below EUR 24.5m (~$27m) quarterly.**

- Brazil is 'well below half' of Latin American IP revenue, below 4.7% of group.
- Market is not ring-fenced so some play may migrate to operators licensed elsewhere; CEO rejected Rothschild Redburn's suggestion closure could be net positive.
- CEO called it a 'populist' overthrow of an 18-month-old framework; studio can reopen 'on short notice' if STF or Congress overturns the measure.
- Staff supported rather than released, so Brazilian cost persists into Q4 without Brazilian revenue.

> “total negative” — CEO

### Outlook

**Structural growth rate has reset to low single digits; guidance to grow at least in line with the market in every region is not being met in Europe.**

- The ~30% of revenue in the Americas and Africa grew ~18%.
- The ~70% in Europe and Asia shrank roughly 1.5%.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — RNG

*Second consecutive double-digit quarter (+10.1%) — RNG supplied 40% of group revenue growth from <16% of the base; North American localisation is the strategy.*

### Growth engine

**RNG revenues rose 10.1% to EUR 83.1m (~$93m), the second consecutive double-digit quarter after years of flat-to-down.**

- Up 3.2% sequentially, following +14.0% in Q2, the first double-digit quarter in roughly three years.
- Nine-month RNG revenue up 10.7% to EUR 241.8m (~$271m); series EUR 75.5m, 75.7m, 78.2m, 80.5m, 83.1m shows steady ~3% sequential compounding.
- Share of group revenue 15.8%, up from 14.9% a year ago and 15.5% in Q2; 2026 YoY path roughly +8% (Q1), +14.0% (Q2), +10.1% (Q3).

### Contribution and strategy

**RNG added EUR 7.6m (~$8.5m) of the group's EUR 19.0m (~$21m) YoY increase, 40% of growth from under 16% of the base.**

- Without RNG the group would have grown ~2.3%.
- Management credits North America's outperformance partly to 'particularly strong RNG performance'; localised North American RNG content planned, Ice Fishing slated for Q4 launch.
- Monopoly-branded content under the Hasbro licence anchors the branded pipeline.
- No RNG-specific KPIs (active titles, aggregation vs proprietary, game rounds, regional revenue) or segment margin disclosed.

### AI positioning

**Management frames AI dealers as a complement sitting alongside RNG rather than a substitute for Live studios.**

- CEO does not believe AI dealers 'will take over live as some people ask'; group is 'working hard' with AI across functions for efficiency.
- Third-party AI-dealer products remove the studio capex barrier underpinning Evolution's Live moat and shorten rivals' time to market in regulated US states.

> “is a possibility… it's like an RNG live game, it's a complement” — CEO

### Our judgment

**RNG is a genuine if modest positive, accretive after three years of drag, but EUR 83m a quarter cannot carry a EUR 2.1bn group.**

- Base still small enough that ~10% growth is credible for several more quarters; North American localisation aligns with fastest-growing regulated region.
- Comparison base stays soft through Q1-27 (Q4-25 RNG was EUR 75.7m, ~$85m), so double-digit rate is partly base effect.
- Segment guidance is to grow at least in line with the market in each region.

## 5. Segment deep-dive — Regional mix & regulatory exposure (group)

*Asia is 37% of revenue by player IP but 4% by operator location — the grey-market gap is unchanged; Europe is losing share; Brazil removes a growth engine from Q4.*

### Two revenue views

**Asia is 37% of revenue by player IP but 4% by operator location; ~EUR 173m (~$194m) is effectively grey-market exposure.**

- By operator location: Europe 64% (EUR 336.3m, -6.0%), North America 15% (EUR 81.0m, +11.0%), Latin America 15% (EUR 76.6m, +39.3%), Asia 4% (EUR 19.1m, +42.5%), Africa & other 2% (EUR 13.1m, +61.7%).
- By player IP: Asia 37% (EUR 192.1m), Europe 33% (EUR 173.7m), North America 16% (EUR 84.3m), Latin America 9% (EUR 49.0m), Africa & other 5% (~EUR 27.0m).
- Regulated markets were 49% of revenue (EUR 258.5m, ~$290m); mobile 77.0% of operator GGR, up from 74.0%.

### Asia

**Asia grew only 1.6% YoY and 0.8% QoQ to EUR 192.1m after -8.9% YoY / -3.7% QoQ in Q2; stabilisation is real but shallow.**

- Asked whether cyber-crime issues were solved, the CEO answered 'No'; company taking 'long-term steps' against video piracy and unauthorised redistribution.
- Interim report no longer mentions the issue; it has become a running condition rather than a disclosed event.
- Every 5% of Asian volatility is roughly 1.8 points of group growth, more than the entire Q3 YoY growth rate; game-rounds/revenue divergence (+22.5% vs +3.7%) most plausibly sits here.

> “no quick solution” — CEO

### Europe

**Europe is the structural worry: IP revenue -4.7% to EUR 173.7m, operator-location revenue -6.0% to EUR 336.3m, with share not being captured.**

- +0.4% QoQ after +3.5% QoQ in Q2; European IP revenue has declined from above EUR 200m (~$224m) per quarter in late 2024.
- Management blamed regulatory change and tax but accepted it is not capturing its share of market growth; fix framed as 'a few stable quarters' of relationship work.

### Americas and Africa

**North America delivered another record at EUR 84.3m (+13.6% YoY, +4.1% QoQ) and remains the cleanest regulated growth story.**

- New Jersey studio is next.
- Latin America (+23.1%) and Africa & other (+23.7%) both records; CEO stressed Latin America's Q3 growth 'didn't include any effects' from Brazil.

### Brazil overhang

**São Paulo studio closed 5 October under PM 1.394/2026; Q4 impact unquantified beyond Brazil being under EUR 24.5m (~$27m) quarterly, <4.7% of group.**

- Lula won the 25 October run-off with 51.76% having campaigned on the ban; resolution rests with the STF (constitutionality) and Congress (conversion).
- Brazil is 'well below half' of LatAm IP revenue; market not ring-fenced; local staff retained for now.
- Brazilian cost without revenue is a drag of perhaps 20-30bps on margin, which management says is absorbed within the unchanged ~66% FY target.

### Our view

**Regional picture supports stability rather than re-acceleration, and the grey-market risk premium is not going away with Asia at 37%.**

- Americas and Africa (30% of IP revenue) grew ~18%; Europe and Asia (70%) shrank ~1.5%.
- Mix is drifting slowly toward regulated, lower-volatility revenue.

## 6. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- **Return to growth:** net revenue +3.7% YoY to EUR 526.1m (~$589m), est. +4.0% at constant currency, after four consecutive declines; all five regions grew QoQ for the first time since 2024. _(early)_
- **RNG now accretive:** +10.1% to EUR 83.1m (~$93m), second straight double-digit quarter (Q2 +14.0%), 9M +10.7%; supplied 40% of group YoY growth from <16% of the base. _(structural)_
- **Americas & Africa records:** North America +13.6% to EUR 84.3m (~$94m), LatAm +23.1% to EUR 49.0m (~$55m), Africa & other +23.7% to ~EUR 27.0m (~$30m) — ~30% of IP revenue growing ~18%. _(structural)_
- **Margin defended:** adj. EBITDA margin 65.8% (-60bps YoY, -10bps QoQ); FY ~66% target reiterated despite Brazil; other opex ex one-offs +0.7% YoY. _(structural)_
- **Cash machine intact:** OCF after investments EUR 334.1m (~$374m), LTM cash conversion 85%, cash EUR 656.4m (~$735m), zero debt, EUR 300m (~$336m) RCF undrawn. _(structural)_
- **Two overhangs closed:** UKGC licence review settled for GBP 4.75m (EUR 5.6m, ~$6.3m) and the $85m Galaxy Gaming deal terminated for a $5.2m fee — both paid, both excluded from adj. EBITDA; total EUR 12.1m (~$13.6m). _(one-off)_
- **Per-share accretion:** 16.9m shares (~8% of the count) repurchased for EUR 1,127.1m (~$1.26bn) since 19 May; basic EPS +4.6% to EUR 1.31 (~$1.47) despite profit for the period -3.6%. _(mixed)_

**Lowlights**

- **Europe losing share:** -4.7% YoY by IP to EUR 173.7m (~$195m), -6.0% by operator location to EUR 336.3m (~$377m); CEO: 'not exciting and we should do better', with no concrete remedy beyond 'a few stable quarters'. _(structural)_
- **Asia stalled, cyber-crime unresolved:** +1.6% YoY / +0.8% QoQ at EUR 192.1m (~$215m); 37% of revenue by IP vs 4% by operator location — grey-market exposure undiminished; regulated share only 49%. _(structural)_
- **Brazil ban:** São Paulo studio closed 5 Oct under PM 1.394/2026; Lula re-elected 25 Oct; up to ~EUR 24.5m (~$27m) of quarterly revenue at risk with the local cost base retained — Q4 will be 'more eventful'. _(one-off)_
- **Reported earnings down:** EBITDA -0.9% to EUR 333.8m (~$374m), margin -290bps to 63.5%; EBIT -2.1% to EUR 290.3m (~$325m); profit for the period -3.6% to EUR 243.3m (~$273m); reported opex +12% to EUR 235.9m (~$264m). _(mixed)_
- **Slight miss, again:** revenue EUR 526.1m vs ~EUR 529m consensus; diluted EPS EUR 1.29 (~$1.44) vs ~1.33 — fifth consecutive quarter of revenue at or below the Street. _(watch)_
- **Activity ≠ revenue, costs ≠ revenue:** game rounds index +22.5% YoY vs revenue +3.7%; headcount +8.1% and personnel costs +5.7% (EUR 124.3m, ~$139m) vs revenue +3.7% — negative operating leverage masked by 'adjusted' and other-opex restraint. _(structural)_
- **Buyback outrunning cash:** Q3 repurchases EUR 823.9m (~$923m) vs OCF EUR 334.1m (~$374m) — 2.4x the quarter's adj. EBITDA; cash down to EUR 656.4m; pace now being slowed to 'align with cash generation' with EUR 873m (~$978m) still to deploy. _(governance)_

## 7. Forecast & Outlook

There was no guidance change. Evolution's only numeric target is the FY2026 EBITDA margin, which stays 'in line with 2025' — i.e. ~66% against an FY25 adjusted margin of 66.1% — and it was reiterated on the call despite the 5 October Brazil closure. There is no revenue guidance; the qualitative ambition is to grow at least in line with the market in each region for both Live and RNG, and to ship 110+ games in 2026. 9M-26 adjusted margin is 65.7%, so Q4 must print ~66.5-67% for the full year to round to 66.0%. The moving parts: RNG (+10.1%) and the Americas/Africa (~+18%) pulling growth up; Europe (-4.7%) and Asia (+1.6%) pulling it down; EUR 12.1m (~$13.6m) of Q3 one-offs now behind; and Brazil removing up to ~EUR 24.5m (~$27m) of quarterly revenue from Q4 while its studio cost persists.

- **FY26 adj. EBITDA margin target:** ~66% (unchanged; FY25 66.1%) (9M-26 at 65.7% → Q4 needs ~66.5-67% to land on 66.0%)
- **Q4 revenue swing: Brazil:** up to -EUR 24.5m (~$27m)/qtr (<4.7% of group; partly offset by offshore leakage and Americas/Africa growth)
- **Q3 one-offs (not recurring):** EUR 12.1m (~$13.6m) (UKGC EUR 5.6m + Galaxy EUR 6.5m; none flagged for Q4)
- **Remaining buyback:** EUR 873m (~$978m) (Pace slowing to ~match EUR 330m (~$370m)/qtr cash generation; runs into Q1-27+)

Our estimates (not company guidance): **Q4-26E net revenue EUR 520-535m (~$582-599m), midpoint ~EUR 527m (~$590m), +2.5% YoY and flat QoQ**, assuming normal Q4 seasonality (+1-2% QoQ) is offset by a EUR 10-20m (~$11-22m) Brazil hole net of leakage to offshore-licensed operators; Live ~EUR 441m (~$494m), RNG ~EUR 86m (~$96m). **Q4-26E adjusted EBITDA margin 66.0-66.5%** (Brazilian cost without revenue costs ~20-30bps; no one-offs assumed), giving **FY26E revenue ~EUR 2.08bn (~$2.33bn), +0.7% YoY, and FY26E adjusted EBITDA ~EUR 1.37bn (~$1.53bn) at ~65.8-65.9%** — a hair below the 66.1% FY25 print but inside management's 'in line' framing; landing exactly on 66.0% requires a ~67% Q4, which we would not underwrite. **Leverage stays negative (net cash), but the pile shrinks:** with OCF after investments of ~EUR 330m (~$370m) a quarter and EUR 873m (~$978m) of programme left to run through Q4 and Q1-27, a straight-line pace of ~EUR 435m (~$487m) a quarter would take cash from EUR 656m toward ~EUR 450m (~$504m) by Q1-27 — hence the explicit slowing; we expect completion to slip into Q2-27 rather than draw the RCF. EPS continues to be flattered by ~8% share-count shrinkage, so look through it to profit for the period, which we model roughly flat YoY in Q4 (~EUR 250m, ~$280m) absent one-offs. For 2027 the swing factors are binary rather than incremental: a Brazil reopening (studio 'ready to accelerate'), a durable fix for Asian video piracy, and whether Europe can stop ceding share; absent those, this is a low-single-digit organic-growth business defending a mid-60s margin against ~8% headcount growth, and the equity's grey-market risk premium persists.
