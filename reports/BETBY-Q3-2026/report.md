## 1. Executive Summary

- **What this is — and is not.** BETBY (legal entity Earlybird Ltd, Malta reg. C 94530; MGA B2B licence MGA/B2B/780/2020, issued 3 Aug 2020; founded 2018) is privately held and unlisted. The 29 Oct 2026 'Q3 results' is a voluntary trading update giving YoY percentage KPIs only — no revenue, GGR base, NGR, EBITDA, net income, EPS, cash or debt. There is no exchange filing, no call transcript and no guidance. Every monetary line in this note is therefore n/a (not estimated); the analysis is of operating momentum. Fiscal year-end 31 Dec is inferred from the calendar-quarter reporting cadence, not from filings.
- **Headline: sportsbook GGR +33.4% YoY**, total bets placed +31.9%, active players +82.7% across the partner network (company release; corroborated by Gaming.net, Yogonet, Focus GN, SBC News, G3). No consensus exists, so 'vs expectations' is not measurable.
- **Decomposition says volume, not monetisation.** GGR grew in line with bet count (GGR per bet ~+1%) while actives grew ~2.5x faster than GGR: implied GGR per active ~-27% and bets per active ~-28% (our arithmetic). The incremental Q3 player is lower-frequency and lower-value — consistent with new operator go-lives and event-driven casual acquisition rather than deeper engagement of the existing base.
- **Sharp flip in the shape of growth vs 2025.** In Q3-25 the same KPIs were GGR +56.07%, bets +24.92%, actives +19.45% (GGR per active +31%, GGR per bet +25%) — stake/hold-led. H1-26 was transitional (GGR +42.7%, bets +21.2%, actives +47%; GGR per bet +18%, GGR per active -3%). Q3-26 is the first period where per-player economics are clearly diluting.
- **Trajectory: decelerating, but off the Q2 trough.** Sportsbook GGR growth went +61% (Q1-26, record March) → +42.7% (H1-26) → +33.4% (Q3-26). Q2 standalone is not published; equal-weighting the Q1/Q2-25 bases implies Q2-26 at ~+24% (range ~20–30% depending on base weights; est.), so Q3 is a modest re-acceleration. Two-year Q3 stack ~+108%.
- **Comp quality flatters Q3.** The Q3-25 release flagged lower sportsbook margins in September 2025 from unfavourable sporting results; some of the +33.4% is hold normalisation rather than volume. The company discloses no handle, hold or NGR, so luck vs structural cannot be split. Nor was the Q3-25 base event-free: they cited a 743% GGR surge in pre-match markets during the FIFA Club World Cup (final 13 Jul 2025).
- **Event stats: qualitative only.** Management credited the Esports World Cup and the concluding stages of the FIFA World Cup (knockout rounds to the 19 Jul final; the group stage sat in Q2). No World Cup GGR, handle, actives or margin was quantified. Their only tournament datapoint (published 21 Aug with Watch & Bet) is internal and self-selected: players at partner brands offering live streaming generated 44.79% higher GGR per player, 24.30% more bets and 32.53% higher average stake than players at non-streaming brands.
- **BETBY Games (proprietary esports feed): GGR +32.2%**, bets +45.4%, actives +123.3% — re-accelerating from +8.43% GGR in Q3-25 and an implied ~+14% in Q2-26 (est.), but on an easy comp. GGR per active ~-41% and GGR per bet ~-9% (derived) — heavier dilution than sportsbook, pointing to cross-sold trial from the sportsbook actives surge rather than standalone demand.
- **Games' mix is shrinking and is no longer disclosed.** Games was 12% of group GGR in Q1-25 and 14% in H1-25; the disclosure has not appeared since. With sportsbook out-growing Games in every subsequent period, our arithmetic puts the share near ~10% (est.). Two-year Q3 stack +43% vs sportsbook +108%.
- **Product pipeline is the forward story, not the Q3 number.** SlotBets launched 8 Sep 2026 (SlotBasketball first; 15-second rounds mapped to live match events; 93–94% RTP; 20x–1,000x multipliers; iFrame-deployable in sportsbook or casino) and was showcased at SBC Summit Lisbon on 1 Oct; Watch & Bet integrated streaming went live 21 Aug; User Chat was added in-quarter. Management explicitly said these had 'limited impact' on Q3 due to timing.
- **Market expansion: regulated Poland** via partnerships with Lebull and Betters — also flagged as limited Q3 impact. Poland taxes betting turnover, so a revenue-share supplier earns on a thin operator-margin base there; the contribution will be volume- rather than mix-driven (our context, not company disclosure).
- **No cost, marketing or margin disclosure.** As a private supplier there is no opex, S&M % of revenue, cost-of-sales, EBITDA or cash-flow data, and no cost-transformation programme to assess. Nothing to bridge.
- **Capital position: unknown.** No balance sheet, net debt, leverage or funding disclosure. Third-party databases (Tracxn) describe BETBY as unfunded/bootstrapped since 2018 — not verified by us. No buyback/dividend relevance.
- **Guidance: none, old or new.** CEO Leonid Pertsovskiy's only forward comment is a qualitative aim to 'finish the year on a strong note' and that the company has 'set ambitious goals'. No numeric targets; no FY-25 or Q4-25 update was found, so the Q4 comp is undisclosed.
- **Management emphasising vs burying.** Emphasised: actives +82.7% / +123.3%, 'momentum', product launches. Not disclosed: GGR base, hold, handle, NGR, partner count, client concentration, churn, and Games' share of GGR (discontinued after H1-25). The actives headline is the least informative KPI when GGR per active is falling ~27%.
- **Client concentration is the unstated risk.** The 2025 base inflation (H1-25 sportsbook GGR +472%, actives +211%, bets +277%) was attributed at the time to new project launches and 'strategic clients'; growth of that shape in a B2B supplier typically rests on a handful of large operators. No top-client disclosure exists.
- **Esports attribution needs care.** BETBY Games is simulated/AI-driven esports-style content, so an Esports World Cup halo accrues more naturally to their separate real-esports odds feed; the release does not separate the two, and the +123% Games actives is most plausibly distribution (new sportsbook actives exposed to the feed) rather than standalone demand.
- **Thesis read-through for listed B2B comps (Kambi, Sportradar, Genius, OpenBet peers).** The update supports the view that World Cup 2026 pushed B2B volume breadth into Q3 and that esports/simulated content remains a ~10% (est.) niche adjunct. The per-player dilution is the datapoint to track: either a healthy top-of-funnel widening ahead of Q4 football density, or the first sign that incremental operator wins are smaller and lower-quality.
- **Share price reaction: n/a** — no listed equity or public debt.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — Sportsbook (core B2B platform)

*GGR +33.4% on actives +82.7%: breadth from new go-lives and tournament casuals, with GGR per active ~-27%; re-accelerating off an implied ~+24% Q2 but well below Q1's +61%, and lapping a soft-hold September 2025.*

### Q3 KPIs and mix

**GGR +33.4% year-on-year in Q3 2026 was almost purely volumetric: bets +31.9%, actives +82.7%, GGR per active roughly -27%.**

- No absolute GGR, handle, hold or NGR disclosed; everything is read off three growth rates.
- GGR per bet roughly +1%; actives grew about two-and-a-half times faster than GGR.
- Bets per active fell roughly 28% by the analyst's arithmetic.
- Incremental Q3 player was lighter and lower-value, consistent with new operator go-lives and World Cup and Esports World Cup casual sign-ups.
- Nothing in the release points to deeper monetisation of the existing base.

### Shape of growth

**Q3 2026 is the first period in which per-player economics are visibly diluting, a sharp change from stake- and hold-led growth in 2025.**

- Q3 2025: GGR +56.07%, bets +24.92%, actives +19.45%, i.e. GGR per active +31% and GGR per bet +25%.
- H1 2026: GGR +42.7%, bets +21.2%, actives +47%; GGR per bet +18%, GGR per active -3%.
- Analyst would not over-read a single quarter; tournament-heavy July into August is when casual breadth should peak.
- Direction needs to reverse in Q4 for the actives headline to mean much; no cohort or retention data given.

### Trajectory and comp

**Growth stepped down from +61% in Q1 to +42.7% for H1 and +33.4% in Q3, but Q3 is a modest re-acceleration off an implied ~24% Q2.**

- Q2 2026 not published; equal-weighting Q1 and Q2 2025 bases implies ~24% (roughly 20–30% depending on base weights; analyst estimate).
- Q2 softness notable given the World Cup group stage sat in June.
- Q3 2025 release flagged lower sportsbook margins in September 2025 from unfavourable results, so part of +33.4% is hold normalisation.
- Q3 2025 cited a 743% GGR surge in pre-match markets during the FIFA Club World Cup, final on 13 July 2025.
- Two-year Q3 stack is ~+108%, the more honest measure of scale gained.

### Tournament attribution

**Management attributed the quarter to the FIFA World Cup knockout rounds, the Esports World Cup and strong network activity, but quantified none of it.**

- No World Cup GGR, handle, actives or margin datapoint and no comparison to any prior tournament.
- Only tournament data: with the 21 August Watch & Bet launch, players at streaming brands generated 44.79% higher GGR per player, 24.30% more bets, 32.53% more stake.
- That is a self-selected comparison; streaming brands are likely larger operators, so treat as a product-marketing claim rather than an uplift estimate.
- Handle, net hold, structural margin, parlay mix and promotional intensity are not disclosed in any period, so volume-versus-price-versus-luck cannot be split.

### Product expansion

**Forward content is product launches, all explicitly flagged as having limited Q3 impact because of timing.**

- SlotBets launched 8 September with SlotBasketball: 15-second rounds split into three five-second windows, 93–94% RTP, multipliers 20x to 1,000x, iFrame deployment in sportsbook or casino lobby.
- Dedicated press conference at SBC Summit Lisbon on 1 October, with intention to extend the format to other sports.
- Watch & Bet integrated streaming went live 21 August; User Chat added in-quarter.

### Markets and guidance

**Entered regulated Poland via Lebull and Betters, but no partner, churn or concentration figures and no segment guidance were given.**

- Poland taxes betting turnover, so a revenue-share supplier earns off a thin operator margin; contribution will be volume- rather than mix-driven (analyst context).
- No partner count, go-live count, client-concentration or churn figures disclosed.
- 2025 base inflation (H1 2025 sportsbook GGR +472%, actives +211%, attributed to new project launches and strategic clients) points to a concentrated client book.
- CEO's only forward comment is a qualitative aim to finish the year strongly.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — BETBY Games (proprietary esports feed)

*GGR +32.2% on actives +123.3% and bets +45.4%: a re-acceleration on an easy +8.4% comp, but GGR per active ~-41% and a shrinking (~10% est.) share of group GGR that the company no longer discloses.*

### Q3 KPIs and dilution

**GGR +32.2% on bets +45.4% and actives +123.3%; dilution heavier than the core book with GGR per active roughly -41%.**

- Bets per active roughly -35% and GGR per bet roughly -9% by the analyst's arithmetic.
- Actives more than doubling while GGR per active falls two-fifths reads as cross-sold trial from the +82.7% sportsbook active surge rather than standalone demand.
- Management grouped the Esports World Cup among drivers, but Games is simulated AI-driven content, so any EWC halo accrues more naturally to the separate real-esports odds feed.
- Release does not separate the two; no Games-specific event data given.

### Comp and trajectory

**A genuine re-acceleration on an easy comp, but the two-year Q3 stack is only ~+43% against ~+108% for sportsbook.**

- Q3 2025 was the weakest disclosed quarter: GGR +8.43%, bets +6.18%, actives +11.85%.
- eKabaddi, eVolleyball and eBoxing added September 2025, so Q3 2026 carried a full year of the expanded portfolio against a base lacking it.
- Games GGR growth: +42% in Q1, +28% for H1 implying ~+14% in Q2 (est.), now +32.2%.
- H1 2026 GGR per bet was +11.5%; Q3 flipped to -9%, so the product is now bet in smaller tickets.

### Group mix share

**Games share of group GGR is drifting down to ~10% (est.) and the company has stopped disclosing it.**

- Games was 12% of group GGR in Q1 2025 and 14% in H1 2025; disclosure has not appeared since.
- Estimate assumes the 14% held in the Q3 2024 base and sportsbook out-grew Games every subsequent period.
- Remains a useful engagement and differentiation tool in dead periods of the real-sport calendar and the content most exposed to the 'younger, digitally native' positioning used for SlotBets.
- At this scale it is not the growth engine the 2025 releases framed it as.
- No Games-specific guidance, pricing, operator-adoption or content-roadmap figures given.

## 5. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- **Widest top-of-funnel in the disclosed series:** sportsbook actives +82.7% and Games actives +123.3% YoY, with bets +31.9% / +45.4%; two-year Q3 sportsbook GGR stack ~+108%. _(mixed)_
- **Re-acceleration off the Q2 trough:** Q3 sportsbook GGR +33.4% vs an implied ~+24% in Q2 (est.) — the World Cup knockouts and Esports World Cup did lift volume into Q3 after a surprisingly soft group-stage quarter. _(early)_
- **BETBY Games re-accelerated to +32.2% GGR** from +8.43% in Q3-25 and ~+14% implied Q2-26 (est.), with bets +45.4% — the expanded content line-up (eKabaddi, eVolleyball, eBoxing) now in the base. _(early)_
- **Product cadence is genuinely differentiated:** SlotBets (8 Sep; a new 'slotbetting' category bridging casino and sportsbook UX, 15-second live-data rounds, 93–94% RTP), Watch & Bet streaming (21 Aug) and User Chat all shipped in one quarter, with management explicitly positioning them as Q4/2027 drivers. _(structural)_
- **Regulated-market expansion:** entry into Poland via Lebull and Betters adds a licensed European footprint to the MGA B2B base, with contribution still to come. _(structural)_
- **Streaming uplift data** (44.79% higher GGR per player, +24.30% bets, +32.53% average stake at streaming-enabled brands during the World Cup) gives operators a commercial case for Watch & Bet — caveat self-selection. _(one-off)_

**Lowlights**

- **Per-player dilution:** implied sportsbook GGR per active ~-27% and Games ~-41% (our arithmetic) — growth is breadth, not monetisation; no cohort or retention data to judge whether the World Cup intake sticks. _(watch)_
- **Deceleration:** sportsbook GGR growth +61% (Q1) → +42.7% (H1) → +33.4% (Q3); the implied Q2 at ~+24% (est.) means the World Cup group stage did not produce a step-change. _(structural)_
- **Soft comp:** Q3-25 carried a weak-hold September; part of the +33.4% is normalisation. With no handle, hold or NGR disclosure, luck cannot be separated from structural growth. _(one-off)_
- **Disclosure quality is poor and getting thinner:** no monetary figures in any period, Games' share of GGR discontinued after H1-25, no Q2 standalone, no Q4-25/FY-25 update found, no partner count, no client concentration. _(governance)_
- **Esports attribution is loose:** the Esports World Cup is cited as a driver, but BETBY Games is simulated content; the +123% actives looks like distribution via the sportsbook rather than standalone demand, and Games' mix has likely slipped to ~10% (est.). _(watch)_
- **Concentration risk unquantified:** the 2025 base (H1-25 GGR +472%, actives +211%) was built on 'strategic clients' and new launches; a B2B book of that shape can swing on one or two operator decisions, and no churn or top-client data is given. _(watch)_

## 6. Forecast & Outlook

There is no guidance to bridge — BETBY has never issued numeric targets, and the only forward statement in the Q3 release is CEO Leonid Pertsovskiy's qualitative aim to 'finish the year on a strong note'. The bridge below is therefore our reconstruction of the 2026 growth path for the division that matters (sportsbook, ~90% of GGR by our estimate), using the company's reported Q1, H1 and Q3 YoY rates and an equal-weight assumption for the undisclosed Q2 base. The moving parts for Q4 are: a dense European club-football calendar with no tournament comp issue (Q4-25 had none either, though the Q4-25 rate itself was never published); first contributions from Poland (Lebull, Betters), SlotBets and Watch & Bet, all small at this stage; and the retention of the Q3 casual intake, which is the swing factor for the actives line.

- **Q1-26 sportsbook GGR (reported):** +61% YoY (record March; easy base (Q1-25 itself +179%))
- **Q2-26 sportsbook GGR (implied, est.):** ~+24% YoY (H1 +42.7% less Q1; equal-weight bases; range ~20–30%)
- **Q3-26 sportsbook GGR (reported):** +33.4% YoY (+~9pts vs implied Q2; -28pts vs Q1; soft-hold Sep-25 comp)
- **Q4-26 sportsbook GGR (our estimate):** +25–35% YoY (no company guidance; Poland/SlotBets marginal; Q4-25 comp undisclosed)

**Revenue:** we expect Q4-26 sportsbook GGR growth of roughly **+25–35% YoY (est.)** and BETBY Games of **+20–35% (est.)**, which would put full-year 2026 sportsbook GGR growth in the **high-30s% (est.)** given Q1 +61%, implied Q2 ~+24% and Q3 +33.4% (quarter weights unknown). The skew is to the downside of that range if GGR per active keeps falling at -25% or worse while actives growth fades as World Cup cohorts churn; the skew is upside only if Q3's active intake proves sticky into the club-football season. Note that BETBY's own revenue is a revenue share on operator NGR plus fees, so **BETBY revenue growth should run below partner GGR growth** wherever new clients come in on ramp pricing, promotional intensity is high, or taxes bite (Poland's turnover tax is the obvious example). **Margin and leverage:** n/a — nothing is disclosed and we will not model what cannot be checked; a bootstrapped supplier growing partner GGR ~2x in two years is, however, a natural strategic target, and a funding or M&A event is a more plausible 2027 catalyst than any disclosed financial milestone. **Disclosure:** the single most useful thing management could do is publish an absolute GGR base, a hold metric and partner counts; until then every print is a momentum signal only.

**What to watch next quarter:**

- Q4-26 / FY-26 trading update (no Q4-25 or FY-25 release was found — whether a year-end print appears, and whether it finally carries absolute GGR, hold or partner-count figures).
- Direction of implied GGR per active and bets per active: dilution of ~-27% needs to narrow in Q4 for the +82.7% actives to be read as franchise growth rather than tournament churn.
- Whether the Games share-of-GGR disclosure is reinstated; our ~10% estimate assumes no Games-specific wins.
- Poland ramp: go-live timing for Lebull and Betters, and any further regulated-market licences (BETBY operates on an MGA B2B licence).
- SlotBets adoption: number of operators live, extension beyond SlotBasketball, and any regulatory classification questions (RNG-free slot mechanics in sportsbook lobbies).
- Client concentration and churn: any named operator migrating in-house or to a rival; the 2025 'strategic client' base is the key fragility.
- Hold normalisation: September–October football results and whether management again flags margin effects, which would confirm Q3's comp benefit.
- Corporate: funding round, strategic investor or acquisition — the most likely route to real financial disclosure.
