# Bally's Intralot S.A. (INLOT) — 9M 2026 Analyst Report
** · Reporting currency: EUR (tables in € millions; ~USD equivalents at EUR/USD 1.097 as of 20 Nov 2026) · Results released 20 Nov 2026 (final 9M results; preliminary results 10 Nov 2026)**
**Market reaction:** Muted: shares −1.9% on 20 Nov 2026 to ~€1.065 per Investing.com's write-up of the results presentation (that source prints the level as "$1.065"; Euronext Athens quotes in EUR — level not verified against the exchange).

> A decent operating quarter wrapped around a bad guidance quarter. UK online grew 12.2% cc to a third straight record NGR with less marketing, Q3 AEBITDA rose 5.1% QoQ to €88.9m (~$97.5m) and the margin recovered 50bps to 31.1% — but management cut FY26 AEBITDA guidance for the second time in three months to €380–395m (~$417–433m) from €402–415m (and ~€422m originally), because the 40% UK remote gaming duty is still only ~55% offset, UK consolidation is "slower than I expected" and US lottery is soft. Pro forma leverage drifted to 4.33x into the January 2027 evoke close, and the implied Q4 (€106–121m vs €88.9m in Q3) looks stretched.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Headline: operations fine, guidance not.** 9M26 revenue €830.3m (~$911m) and AEBITDA €273.6m (~$300m, 33.0% margin) — not comparable with 9M25 (€263.9m / €90.1m) because Bally's International Interactive (BII) was consolidated from 8 Oct 2025. On the company's own pro forma comparable, 9M AEBITDA fell 14.7% from €320.8m. Q3 revenue €286.1m (~$314m), +3.6% QoQ; AEBITDA €88.9m (~$97.5m), +5.1% QoQ; margin 31.1%, +50bps QoQ but −540bps YoY (3Q25: 36.5%).
- **Second guidance cut in three months.** FY26 AEBITDA now €380–395m (~$417–433m): BII €270–280m (~$296–307m) and legacy Intralot €110–115m (~$121–126m). August: €402–415m (BII €280–295m, legacy €112–120m). March plan, reaffirmed in May: ~€422m (BII c.€299m, legacy c.€124m). Midpoint −€21m (−5%) vs August, −€35m (−8%) vs the original plan. Both ranges now explicitly embed GBP, USD and TRY translation.
- **Which division drove it: ~70% BII.** CEO Robeson Reeves put the BII gap at ~€15m — "I got the pace of consolidation wrong, because I expected subscale operators to fall away much faster" — plus a deliberate choice not to strip cost so the team can "hit the ground running" at evoke close. The legacy gap is €5–7.5m "depending on jackpots" (US lottery). Edison (company-sponsored) sits at €387.5m, the midpoint.
- **The UK duty is the whole P&L story.** Remote gaming duty rose from 21% to 40% on 1 Apr 2026. Gross AEBITDA hit ~€34m (~$37m) in Q3 (in line with Q2), ~€68m (~$75m) in 9M. 9M offset 48.5% (+€26.2m organic gross-profit growth, +€6.8m fixed-cost actions). Q3 offset ~55% measured YoY — which arithmetically implies ~42% in Q2 on the same basis; the ~65% cited at H1 was measured against the pre-duty run-rate, so the metric's basis has changed. Ex-duty, BII AEBITDA would be +14.4% YoY.
- **UK top line is genuinely strong.** UK online revenue +12.2% cc YoY in Q3 (Q1 +10.5%, Q2 +11.6%) — three consecutive accelerations and a third straight record NGR quarter, delivered with marketing lower both YoY and QoQ. 9M UK online +11.4%. Spain hit a record €13.3m (~$14.6m), +18.9% YoY on a larger active base. No actives, ARPU, product split or market-share data are disclosed.
- **BII economics.** Q3 revenue €201.6m (~$221m), +4.0% QoQ; 9M €579.2m (~$635m), +10.9% cc pro forma; AEBITDA €62.8m (~$69m) at 31.1% (Q2 31.0%, pre-duty Q1 39.5%); 9M €195.6m (~$215m) at 33.8% — we derive −15.2% vs a €230.7m pro forma 9M25 base. BII is 70% of group revenue and 71% of AEBITDA.
- **Legacy Intralot is drifting.** 9M revenue €251.1m (~$276m), −4.9% reported / −3.4% cc; AEBITDA €78.1m (~$86m), −13.4% / −11.9% cc; margin 31.1% vs 34.2%. Legacy B2B AEBITDA €58.4m (~$64m), −15.6% cc, margin 30.6% vs 34.1%, on US revenue −11.0% cc to €119.6m (~$131m) (record 2025 jackpots, lower equipment sales); rest of B2B +3.6% cc. Q3 group B2B AEBITDA €20.2m fell €4.3m QoQ (margin 32.0% vs 37.6%) — not explained in the release.
- **Legacy B2C is the bright spot, in lira.** Bilyoner wagers +28.4% LC vs a Turkish online sports-betting market up 17.8% (share gains); revenue +20.6% LC but only +3.4% in EUR to €45.8m (~$50m) on an 18.2% average lira depreciation; Q3 revenue +25.8% QoQ and AEBITDA +€4.7m QoQ after a Q2 hit from seasonality and a remuneration-structure amendment. Argentina +21.1% to €14.3m (~$15.7m). Legacy B2C AEBITDA €19.7m (~$21.6m), +1.8%.
- **Below the line, losses are structural for now.** 9M EBT −€21.4m (~−$23m) vs +€8.8m; NIATMI −€40.9m (~−$45m) vs −€3.1m, on net finance costs of €109.7m (~$120m; 9M25 €20.5m), D&A €130.7m (incl. €38.2m PPA) and transaction fees €27.7m (~$30m). Q3 NIATMI −€26.6m (~−$29m). Pro forma LTM PAT is €74.2m vs a reported LTM loss we derive at ~€98.6m. Derived GAAP EPS ~−€0.022 (~−$0.024) on ~1.87bn shares (share capital €560.3m / €0.30 nominal) — not company-reported; no adjusted EPS disclosed.
- **Cash flow fine, net debt still creeping up.** 9M OCF €206.6m (~$227m; Q3 €73.2m); net capex €133.3m (~$146m) incl. the €85.0m (~$93m) Victoria 15-year monitoring-licence premium; company-defined FCF €142.9m (~$157m) excludes that premium; FCF to equity €54.5m (~$60m) after €88.4m net interest. Adjusted net debt €1,629.9m (~$1.79bn), +€136.0m YTD (licence €85.0m, deal fees/bond costs €19.5m, other investing €4.0m, non-cash €26.5m), flat QoQ vs €1,618.9m. PF leverage 4.33x vs 4.05x at Q2 as the duty annualises; PF LTM AEBITDA has slid €431m (Dec-25) → €427m (Mar-26) → €399.9m (Jun-26) → €376.7m (Sep-26).
- **Liquidity rebuilt with new sterling debt.** £261.8m (~€306m / ~$336m) three-year senior secured term facility signed in July; Tranche A £157.1m (€183.2m / ~$201m) drawn, €65m RCF repaid in August. Liquidity €472.4m (~$518m) = €312.4m cash incl. restricted + undrawn €160m RCF (Jun-26: €287.3m). Funded debt €1,882m: €130m 6.00% retail bond 2029, €600m 6.75% SSNs 2031, €300m FRNs 2031, £400m 2025 term loan (€466.7m), £157.1m 2026 term loan, €200m Greek bank facility.
- **Capital returns while 4.3x levered.** €30.0m (~$33m) dividend paid plus €25.5m (~$28m) of buyback/TRS-collateral outflows in 9M — i.e. €55.5m returned against €54.5m of FCFE — and a €43.3m (~$47.5m) total-return-swap liability (Deutsche Bank acquiring shares on the company's behalf) sits outside the "Total Debt" definition. Funding shareholder returns with new debt into a leveraging acquisition is a governance point for both bondholders and minorities.
- **evoke on track for January 2027.** All-share acquisition announced 5 June (equity value c.£243m / ~€284m / ~$312m per iGB; EV c.€1.6bn per Edison at the indicated 50p). evoke shareholders approved with 99.63% (18 Aug), BYLOT EGM 18 Sep; UK gaming-regulatory and UK merger-control conditions satisfied; remaining gaming approvals and the Gibraltar court sanction expected by end-2026. The 10 Nov preliminary release said "by Q1-27"; the 20 Nov final firmed this to a January close. No synergy number yet — only "significant cost synergies and growth opportunities". Until close, the two are run as separate competitors.
- **Lottery contract renewals bought duration, at a cost.** 2026 wins/renewals: Victoria 15-year EGM monitoring licence (Apr), BCLC shared services (Apr), Polla Chilena (Apr), Hellenic Lotteries (May), OLG Ontario lottery technology (Jun), PLI Ireland to 2034 (Jul); US new-contract go-lives in Montana and Idaho, with Arkansas and New Hampshire in 1H-27. Normalised items show the price of staying in the game: Maryland litigation/RFP costs, the completed Texas RFP (outcome not disclosed), New York and South Carolina RFP preparation, a Croatia software-delay penalty, an ODIE receivable impairment and a Turkish KYC fine.
- **Mix has flipped to online.** 9M26 revenue: iGaming & sports betting 75.0% (19.8% in 9M25), lottery 20.3% (68.2%), VLT monitoring 4.0%, casino/other 0.7%; B2C 76.9% / B2B 23.1%. Regionally (chart-derived) UK €540.6m (65%), America ~€150.7m (18%, −9% YoY), Europe €70.7m (9%), RoW €68.3m (8%).
- **Cost lines.** Gaming tax €192.2m (~$211m) in 9M, 23.1% of revenue (€76.8m / 26.8% in Q3, ~38% of BII revenue vs ~22% pre-duty); marketing €70.7m (~$78m), 8.5% of revenue (Q3 €21.7m / 7.6%, below the ~€24.5m/qtr 1H run-rate); personnel €130.5m (15.7%; Q3 €42.7m / 14.9%). AEBITDA excludes €21.4m of non-recurring items in 9M (€5.4m in Q3).
- **Implied Q4 arithmetic looks stretched (our estimate).** Guidance implies Q4 AEBITDA of €106–121m (~$117–133m) vs €88.9m in Q3 and vs a pro forma Q4-25 of ~€103m (derived: PF LTM €376.7m less 9M26) — flat-to-up YoY despite ~€34m of incremental duty in Q4. BII alone needs €74–84m vs €62.8m in Q3 (+19–34% QoQ), i.e. a >100% YoY duty offset after 55% in Q3. Q4 is seasonally the strongest UK quarter and marketing is already pared, but we regard the bottom of the range as the realistic target with risk below it.
- **Management/strategy.** CEO Robeson Reeves (also Bally's Corp CEO), CFO Andreas Chrysos, COO & President Chrysostomos Sfatos, CFO Interactive Katherine Gomaniouk presented. Priorities stated on the call: revenue growth over further marketing cuts; retain cost base for evoke integration; Q2 "was the low point". Bally's Corp consolidates the company and reported its Q3 (Bally's Intralot B2C revenue +22% YoY in USD) on 9/16 Nov.
- **Thesis read-through.** BII has shown it can grow through a 40% duty but cannot yet defend EBITDA; the equity case has migrated almost entirely to UK consolidation plus the evoke cost-synergy story, carried on 4.3x leverage pre-close. Two cuts have cost credibility, so the next catalysts are binary: Q4 delivery against the implied step-up, the evoke closing (Gibraltar court, remaining licences) and the first quantified synergy/pro forma leverage disclosure in Q1-27.
- **Share reaction muted:** −1.9% on 20 Nov per Investing.com's slide write-up (level printed as "$1.065", almost certainly €1.065 on Euronext Athens — unverified against the exchange).

## 3. Segment deep-dive — Bally's International Interactive (BII) — UK & Spain online B2C

*UK NGR at a third straight record on +12.2% cc with less marketing, but the 40% duty is still only ~55% offset and the FY26 BII range was cut again to €270–280m; implied Q4 needs a 19–34% sequential jump.*

### Revenue and UK growth

**Q3 revenue €201.6m (~$221m), +4.0% QoQ, with UK online revenue +12.2% cc, a third straight record NGR quarter on lower marketing.**

- 9M revenue €579.2m (~$635m), 70% of group, +10.9% YoY cc pro forma; consolidated from 8 October 2025.
- UK is €540.6m (~$593m) of 9M revenue, roughly 93% of the division; Q1 +10.5%, Q2 +11.6%, Q3 +12.2% cc.
- Spain record €13.3m (~$14.6m), +18.9% YoY and roughly +7% versus ~€12.4m derived for Q2.
- No actives, ARPU, product or market-share data disclosed; falling marketing intensity suggests retention- and cross-sell-led growth, but equally consistent with harvesting ahead of a merger.

> “a larger active player base” — Management

### UK duty impact

**The 21% to 40% UK remote gaming duty step-up costs ~€34m (~$37m) per quarter; 9M BII AEBITDA €195.6m, −15.2% pro forma.**

- Group gaming tax €76.8m (~$84m) in Q3, 26.8% of group revenue and roughly 38% of BII revenue versus roughly 22% in pre-duty Q1 (derived).
- Q3 BII AEBITDA €62.8m (~$69m), +4.5% QoQ, 31.1% margin (Q2 31.0%, Q1 39.5%); 9M €195.6m (~$215m) at 33.8% versus €230.7m pro forma 9M25.
- Ex-duty BII AEBITDA would have grown 14.4%; bridge shows +€26.2m organic gross profit and +€6.8m fixed-cost actions, 48.5% of 9M hit offset.
- Q3 offset ~55% YoY, implying only ~42% in Q2 on the same basis; H1's ~65% was measured against pre-duty run-rate, a basis change.
- Reeves called Q2 the low point; Q3 supports that at margin level but not yet at a rate that protects the full year.

> “now that the duty is in every quarter” — Management

### Marketing and cost base

**Group marketing €21.7m (~$24m) in Q3, 7.6% of revenue; BII marketing roughly €19m, ~9.5% of divisional revenue, down low-double-digits sequentially.**

- 1H averaged ~€24.5m per quarter; 9M €70.7m, 8.5% of revenue; legacy run-rate ~€2.6m in 3Q25.
- CEO prioritises revenue growth over further marketing cuts and wants the team ready at evoke completion.
- BII is carrying integration-readiness cost that depresses the standalone margin.

> “hasn't removed as much cost as I may have wanted” — CEO

### FY26 guidance cut

**FY26 BII AEBITDA cut again to €270–280m (~$296–307m) from €280–295m in August and c.€299m at the March plan.**

- August top end was the consolidation scenario; Reeves quantified the gap at ~€15m.
- Attributed to slower UK consolidation plus the retained cost base.

> “I expected subscale operators to fall away much faster and their players to move to larger-scale brands” — Reeves

### Implied Q4 risk

**Q4 needs €74.4–84.4m (~$82–93m) AEBITDA versus €62.8m in Q3, a 19–34% sequential step-up; we see bottom of range as realistic with risk below.**

- Q4 is seasonally the strongest UK online quarter and marketing already pared.
- Derived pro forma Q4-25 BII AEBITDA was only around €70–78m, so range implies more than fully offsetting the ~€34m YoY duty hit after 55% in Q3.

### evoke acquisition

**All-share evoke deal (equity c.£243m / ~€284m / ~$312m; EV c.€1.6bn at 50p) targets January 2027 close, firmed from Q1-27.**

- Cleared UK gaming-regulatory and merger-control conditions and both shareholder votes (evoke 99.63%); awaits remaining gaming approvals and Gibraltar court sanction by end-2026.
- Until close the two run as competitors; no synergy figure beyond significant cost synergies.
- April 2027 remote betting duty increase matters less for casino/bingo but hits William Hill sportsbook in first full year of combined ownership.

> “significant cost synergies” — Management

## 4. Segment deep-dive — Intralot legacy B2B — lottery technology, VLT monitoring & services

## 6. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- UK online revenue +12.2% cc YoY in Q3 — a third consecutive acceleration (10.5% → 11.6% → 12.2%) and third straight record NGR quarter, achieved with marketing down YoY and QoQ (group marketing €21.7m, 7.6% of revenue, vs ~€24.5m/qtr in 1H). Evidence that scale brands can grow through a 40% duty. _(structural)_
- Spain online at a record €13.3m (~$14.6m), +18.9% YoY on a larger active base — small (~6–7% of BII) but a second growth leg outside the UK. _(early)_
- Sequential recovery: Q3 AEBITDA €88.9m (~$97.5m), +5.1% QoQ, margin +50bps to 31.1%; BII AEBITDA +€2.7m QoQ to €62.8m; duty offset improved to ~55% YoY from ~42% implied for Q2; management says Q2 was the low point. _(mixed)_
- Bilyoner is taking share — wagers +28.4% LC vs a Turkish market +17.8%, revenue +20.6% LC, AEBITDA +21.5% LC — and drove a €4.7m QoQ AEBITDA swing in Q3; Argentina +21.1% revenue. Translation (TRY −18.2%) hides it in euros. _(structural)_
- Liquidity rebuilt to €472.4m (~$518m) from €287.3m: £261.8m (~€306m / ~$336m) three-year senior secured sterling term facility signed in July, Tranche A £157.1m drawn, €65m RCF repaid, €160m RCF fully undrawn; adjusted net debt flat QoQ at €1,629.9m (~$1.79bn). _(one-off)_
- evoke de-risked: UK gaming-regulatory and UK merger-control conditions satisfied, evoke shareholders 99.63% in favour, BYLOT EGM done; close firmed to January 2027 from "Q1-27" ten days earlier. The scale answer to the duty is arriving. _(structural)_
- Lottery contract duration restored: Victoria 15-year EGM monitoring, OLG Ontario, PLI Ireland to 2034, Hellenic Lotteries, Polla Chilena, BCLC; US go-lives in Montana and Idaho with Arkansas/New Hampshire in 1H-27 — the 2026/27 expiry overhang is gone. _(structural)_

**Lowlights**

- Second FY26 AEBITDA cut in three months: €380–395m (~$417–433m) vs €402–415m in August and ~€422m in March/May; midpoint −8% from the original plan. ~€15m of the gap is BII (slower UK consolidation, cost deliberately retained for evoke), €5–7.5m legacy (jackpots). Credibility is now the scarce resource. _(structural)_
- Implied Q4 looks stretched: guidance needs Q4 AEBITDA of €106–121m vs €88.9m in Q3 and ~€103m pro forma in Q4-25 (derived) — flat-to-up YoY despite ~€34m of incremental duty. BII alone needs €74–84m vs €62.8m (+19–34% QoQ), i.e. >100% duty offset after 55% in Q3. _(watch)_
- The duty is only half-mitigated: 48.5% of the ~€68m (~$75m) 9M gross hit offset; BII AEBITDA −15% YoY pro forma to €195.6m with the margin at 33.8% vs 39.5% in pre-duty Q1; group gaming tax €192.2m (23% of revenue). Ex-duty growth of +14.4% is real but not bankable. _(structural)_
- Legacy B2B profitability is falling faster than revenue: 9M AEBITDA €58.4m (~$64m), −15.6% cc, margin 30.6% vs 34.1%; group B2B AEBITDA −€4.3m QoQ in Q3 to €20.2m (32.0% margin vs 37.6%) on only −€2.0m of revenue — unexplained in the release, and management conceded "some truth" to the mispriced-contracts question. _(mixed)_
- Leverage up to 4.33x PF from 4.05x as the duty annualises; NIATMI −€40.9m (~−$45m); net finance cost €109.7m (~$120m); FCFE €54.5m (~$60m) versus €55.5m of dividends, buybacks and TRS collateral — shareholder returns funded by new sterling debt ahead of a leveraging acquisition, with a €43.3m TRS liability outside the debt definition. _(governance)_
- The duty-mitigation KPI changed basis: ~65% at H1 (vs pre-duty run-rate) became ~55% at Q3 on a YoY basis, which implies Q2 was ~42% on the new basis. The direction is improving, but the optics have been managed. _(governance)_
- One-off noise accumulating: €27.7m (~$30m) transaction fees in 9M, Turkish KYC fine, Croatia software-delay penalty, ODIE receivable impairment, Maryland litigation and multi-state RFP costs (Texas outcome undisclosed), €85.0m Australian licence premium excluded from the FCF definition; TRY −18.2% and GBP translation (€7.5m adverse FX on sterling loans). _(one-off)_

## 7. Forecast & Outlook

The FY26 guidance bridge is a two-step walk-down driven roughly 70/30 by BII and legacy. The March plan (~€422m, reaffirmed with Q1) assumed c.€299m from International Interactive and c.€124m from legacy Intralot. In August, after the first full quarter at 40% RGD, BII was cut to €280–295m (the top end conditional on UK consolidation) and legacy to €112–120m. On 20 November BII went to €270–280m and legacy to €110–115m — €380–395m (~$417–433m) combined. The moving parts, per the CEO: UK consolidation slower than assumed (sub-scale operators not exiting post-duty), cost base deliberately retained for evoke integration, a weak US jackpot environment, and GBP/USD/TRY translation now explicitly inside the range. What has not moved is the UK top line, which keeps accelerating.

- **Original FY26 AEBITDA guidance (Mar-26; reaffirmed May):** ~€422m (~$463m) (BII c.€299m + legacy c.€124m)
- **H1 cut (31 Aug-26):** €402–415m (~$441–455m) (−€13.5m at mid; BII €280–295m (top = consolidation case), legacy €112–120m)
- **Q3 cut (20 Nov-26):** €380–395m (~$417–433m) (−€21m at mid; BII −~€15m (slower consolidation, retained cost), legacy −€5–7.5m (jackpots); FX embedded)
- **Implied Q4-26 AEBITDA (our arithmetic):** €106–121m (~$117–133m) (vs Q3 €88.9m and PF Q4-25 ~€103m (derived); BII needs €74–84m vs €62.8m)

**Revenue:** we model FY26 group revenue of ~€1.12–1.13bn (~$1.23–1.24bn): 9M €830.3m plus a Q4 of ~€290–300m, with BII at ~€205–210m on normal UK Q4 seasonality and legacy ~€85–90m on lottery Q4 seasonality and a steady Bilyoner (estimate). **AEBITDA:** we sit at ~€375–385m (~$411–422m), at or just below the bottom of the new range — BII ~€265–272m (Q4 €70–76m, assuming the YoY duty offset improves to ~65% against a derived Q4-25 base of ~€70–78m) and legacy ~€108–112m (Q4 €30–34m). Hitting the midpoint requires BII to offset >100% of the Q4 duty hit; we do not underwrite that after 42%/55% in Q2/Q3. **Margin:** FY26 ~33–34%; Q4 ~35–36% on seasonality and low marketing. **Leverage:** year-end adjusted net debt ~€1.60–1.65bn (Q4 FCF seasonally positive, no further licence payments disclosed, TRS purchases ongoing) gives standalone PF leverage of ~4.2–4.4x; the real number is post-evoke, where evoke's own debt (EV c.€1.6bn per Edison) and ~18% EBITDA margin dilute group margin toward the high-20s before synergies — the first synergy quantification in Q1-27 is the single most important disclosure ahead. **2027 shape (estimate):** UK duty stops being a YoY headwind after Q1-27; UK revenue +8–10% with marketing held; remote betting duty step-up in April 2027 is a modest headwind for BII's casino-led book but a real one for William Hill; legacy flat-ish as Arkansas/New Hampshire go live and jackpots mean-revert. Swing factors: Q4 UK NGR and marketing, US jackpot runs, TRY, the Gibraltar court timetable and any CMA/licence surprise, and refinancing of the €130m 6.00% retail bond due 2029 within a 4x+ structure.

**What to watch next quarter:**

- Q4-26 BII AEBITDA ≥ €74m (bottom of guidance) and the YoY duty-offset ratio moving from ~55% toward ≥65% — the acid test of both the consolidation thesis and guidance credibility; also whether management keeps the YoY measurement basis.
- evoke closing mechanics: remaining gaming approvals and Gibraltar court sanction by end-December, January 2027 completion, then the first quantified cost-synergy target and pro forma combined leverage (we expect well above 4x at close).
- US lottery: jackpot cycle through the holiday season, Arkansas and New Hampshire go-lives in 1H-27, the Maryland RFP outcome and litigation, and the undisclosed Texas RFP result; any sign of renewal-driven margin dilution in legacy B2B.
- UK regulatory second leg: the April 2027 remote betting duty increase (Nov-25 Budget) and any further affordability/stake-limit moves; evidence (or not) of sub-scale operator exits that management's thesis depends on.
- FX translation: TRY (−18.2% avg in 9M) on Bilyoner, GBP on both BII earnings and the £557m of sterling term debt (€7.5m adverse non-cash FX in 9M), USD on US lottery.
- Capital allocation and governance: TRS share purchases via Deutsche Bank and dividends while PF leverage is 4.33x and rising; Fitch Negative outlook (per secondary sources); the €130m retail bond 2029 and RCF covenant headroom; Bally's Corp control and related-party flows.
- Disclosure gaps that would change the analysis: actives/ARPU/product split for BII, legacy B2B quarterly EBITDA (the Q3 −€4.3m QoQ drop was unexplained), and detail on Bilyoner's amended remuneration structure.
