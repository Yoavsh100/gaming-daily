** · Reporting currency: USD (tables in millions unless stated) · Results released 22 Oct 2026 (after US close); call 5pm ET same day**
**Market reaction:** Shares closed $72.27 on 22 Oct (+2.4% ahead of the after-close print), fell ~2.9% in after-hours trade to ~$70.19, opened 23 Oct at $69.51 (-3.8%, intraday low $69.31) and recovered to close at $71.69 (-0.8%) on ~1.9m shares (c.2x normal volume). Stock -15% YTD, 52-week range $65.00-91.41, market cap ~$5.1-5.2bn; Stifel cut its target to $80 from $92 (Hold) on 23 Oct, two weeks after Deutsche Bank's upgrade to Buy ($99).

> A "down 1%, up 2% underlying" quarter: revenue $990.5m (-1.4% YoY, ~1.1-1.3% below the ~$1.0bn consensus) and adj. EBITDAR $319.6m (-0.7%) were held up by Midwest & South (+2.5% adjusted), Online (+16%) and Managed (+13%), while the Las Vegas destination customer deteriorated further at the Orleans and Downtown (LV Locals EBITDAR -5%, Downtown -20%). Capital return of ~$180m and a 9.4% lower share count are doing the EPS work (adj. ## 1. Executive Summary

- **Headline miss on revenue, in-line-to-slight-beat on profit.** Q3-26 revenue $990.5m (-1.4% YoY) vs ~$1.0bn consensus (Zacks: -1.3% surprise); adj. EBITDAR $319.6m (-0.7% YoY) vs $321.8m; adj. EBITDA $290.6m vs ~$287m StockStory consensus; adj. EPS $1.67 vs a $1.62-1.70 consensus range (Zacks $1.65). GAAP net income $124.9m / $1.71 vs $1.44bn / $17.81 a year ago, which included the $1.4bn after-tax gain on the FanDuel stake sale and $65.1m of impairments.
- **Management's preferred framing is '+2% revenue / +1% EBITDAR' after stripping four items:** the Nov-2025 closure of Sam's Town Tunica, the 1-Aug-2026 sale of Sam's Town Shreveport to Bally's ($30m), a property-tax step-up at Kansas Star, and lower pass-through tax reimbursements in Online. We accept the first two as portfolio actions; the Kansas Star tax is a permanent cost and should not be adjusted out.
- **The quarter's problem is one customer in one city.** Las Vegas Locals revenue -1.6% to $207.4m and adj. EBITDAR -5.1% to $87.4m (consensus $211.2m / $90.2m), margin 42.1% (-160bps YoY). Ex-Orleans and Suncoast the segment grew revenue and EBITDAR 3% (gaming +3.5%) at a 47% margin; the drag is 'destination' (non-local) business at the Orleans, which management says intensified in Q3 versus the ~$5m/qtr EBITDAR headwind disclosed in Q2, plus Suncoast construction.
- **Downtown Las Vegas fell hardest:** revenue -10.9% to $47.4m, adj. EBITDAR -20.3% to $12.9m (consensus ~$14.3m), margin 27.1% (-320bps). Causes cited: destination softness, four storms hitting Hawaii in the last six weeks of the quarter, and a spike in Hawaiian airfares well above the ~20% national rise, now moderating. 9M Downtown EBITDAR -13.8%.
- **Midwest & South remains the ballast:** revenue +0.3% to $540.5m, adj. EBITDAR flat at $201.6m (slight beat vs $200.0m), 37.3% margin. Adjusted for Tunica/Shreveport/Kansas Star tax, revenue +~3%, gaming revenue +>2%, EBITDAR +~$5m (+2.5%) at a 38% adjusted margin; 9M EBITDAR +2.9%. Management guided 2027 growth 'largely consistent' with 2026.
- **Online and Managed both beat and guidance was steered to the top of the ranges.** Online adj. EBITDAR +15.9% to $10.9m (consensus $9.1m) on net online revenue +23.6% to $33.2m; Managed & Other +13.3% to $29.8m (consensus $28.1m) on Sky River management fees +16.4%. FY26 ranges unchanged at $35-40m (Online) and $113-117m (Managed), now 'high end' — implying ~$10m and ~$28m in Q4.
- **Consumer read-through is two-speed:** core and retail customers growing, ADT/spend up mid-single digits, unrated play strong across Las Vegas and the regionals; softness confined to the low end of the database (gas prices/inflation). August was 'very soft' portfolio-wide, July good, September 'more normal' — the Q3 weakness is not purely a Las Vegas story.
- **Operating cost control is the quiet positive.** Total opex -8.0% YoY (impairment lapping), but SG&A only +1.3% to $105.4m, corporate expense -4.0% to $29.4m (net corporate -$22.9m vs -$24.3m consensus), maintenance & utilities +1.5%. GAAP operating income +39% to $194.9m on the absence of last year's $65.1m impairment and a $24.2m gain on asset sales booked in 'project development, preopening and writedowns' (a $5.4m net credit).
- **Below the line, D&A is the structural EPS headwind:** $91.3m vs $73.7m (+24% YoY; 9M +31%) as the capex programme (Cadence Crossing, Treasure Chest, Suncoast, hotel remodels) is capitalised. Adjusted earnings fell 12.6% to $121.7m while adj. EPS fell only 2.9% thanks to a 9.7% lower diluted share count (73.0m vs 80.9m).
- **Capital return is on autopilot:** $164m of buybacks (~2.0m shares) plus $15m of dividends ($0.20/share paid 15 Oct) in Q3; ~$475m repurchased YTD; $387m left on the authorisation; target remains $150m/qtr, putting FY26 total return at >$680m (~$9.80/share on 70.7m shares). Share count 71.2m at quarter-end, 70.67m at 23 Oct.
- **Balance sheet is loosening, deliberately.** Cash $297.2m, total debt ~$2.7bn ($1.0bn 4.75% notes due Dec-2027, $900m 4.75% notes due 2031, ~$800.9m of credit facility), net debt ~$2.4bn. Company-defined leverage 2.2x traditional / 2.7x lease-adjusted vs 1.8x / 2.4x at Q1-26. The entire credit facility ($800.9m) is classified as current because of a 1-Sep-2027 springing maturity tied to the 2027 notes; management will refinance 'later this year or 1H27'.
- **Cash flow optics are distorted by the FanDuel tax bill.** 9M operating cash flow $313.4m vs $701.4m, after $364.2m of payments for transferable federal energy tax credits used to settle federal tax on the FanDuel sale ($73.8m in Q1, the balance in Q2). Underlying 9M OCF is ~$678m (our arithmetic) against 9M capex of $456.0m.
- **Capex guidance trimmed to ~$650m from $650-700m:** $250m maintenance, $75m Orleans hotel remodel (1,800 rooms, done November), $50m growth (Cadence Crossing completion, Par-A-Dice design) and $275m for the Virginia resort (was $300m). Q3 capex $159m; implied Q4 ~$194m.
- **Growth pipeline is land-based and 2027-28 weighted:** Norfolk, VA ($750m JV with the Pamunkey Tribe; 65k sq ft casino, 200 rooms) on time/budget for Nov-2027 opening, topping-out by year-end; Sky River phase 2 (300-room hotel, spa, event centre) 2028; Par-A-Dice (IL) land-based rebuild construction late 2027; Amelia Belle (LA) land-based conversion construction late 2027 subject to approvals; Orleans property-wide renovation starts early 2027 in back-of-house areas.
- **Las Vegas outlook is 'less bad', not 'better':** Q4 destination trends guided to revert to the pre-Q3 run-rate (i.e. still negative), ~350 Orleans rooms back for weekends/NFR, Suncoast a 'very modest' Q4 contributor with 400+ rooms being remodelled through Q1-27, Cadence Crossing at full run-rate by Q1-27 (it contributed ~a third of the 3% ex-Orleans/Suncoast growth). 2027: 'modest growth' in LV Locals EBITDAR; ~50% margins remain the frame for the non-destination book.
- **M&A posture unchanged but appetite 'as big as ever':** right asset, right price, stable tax/regulatory jurisdiction; management dismissed rumours, said compressed sector multiples neither raise nor lower appetite, and that low leverage exists precisely so buybacks and capex need not be sacrificed if a deal appears. Online M&A limited to 'bite-size' capability deals; supportive of iGaming legalisation (Virginia relevant given Norfolk). Pennsylvania skill-game removal should help Valley Forge (unquantified).
- **Market reaction was muted but negative:** shares closed $72.27 on 22 Oct (+2.4% pre-print), fell ~2.9% after hours to ~$70.19, opened 23 Oct at $69.51 (-3.8%) and recovered to close $71.69 (-0.8%). Stock -15% YTD, 52-week range $65.00-91.41, market cap ~$5.1-5.2bn. Stifel cut its target to $80 from $92 (Hold) on 23 Oct.
- **Thesis read-through:** the diversified, low-promo, locals/regional model is intact and the non-Las Vegas 85% of EBITDAR is growing low-single digits with margins held; but the Orleans/Downtown destination exposure (~15% of EBITDAR) has now been a negative for four-plus quarters and worsened in Q3, and the EPS algorithm is increasingly buyback-dependent while leverage drifts up into a 2027 refinancing at a higher coupon. We see FY26 adj. EBITDAR around $1.31-1.32bn (9M $987.5m + Q4E ~$325-335m) — flat-to-down ~2-3% on a reported basis.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — Las Vegas Locals

*Two businesses in one segment: the locals book is growing 3% at 47% margins, but the Orleans' destination customer and Suncoast construction pulled EBITDAR down 5% YoY and 18% QoQ.*

### Headline results

**Revenue fell 1.6% YoY to $207.4m and adjusted EBITDAR dropped 5.1% to $87.4m, missing consensus on both lines.**

- Revenue -8.2% QoQ on normal summer seasonality; margin 42.1%, down ~160bps YoY and ~500bps from Q2's 47.1%.
- Missed three-analyst consensus of $211.2m revenue / $90.2m EBITDAR by 1.8% and 3.1% respectively.
- 9M EBITDAR down 5.7% to $293.8m.
- Ex-Orleans/Suncoast, revenue and EBITDAR grew 3%, gaming revenue up 3.5%, margins 47%, same cadence as Q2 (+4% revenue) and prior quarters.
- Cadence Crossing (replaced Jokers Wild in Henderson) was roughly a third of that 3%, so same-store locals growth is nearer 2%.

### Destination drag

**The Orleans destination softness intensified in Q3 and management declined to quantify it, the number the market wanted and did not get.**

- In Q2 the CFO sized the drag at ~$5m EBITDAR per quarter, 'very consistent since Q3 of last year'.
- July fine, August weaker than anticipated, September better than August but not enough to offset; Q3 seasonality amplified the shortfall.
- Suncoast renovation disruption, ~$3m in Q2, was 'a little less than expected' in Q3.
- No new promotional shock from the Strip or Palms/neighbouring locals; Strip room rates aggressive for over two months.
- Boyd is not discounting Orleans rooms to chase unprofitable volume; we see this as right long term but revenue will look worse than discounting peers.

> “very consistent since Q3 of last year” — CFO

### Locals fundamentals

**The locals case remains intact: core and retail segments grew, unrated play was strong, and weakness is confined to the low end.**

- ADT/spend from core customers rose mid-single digits or better.
- Southern Nevada population above 2.4m growing at twice the national rate; second-highest job growth among the 30 largest metros; wage growth near 6%.
- Ex-Orleans/Suncoast 47% summer margins match Q3 of the last two years; other quarters 'hovering around 50%'.
- The 50% margin frame offered for 2027 is a non-summer number, not a step-up.

### Outlook and sequencing

**Outlook is sequenced rather than guided, with 2027 LV Locals EBITDAR 'modest growth' dependent on destination trends holding at pre-Q3 levels.**

- 1,800-room Orleans remodel completes in November, returning ~300-350 rooms out of service daily for a year; useful for weekends, holidays and National Finals Rodeo.
- Suncoast's 400+ rooms remodelled through Q1-27; Q4 a 'very modest' contribution, ramp in Q1-Q2 2027; Cadence Crossing full run-rate around Q1-27.
- Orleans property-wide renovation, a 30-year 'major maintenance upgrade', starts early 2027 in non-guest areas; no disruption guided for 2027.
- Q4 destination trends guided 'similar to what we saw before Q3' — not flat, not growing, not less bad.
- Management declined to break out the destination book; given four consecutive calls of focus, we think disclosure would help the multiple.

> “similar to what we saw before Q3” — CFO

## 4. Segment deep-dive — Downtown Las Vegas

*Hawaiian storms and airfares on top of market-wide destination softness: EBITDAR -20% YoY, the third straight quarter of double-digit declines.*

### Headline results

**EBITDAR fell 20.3% to $12.9m, the third straight quarter of double-digit declines, missing ~$14.3m consensus.**

- Revenue -10.9% YoY to $47.4m, -9.0% QoQ; margin 27.1%, down ~320bps YoY and ~530bps QoQ.
- 9M EBITDAR down 13.8% to $48.7m on revenue -6.8%; Q3 accelerated an existing trend.
- Segment is only ~4% of group EBITDAR, but the margin decrement at this revenue scale is notable.

### Drivers

**Three overlapping drivers management could not disaggregate: destination softness, four Hawaiian storms and a sharp Hawaiian airfare spike.**

- Same Southern Nevada destination softness affecting the Orleans.
- Four storms hit the Hawaiian Islands in the final six weeks of the quarter, described as unprecedented in that window.
- Hawaiian airfares spiked well above the ~20% national rise in 2H of the quarter; moderating but still elevated.
- California, Fremont and Main Street Station are disproportionately exposed to Hawaiian visitation via the Vacations Hawaii charter programme, a known concentration risk.

### Outlook

**Weather and airfare should mean-revert into the holidays, but the structural destination softness will not be fixed by the calendar.**

- Q2 commentary had core and Hawaiian play 'consistent with recent quarters', softness confined to the broader downtown destination customer.
- We would not model a return to 30%+ margins of 2024-25 until destination visitation stabilises.
- Near-term question is whether Q4 EBITDAR recovers to the mid-teens.

## 5. Segment deep-dive — Midwest & South

*Flat reported, +2.5% adjusted EBITDAR at a 38% underlying margin; the regional portfolio is doing exactly what the model says it should, with 2027 guided to look like 2026.*

### Headline results

**Revenue rose 0.3% to $540.5m and EBITDAR was flat at $201.6m, a modest beat against ~$200.0m consensus at a steady 37.3% margin.**

- Revenue slightly below the $543.7m estimate; margin -10bps YoY, -20bps QoQ.
- Reported figures absorb Sam's Town Tunica closure (November 2025), Sam's Town Shreveport sale to Bally's for $30m on 1 August, and Kansas Star property tax step-up.
- Adjusted: revenue up nearly 3%, gaming revenue over 2%, EBITDAR up ~$5m or ~2.5%, 38% margin consistent with Q3 of recent years.
- 9M revenue +2.5%, EBITDAR +2.9% at 37.2% margin; the only property segment growing year-to-date.

### Demand drivers

**Growth came from core and retail guests, unrated play and capital returns, though August was 'a very soft month' and low-end customers show inflation sensitivity.**

- Returns on Treasure Chest's land-based move, the Ameristar St. Charles hotel and floor refreshes.
- July good, August very soft, September 'more normal'; no distinction between South, East Coast and Midwest consumer behaviour.
- Some 2026 strength attributed to consumers staying closer to home and earlier tax-bill refunds, an honest acknowledgement of a tailwind.

> “a very soft month” — Management

### Development pipeline

**The pipeline is entirely land-based and back-end loaded, led by the $750m Norfolk, Virginia resort opening November 2027.**

- Norfolk with the Pamunkey Indian Tribe: 65k sq ft casino, 200 rooms, eight F&B outlets, serving 1.8m Hampton Roads residents; on time and budget, topping-out by year-end.
- Par-A-Dice moves from riverboat to all-new single-level facility, construction starting late 2027; Amelia Belle land-based conversion, construction late 2027 subject to approvals.
- Pennsylvania's removal of unregulated skill games should lift Valley Forge; nothing to report yet.
- Lattner will look at Chicago-area VGT licences but is explicitly not a needle-mover.

### Guidance

**2027 growth guided 'largely consistent' with 2026, i.e. low-single-digit same-store EBITDAR growth; we model Q4-26 reported EBITDAR at $195-200m.**

- No sign in the databases that the trend is turning.
- Shreveport's absence subtracts from reported revenue and EBITDAR through Q2-27; Kansas Star tax step-up is permanent.
- Virginia preopening costs build through 2027, reported below EBITDAR.
- Q4-26 estimate of $195-200m allows for Shreveport and normal Q4 seasonality.

## 6. Segment deep-dive — Online (Boyd Interactive + market access)

*First clean post-FanDuel comparison: net online revenue +24%, EBITDAR +16% to $10.9m, FY26 guidance steered to the top of $35-40m.*

### Headline results

**First clean post-FanDuel comparison: net online revenue rose 23.6% to $33.2m and EBITDAR grew 15.9% to $10.9m versus $9.1m consensus.**

- Reported revenue -6.7% to $154.5m, but $121.2m is zero-margin pass-through reimbursements (-12.6%) and should be ignored.
- Net revenue +4.4% QoQ; ~33% margin on net revenue versus 35% a year ago as mix shifts to Boyd Interactive B2C/B2B.
- 9M EBITDAR down 45.7% to $29.9m because the 1H25 base carried old FanDuel market-access economics before the July-2025 sale of the 5% stake for $1.76bn.

### Boyd Interactive

**Stardust (New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Ontario) plus B2B delivered 'double-digit revenue and EBITDAR growth' again, driven by online casino.**

- Business described as very efficient with comfortable customer-acquisition costs and no need to 'juice' marketing, unlike peers' spend-led strategies.
- FY26 EBITDAR guidance of $35-40m (raised $5m at Q2) unchanged but 'directed toward the high end'.
- With 9M at $29.9m, that implies ~$10m in Q4, roughly flat sequentially.

> “double-digit revenue and EBITDAR growth” — Management

### Strategy

**A small (~3% of group EBITDAR) but genuinely growing, capital-light line; management supports regulated iGaming anywhere in the US.**

- Virginia is the obvious live question given Norfolk; brand (Stardust or others) decided case by case.
- Online M&A limited to 'bite-size' capability deals on the same return hurdles as land-based.

## 8. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- **Core customer intact across the portfolio (structural):** core and retail play grew, unrated play strong, ADT up mid-single digits; LV Locals ex-Orleans/Suncoast +3% revenue and EBITDAR (gaming +3.5%) at 47% margins; Midwest & South +~3% revenue / +2.5% EBITDAR adjusted at a 38% margin, 9M segment EBITDAR +2.9%. _(structural)_
- **Online and Managed both beat and were steered to the top of guidance:** Online EBITDAR +15.9% to $10.9m (vs $9.1m consensus) on net online revenue +23.6%; Managed & Other +13.3% to $29.8m (vs $28.1m) on Sky River fees +16.4%. Combined ~$40m/qtr of capital-light EBITDAR growing double digits. _(structural)_
- **Cost discipline held in a soft quarter:** SG&A +1.3%, corporate expense -4.0% (net corporate -$22.9m vs -$24.3m consensus), M&S margin flat at 37.3%, group adj. EBITDAR margin +20bps YoY to 32.3% despite the revenue miss. Management: 'nothing special… day-to-day combat' on opex. _(structural)_
- **Capital return and share count:** ~$180m returned in Q3 ($164m buybacks, $15m dividends), ~$475m repurchased YTD, FY26 total >$680m (~$9.80/share); diluted share count -9.7% YoY, period-end 71.2m (-9.4%). $387m authorisation remaining; $150m/qtr target reiterated with history of leaning in on dislocation. _(structural)_
- **Capex guide trimmed to ~$650m from $650-700m** (Virginia $275m vs $300m), with Norfolk on time/budget for Nov-2027 and no disruption guided at the Orleans in 2027 as the renovation starts in back-of-house areas. _(one-off)_
- **Sequencing of Las Vegas self-help is visible:** ~1,800 Orleans rooms back in November after 300+ out daily for a year, Suncoast public areas complete (guest response 'enthusiastic'), Cadence Crossing ahead of revenue expectations and at full run-rate by Q1-27, Pennsylvania skill-game removal a free option for Valley Forge. _(early)_
- **GAAP optics improve:** operating income +39% to $194.9m (lapping $65.1m impairment; $24.2m asset-sale gain), 9M interest expense -29% to $93.7m after FanDuel-funded debt paydown. _(one-off)_

**Lowlights**

- **Las Vegas destination softness worsened, not eased:** LV Locals EBITDAR -5.1% YoY / -17.9% QoQ to $87.4m (3.1% below consensus), margin 42.1% (-160bps YoY); the ~$5m/qtr headwind disclosed at Q2 'intensified' in Q3 and was not quantified. Same narrative for 'three or four quarters'; Q4 guided only to revert to the prior (negative) trend. _(structural)_
- **Downtown Las Vegas EBITDAR -20.3% to $12.9m, margin 27.1% (-320bps):** storms and Hawaiian airfares are one-off, but destination softness is the same market-wide problem and 9M segment EBITDAR is -13.8%. _(mixed)_
- **Revenue missed (~1.1-1.3% below ~$1.0bn) and adjusted earnings fell 12.6% to $121.7m;** adj. EPS -2.9% to $1.67 only because the share count is 9.7% lower. Group adj. EBITDAR is -2.9% for the nine months with margin -90bps; D&A +24% YoY is a growing structural drag on EPS as capex is capitalised. _(structural)_
- **Leverage rising into a refinancing:** traditional leverage 2.2x (1.8x at Q1-26), lease-adjusted 2.7x (2.4x); net debt ~$2.4bn; $800.9m of credit-facility borrowings classified current because of a 1-Sep-2027 springing maturity tied to the $1.0bn 4.75% notes due Dec-2027. Refinancing at today's coupons is likely a ~$12-18m p.a. interest headwind (our estimate). _(watch)_
- **Cash generation this year is thin on a reported basis:** 9M operating cash flow $313.4m (vs $701.4m) after $364.2m of energy-tax-credit payments for the FanDuel tax bill, against $456m capex and ~$475m buybacks; cash fell to $297.2m. Underlying OCF (~$678m) is fine, but FY26 free cash flow after $650m capex does not cover the $680m+ capital-return programme. _(one-off)_
- **August was 'very soft' everywhere, including Midwest & South,** and low-end database customers are showing inflation/gas-price sensitivity; management also credited part of 2026 regional strength to stay-close-to-home behaviour and tax refunds — tailwinds that may not repeat. _(watch)_
- **Online reported revenue -6.7% and 9M Online EBITDAR -45.7%** as FanDuel market-access economics roll off; the segment is small (~3% of EBITDAR) and the headline line will stay noisy because of zero-margin pass-through reimbursements. _(mixed)_

## 9. Forecast & Outlook

Boyd does not issue group guidance, so the 'bridge' is segment-level and capital-allocation guidance. Nothing was cut: Online FY26 adj. EBITDAR stays $35-40m and Managed & Other $113-117m, but both are now explicitly 'high end' after Q3 beats (implying ~$10m and ~$28m in Q4). The one numerical change is capex, trimmed to ~$650m from $650-700m (Virginia $275m vs $300m; $250m maintenance; $75m Orleans rooms; $50m growth), implying ~$194m in Q4. Buybacks stay at $150m/qtr. For the property segments the 'guidance' is qualitative: Q4 Las Vegas destination trends similar to pre-Q3 (still negative), Suncoast a 'very modest' Q4 contributor ramping in Q1-Q2 2027, Cadence Crossing at full run-rate by Q1-27, LV Locals 'modest' EBITDAR growth in 2027 with ~50% non-summer margins ex the destination assets, and Midwest & South 2027 growth 'largely consistent' with 2026. The division that moved the needle this quarter was Las Vegas Locals (Orleans destination + Suncoast), with Downtown adding to the miss; Online, Managed and corporate costs offset about half of it.

- **Online FY26 adj. EBITDAR:** $35-40m → 'high end' (~$40m) (range unchanged; 9M $29.9m, Q4 implied ~$10m)
- **Managed & Other FY26 adj. EBITDAR:** $113-117m → 'high end' (~$117m) (range unchanged; 9M $88.9m, Q4 implied ~$28m)
- **FY26 capital expenditure:** $650-700m → ~$650m (-$25m at midpoint; Virginia $300m → $275m; Q4 implied ~$194m)
- **Capital return / balance sheet:** $150m/qtr buybacks + $0.20 dividend; >$680m FY26 (unchanged; leverage 2.2x/2.7x; refinance Dec-27 notes late-26/1H27)

**Q4-26E (our estimates):** revenue ~$0.99-1.01bn (flat to -1% YoY as Shreveport/Tunica and lower pass-through reimbursements offset Cadence and Orleans rooms), adj. EBITDAR ~$325-335m built as LV Locals ~$98-102m (normal mid/high-teens sequential uplift off a depressed Q3, Suncoast minimal), Downtown ~$15-16m, Midwest & South ~$195-200m, Online ~$10m, Managed ~$28m, corporate ~-$24m. That puts **FY26E adj. EBITDAR at ~$1.31-1.32bn** (9M actual $987.5m + Q4E), down ~2-3% reported. Q4 adj. EPS ~$1.75-1.85 (EBITDAR less ~$29m rent, ~$92m D&A, ~$33m net interest, ~$9m SBC, ~24% tax, ~70.6m shares) → **FY26E adj. EPS ~$6.95-7.05**. **Margin:** group adj. EBITDAR margin ~32.5-33% for FY26, roughly 90bps below FY25's nine-month level; property margins hold (M&S ~37-38%, LV Locals back toward mid-40s in Q4) and the drag is mix (Downtown, Orleans) rather than cost creep. **Leverage direction is up:** Q4 OCF normalises (~$230-250m with no tax-credit payments) but ~$194m capex plus ~$165m of buybacks/dividends pushes net debt toward ~$2.5bn and traditional leverage to ~2.3x at year-end; through 2027, with Virginia's remaining spend, the Orleans renovation, Par-A-Dice/Amelia Belle pre-construction and $600m+ of annual returns, we expect 2.4-2.5x (lease-adjusted ~3.0x) before Norfolk contributes from 2028 — still comfortable, but the 'low-leverage optionality' argument is being spent. The $1.0bn 4.75% 2027 notes must be refinanced (or taken out with the facility) before the 1-Sep-2027 springing maturity; at a ~6-6.5% coupon that is a ~$12-18m annual EPS headwind (~$0.13-0.19/share). **2027 shape:** M&S low-single-digit growth, LV Locals modest growth contingent on destination stabilising, Online ~$45-50m and Managed ~$120m+ (our estimates), Downtown flat at best, Virginia preopening costs below EBITDAR in 2H27. **Swing factors:** the Orleans destination customer (every $1m/qtr of headwind is ~0.3% of group EBITDAR), Hawaiian airfares, the August-type consumer air-pocket at the low end, the refinancing coupon, and whether management leans harder into buybacks at ~$70 — history says they do.

**What to watch next quarter:**

- Q4 Las Vegas Locals EBITDAR: does the Orleans destination drag revert to the ~$5m/qtr run-rate, and does the segment print ≥$98m with the 1,800 Orleans rooms back for NFR/holidays?
- Suncoast ramp: ~$3m/qtr of disruption to recover from Q1-27, with 400+ rooms out until then; LV Locals 2027 'modest growth' and ~50% non-summer margin claims.
- Refinancing of the $1.0bn 4.75% notes due Dec-2027 (springing facility maturity 1-Sep-2027): timing, coupon, and whether the $800.9m current classification is cleared by year-end.
- Leverage path vs the $150m/qtr buyback: 2.2x→~2.3x at YE26 and 2.4-2.5x through 2027 on our numbers; any M&A ('appetite as big as ever') would stack on top.
- Downtown: Hawaiian airfare normalisation and storm recovery; whether Q4 EBITDAR recovers to the mid-teens or the destination component keeps margins below 30%.
- Consumer: repeat of the August air-pocket, low-end database softness, and whether the 2026 stay-close-to-home/tax-refund tailwinds in Midwest & South fade in 2027.
- Online: Q4 ~$10m delivery; Virginia iGaming legislation; any 'bite-size' Boyd Interactive M&A; continued decline in pass-through reimbursements distorting the reported line.
- Pipeline execution: Norfolk topping-out by YE26 and Nov-2027 opening; Sky River phase 2 (2028); Par-A-Dice and Amelia Belle construction starts late 2027 and their capex envelopes; Pennsylvania skill-game removal uplift at Valley Forge.
