# DoubleDown Interactive (DDI) — Q3 2026 Analyst Report
** · Reporting currency: USD (company reports in US$; tables in US$ millions unless stated) · Results released 11 Nov 2026 (6-K filed 11 Nov; 6-K/A 12 Nov)**
**Market reaction:** Shares were little changed on the day (Investing.com described regular-session trading as "little changed"); the stock is pinned to the $16.25 tender price, with the Motley Fool quote page showing DDI at $15.70 (-0.1%), ~3.5% below the offer, in the days after the print. Exact 11/12 Nov closing levels could not be verified from a primary source. Roth/MKM downgraded on 12 Nov citing the likely tender.

> Operationally clean: a record 58% direct-to-consumer mix lifted adj. EBITDA 7.2% to $40.2m (42.7% margin, +360bps) on revenue down 1.6% to $94.3m, while reported profit fell 44% on an $8.2m net FX loss and a 37% quarterly tax rate. The number that actually matters is DoubleU Games' $16.25/ADS tender, which values the ex-cash business at roughly 1.7x annualised adj. EBITDA; this quarter is now a datapoint in that negotiation rather than a trading event.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Beat on revenue and adj. EBITDA, miss on EPS.** Q3-26 revenue of $94.3m (-1.6% YoY, flat QoQ) came in ahead of the ~$92.6m consensus cited by Investing.com, and management explicitly claims a beat on revenue and adj. EBITDA ($40.2m, +7.2%). Per-ADS EPS of $0.37 was well short of the ~$0.59 forecast, entirely because of below-the-line FX losses and a one-quarter tax-rate spike; operating profit actually rose 7.2% to $37.5m.
- **Mix: social casino shrank, iGaming grew.** Social casino/free-to-play revenue fell 3.5% YoY to $76.8m (-0.6% QoQ), attributed to lower revenue from the legacy DoubleDown titles ex-WHOW; SuprNation iGaming rose 7.4% to $17.4m (+2.6% QoQ), led by the Los Vegas brand. 9M revenue is +7.0% to $282.7m, but that is almost entirely WHOW Games (acquired 14 Jul 2025) annualising.
- **DTC is the whole margin story.** Direct-to-consumer revenue hit $44.6m (58.0% of social casino, from 20.9% in Q3-25 and 52.4% in Q2-26) while third-party platform revenue nearly halved to $32.2m (-48.8% YoY). Cost of revenue dropped 20.5% to $22.2m, or 23.5% of revenue (-560bps YoY), despite higher UK gaming duty in iGaming. CFO Sigrist: DTC was below 5% at the start of 2024; gains from here get harder and he would not give a ceiling.
- **Margin expansion is real and still compounding.** Adj. EBITDA margin of 42.7% is +360bps YoY and +110bps QoQ; operating margin 39.8% (+330bps). 9M adj. EBITDA is $117.7m (+15.6%) at 41.6%. On our derivation (group adj. EBITDA less the iGaming segment's PBT+D&A), social casino runs at ~$40.1m / ~52% margin, up ~290bps YoY.
- **Below the line swamped the operating beat.** Net finance result swung to -$8.2m (finance cost $10.0m vs a $1.6m credit a year ago; finance income $1.8m vs $4.5m), a $14.3m YoY swing, driven by realised and unrealised FX losses on USD-denominated financial assets re-measured at the KRW-functional parent. A $7.8m translation gain in OCI partly offsets economically. Q3 ETR was 37.3% (vs 20.3%) under the annual-ETR method; the 9M rate of 25.4% is the better guide. Attributable profit fell 43.9% to $18.4m.
- **Core decay is tracking the market, not beating it.** US social casino revenue (the cleanest proxy for legacy DoubleDown Casino) fell 6.5% YoY to $57.0m and 2.5% QoQ; 9M -5.2%. The CEO cited Eilers & Krejcik's forecast of a ~7% global social casino decline in 2026 and claimed outperformance, but the outperformance lives in International (+6.4% YoY to $19.8m, WHOW-driven), not in the core.
- **Customer economics reshaped by WHOW, flat underneath.** Avg MAU 1.23m (+10% YoY incl. WHOW, -1.6% QoQ), DAU 575k (+3.6% YoY, -3.7% QoQ), payer conversion 9.5% (7.8%), ARPDAU $1.44 ($1.39), avg monthly revenue per payer $216 ($272). Implied monthly payers (MAU x conversion) are ~117k, essentially flat on Q2-26's ~118k; DAU/MAU engagement slipped to 46.7% from 49.6%. Note Q3-25 KPIs exclude WHOW, so the YoY moves are mostly perimeter.
- **Marketing re-allocated, not cut.** Group S&M was $15.1m (16.0% of revenue vs 16.4%; +$1.2m QoQ). Within that, social casino advertising rose 26% YoY to $8.5m (11.0% of segment revenue, +260bps) to push DTC promotion, while iGaming advertising was slashed 41% to $3.9m (22.3% of segment revenue vs 40.6%) to absorb the UK duty increase.
- **iGaming reached breakeven by cutting UA.** SuprNation segment PBT narrowed to -$0.8m (from -$2.6m); PBT + D&A is ~+$0.1m, the first positive quarter on our derivation (9M: +$0.7m vs -$3.2m). Management says product changes, marketing adjustments and expense control mitigated 'much' of the higher UK gambling tax; brands five and six are planned to diversify. A 41% UA cut buys this quarter's P&L at the expense of future cohorts.
- **Cost lines to watch: G&A up, R&D down.** G&A rose 26.5% YoY to $16.5m (17.5% of revenue vs 13.6%); the release does not itemise it, but special-committee/advisory costs tied to the DUG process and the full WHOW cost base are the obvious candidates. R&D fell 27% to $3.1m (3.2% of revenue), the lowest in recent quarters, for a business that says it ships new slot content weekly.
- **Cash generation fine, timing noisy.** OCF of $23.6m was -29% YoY purely on cash taxes ($17.2m paid in Q3-26 vs $7.2m); 9M OCF $94.7m (+0.5%) and derived FCF (OCF less $0.2m capex and $1.4m leases) of $93.0m is 79% of adj. EBITDA. No buyback, no dividend, no M&A cash out in 2026.
- **Balance sheet: $582.1m cash + short-term investments, $36.8m related-party loan, net cash $545.3m** (+$24m vs $521m at Q2; +$90m vs $455m at Dec-25), i.e. ~$11.00 per ADS. The 4.60% DoubleU Games loan was extended from May 2026 to May 2028 and reclassified to long-term. Gross debt is ~0.2x annualised adj. EBITDA.
- **The tender offer: $16.25/ADS ($325/common share) in cash from DoubleU Games (67.05% owner)**, agreed 9 Nov and commenced 10 Nov, replacing the 29 Apr non-binding $11.25 proposal (+44%). Conditions: minimum tender taking DUG to 95% (reducible to 90% with DDI consent), a non-waivable majority-of-the-minority (>50% of unaffiliated ADSs), and an unrevoked board recommendation; no financing condition; second-step squeeze-out under the Korean Commercial Act at the same price. Kroll (Duff & Phelps) gave the fairness opinion.
- **Tender valuation arithmetic (ours).** 49.55m ADS-equivalents x $16.25 = ~$805m equity value; less $545m net cash = ~$260m EV, or ~1.7x 9M-annualised adj. EBITDA (~$157m) and ~2.1x annualised FCF (~$124m). Minority cash-out is ~$265m. Net cash alone is ~$11/ADS, so the offer ascribes ~$5.25/ADS to a business producing ~$2.50/ADS of FCF a year. The special committee extracted a 44% bump; the majority-of-the-minority condition is the minorities' only remaining lever.
- **Governance read: management shut the door.** The company refused all questions on the offer on the call, and the agreement imposes 'certain constraints' on ordinary-course operations even as the CEO repeated that M&A in casual gaming, online gambling and mobile entertainment 'remains a strategic priority'. Roth/MKM downgraded on 12 Nov citing the likely tender; MarketBeat shows a consensus 'Hold'.
- **Product and AI: lots of narrative, no quantification.** Weekly slot launches, a new link-jackpot system and a simplified mobile lobby in development; AI framed around three pillars (content velocity, monetisation via behavioural predictive models tuning in-game economies and offers, UA targeting/ROAS). Asked for a measured ARPDAU lift from AI, the CEO gave none.
- **No guidance, none expected.** DDI does not issue numerical guidance and introduced none; the only steers were that DTC gains get harder from ~60%, the social casino market is contracting ~7%, and SuprNation will add brands. Our FY26E: revenue ~$377m, adj. EBITDA ~$158m (~42%), year-end net cash ~$565-570m.
- **Thesis read-through.** The DTC conversion has delivered ~400-500bps of structural margin in two years and is not finished, but the legacy title is ex-growth at -6% and from here the DTC tailwind (~$1m/qtr per +5pp of mix on our maths) no longer fully offsets core decay (~$1.7m/qtr of EBITDA at a 50% drop-through). That makes FY27 adj. EBITDA a plateau-to-erosion story absent M&A, which is exactly the window in which the controlling shareholder has chosen to take out minorities at ~1.7x EBITDA.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — Social Casino (DoubleDown Casino + WHOW Games)

*Record 58% DTC mix drives ~52% segment EBITDA margin, but the US core is shrinking at -6.5% YoY, in line with the market it claims to be beating.*

### Revenue and geography

**Segment revenue fell 3.5% YoY to $76.8m; US core declined 6.5% YoY to $57.0m, tracking the market rather than beating it.**

- Revenue -3.5% YoY to $76.8m and -0.6% QoQ, attributed to lower revenue from legacy DoubleDown titles excluding WHOW Games.
- First clean YoY comparison since WHOW (closed 14 July 2025; Q3-25 carried eleven of thirteen weeks of WHOW).
- US -6.5% YoY to $57.0m, -2.5% QoQ (9M -5.2% to $173.0m); International +6.4% to $19.8m (9M +58% to $58.1m); Canada flat-to-down at $4.7m.
- CEO cited Eilers & Krejcik's forecast of almost 7% contraction in the 2026 global social casino market; outperformance is a perimeter effect.
- Segment is 81% of group revenue and, on our derivation, essentially all of group EBITDA.

### DTC channel shift

**DTC revenue reached $44.6m, 58.0% of segment revenue, up from 20.9% in Q3-25, driving the bulk of the $5.7m fall in group cost of revenue.**

- DTC mix: 20.9% in Q3-25, 44.2% in Q1-26, 52.4% in Q2-26, 58.0% now; third-party platform revenue fell 48.8% YoY to $32.2m.
- Removal of roughly $30.8m of store-fee-bearing revenue at ~30% take rate explains most of the 20.5% fall in group cost of revenue to 23.5% of revenue.
- Sigrist said DTC was below 5% at start of 2024, knows no one else approaching this level, gave no ceiling, conceded gains become harder.
- Product-side incentives (messaging, promotion, storefront bonusing) still have traction given the 52% to 58% step in one quarter.
- DTC storefront pricing is typically more generous, so part of the revenue decline is the cost of buying the mix shift; top-line decay slightly overstates attrition.

### Customer economics

**KPIs blend two different businesses: payer conversion rose to 9.5% from 7.8% while revenue per payer fell to $216 from $272; ARPDAU $1.44.**

- Average MAU +10.2% YoY to 1.23m, DAU +3.6% to 575k, but comparisons include WHOW in 2026 and exclude it in 2025.
- Sequentially MAU -1.6%, DAU -3.7%; DAU/MAU engagement 46.7% vs 47.7% in Q2 and 49.6% a year ago.
- ARPDAU $1.44 from $1.39 ($1.42 in Q2) as higher conversion outweighed lower spend per payer.
- Roughly 117k monthly payers, flat on Q2-26's ~118k; 117k x $216 x 3 reconciles within $1m of segment revenue, so KPIs are internally consistent.
- Sigrist was candid the company has fewer players and the job is to monetise and retain them; an accurate description of a harvest strategy.

### Marketing and profitability

**Advertising rose 26% YoY to $8.5m yet derived segment adj. EBITDA is roughly $40.1m, a ~52% margin, up from ~49% a year ago.**

- Advertising 11.0% of segment revenue vs 8.4% a year ago and 10.8% in Q2, consistent with migrating payers to web storefront.
- Segment profit before tax fell 31% to $30.1m because the ~$8m net FX loss and parent interest income sit in this segment.
- Derived adj. EBITDA ~$40.1m, ~52% margin vs ~49% a year ago and ~51% in Q2; D&A $1.8m, +12% YoY (9M +146%) on WHOW amortisation.
- Product: weekly slot launches, new link-jackpot system, simplified mobile lobby under development because layered meta content made UX complex.
- AI framed as three pillars (content velocity, behavioural models, UA automation); no measured ARPDAU lift given when Texas Capital asked.

### Outlook

**No segment guidance; the arithmetic turns against the segment in FY27 unless the decay rate improves or WHOW outgrows its German market.**

- DTC lever has perhaps 5-10pp left at diminishing benefit, worth roughly $1m quarterly EBITDA per 5pp of mix on a ~$77m base.
- A 6% annual decline on the $57m US base removes ~$3.4m quarterly revenue, or ~$1.7m EBITDA at 50% drop-through.
- This is the fundamental backdrop against which the controlling shareholder has priced its tender.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — iGaming (SuprNation)

*Revenue +7.4% to $17.4m and the first breakeven quarter on our derivation, achieved by cutting advertising 41% to offset the UK duty step-up; the next test is whether growth survives the lower UA budget.*

### Revenue growth

**Revenue grew 7.4% YoY to $17.4m and 2.6% QoQ, credited to the newest brand Los Vegas; quality of growth cannot be tested externally.**

- 9M revenue $51.6m (+14.9%); segment is 18.5% of group revenue, up from 16.9% a year ago.
- Los Vegas designed for broader appeal, blending casino nostalgia with modern UX, unlike the richer-themed first three brands; real-time analytics optimise acquisition and retention.
- Macquarie's question on whether Los Vegas brings incremental players or higher spenders was answered on design and UA efficiency, not cohort metrics.
- No actives, deposits, NGR margin or market shares disclosed.

### UK duty and marketing

**Advertising was cut 40.9% YoY to $3.9m to mitigate the higher UK Remote Gaming Duty, a cut that typically hits depositor cohorts two to four quarters later.**

- RGD increase announced in November 2025 Budget, effective April 2026, is not quantified in DDI's filings; UK not explicitly disclosed as largest market.
- Management said product changes, marketing adjustment and expense control mitigated much of the tax impact; higher duty flowed through group cost of revenue.
- Advertising 22.3% of segment revenue vs 40.6% a year ago (19.9% in Q2-26; 9M 25.7% vs 38.6%).
- Sequential spend uptick of +15% QoQ suggests management knows the cohort risk.

### Profitability

**Segment PBT narrowed to -$0.8m from -$2.6m, implying roughly +$0.1m adj. EBITDA, the first positive quarter on our derivation.**

- Q2-26 PBT was -$1.0m; adding back $0.9m D&A gives ~+$0.1m vs -$1.7m a year ago; 9M ~+$0.7m vs -$3.2m in 9M-25.
- On a ~0.5% margin this is breakeven rather than a profit engine; no interest income or FX, so derivation is clean.
- Strategy is portfolio expansion to dilute regulatory and tax risk; fifth and sixth brands planned with no timing or market given.
- No segment guidance provided.

### Our read

**SuprNation no longer dilutes group margin, but remains sub-scale (~$70m annualised) in a market moving against small brands.**

- Tax, affordability and advertising rules in the UK and Western Europe are moving against small brands.
- 7% growth on a 41% lower UA budget is good yield management, not yet evidence of a durable second leg.
- Real-money optionality behind the 2023 acquisition remains unproven at the scale DDI needs to offset social casino decay.

## 5. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- DTC mix hit a record 58.0% of social casino revenue (+5.6pp QoQ, +37pp YoY); third-party platform revenue -48.8% YoY to $32.2m, cost of revenue -560bps to 23.5% of revenue. Two years ago this was under 5%. _(structural)_
- Adj. EBITDA +7.2% to $40.2m on revenue -1.6%; margin 42.7% (+360bps YoY, +110bps QoQ) and operating margin 39.8% (+330bps). 9M adj. EBITDA +15.6% to $117.7m. _(structural)_
- Net cash $545.3m (~$11.00/ADS), +$24m QoQ and +$90m YTD; 9M derived FCF $93.0m = 79% of adj. EBITDA with negligible capex and zero M&A cash out in 2026. _(structural)_
- iGaming reached breakeven (derived EBITDA ~+$0.1m vs -$1.7m) while still growing revenue 7.4% to $17.4m; UK duty increase 'largely' mitigated per management. _(mixed)_
- International social casino +6.4% YoY to $19.8m (9M +58%) on WHOW Games, now 26% of segment revenue, diluting dependence on the US core (74%, from 83% in 9M-25). _(mixed)_
- The special committee extracted a 44% bump from DUG's April $11.25 proposal to $16.25/ADS, with a non-waivable majority-of-the-minority condition and a Kroll/Duff & Phelps fairness opinion. _(one-off)_

**Lowlights**

- US social casino revenue (the legacy DoubleDown Casino proxy) -6.5% YoY to $57.0m and -2.5% QoQ (9M -5.2%), i.e. tracking the ~7% market decline management says it is outperforming; DAU/MAU engagement down to 46.7% from 49.6%. _(structural)_
- Attributable profit -43.9% to $18.4m ($0.37/ADS vs ~$0.59 consensus): net finance result swung $14.3m YoY to -$8.2m on FX losses on USD-denominated assets at the KRW parent, and Q3 ETR spiked to 37.3% under the annual-rate method. _(one-off)_
- OCF -29% YoY to $23.6m as cash taxes paid rose to $17.2m from $7.2m; the OCF/adj. EBITDA conversion fell to 59% in the quarter (80% for 9M). _(one-off)_
- The tender values the ex-cash business at ~$260m EV, ~1.7x annualised adj. EBITDA and ~2.1x annualised FCF; management refused all questions on it, and the agreement constrains ordinary-course operations while the CEO still talks up M&A. _(governance)_
- G&A +26.5% YoY to $16.5m (17.5% of revenue) is not itemised; R&D -27% to $3.1m (3.2% of revenue) sits awkwardly with a weekly-content narrative. _(watch)_
- iGaming's breakeven was bought with a 41% cut in advertising (22% of segment revenue vs 41%); in a UA-driven European casino market that tends to show up in cohorts 2-4 quarters out. No actives, deposits or NGR-margin disclosure to monitor it. _(watch)_
- Average monthly revenue per payer -20.6% YoY to $216 and implied payers flat QoQ at ~117k; the KPI set is now a blend of two very different businesses and the clean core trend is only visible in the geographic split. _(mixed)_

## 6. Forecast & Outlook

DoubleDown does not issue numerical guidance and introduced none; there is therefore no company bridge to reconcile. The directional steers were (i) the global social casino market contracting ~7% in 2026 (Eilers & Krejcik, cited by the CEO), (ii) DTC gains becoming harder from ~58-60% with no stated ceiling, (iii) SuprNation adding a fifth and sixth brand to diversify away from UK tax exposure, and (iv) M&A remaining a 'strategic priority' subject to the constraints of the tender agreement. The bridge below is therefore our own estimate: the 9M actuals plus a Q4-26 built from a US core still declining ~6%, International roughly flat-to-up on WHOW, iGaming ~$17.5-18m, and a DTC mix around 60% holding cost of revenue near 23-24% of sales.

- **9M-26 adj. EBITDA (actual):** $117.7m (+15.6% YoY; 41.6% margin)
- **Q4-26E adj. EBITDA (our est.):** ~$40-41m (on ~$94-96m revenue; 42-43% margin)
- **FY26E adj. EBITDA (our est.):** ~$158m (~42% margin on ~$377m revenue)
- **YE-26 net cash (our est.):** ~$565-570m (+$20-25m QoQ; ~$11.4/ADS)

**Q4-26:** we model revenue of ~$94-96m (social casino ~$76-78m, with US down ~6% YoY offset by WHOW/International; iGaming ~$17.5-18m), adj. EBITDA ~$40-41m at a 42-43% margin as DTC edges toward 60%, and reported profit highly sensitive to KRW/USD given ~$580m of largely USD-denominated financial assets at a KRW-functional parent. **FY26E:** revenue ~$377m, adj. EBITDA ~$158m (~42%), 9M-annualised FCF ~$124m, year-end net cash ~$565-570m with gross debt limited to the $36.8m DUG loan (now due May 2028). **FY27 is the issue.** On our arithmetic each further +5pp of DTC mix adds only ~$1m of quarterly EBITDA, while a 6% decline on the $57m US base removes ~$1.7m of quarterly EBITDA at a 50% drop-through; the DTC tailwind therefore stops fully offsetting core decay next year, and adj. EBITDA moves from compounding to plateau-to-erosion (we would pencil FY27E adj. EBITDA flat-to-down low-single-digits, ~$150-158m) unless WHOW accelerates, SuprNation's brands five and six scale, or capital is deployed. **Leverage** is irrelevant (net cash ~3.5x annualised EBITDA); the capital question is entirely who owns the cash. **Swing factors:** the tender outcome (majority-of-the-minority), FX, the DTC ceiling, the pace of US decay, UK duty/affordability on SuprNation, and whether R&D at 3% of sales is enough to hold the DoubleDown Casino cohort.

**What to watch next quarter:**

- Tender offer mechanics: expiry date/extensions, the non-waivable majority-of-the-minority tally (>50% of unaffiliated ADSs), whether DUG consents to drop the minimum from 95% to 90%, any competing/bumped price, and the Schedule 14D-9 valuation ranges from Kroll vs our ~1.7x EV/EBITDA.
- DTC mix in Q4-26: whether it clears 60% and whether cost of revenue holds at 23-24% of revenue; watch for any Apple/Google policy or litigation changes on external payment links that could widen or close the channel.
- US social casino revenue (the legacy-title proxy): a decline worse than ~-6% YoY, or DAU/MAU below ~46%, would signal cohort erosion beyond market rate; implied payers (MAU x conversion) below ~115k.
- Below-the-line noise: FX on USD-denominated assets (finance income/cost), the Q4 effective tax rate normalising toward ~25%, and cash taxes paid vs the $25.0m 9M run-rate.
- iGaming cohort health after the 41% UA cut: Q4/Q1 revenue growth and advertising % of segment revenue (22% in Q3); timing and markets for brands five and six; any UK affordability-check or advertising-rule tightening.
- Cost discipline: G&A (17.5% of revenue, not itemised; deal/committee costs likely) and R&D (3.2% of revenue) trajectory into FY27, and WHOW intangible amortisation.
- Capital deployment: whether the stated M&A pipeline (casual gaming, online gambling, mobile entertainment) is real or shelved under the tender agreement's ordinary-course constraints; the $36.8m DUG related-party loan.
