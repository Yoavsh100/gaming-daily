# Bloomberry Resorts Corporation (BLOOM) — Q3 2026 Analyst Report
** · Reporting currency: PHP (Philippine peso); tables in PHP millions with USD equivalents in parentheses at USD/PHP 63.94 · Results released 16 Nov 2026 (board-approved 13 Nov 2026; PSE: BLOOM; FY end 31 Dec)**
**Market reaction:** Shares fell P0.09 (-4.79%) to close at P1.79 (~$0.028) on Monday 16 Nov 2026, the release day, against a 2.0% drop in the PSEi to 5,481.88 — its lowest close in more than six years on domestic stagflation concerns. Market cap ~P20.6bn (~$323m) on 11.53bn shares outstanding; EV ~P97.7bn (~$1.53bn). (17 Nov close not verified; PSEi rebounded 0.35% to 5,500.88.)

> A hold-flattered but genuinely cleaner quarter. Consolidated EBITDA rose 64% YoY to P3.15bn (~$49m) on a 22.9% margin (+770bps), but P350m (~$5.5m) of that is VIP hold running at 3.37% against the 2.85% the company itself calls normal; hold-normalised EBITDA was +32% in Q3 and -5% over nine months, mass-table GGR fell 8%, and visitation dropped 4-9% at both Solaire properties. The P77bn (~$1.21bn) net-debt stack at ~6x annualised EBITDA (our estimate), with the P72bn facility's amortisation tripling from year three, remains the thesis constraint — the shares fell 4.8% to P1.79 on a day the PSEi hit a six-year low.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Headline: EBITDA +64% YoY to P3.15bn (~$49m), net loss narrowed to P445m (~$7.0m) from P1.75bn.** Net revenue +8.6% to P13.75bn (~$215m), GGR +4.8% to P15.32bn (~$240m), cash opex -1.2% to P10.60bn (~$166m). Sequentially EBITDA fell 8% from 2Q26's P3.43bn (~$54m) and the net loss widened from P345m (~$5.4m). No published sell-side consensus could be verified for a PSE-listed name of this size, so 'beat/miss' is not assessable; versus the prior-year quarter the operating turnaround is unambiguous.
- **Hold did the heavy lifting: VIP hold 3.37% vs 2.29% in 3Q25 and the 2.85% 'normal'.** Company-disclosed hold-normalised EBITDA was P2.80bn (~$44m), +31.8% YoY, with reported EBITDA 12.5% above it. On nine months the picture inverts: reported EBITDA +8.1% to P9.55bn (~$149m) but hold-normalised EBITDA -5.4% to P8.53bn (~$133m), margin 21.5% vs 22.6%. The underlying business is not yet growing.
- **Volumes were flat, not growing.** Consolidated rolling chip +1.2% to P77.9bn (~$1.22bn), mass drop +1.9% to P15.6bn (~$244m), slot coin-in +0.8% to P121.2bn (~$1.90bn). Visitation fell 4.3% at Solaire Entertainment City (1.13m) and 8.5% at Solaire Quezon City (1.03m); F&B covers fell 5.7% and 12.3% respectively. Revenue per visitor is rising; the funnel is shrinking.
- **Mass tables are the weak link: GGR -8.4% to P5.49bn (~$86m) on hold of 35.2% vs 39.1%.** SQC mass GGR fell 19.1% on 3.5% lower drop; SEC mass GGR fell 2.5% despite 6.1% higher drop as hold slid to 41.1% from 44.8% (and from an outlier 55.3% in 2Q26). We estimate mass hold at prior-year levels would have added ~P0.6bn (~$9.6m) of GGR — a partial offset to the VIP hold windfall that the company does not normalise for.
- **Promotional intensity cut: contra-revenue -10.1% to P3.05bn (~$48m), 19.9% of GGR vs 23.2% (and 22.8% in 2Q26).** The 17-Q attributes it to lower rebates and promotions to mass patrons. This P343m (~$5.4m) swing is roughly 28% of the YoY EBITDA improvement and is a management choice, not luck — though it coincides with the mass-volume softness above.
- **Cost programme is delivering, but is unquantified.** Cash opex fell 1.2% YoY and 0.5% QoQ to P10.60bn (~$166m) — 77.1% of net revenue vs 84.8% — via advertising & promotions, outside services, utilities and cost of sales; 9M cash opex +1.7% to P31.38bn (~$491m). No bad-debt provisions were booked in 3Q26, 2Q26 or 3Q25. Management gives no savings target, amount or end-year; the 'programme' is a run-rate discipline rather than a sized plan.
- **Q3 EBITDA bridge (3Q25 → 3Q26): P1,923m + GGR P703m − PFRS15 allocation P72m + lower contra P343m + non-gaming P119m + lower cash opex P133m = P3,149m.** Gaming (net) explains P974m (~$15m) of the P1,226m (~$19m) improvement, of which ~P350m is above-normal VIP hold.
- **Below EBITDA: interest expense -5.4% to P1.93bn (~$30m), a P109m (~$1.7m) saving; 9M saving P519m (~$8.1m).** D&A flat at P1.89bn (~$30m); FX gain P217m (~$3.4m) vs P247m. Interest cover (EBITDA/interest) is 1.6x in Q3 and 1.7x over 9M — thin. Management explicitly flagged 'rising benchmark rates' as a risk to future debt-service costs; P68.2bn (~$1.07bn) of the P72bn facility is floating (3M BVAL/BSP TDF + 1.75%), the P40bn facility was converted to fixed.
- **9M net loss P915m (~$14.3m) vs net income P160m (~$2.5m), swung by one-offs:** 1Q25 carried a P2.9bn (~$45m) non-cash gain on the P40bn facility modification; 1Q26 carried a P403m (~$6.3m) gain on the Jeju Sun casino-licence sale. Attributable 9M loss P947m (~$14.8m); LPS P0.089 (~$0.0014) vs EPS P0.016.
- **Guidance: none, old or new — Bloomberry does not issue numerical guidance.** The Q4 message is qualitative: 'mid-way into the final quarter … focused on driving revenue growth and further optimizing our cost structure' (Razon). Our FY26E EBITDA is ~P12.4-12.5bn (~$195m) with a ~P1.4-1.6bn (~$23m) net loss (estimate, see Forecast).
- **Solaire Resort Entertainment City (SEC): EBITDA +60% to P1.98bn (~$31m), margin 22.4% (+730bps).** GGR +3.8% to P10.38bn (~$162m) entirely from VIP hold (3.00% vs 2.25%); rolling chip -5.0%; mass GGR -2.5%; slots +1.0%. Net profit P351m (~$5.5m) vs P411m loss. 9M EBITDA -1.2% to P6.30bn (~$99m).
- **Solaire Resort Quezon City (SQC): EBITDA +55% to P1.22bn (~$19m), margin 25.7% (+740bps) — now the higher-margin property.** GGR +7.1% to P4.94bn (~$77m): VIP rolling chip +92% at a 6.08% hold (VIP GGR +315%), slots +12.8%, mass -19.1%. Still a P771m (~$12.1m) net loss after P1.19bn D&A and P800m interest; needs ~P2.0bn quarterly EBITDA to break even at the net line. 9M EBITDA +24.7% to P3.66bn (~$57m).
- **Non-gaming +3.6% to P3.46bn (~$54m); occupancy 77.7% SEC / 77.8% SQC.** Hotel & F&B +4.3% to P1.99bn (~$31m), retail & others +0.9% to P1.40bn (~$22m) (SQC retail -18%). Cash share of hotel revenue fell at both properties (SEC 43.1%, SQC 34.2%) — more rooms being comped to support the floor.
- **Balance sheet: cash P30.35bn (~$475m), debt P107.4bn (~$1.68bn), net debt P77.1bn (~$1.21bn), down P1.8bn YTD.** Net debt/equity (company definition) 1.71x vs 1.76x at Dec-25 and 1.66x at Sep-25; covenant compliance confirmed. Current portion of long-term debt jumped to P5.52bn (~$86m) from P2.23bn as the P72bn facility enters year-3 amortisation (P6.48bn/yr (~$101m) vs P2.16bn in years 1-2). Our estimate of net debt/annualised EBITDA is ~6.1x.
- **Cash flow: 9M operating cash flow +26% to P10.02bn (~$157m); capex -21% to P2.50bn (~$39m); interest paid P4.95bn (~$77m); principal P1.72bn (~$27m); no dividend (P893m (~$14m) paid in 9M25).** Cash rose P3.84bn (~$60m), but P3.0bn of that is net new short-term BDO loans (P8.0bn drawn, P5.0bn repaid). Free cash flow after debt service was ~P0.85bn (~$13m) — essentially break-even.
- **Portfolio: Jeju Sun's casino licence has been sold; the Korean unit is now a hotel.** Demerger into Heaven Co. and sale to Blue One completed 4 March 2026 for KRW 10.0bn (KRW 7.0bn received; KRW 3.0bn due by 27 Feb 2027; USD not converted — KRW rate not verified). Jeju Sun Q3 EBITDA P31m (~$0.5m) vs an LBITDA of P15m; GGR nil.
- **Online: platform churn, zero disclosure.** MegaFUNalo! (launched June 2025) was shut on 1 May 2026; in-house FUNaloMax soft-launched 6 April and formally launched 3 July 2026; OnlyFun launched October 2026 under the 'Solaire Online' umbrella. No online revenue, GGR, actives or marketing split is disclosed — a gap given the capital and promotional spend going in.
- **Land bank: Solaire Properties has bought 223 lots / 2.04m sqm in Paniman, Ternate (Cavite) for a third integrated resort; 'development timeline yet to be finalized'.** Option value, but also a capital-allocation overhang at ~6x leverage. Bloomberry is a subsidiary of Prime Strategic Holdings (Razon); 11.53bn shares outstanding.
- **Share price: -4.8% to P1.79 on 16 Nov vs PSEi -2.0% to 5,481.88 (lowest close in 6+ years, 'stagflation fears').** Market cap ~P20.6bn (~$323m); EV ~P97.7bn (~$1.53bn), ~7.7x annualised 9M EBITDA (our estimate). The equity is a thin sliver on a large debt stack — hold volatility and rates dominate the stock.
- **Thesis read-through: operating leverage is real (every P1 of net revenue growth dropped through at >100% this quarter), but the base is volume-flat and hold-dependent.** De-leveraging requires EBITDA above ~P14bn/yr (~$219m) to cover ~P7.7bn interest, ~P5.6-6.5bn amortisation and ~P3.3bn capex; FY26E lands ~P12.5bn. The P30bn cash cushion buys 2-3 years, but the path to equity value runs through mass-volume recovery at both properties, not through hold.
- **Data gaps flagged:** no market-share data (PAGCOR industry GGR not retrievable this session), no consensus, no online KPIs, no FY25 EBITDA for an LTM multiple; the 17-Q narrative cites a 3Q25 LPS of P0.134 against P0.165 in its own tables and the press release (we use P0.165); the 17-Q repeats SEC's 9M F&B covers (1,669,859) under SQC — treated as a typo.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — Solaire Resort Entertainment City (SEC) — Parañaque, Entertainment City

*EBITDA +60% to P1.98bn (~$31m) on a 22.4% margin, but GGR growth of 3.8% came entirely from a 3.00% VIP hold; rolling chip -5%, mass GGR -2.5%, visitation -4.3%.*

### GGR and VIP hold

**GGR rose 3.8% YoY to P10.38bn (~$162m), but growth came entirely from a 3.00% VIP hold while the rest of the floor shrank.**

- GGR was down 9.7% on the quarter; composition matters more than the headline.
- VIP rolling-chip volume fell 5.0% YoY to P68.5bn (~$1.07bn) and 4% QoQ.
- VIP GGR rose 26.3% to P2.05bn (~$32m) on 3.00% hold versus 2.25% a year ago and the 2.85% company norm.
- Management concedes in the 17-Q that hold-normalised VIP revenue would have grown just 6.1%.
- VIP added P427m (~$6.7m) against a total GGR increase of only P378m (~$5.9m); VIP is now 20% of SEC GGR versus 16%.

### Mass and slots

**Mass GGR fell 2.5% to P3.78bn (~$59m) as hold slipped to 41.1% despite drop up 6.1%; slots were steady at P4.55bn (~$71m).**

- Mass drop rose 6.1% YoY to P9.18bn (~$144m) and 21% QoQ; hold fell from 44.8% and from an unusually high 55.3% in 2Q26.
- Mass GGR fell 10% sequentially; the 14-point sequential hold swing on a 21% drop jump is not explained in the filings.
- Pattern is consistent with a mix shift toward premium-mass direct play rather than pure luck; management should clarify before drop growth is treated as a demand signal.
- Slots: coin-in +0.6% to P78.1bn (~$1.22bn), hold flat at 5.8%, GGR +1.0%.
- Slots are now 44% of SEC GGR, mass 36% and VIP 20%.

### Promotions, costs, EBITDA

**A lighter promotional load and 1.8% lower cash opex lifted EBITDA 60.2% YoY to P1.98bn (~$31m) at a 22.4% margin.**

- Contra-revenue fell 9.7% YoY to P2.39bn (~$37m), 23.0% of GGR versus 26.4% in 3Q25 and 26.6% in 2Q26, on lower rebates and promotions to mass patrons.
- After PFRS 15 allocations of P1.34bn (~$21m), net gaming revenue rose 8.5% to P6.65bn (~$104m); net revenue +7.5% to P8.83bn (~$138m), 75% gaming.
- Cash opex P6.85bn (~$107m), 77.6% of net revenue against 84.9%, flat QoQ.
- EBITDA -17.4% QoQ; margin +730bps YoY, -360bps QoQ.
- After D&A P669m (~$10m), interest P1.13bn (~$18m) and a P169m (~$2.6m) FX gain, net profit was P351m (~$5.5m) versus a P411m loss a year ago and P667m in 2Q26.

### Non-gaming and visitation

**Non-gaming revenue grew 4.7% to P2.17bn (~$34m), but visitation fell 4.3% to 1.13m, the number to watch for an 80% mass-and-slots property.**

- Hotel and F&B +0.7% to P1.11bn (~$17m); retail and others +8.2% to P1.01bn (~$16m); interest income P50m; non-gaming +10.8% QoQ.
- Occupancy 77.7% (74.3% in 3Q25; 78.4% in 2Q26).
- Cash share of hotel revenue slipped to 43.1% from 45.1% as more rooms are comped to gaming patrons; cash share of F&B rose to 59.9% from 56.9%.
- Covers fell 5.7% to 515,728 but average check rose 6.7% to P1,268 (~$19.8).

### Nine-month view

**Nine-month SEC is flat: GGR P31.85bn (~$498m, -0.2%), EBITDA P6.30bn (~$99m, -1.2%) at 23.8%, with the top line volume-flat and hold-dependent.**

- VIP GGR +13% to P6.61bn (~$103m) on 3.43% hold (+81bps) masking a 14% fall in rolling chip to P193.0bn (~$3.02bn).
- Mass GGR -6% to P11.86bn (~$185m) on 2% lower drop and 2.3pts lower hold; slots flat at P13.38bn (~$209m) on 11% lower coin-in offset by 0.7pts higher hold.
- Contra 24.5% of GGR (24.8%); net revenue P26.49bn (~$414m, +0.2%); cash opex +0.6% to P20.18bn (~$316m); margin 23.8% (24.1%).
- Net profit P1.49bn (~$23m, +59%) on P313m lower interest; occupancy 76.7% (72.6%).
- Cost base is held and promotional discipline is real, but SEC has yet to grow visitation against Quezon City cannibalisation and a softer Manila consumer. Market share n/a.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — Solaire Resort Quezon City (SQC, formerly Solaire Resort North) — opened 25 May 2024

## 6. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- **Cost discipline is holding against inflation:** cash opex -1.2% YoY and -0.5% QoQ to P10.60bn (~$166m), 77.1% of net revenue vs 84.8%; 9M +1.7%. SEC opex -1.8%, SQC flat. No bad-debt provisions for three consecutive quarters; allowances cover 86% of >90-day receivables. _(structural)_
- **Promotional intensity down 330bps:** contra-revenue 19.9% of GGR (23.2% in 3Q25, 22.8% in 2Q26), worth P343m (~$5.4m) of the YoY EBITDA uplift; SEC 23.0% (26.4%), SQC 13.5% (16.3%). A management lever, not luck — but it coincides with softer mass volumes. _(mixed)_
- **SQC margin ramp is real:** EBITDA +55% to P1.22bn (~$19m) at 25.7% (+740bps), with slots GGR +12.8% on 1.2% higher coin-in and a 6.2% hold; 9M EBITDA +24.7% at 26.2%. Rolling chip +92% shows a premium-direct book forming. _(early)_
- **Interest burden falling:** Q3 interest expense -5.4% to P1.93bn (~$30m), P109m (~$1.7m) saved; 9M P519m (~$8.1m) saved; interest paid -13.5% to P4.95bn (~$77m). P40bn facility now fixed-rate. _(structural)_
- **Cash generation improved:** 9M operating cash flow +26% to P10.02bn (~$157m) on higher EBITDA and lower working capital; capex -21% to P2.50bn (~$39m); cash up P3.84bn (~$60m) to P30.35bn (~$475m); net debt down P1.8bn YTD to P77.1bn (~$1.21bn). _(mixed)_
- **Non-gaming steady at SEC:** revenue +4.7% to P2.17bn (~$34m), occupancy 77.7% (+340bps), retail +8.2%, average check +6.7% to P1,268 (~$19.8); group hotel & F&B +4.3%. _(structural)_
- **Jeju Sun de-risked:** casino licence sold (KRW 10bn), P404m (~$6.3m) gain booked, unit now EBITDA-positive (P31m vs LBITDA P15m) with 9M LBITDA of P116m eliminated. _(one-off)_
