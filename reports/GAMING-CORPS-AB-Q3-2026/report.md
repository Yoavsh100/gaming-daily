## 3. Segment deep-dive — Group operating business – B2B online-casino content (single reportable segment)

*Revenue +130% YoY with opex +6% QoQ delivers real operating leverage; but capitalisation (20% of sales) and a non-cash FX gain make the reported EBITDA/net-loss look two to three times better than the underlying.*

### Revenue growth

**Net sales of SEK 29.2m (~$2.90m) rose +130% YoY and +38% QoQ, extending a six-quarter run from SEK 10.2m; 9M sales SEK 65.3m (~$6.50m), +94%.**

- Sequence over six quarters: 10.2 → 12.7 → 15.1 → 15.1 → 21.1 → 29.2; prior-year quarter SEK 12.7m (~$1.26m), Q2 SEK 21.1m (~$2.10m).
- Growth attributed to 2,540+ live online casinos (2,310+ at June, 2,000+ a year ago), seven proprietary titles in quarter, 24 year-to-date versus 18.
- Rising share of direct operator integrations alongside aggregators IGT Digital, EveryMatrix, BetConstruct, Games Global, 1X2, Light & Wonder, Playtech.
- Pure supplier: no GGR, wagers, active players, ARPU or product/geography split disclosed; 'total bets' chart is indexed with no absolute figures.
- Only sub-split: parent unconsolidated net sales SEK 12.5m (~$1.24m), +62% YoY, balance via Malta subsidiary; Gaming Intelligence and Inderes/MFN figures match.

### EBITDA and capitalisation

**Reported EBITDA SEK −0.8m (~$−0.08m) flatters: stripping SEK 5.8m capitalised development (20% of sales), underlying EBITDA was SEK −6.6m, a 48% sequential improvement.**

- Reported EBITDA from SEK −7.6m (~$−0.75m) a year ago and SEK −5.8m (~$−0.57m) in Q2; margin −2.7% against −59.9%.
- Own work capitalised SEK 5.8m (~$0.58m) versus SEK 1.4m a year ago and SEK 6.9m in Q2.
- EBITDA before capitalisation SEK −6.6m (~$−0.66m) against SEK −9.0m (~$−0.89m) in Q3-25 and SEK −12.7m (~$−1.26m) in Q2.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — Balance sheet, cash flow & recapitalisation (deep dive)

*SEK 0.36m of cash and SEK −27.8m of equity at 30 Sep, financed by 2.5%-a-month bridge money; fixed post-period with SEK 45.2m of directed issues at SEK 0.65 (32% dilution, ~SEK 23m net cash) — roughly two quarters of runway at the Q3 burn rate.*

### Cash and burn

**Cash fell to SEK 0.36m (~$0.04m) at 30 September after free cash flow of SEK −11.9m (~$−1.18m), nearly three times the Q3-25 burn, bridged by SEK 11.8m of short-term loans.**

- Cash down from SEK 1.5m (~$0.15m) at June and SEK 7.3m (~$0.72m) at December; management did not lead with this.
- Operating cash flow SEK −5.2m (~$−0.51m); capitalised development roughly SEK −7.0m (net investing SEK −6.7m, ~$−0.67m); Q3-25 burn SEK −4.1m (~$−0.41m).
- June bridge facility ~SEK 13m (~$1.3m), including ~EUR 1m (~SEK 11m, ~$1.1m) from 29% shareholder Denwena at 2.5% per month, unsecured, due 31 December 2026, approved at 3 August EGM.
- 9M free cash flow SEK −30.9m (~$−3.08m) versus SEK −27.1m (~$−2.70m) as capitalised development nearly tripled (SEK 16.4m vs 6.1m).

## 5. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- **Growth is accelerating, not decelerating**: net sales SEK 29.2m (~$2.90m), +130% YoY and +38% QoQ, the sixth consecutive quarter of sequential growth; 9M +94% to SEK 65.3m (~$6.50m). Management says October continued to grow. _(structural)_
- **Genuine operating leverage**: total cash opex SEK 36.0m (~$3.58m) rose only 6% QoQ against +38% revenue; external costs fell to 81% of sales (118% LY) and personnel to 42% (53%). EBITDA ex-capitalisation improved 48% QoQ to SEK −6.6m (~$−0.66m). _(structural)_
- **Distribution breadth is compounding**: 2,540+ live casinos (+27% YoY), 24 releases YTD (vs 18), and tier-1 agreements in one quarter — Entain, evoke, Games Global, Bally's Intralot, Playtech, Loto-Québec — plus Veikkaus and Apollo Games (Czech) post-period; the state-lottery/monopoly channel is a differentiated route to regulated markets. _(structural)_
- **Cost driver removed**: termination of the Denwena/Degen Studios agreements (14 Jul) cut team headcount from 87 to 74 (−15%), capped remaining collaboration at ~EUR 0.6m (~$0.7m), and eliminated the milestone-warrant dilution overhang at SEK 1.34; management guides further savings to flow gradually. _(one-off)_
- **Recapitalisation done, with insiders and the largest holder participating**: SEK 45.2m (~$4.50m) gross at SEK 0.65 (~$0.065) fully subscribed across both placements, extinguishing the 2.5%-a-month bridge via ~SEK 19.2m (~$1.91m) set-off and restoring positive pro-forma equity (~SEK +15m, ~$1.5m, analyst est.). _(mixed)_
- **North America optionality**: Ontario launch showed 'strong early results', Loto-Québec gives direct access to the regulated Québec market, and a new third-party US/Canada distribution structure on better terms is promised 'shortly'. _(early)_

**Lowlights**

- **The quarter ended with SEK 0.36m (~$0.04m) of cash and SEK −27.8m (~$−2.76m) of equity** — solvency negative for a third quarter — kept afloat only by SEK 11.8m (~$1.17m) of short-term bridge loans drawn in Q3, part of it from the 29% shareholder at 2.5% per month. Management's letter does not mention any of this. _(structural)_
- **Quality of earnings**: SEK 5.8m (~$0.58m) of capitalised development (20% of sales, 4x YoY) and a SEK +1.7m (~$+0.17m) non-cash FX gain flatter the P&L. EBITDA ex-capitalisation SEK −6.6m (~$−0.66m), net loss ex-FX SEK −4.2m (~$−0.42m); 9M free cash flow SEK −30.9m (~$−3.08m) was worse than 9M-25 (SEK −27.1m) despite the better P&L. _(structural)_
- **Amortisation headwind building**: capitalised development NBV SEK 21.7m (~$2.16m), 2.5x December; Q3 D&A SEK 2.8m (~$0.28m) vs SEK 1.0m in Q2. The P&L will have to absorb this as the asset amortises, so EBIT will lag EBITDA for several quarters. _(watch)_
- **32.3% dilution via two directed issues at a 17.7% VWAP discount with no pre-emption rights**, with insiders (CEO, board members) and Denwena — whose commercial deal the company had just terminated for driving costs without revenue — among the subscribers; the EGM pushed back by rejecting the Svea Bank set-off, leaving that ~SEK 2.34m (~$0.23m) claim as debt. _(governance)_
- **CFO Fredrik Geijer resigned on 16 Nov after nine months**, three days after the report and in the middle of a recapitalisation; shares −6.1%. The report is unaudited and prepared under Swedish K3 rather than IFRS. _(governance)_
- **Disclosure is thin for a 130%-growth story**: no GGR, wagers, active players, ARPU, product or geographic revenue split, no customer/aggregator concentration, no numeric guidance, and the accounting treatment of the SEK 4.4m (~$0.44m) platform sale to Degen Studios is not visible in the Q3 statements [unverified] — if any of it sits in net sales, underlying growth is overstated. _(watch)_
- **Working-capital unwind**: ~SEK 4.7m (~$0.47m) outflow in Q3 as stretched payables (SEK 14.7m → 12.1m) and accruals (SEK 11.3m → 6.2m) came down; payables remain ~3x the year-ago level, so a further portion of the new cash is likely to go to creditors rather than growth. _(one-off)_

## 6. Forecast & Outlook

There is no formal numeric guidance to bridge — the company explicitly declines to forecast revenue. What changed between Q2 and Q3 is the financial framing, and it was driven entirely by the group's single operating business: at Q2 (14 Aug) the stance was 'liquidity strengthened by a ~SEK 13m bridge loan' with Denwena's EUR 1m tranche at 2.5%/month due 31 Dec 2026 and a solvency-negative balance sheet; by Q3 (13 Nov) the bridge has been converted into equity through SEK 45.2m (~$4.50m) of directed issues at SEK 0.65, ~SEK 19.2m (~$1.91m) of debt is set off, ~SEK 23.1m (~$2.30m) of net cash is expected, the share count rises to 214.9m, and the board (per secondary coverage of the 14 Oct release) has pointed to positive operating cash flow before end-2026. The moving parts are (i) revenue momentum — October 'healthy growth' and a 3-releases-a-month cadence; (ii) cost base — Denwena/Degen savings still flowing through, team already down to 74; (iii) capitalisation — SEK 5.8m/quarter that sits below operating cash flow but above free cash flow; and (iv) working-capital normalisation.

- **FY26E net sales (analyst):** ~SEK 99–101m (~$9.9–10.0m) (vs FY25 SEK 48.7m (+~105%); 9M actual 65.3m + Q4E 33–36m)
- **Q4-26E EBITDA (analyst):** reported ~SEK +1m to +3m (~$0.1–0.3m) (ex-capitalisation still ~SEK −2m to −4m; breakeven ex-cap needs ~SEK 36m quarterly sales)
- **Pro-forma equity / net debt post placements:** ~SEK +15m (~$1.5m) / ~zero (from SEK −27.8m / ~SEK 41.2m at 30 Sep (analyst est., before Q4 losses))
- **Share count:** 214.9m (+69.4m (+47.7%) vs 145.4m; 32.3% dilution)

**Revenue**: we model Q4-26 net sales of SEK 33–36m (~$3.3–3.6m), +15–25% QoQ, taking FY26 to ~SEK 99–101m (~$9.9–10.0m), roughly double FY25 — consistent with 'healthy growth' in October, the higher release cadence and new distribution going live (Veikkaus, Loto-Québec, Playtech, Games Global), but deliberately below the +38% Q3 step because Q3 likely absorbed a cluster of integrations and the 3-release target is below Q3's seven. **Margin**: with cash opex of ~SEK 36–37m/quarter (Denwena savings offset by growth hires), Q4 reported EBITDA should turn modestly positive (SEK +1m to +3m) if capitalisation stays ~SEK 6m, while EBITDA ex-capitalisation remains ~SEK −2m to −4m; breakeven on the honest measure is a ~SEK 36m-revenue event, plausibly Q1/Q2-27. FY26E reported EBITDA ≈ SEK −15m (~$−1.5m), net loss ≈ SEK −24m to −26m (~$−2.4 to −2.6m) with D&A rising toward SEK 3.5m/quarter. **Cash/leverage**: ~SEK 23m net proceeds against a Q3 free-cash-flow burn of SEK 11.9m implies a year-end cash balance of roughly SEK 12–17m if Q4 burn moderates to SEK 6–10m (working-capital unwind is the swing), and pro-forma net debt of ~SEK 6–10m (~$0.6–1.0m) by December versus ~SEK 41m at September — leverage direction is sharply down, but absolute liquidity remains thin and a further raise in H2-27 cannot be excluded unless ex-capitalisation EBITDA turns positive by Q2-27. **Swing factors**: (1) whether the board's 'positive operating cash flow before end-2026' is met and how it is defined; (2) capitalisation rate — any step-up flatters EBITDA but burns cash; (3) the North American distribution restructuring and timing of US operator launches; (4) payables normalisation; (5) CFO succession and audit of the FY26 accounts, including going-concern language. All figures above are analyst estimates, not company guidance.

**What to watch next quarter:**

- Q4-26 report (19 Feb 2027): operating cash flow vs the board's 'positive before end-2026' statement, year-end cash after the ~SEK 23m net proceeds, and whether Q4 free cash flow is better than SEK −6m.
- Capitalised development as % of sales (20% in Q3) and the D&A run-rate — track EBITDA ex-capitalisation, not reported EBITDA.
- Confirmation of the exact cash vs set-off split and issue costs in the Q4 cash-flow statement, plus whether the SEK 4.4m Degen platform sale was booked in revenue, other income or investing [unverified].
- CFO appointment and the FY2026 annual report/auditor's report (going-concern and listing working-capital statements); the Q3 report is unaudited.
- Details of the new North American third-party distribution deal and the first US operator go-lives; progress in Ontario/Québec and with Veikkaus.
- Svea Bank's ~SEK 2.34m claim (set-off rejected by the EGM) and the remaining ~SEK 22m of investor loans — maturities and interest cost post-recapitalisation.
- Denwena's ~29% stake and board representation after the commercial split; any further related-party transactions or selling.
- Working capital: trade payables (SEK 12.1m) and accrued liabilities normalising toward year-ago levels would consume cash.
- Any disclosure of GGR/wagers, player counts or customer concentration — the growth story currently rests on net sales and 'casinos live' alone.
