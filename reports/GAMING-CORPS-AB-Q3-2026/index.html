Q3 2026 · results · analyst report

Gaming Corps AB

Released 13 Nov 2026 · Currency SEK (tables in SEK millions; ~US$ at USD/SEK 10.05) · Data confidence: MEDIUM

Market reaction: Shares +18.1% to SEK 0.76 (~$0.076) on 13 Nov 2026 (MFN price feed on the day of the release), from SEK 0.64 the prior close; then −6.1% to SEK 0.71 on 16 Nov on the CFO's resignation and +5.9% to SEK 0.75 on 17 Nov on the Apollo Games/Czech agreement. Quarter-end close was SEK 0.67 (~$0.067), −34% YoY vs SEK 1.02, giving a market cap of SEK 97.2m (~$9.7m) pre-placement; ~SEK 163m (~$16.2m) at SEK 0.76 on the enlarged 214.9m share count.

The one-line verdict

A genuinely strong top line — net sales SEK 29.2m (~$2.90m), +130% YoY and +38% QoQ with cash opex up only 6% QoQ — but the near-breakeven EBITDA is flattered by SEK 5.8m (~$0.58m) of capitalised development and the net loss by a SEK 1.7m (~$0.17m) non-cash FX gain; underlying EBITDA was SEK −6.6m (~$−0.66m) and free cash flow SEK −11.9m (~$−1.18m), roughly 3x the year-ago burn. The quarter closed with SEK 0.36m (~$0.04m) of cash and SEK −27.8m (~$−2.76m) of equity, forcing SEK 45.2m (~$4.50m) of directed issues at SEK 0.65 (32% dilution, ~SEK 23m net cash) — and the CFO resigned three days after the report. Shares +18% on the day; the real test is whether Q4 delivers the positive operating cash flow the board has pointed to.

1Executive summary

  • Headline growth is real and accelerating: net sales SEK 29.2m (~$2.90m), +130.4% YoY from SEK 12.7m (~$1.26m) and +38.4% QoQ from SEK 21.1m (~$2.10m); 9M net sales SEK 65.3m (~$6.50m), +94.4%. The sequential run is 10.2 → 12.7 → 15.1 → 15.1 → 21.1 → 29.2 over six quarters. There is no sell-side consensus; Gaming Intelligence and Inderes/MFN corroborate the company figures.
  • Reported EBITDA near breakeven — but read the capitalisation line: EBITDA SEK −0.8m (~$−0.08m) vs SEK −7.6m (~$−0.75m) a year ago and SEK −5.8m (~$−0.57m) in Q2; margin −2.7% vs −59.9%. 'Own work capitalised' was SEK 5.8m (~$0.58m), 20% of sales and 4x the year-ago SEK 1.4m. EBITDA before capitalisation was SEK −6.6m (~$−0.66m) (−22.7% margin) vs SEK −9.0m (~$−0.89m) YoY and SEK −12.7m (~$−1.26m) QoQ — a 48% sequential improvement, not breakeven.
  • Net loss SEK −2.6m (~$−0.25m), EPS SEK −0.02 (~$−0.002), helped by a SEK +1.7m (~$+0.17m) non-cash FX gain on long-term intra-group receivables. Ex-FX the loss was SEK −4.2m (~$−0.42m) vs SEK −7.3m (~$−0.72m) in Q2 — the CEO's '65% improvement vs Q2' is ~42% on an underlying basis. EBIT SEK −3.6m (~$−0.36m) vs SEK −7.9m.
  • Operating leverage is the bull case: total cash opex (external + personnel + other) SEK 36.0m (~$3.58m), +6% QoQ against +38% revenue. 3Segment deep-dive: Group operating business – B2B online-casino content (single reportable segment)

Revenue +130% YoY with opex +6% QoQ delivers real operating leverage; but capitalisation (20% of sales) and a non-cash FX gain make the reported EBITDA/net-loss look two to three times better than the underlying.

Revenue growth

Net sales of SEK 29.2m (~$2.90m) rose +130% YoY and +38% QoQ, extending a six-quarter run from SEK 10.2m; 9M sales SEK 65.3m (~$6.50m), +94%.

  • Sequence over six quarters: 10.2 → 12.7 → 15.1 → 15.1 → 21.1 → 29.2; prior-year quarter SEK 12.7m (~$1.26m), Q2 SEK 21.1m (~$2.10m).
  • Growth attributed to 2,540+ live online casinos (2,310+ at June, 2,000+ a year ago), seven proprietary titles in quarter, 24 year-to-date versus 18.
  • Rising share of direct operator integrations alongside aggregators IGT Digital, EveryMatrix, BetConstruct, Games Global, 1X2, Light & Wonder, Playtech.
  • Pure supplier: no GGR, wagers, active players, ARPU or product/geography split disclosed; 'total bets' chart is indexed with no absolute figures.
  • Only sub-split: parent unconsolidated net sales SEK 12.5m (~$1.24m), +62% YoY, balance via Malta subsidiary; Gaming Intelligence and Inderes/MFN figures match.

EBITDA and capitalisation

Reported EBITDA SEK −0.8m (~$−0.08m) flatters: stripping SEK 5.8m capitalised development (20% of sales), underlying EBITDA was SEK −6.6m, a 48% sequential improvement.

  • Reported EBITDA from SEK −7.6m (~$−0.75m) a year ago and SEK −5.8m (~$−0.57m) in Q2; margin −2.7% against −59.9%.
  • Own work capitalised SEK 5.8m (~$0.58m) versus SEK 1.4m a year ago and SEK 6.9m in Q2.
  • EBITDA before capitalisation SEK −6.6m (~$−0.66m) against SEK −9.0m (~$−0.89m) in Q3-25 and SEK −12.7m (~$−1.26m) in Q2.
  • Other external costs +58% YoY to SEK 23.6m (~$2.35m), flat QoQ (+2.5%), 81% of sales from 118%; personnel +84% YoY, +14% QoQ to SEK 12.2m, 42% of sales from 53%.
  • Total cash opex SEK 36.0m (~$3.58m), +6% QoQ, so operating leverage came entirely from the top line.

Headcount and Denwena/Degen

Team fell 15% to 74 (36 consultants) from 87 after the 14 July termination of Denwena/Degen Studios agreements; cost benefit guided to come gradually.

  • Management says the agreements had 'driven significant costs' without commensurate revenue.
  • Remaining collaboration capped at ~EUR 0.6m (~$0.7m); a SEK 4.4m (~$0.44m) technology-platform sale to Degen was proposed.

4Segment deep-dive: Balance sheet, cash flow & recapitalisation (deep dive)

SEK 0.36m of cash and SEK −27.8m of equity at 30 Sep, financed by 2.5%-a-month bridge money; fixed post-period with SEK 45.2m of directed issues at SEK 0.65 (32% dilution, ~SEK 23m net cash) — roughly two quarters of runway at the Q3 burn rate.

Cash and burn

Cash fell to SEK 0.36m (~$0.04m) at 30 September after free cash flow of SEK −11.9m (~$−1.18m), nearly three times the Q3-25 burn, bridged by SEK 11.8m of short-term loans.

  • Cash down from SEK 1.5m (~$0.15m) at June and SEK 7.3m (~$0.72m) at December; management did not lead with this.
  • Operating cash flow SEK −5.2m (~$−0.51m); capitalised development roughly SEK −7.0m (net investing SEK −6.7m, ~$−0.67m); Q3-25 burn SEK −4.1m (~$−0.41m).
  • June bridge facility ~SEK 13m (~$1.3m), including ~EUR 1m (~SEK 11m, ~$1.1m) from 29% shareholder Denwena at 2.5% per month, unsecured, due 31 December 2026, approved at 3 August EGM.
  • 9M free cash flow SEK −30.9m (~$−3.08m) versus SEK −27.1m (~$−2.70m) as capitalised development nearly tripled (SEK 16.4m vs 6.1m).

Working capital and solvency

H1's SEK +8.7m working-capital inflow partially unwound (~SEK 4.7m outflow); equity was SEK −27.8m (~$−2.76m), solvency negative for a third consecutive quarter.

  • Trade payables down to SEK 12.1m (~$1.20m) from SEK 14.7m, accrued liabilities to SEK 6.2m (~$0.62m) from SEK 11.3m; payables still ~3x year-ago SEK 4.4m, further normalisation likely.
