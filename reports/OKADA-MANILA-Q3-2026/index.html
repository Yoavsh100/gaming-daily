Okada Manila

The one-line verdict

A flat-revenue, hold-flattered quarter. Q3-26 revenue of ₱7,841m (~$124.7m) was unchanged YoY and GGR rose just 0.9% to ₱7.04bn (~$111.9m) only because live mass-table hold jumped 380bps to 32.2%; VIP rolling volume fell another 28% and adjusted segment EBITDA dropped 13% to ₱962m (~$15.3m), a 12.3% margin (-180bps). The sequential rebound from a dire Q2 (₱333m) keeps parent UEC's "2H26 return to IR operating profit" target technically alive, but hold-adjusted GGR was still down ~1% and the cost base grew 2% on flat revenue — this is stabilisation, not recovery.

1Executive summary

  • Headline: flat revenue, lower profit. Tiger Resort, Leisure & Entertainment Inc (TRLEI, operator of Okada Manila; FYE 31 Dec; unlisted, 100% owned by TSE-listed Universal Entertainment Corp, 6425) reported preliminary Q3-26 (Jul–Sep) total revenue of ₱7,841m (~$124.7m), essentially unchanged YoY (Gaming Intelligence calls it a 'marginal improvement'), with casino GGR +0.9% to ₱7.04bn (~$111.9m) and adjusted segment EBITDA -13.0% to ₱962m (~$15.3m). There is no sell-side consensus for the unlisted entity; the release is unaudited and precedes UEC's consolidated Q3 (expected mid-Nov).
  • Mix shift is the whole story. Mass-table GGR +19.7% to ₱3,010m (~$47.9m) more than offset VIP -21.4% to ₱1,150m (~$18.3m), gaming machines -4.0% to ₱2,880m (~$45.8m) and non-gaming -6.9% to ₱804m (~$12.8m). VIP is now 16.3% of GGR (Q3-25: 20.9%; FY23: ~35% per Fitch); mass tables 42.8%, machines 40.9%.
  • Luck did the heavy lifting on mass. Live mass-table hold was 32.2% vs 28.4% a year ago (+380bps); at the prior-year hold, mass revenue would have been ~₱2.62bn, i.e. ~₱390m lower (estimate). Mass drop grew a more modest +5.4% to ₱9.01bn (~$143m).
  • But slots were unlucky, so net luck was roughly neutral. Machine win rate fell to 5.1% from 5.7% on handle +5.6% — at prior-year hold machine GGR would have been ~₱340m higher. VIP hold 3.9% vs 3.6% added ~₱90m. Our hold-adjusted GGR is ~₱6.90bn, -1.2% YoY (estimate) — the 'return to growth' is a rounding event, not a trend break.
  • VIP remains in structural retreat. Rolling chip volume -27.7% to ₱29.4bn (~$468m), after -22.5% in Q2 (when average VIP tables were cut to 73 from 87). 9M-26 VIP GGR -35.1% to ₱3.59bn (~$57.1m). The POGO ban and weak Korean/Chinese visitation are not reversing.
  • Sequential recovery from a trough quarter. GGR +16.1% QoQ (Q2-26: ₱6.06bn) and adj. EBITDA ~2.9x Q2's ₱333m (~$5.3m); margin +750bps QoQ to 12.3%. Mass-table GGR +36.7% QoQ, VIP +15% QoQ, machines +0.7% QoQ.
  • Cost base is rising on flat revenue. Revenue less adj. EBITDA (opex incl. PAGCOR licence fees and promotional spend) was ₱6,879m, +2.2% YoY (~+₱144m) — consistent with UEC's May warning that customer-acquisition costs are rising amid 'more intense' Manila competition and its August reference to 'strategic investments'. All of the EBITDA decline is cost, not revenue.
  • Footfall up, spend per head down. Visitors +11.8% to 1.45m, hotel occupancy 95.0% (+810bps) — but ADR -4.5% to ₱9,473 (~$151), RevPAR +4.4% to ₱9,000 (~$143), and non-gaming revenue fell 6.9%. GGR per visitor was ₱4,853 (~$77), -9.6% YoY (estimate). The pattern (more rooms, cheaper, less cash non-gaming) reads as comp-driven reinvestment in mass/premium-mass.
  • Nine-month picture remains poor. 9M-26 GGR ₱19.56bn (~$311m), -10.6%; total revenue ~₱22.2bn (~$354m), -9.7% (derived); adj. EBITDA just under ₱2.13bn (~$33.9m), -47.4% (GGRAsia; AGB -47.3%); margin ~9.6% vs ~16.4%. Visitors 4.33m (+5.4%).
  • Parent target: IR back to operating profit in 2H26 — unchanged, marginally on track. With H1-26 results (10 Aug) UEC guided to a 2H26 IR-segment operating profit after an H1 operating loss of ¥1.37bn (~$8.7m) and adj. EBITDA ¥2.74bn (~$17.3m), -62.5%. H1 implies ~¥2bn/quarter of D&A-type charges (~₱0.8bn), so Q3's ₱962m EBITDA points to only a ~₱0.1–0.15bn segment operating profit (estimate) — achieved, but hold-dependent.
  • Recovery levers are small or unproven. UEC's plan: OKADA PLAY online (online mass-table GGR ₱64m vs ₱44m — <1% of GGR), premium-mass acquisition, MICE/large-scale events, Japan/Asia marketing, cost optimisation; Disney-themed hospitality offerings start in Q4-26. Hotel named 'Best Hotel – Manila' at TTG Travel Awards 2026.
  • Credit is the binding constraint. Fitch cut UEC to CCC+ from B- (Jul-26) and on 3 Oct forecast negative FCF in 2026–27 (FCF margin -3.1% / -0.1%), annual EBITDA of ~¥19bn (~$120m) through 2028 versus ~¥20bn (~$127m) needed for cash interest plus capex, EBITDAR fixed-charge cover below 1.5x, and derives bond recovery from Japan operations only because Philippine banks rank senior on the IR assets. S&P moved its outlook to negative in May-26. No significant maturities before Aug-2029.
  • Parent group is being carried by pachislot. UEC H1-26 net sales ¥76.6bn (~$485m, +23.1%), adj. EBITDA ¥15.2bn (~$96m, +148%), net income ¥62m (~$0.4m) vs a ¥9.9bn loss; IR net sales ¥30.13bn (~$191m, -13.0%). Okada Manila is now the group's sole drag.
  • Market backdrop is a correction, not a cycle. PAGCOR's chairman has warned industry GGR could fall up to 19% from 2025's ₱396bn; Philippine GGR fell ~20% in Q2-26; Maybank expects land-based pressure through 2H26 on inflation, utility costs and absent high-rollers; online migration continues. New PAGCOR slot-rebate rules for licensed casinos took effect 14 Oct (headline only; detail unverified).
  • Peso at a record low is a translation headwind. USD/PHP closed ₱62.87 on 14 Oct and breached ₱63 on 15 Oct; UEC's notes are USD-denominated while Okada EBITDA is PHP, shrinking dollar coverage even on flat peso earnings.
  • Management emphasis vs. what is buried. The release leads with visitation +11.8%, 95% occupancy and mass +19.7%; it does not discuss the hold uplift, the 2% opex creep, the ADR cut or the trajectory of VIP table count. Fitch's framing — mass growth 'insufficient to offset' structural VIP decline — is the more honest read.
  • Thesis read-through. Volume indicators (mass drop +5.4%, machine handle +5.6%, visitors +12%) say the mass floor has stabilised; profit indicators (EBITDA -13% on flat revenue, 9M margin ~9.6%) say the property is paying for that stability. Annualised Q3 EBITDA (~₱3.8bn / ~$61m) is below what the parent needs from the IR to be cash-neutral; the burden stays on pachislot and, ultimately, the 2029 refinancing window.
  • Share price. TRLEI is unlisted; the move in parent UEC (6425) on 14–15 Oct could not be verified from the sources read [unverified].

3Segment deep-dive: Gaming — Okada Manila casino floor (VIP / Mass tables / Gaming machines)

GGR +0.9% to ₱7.04bn (~$111.9m) on a 380bps mass-hold tailwind; hold-adjusted GGR ~-1%; VIP volume -28%.

Headline GGR

Casino GGR rose 0.9% YoY to ₱7,037m (~$111.9m) in the September quarter, which the parent presents as stabilisation after four double-digit declines.

  • GGR +16.1% QoQ; prior declines were Q4-25 -34%, Q1-26 -17.2%, Q2-26 -14.6%.
  • The composition matters more than the headline.
  • Analyst luck-adjusted GGR is ~₱6.90bn, -1.2% YoY: favourable mass hold and unfavourable machine hold broadly cancel.
  • The real underlying story is the mix shift from VIP to mass.

Mass tables

Mass-market tables generated ₱3,010m (~$47.9m), +19.7% YoY and +36.7% QoQ, but mostly on hold; underlying growth is mid-single digits.

  • Mass drop ₱9,010m (~$143.3m), +5.4%; live-table win rate 32.2% versus 28.4% a year earlier.
  • Live mass revenue +19.2%; online mass tables via OKADA PLAY ₱64m (~$1.0m) versus ₱44m, still under 1% of GGR.
  • At a 28.4% live hold mass would have been roughly ₱2.62bn, +4.5% YoY, in line with drop growth.
  • Respectable in a Philippine market running ~20% down but nowhere near the headline.

VIP

VIP table GGR fell 21.4% YoY to ₱1,150m (~$18.3m) on rolling chip volume -27.7%; the decline is treated as structural.

  • Rolling chip volume ₱29,410m (~$467.8m), a further ~28% below Q2's ₱40.6bn (~$646m); win rate 3.9% (vs 3.6%) cushioned revenue by ~₱90m.
  • Q2 saw VIP win -56% and average VIP tables cut to 73 from 87; Q3 does not disclose table count, read as a deliberate omission.
  • VIP is 16.3% of GGR versus 20.9% a year ago and ~35% in 2023 (Fitch); 9M-26 VIP GGR ₱3.59bn (~$57.1m), -35.1%.
  • With POGO banned and Korean and mainland Chinese visitation depressed, no catalyst; question is whether space is redeployed to premium mass.

Gaming machines

Machine GGR was ₱2,880m (~$45.8m), -4.0% YoY but +0.7% QoQ, as handle rose 5.6% while win rate fell to 5.1% from 5.7%.

  • At the prior-year win rate machine GGR would have been ~₱3.22bn, up ~7%; implied handle roughly ₱56bn (~$0.9bn) is a genuine volume positive.
  • Whether the 60bps hold decline is variance or structural loosening (free-play, promotional reinvestment, PAGCOR slot-rebate rules from 14 October) is the key Q4 question.
  • Machines are 41% of GGR and the single largest profit pool on the floor.

Profitability and costs

Revenue was flat while adjusted segment EBITDA fell 13.0% to ₱962m (~$15.3m), a 12.3% margin, -180bps YoY but +750bps QoQ.

  • Q2 margin trough was 4.8%; revenue less EBITDA (PAGCOR fees, promotional allowances, labour, marketing) rose 2.2% to ₱6,879m (~$109.4m).
  • UEC flagged in May that Manila competition was 'more intense' with rising customer-acquisition costs, and in August that Q2 included 'strategic investments'; Q3 shows spend persisting.
  • Promotional cost as a percentage of drop or handle is undisclosed; wanted before accepting mass-volume growth is margin-accretive rather than bought.
  • 9M-26 GGR ₱19.56bn (~$311m), -10.6%; adjusted EBITDA just under ₱2.13bn (~$33.9m), -47.4%; Q3 is 45% of year-to-date profit.

Guidance

No numeric segment guidance; UEC targets an IR-segment operating profit in 2H26, and Q3 EBITDA covers depreciation-type charges with only ~₱0.1–0.15bn to spare.

  • Levers cited: online expansion, premium-mass acquisition, Japan/Asia marketing and cost optimisation.
  • H1 implies ~₱0.8bn/quarter of depreciation-type charges; spare cover is the analyst's estimate.

4Segment deep-dive: Non-gaming, hotel & visitation

Visitors +11.8% and occupancy 95%, but ADR -4.5% and non-gaming revenue -6.9% to ₱804m (~$12.8m) — comps, not cash.

Visitation and occupancy

Visitors rose 11.8% to approximately 1.45 million and the 993-room hotel ran at 95.0% occupancy, versus 86.9% a year earlier.

  • Occupancy was 91.4% in Q2; the parent leads with the headline.
  • Analyst reads the occupancy figure as a marketing input rather than a hospitality output.

Hotel yield

ADR fell 4.5% to ₱9,473 (~$151) and RevPAR rose only 4.4% to ₱9,000 (~$143), down from ₱9,242 (~$147) in Q2.

  • Filling 8 more points of rooms while cutting rate and seeing cash non-gaming fall implies more complimentary occupancy.
  • Consistent with the mass-reinvestment push visible on the gaming floor.

Non-gaming revenue

Non-gaming revenue declined 6.9% YoY and 13.4% QoQ to ₱804m (~$12.8m), 10.3% of total revenue versus 11.0% a year ago and 13.3% in Q2.

  • Covers hotel, F&B, retail and entertainment.
  • 9M-26 non-gaming revenue ₱2,680m (~$42.6m), -2.2%, on visitation of 4.33 million, +5.4%.
  • Management said in August it had 'intensified' efforts to expand non-gaming revenue via large-scale events; Q3 numbers do not yet show it.

Spend per visitor

GGR per visitor was ~₱4,853 (~$77), -9.6% YoY, and total revenue per visitor ~₱5,408 (~$86), -10.5%; spend per visitor is deteriorating.

  • Q3-25 GGR per visitor ~₱5,369; nine-month GGR per visitor ~₱4,517, -15% (all analyst-derived).
  • Partly arithmetic of losing high-value VIP players while adding low-value event foot traffic.
  • Also underlines that incremental visitation is being bought at lower yield.

Forward catalysts

Catalysts are Disney-themed hospitality from Q4-26, MICE promotion and the TTG 'Best Hotel – Manila' award, none quantified; a genuine turn needs RevPAR growth with stable ADR.

  • President and COO Nobuki Sato highlighted the TTG Travel Awards 2026 recognition.
  • Peso at a record low (₱63/$ on 15 October) improves inbound economics for Korean and Japanese visitors, the main source-market upside.
  • Analyst wants Q4 non-gaming revenue tracking occupancy before crediting the hotel with a turn.

5Segment deep-dive: Parent context — Universal Entertainment Corp (TSE: 6425): IR segment & credit

Group profit is pachislot-driven; the IR segment lost ¥1.37bn (~$8.7m) in H1-26 and Fitch (CCC+) sees negative FCF through 2027.

IR segment H1-26

IR net sales fell 13.0% YoY to ¥30.13bn (~$191m), the operating loss widened to ¥1.37bn (~$8.7m) from ¥1.32bn, and adjusted EBITDA fell 62.5% to ¥2.74bn (~$17.3m).

  • UEC consolidates Okada Manila as its Integrated Resort segment in yen; the TRLEI peso release leads the segment print due mid-November.
  • H1-26 is Jan–Jun; UEC's 'second quarter' is cumulative.
  • Q3 TRLEI numbers suggest the 2H26 operating-profit target is being met by a thin margin and with help from hold.
“promoting cost optimization and prioritizing the allocation of management resources”Management

Group results

Group swung to net income of ¥62m (~$0.4m) from a ¥9.87bn loss, entirely on the Japanese pachinko/pachislot recovery.

  • Net sales ¥76.6bn (~$485m), +23.1%; adjusted EBITDA ¥15.2bn (~$96m), +148%; segment operating profit ¥14.2bn (~$90m), +174%.
  • Management intends to return the IR segment to operating profit in 2H26 via OKADA PLAY, premium-mass customers, Japan/Asia marketing and large-scale events.

Credit ratings

Fitch downgraded UEC to CCC+ from B- in July 2026 and models negative free cash flow in 2026 and 2027; the credit picture frames everything.

  • Fitch cited 'material deterioration' in the IR segment; FCF margins of -3.1% and -0.1%; EBITDAR fixed-charge coverage at or below 1.5x through 2027.
  • Annual EBITDA of about ¥19bn (~$120m) through 2028 against roughly ¥20bn (~$127m) needed for cash interest and capex.
  • Fitch derives USD senior note recovery from post-restructuring Japanese cash flows only, as local banks hold senior claims on Philippine IR assets.
  • S&P revised its outlook to negative in May 2026 on stiff Philippine competition.
  • No significant maturities before August 2029 buys time but not optionality; record-low peso erodes dollar coverage even on flat local earnings.

Analyst read

Okada Manila does not need to grow to satisfy the parent's near-term objectives; it needs to stop consuming cash.

  • Annualising Q3's ₱962m gives ~₱3.8bn (~$61m, ~¥9.7bn) of IR EBITDA, roughly half the group total Fitch models and insufficient to cover IR's share of interest.
  • Swing factor (i): whether the mass floor holds Q3 volumes at normal hold without further promotional escalation.
  • Swing factor (ii): whether UEC monetises or restructures anything at the IR level.
  • Swing factor (iii): whether pachislot demand holds long enough to carry the refinancing conversation into 2028.

6Highlights & lowlights

▲ Highlights

  • Mass-floor volumes are genuinely growing: mass table drop +5.4% to ₱9.01bn (~$143m), machine handle +5.6%, visitors +11.8% to 1.45m — in a Philippine market running ~20% down. Hold-adjusted mass GGR ~+4–5%. structural
  • Sharp sequential recovery from the Q2 trough: GGR +16.1% QoQ, adj. EBITDA ₱962m (~$15.3m) vs ₱333m (~$5.3m), margin 12.3% vs 4.8% — enough to put UEC's 2H26 IR operating-profit target narrowly on track (est. ~₱0.1–0.15bn Q3 segment EBIT). mixed
  • Live mass-table hold of 32.2% (+380bps) added ~₱390m to mass GGR — a windfall, but one that partly offset an unlucky 5.1% machine hold (-60bps, ~-₱340m), so the reported GGR is not far from luck-neutral. one-off
  • Hotel metrics at record levels: 95.0% occupancy (+810bps), RevPAR +4.4% to ₱9,000 (~$143); 'Best Hotel – Manila' TTG award; Disney-themed offerings launch in Q4-26. mixed

Guidance bridge.
