# Danske Spil () — 9M 2026 Analyst Report
** · Reporting currency: DKK (tables in millions; USD equivalents at USD/DKK 6.76 as of 12 Nov 2026) · Results released 12 Nov 2026**
**Market reaction:** Not applicable — Danske Spil A/S is unlisted (Danish state-controlled company, CVR 64 01 17 15, with national sports federations DIF and DGI as minority shareholders); there is no quoted equity or public debt to reference. No market reaction can be reported.

> A flat nine months that hides a Q3 downturn: 9M-26 BSI (GGR) +0.5% to DKK 3,841m (~$568m) and net profit +1.5% to DKK 1,547m (~$229m) were carried entirely by H1; derived Q3 BSI fell 1.4% to DKK 1,255m (~$186m) and net profit fell 4.2% to DKK 503m (~$74m) as the lottery jackpot cycle turned (Danske Lotteri Spil -5.9%) and only cost cuts and a +7.5% licensed-games segment held EBIT margin flat at 52.7%. FY26 guidance (BSI DKK 5,200–5,300m / net profit DKK 1,950–2,050m) is unchanged and, with an October Eurojackpot max-pool run, remains achievable in the upper half — but the equity story is a monopoly lottery cash-cow with a shrinking retail footprint and a sub-scale, duty-heavy online business that is not out-growing its market.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Headline in line with the run-rate, no consensus to beat.** Danske Spil A/S (unlisted, Danish state-controlled; FY ends 31 Dec; IFRS recognition/measurement; unaudited quarterly) reported 9M-26 gross gaming revenue (BSI) of DKK 3,841m (~$568m), +0.5% (+DKK 21m) YoY, EBIT of DKK 2,004m (~$296m), +2.2%, and net profit of DKK 1,547m (~$229m), +1.5% (+DKK 23m). All figures are reported IFRS; the company publishes no adjusted metrics.
- **Q3 standalone turned negative.** Derived Q3-26 (9M less H1): BSI DKK 1,255m (~$186m), -1.4% YoY and -9.2% QoQ; EBIT DKK 662m (~$98m), -1.3%; net profit DKK 503m (~$74m), -4.2% YoY. The 2026 quarterly BSI path is now Q1 -5.6%, Q2 +8.7% (record Eurojackpot May draw + World Cup), Q3 -1.4% — i.e. the year is being made or lost by the jackpot calendar.
- **Mix: licensed games up, lottery down — the division that drove the quarter was Danske Lotteri Spil (negatively).** 9M BSI: Danske Lotteri Spil DKK 2,193m (~$324m), -2.1% (Q3 -5.9%); Danske Licens Spil DKK 1,238m (~$183m), +6.6% (Q3 +7.5%); Klasselotteriet DKK 205m (~$30m), -2.4%; Elite Gaming DKK 204m (~$30m), -2.9%. Management cites fewer Eurojackpot high pools, smaller Lotto pools and lower in-store Quick scratchcard sales, only partly offset by Vikinglotto; licensed growth came from online casino and the new poker platform.
- **Profit growth is a cost story, not a revenue story.** Gross profit from games fell 0.3% to DKK 2,881m (~$426m) (margin 75.0%, -67bps) as gaming duties rose DKK 17m to DKK 474m (~$70m) and other gaming-related (platform/content) costs rose DKK 34m (+16.6%). Below that, 'other costs incl. D&A' fell DKK 55m (-5.7%) to DKK 902m (~$133m): other external costs -DKK 30m (lower marketing), staff -DKK 13m, D&A -DKK 13m. Every krone of the DKK 44m EBIT improvement came from opex.
- **Margins: group EBIT margin 52.2% (+87bps 9M); Q3 flat at 52.7%.** Danske Lotteri Spil EBIT margin 73.7% (+134bps), Danske Licens Spil 18.6% (+264bps), Klasselotteriet 71.2% (+598bps). Derived EBITDA (EBIT + D&A) DKK 2,122m (~$314m), +1.5%, margin 55.2% (+51bps). Net margin 40.3% (+38bps); effective tax rate 22.2%.
- **Guidance unchanged, but now explicitly carved around the Elite Gaming disposal.** FY26 BSI DKK 5,200–5,300m (~$769–784m) and net profit DKK 1,950–2,050m (~$288–303m), 'excluding the conditional sale of Elite Gaming'. Implied Q4-26: BSI DKK 1,359–1,459m vs Q4-25 DKK 1,338m (+1.6% to +9.0%); net profit DKK 403–503m vs Q4-25 DKK 484m (-16.7% to +3.9%). The profit range midpoint implies a Q4 decline, which looks conservative given October's Eurojackpot max pool.
- **Portfolio simplification: Elite Gaming (land-based slots) being sold.** Announced 17 Sep 2026 to 87KDSL A/S (owner of Dan-Automater Struer; Erhvervsinvest-backed), approved at an EGM on 16 Sep, conditional on Danish Competition and Consumer Authority clearance; price undisclosed. Elite Gaming is 5.3% of 9M BSI but only 0.4% of EBIT (DKK 9m, ~$1m) with segment assets of DKK 72m (~$11m); effect will be booked on completion.
- **Capital position is fortress-like and debt-free.** 30 Sep: cash DKK 1,399m (~$207m) + securities DKK 832m (~$123m) = DKK 2,231m (~$330m), +2.5% YoY; equity DKK 1,851m (~$274m), equity ratio 58.8%; no borrowings disclosed (long-term liabilities DKK 97m). 9M operating cash flow DKK 1,860m (~$275m), -3.4%, 93% of EBIT (vs 98%); capex (additions) DKK 127m (~$19m), -8.6%; derived FCF DKK 1,733m (~$256m).
- **Distributions: DKK 1,094m (~$162m) paid to owners in 9M-26** (vs DKK 1,164m), per the equity statement — Danske Spil distributes essentially all profit via the state 'udlodningsmidler' (FY25: DKK 1.8bn to good causes; total societal contribution incl. duties and corporate tax DKK 3,148m (~$466m)). No buybacks (state-owned, DKK 1m share capital; EPS not meaningful).
- **Financial items are noise but swung the quarter.** 9M financial items -DKK 16m (vs -DKK 5m); Q3 alone -DKK 17m after +DKK 26m in Q2 (unrealised securities gains) and -DKK 25m in Q1 — DKK 43m QoQ swing explains most of the Q3 PBT drop (-15.9% QoQ) beyond operations.
- **Retail network is contracting fast.** Dealers 3,464 at 30 Sep vs 3,759 a year ago (-295, -7.8%) and 3,622 at 30 Jun (-158 in one quarter). Dealer commission fell DKK 21m (-7.8%) to 6.4% of BSI; c.70% of group sales were online in FY25. Good for margin, bad for Quick scratchcards and impulse lottery sales.
- **Headcount up, staff cost down, capitalisation up — watch.** Average FTEs 480 (+1.7%) while staff costs fell 4.5%; additions to the reserve for development projects (capitalised development) rose to DKK 122m (~$18m) from DKK 99m. Not alarming at this scale, but P&L support from capitalisation should be monitored.
- **No formal cost-transformation programme exists.** Savings are ad hoc (marketing, D&A roll-off, procurement) with no quantified multi-year target disclosed — n/a. The 9M opex reduction of DKK 55m is the only hard number.
- **Market context (Danish Gambling Authority, secondary): Q3 was a volatile betting quarter and a strong casino quarter.** July total market +17.2% (online casino +22%, betting +21.3%, World Cup final); August -7.1% to DKK 678m (betting -42.7% to DKK 132m; online casino +12.5% to DKK 415m); September +22.1% to DKK 685m (betting +64.6%; online casino DKK 400m, +17.9%, 58% of market GGR). Danske Licens Spil's +7.5% total Q3 growth implies it is trailing market online-casino growth of mid/high-teens — share loss risk, with Stake's entry flagged in the FY25 report.
- **Regulation: 'Spilpakke 1' (broad political agreement, 24 Oct 2025)** — advertising restrictions (ban on celebrities/experts in gambling ads, minimum 25-year age for ad participants, TV 'whistle-to-whistle' ban in the agreement), tougher action against unlicensed operators, dealer approval scheme. The implementing bill (L 127) lapsed; new legislation was proposed in October 2026 for 2027. Marketing curbs structurally favour the highest-awareness incumbent — Danske Spil welcomed the deal — but also cap its own Oddset marketing.
- **Responsible-gambling positioning is explicitly a revenue headwind.** Management again states its higher standards (AI 'spilscanner', deposit limits, under-25 online spend held below DKK 130/month in FY25) weigh on BSI versus the rest of the market. This is the quid pro quo for the lottery monopoly and should be read as a permanent growth drag on Danske Licens Spil.
- **Lottery monopoly remains the engine.** Danske Lotteri Spil delivered 81% of 9M segment EBIT (DKK 1,617m of DKK 2,002m ex-parent); Klasselotteriet another 7%. Licensed games (DLiS) are 32% of BSI but only 11% of segment EBIT after the 28% GGR duty and rising platform costs.
- **Management/board: no changes.** CEO Nikolas Lyhne-Knudsen and CFO Søren Skovdal Rasmussen signed the report in Copenhagen on 12 Nov 2026; chair Lars Krarup, deputy Charlotte Bach Thomassen; two new employee-elected directors since Q3-25. Subsequent events: none disclosed.
- **Thesis read-through.** Earnings are defensible (52% EBIT margin, zero debt, ~90%+ cash conversion) but structurally ex-growth: FY26 guidance midpoints (BSI DKK 5,250m, net profit DKK 2,000m) are +1.8%/-0.4% on FY25. The variables that matter are the Eurojackpot/Lotto pool cycle (Q4 starts with a 750m max pool in October), the pace of online-casino share loss, and the 2027 advertising regime — not anything management controls quarter to quarter.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — Danske Lotteri Spil (lottery monopoly: Lotto, Eurojackpot, Vikinglotto, Keno, Quick, Alt eller Intet, Spil Sammen, Plus)

*The whole 9M shortfall landed in Q3: BSI -5.9% to DKK 717m on a soft jackpot cycle and retail scratchcard erosion, yet EBIT margin rose 164bps to 74.5% on marketing and commission savings.*

### Market position

**DLS retains the statutory lottery monopoly and generated DKK 3,011m (~$445m) of a just-under DKK 3.5bn category in 2025, roughly 85%.**

- Danske Lotteri Spil holds the statutory monopoly for number games and scratchcards in Denmark.
- On Danish Gambling Authority 2025 figures via secondary coverage, DLS alone was roughly 85% of category GGR.
- Together with Klasselotteriet the share is over 90% [estimate from secondary data].

### Q3 revenue shortfall

**The whole 9M shortfall landed in Q3: BSI DKK 717m (~$106m), -5.9% year on year, after an exactly flat first half.**

- Nine-month BSI DKK 2,193m (~$324m), down 2.1% or DKK 46m; H1 was DKK 1,476m vs 1,477m.
- Q3 fell 12.2% sequentially from a DKK 817m Q2 inflated by a record May Eurojackpot draw (358,000 tickets for a DKK 750m draw) and Easter Lotto.
- Management cited fewer high Eurojackpot pools, smaller Lotto pools and lower in-store Quick scratchcard sales, partly offset by Vikinglotto growth.
- Demanding comparison: Q3-25 had grown 5.2% on Eurojackpot and Quick strength.

### Profit and margin

**Profit held up far better than revenue: 9M EBIT DKK 1,617m (~$239m), -0.2%, with Q3 margin up 164bps to 74.5%.**

- 9M EBIT fell DKK 4m on DKK 46m lower BSI, lifting margin 134bps to 73.7%; Q3 EBIT DKK 534m (~$79m), -3.8% (Q2-26 margin: 75.4%).
- Dealer commission fell DKK 21m (-7.8%) to 6.4% of BSI as sales migrate online (c.70% of group sales digital in FY25).
- Marketing cut within other external costs (-DKK 30m); D&A down DKK 13m; segment capex additions DKK 51m vs DKK 73m.
- DLS is 81% of nine-month segment EBIT; profit flows to state udlodningsmidler (DKK 1.8bn in FY25), so the state prioritises stability over growth.

### Retail footprint

**Retail distribution is shrinking at a pace that deserves attention: 3,464 dealers at 30 September, -295 (-7.8%) year on year.**

- 3,622 dealers at 30 June, so 158 outlets lost in a single quarter.
- In-store Quick decline is the first-order consequence; second-order risk is losing impulse and jackpot-chasing purchases from the roughly 30% buying in kiosks and supermarkets.
- Management frames the online shift as responsibility-led (limits, AI monitoring, Spil med omtanke tools) and margin-accretive; numbers support the margin point.
- A 7.8% annual contraction in physical distribution is not costless for a product whose weekly habit is anchored in retail.

### Q4 outlook

**Q4 set-up is more favourable than Q3; we pencil in roughly flat to +3% Q4-26 BSI of DKK 770–795m (estimate).**

- Eurojackpot pool rolled from DKK 508m on 25 September to the DKK 750m (~$111m) cap by 6 October, staying at cap for at least three draws until won 13 October — first max-pool run since May.
- Hard comparison: Q4-25 included a DKK 149m Danish Eurojackpot winner on 2 December and record Christmas sales for Det Store Jule Lotto and Quick.
- Our derived FY25 Q4 DLS BSI is DKK 772m (FY25 DKK 3,011m less 9M DKK 2,239m); implies FY26 DLS BSI about DKK 2,960–2,990m, -1% to -2%.
- The company does not guide at segment level.
- Spilpakke 1 marketing curbs (no celebrities or experts, 25-year minimum ad age) apply to lottery too but are a minor constraint given saturated brand awareness.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — Danske Licens Spil (licensed games: Oddset/eOddset/Tips sportsbook, Casino, Live Casino, Tivoli Casino, Poker, Bingo)

*Growth segment of the group (+6.6% 9M / +7.5% Q3) with EBIT +24% and margin +264bps — but with no product split disclosed and the Danish online-casino market growing mid/high-teens, the evidence points to share loss.*

### Growth and EBIT

**Growth segment of the group: 9M BSI +6.6% to DKK 1,238m (~$183m) and EBIT +24.3% to DKK 230m, margin +264bps to 18.6%.**

- Derived Q3 BSI DKK 403m (~$60m), +7.5% year on year, -5.2% sequentially from a World Cup-boosted Q2 of DKK 425m; 2026 trajectory Q1 +3.5%, Q2 +9.0%, Q3 +7.5%.
- Management cited continued online casino progress and the new poker platform launched in H1; sports betting not called out, though H1 noted a strong World Cup for Oddset.
- Q3 EBIT DKK 73m (~$11m), +10.6%, margin 18.1% (+51bps year on year, -47bps versus Q2).
- FY25 segment BSI DKK 1,584m (~$234m) and net profit DKK 171m were both down on 2024, so 2026 is a clear recovery year.
- No disclosure of handle, sports margin, promotional spend, monthly actives or ARPU — impossible to separate volume, price and luck.

### Market share

**With Danish online casino growing mid/high-teens, DLiS's +7.5% in Q3 points to continued share loss to international operators.**

- July market GGR +17.2% (online casino +22%, betting +21.3%); August -7.1% to DKK 678m as betting collapsed 42.7% to DKK 132m while online casino rose 12.5% to DKK 415m.
- September rebounded 22.1% to DKK 685m on betting +64.6% and online casino DKK 400m, +17.9%, 58% of the month's market GGR.
- In 2025 Danish online casino was DKK 4.31bn, +12.1%, or 38% of a c.DKK 11.5bn market.
- FY25 report flagged Stake's market entry and 'hard competition'; no share figure given as Danske Spil does not publish one and no verified betting denominator exists.

### Segment economics

**Economics are structurally thin: 28% duty and rising platform fees mean each incremental licensed krone carries a materially lower gross margin than lottery.**

- Group gaming duty rose DKK 17m to DKK 474m, which management tied to higher DLiS BSI.
- Other gaming-related costs (OpenBet sportsbook, EveryMatrix casino/bingo platform and content fees) rose DKK 34m or 16.6%.
- Group gross-profit margin dropped 185bps to 74.5% in Q3 despite only a two-percentage-point mix shift.
- Incremental EBIT margin on the DKK 77m BSI increase was 58%, achieved by cutting marketing — self-limiting where competitors spend aggressively.

### Policy and Q4 outlook

**Policy is the swing factor for two years; we estimate Q4 BSI DKK 445–455m, +5% to +8%, with EBIT margin around 17–18%.**

- Spilpakke 1 (24 October 2025) proposed banning celebrities and experts, a 25-year ad age, a whistle-to-whistle TV ban, dealer approval and stronger enforcement; the bill lapsed and a new proposal was tabled October 2026 for 2027.
- Net relative effect is positive for Danske Spil's brand awareness, but Oddset loses its sports-ambassador playbook.
- Management stresses responsibility standards (deposit limits, AI 'spilscanner', under-25 online spend below DKK 130 a month in FY25) cost BSI — a permanent structural growth discount.
- Q4 estimate is on a derived Q4-25 base of DKK 423m (FY25 DKK 1,584m less 9M DKK 1,161m), with no major football tournament and usual casino seasonality.

## 5. Segment deep-dive — Det Danske Klasselotteri (Klasselotteriet, monthly class lottery since 1753)

*Small, high-margin and lapping a product-change boost: BSI -2.4% on fewer players and a higher payout ratio, but EBIT +6.6% with margin up ~600bps to 71.2%.*

### Revenue

**Nine-month BSI fell 2.4% (DKK 5m) to DKK 205m (~$30m) as the business laps the '15 er magisk' product-change boost.**

- Derived Q3 BSI DKK 67m (~$10m), -2.9% year on year and -4.3% versus Q2.
- Management describes a 'minor decline' linked to the number of players, and in Q1 also to a higher payout ratio.
- 9M-25 BSI had jumped 14% (DKK 210m vs 184m) on the product change implemented in Q4-24, so this is lapping a step-up, not deteriorating.

### Margin expansion

**EBIT rose 6.6% to DKK 146m (~$22m) with margin up 598bps to 71.2% despite the softer top line.**

- Derived Q3 EBIT DKK 48m (~$7m), +4.3%, at a 71.6% margin (Q3-25: 66.7%; Q2-26: 71.4%).
- Essentially no variable platform cost and limited marketing; expansion is mostly operating leverage plus lower amortisation.
- Capex is negligible (DKK 2m).

**Subscription-based monthly draws give annuity-like revenue from a loyal, older base; demographic attrition is the risk.**

- Draw on the 15th of each month with a DKK 46m prize pool and up to 15 million-krone prizes, sold as whole, half, quarter and eighth tickets.
- The player-count comment hints at demographic attrition.
- Segment assets DKK 392m, +4%, are heavy relative to revenue, reflecting cash and prize provisions rather than operating assets.

- FY25 not separately verified from primary sources.
- At a 71% margin it contributes 7% of segment EBIT on 5% of BSI.
- A steady contributor to the state distributions.

## 6. Segment deep-dive — Elite Gaming (land-based gaming machines) — held for sale

*Non-core and on its way out: BSI -2.9% to DKK 204m, EBIT DKK 9m (4.4% margin); conditional sale to 87KDSL announced 17 Sep 2026, price undisclosed, guidance excludes the effect.*

### Results

**Non-core and on its way out: 9M BSI -2.9% to DKK 204m (~$30m), EBIT DKK 9m at a 4.4% margin.**

- Derived Q3 BSI flat year on year at DKK 67m (~$10m), -4.3% versus Q2; EBIT up from DKK 8m.
- The business is 5.3% of group BSI but 0.4% of EBIT — the entire rationale for the disposal.
- Operates land-based machines in Danish arcades and restaurants, has removed more than a quarter of its machines shifting to server-based digital terminals; workforce 20–50 (secondary registry data).

### Conditional sale

**On 17 September 2026 Danske Spil agreed to sell the subsidiary to 87KDSL A/S at an undisclosed price, pending competition clearance.**

- 87KDSL already owns Dan-Automater Struer A/S (sister company Compugame-DAE manufactures machines) and is backed by private-equity fund Erhvervsinvest (secondary).
- Approved at an extraordinary general meeting on 16 September; conditional on Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen clearance.
- Buyer's stated aim is to secure profitable slot-machine earnings through consolidation.

> “limited strategic coherence” — CEO

### Accounting treatment

**Financial effect recognised only on completion; no held-for-sale reclassification or gain/loss estimate given, and guidance excludes the sale.**

- Segment still carries DKK 72m (~$11m) of assets, down from DKK 94m.
- Given undisclosed consideration against a small, declining asset base, we would not expect a material gain, but a modest one is plausible on the buyer's synergy case.
- Any such item sits outside the unchanged guidance, which is explicitly 'without recognition of the conditional sale'.

### Deconsolidation impact

**Deconsolidation would strip roughly DKK 270m annualised BSI (c.5% of group) and about DKK 12m EBIT, modestly lifting group margins.**

- Machine duty and site commissions are high relative to lottery, so gross-profit and EBIT margins rise.
- Removes the group's only exposure to the land-based slot regime, including Spilpakke 1 dealer-approval and arcade-definition provisions.
- For modelling we keep Elite Gaming consolidated through year-end; Q4 closing possible but not assumed.

## 7. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- Licensed games are compounding: Danske Licens Spil BSI +6.6% (9M) / +7.5% (Q3) to DKK 1,238m (~$183m) with EBIT +24.3% to DKK 230m (~$34m) and margin +264bps to 18.6%, driven by online casino and the new poker platform — the segment is already ahead of its FY25 net-profit run-rate. _(structural)_
- Cost discipline converted flat revenue into profit growth: 'other costs incl. D&A' -DKK 55m (-5.7%) to DKK 902m (~$133m); group EBIT margin +87bps to 52.2%; derived EBITDA margin 55.2% (+51bps). _(mixed)_
- Lottery margin resilience: Danske Lotteri Spil EBIT fell only DKK 4m (-0.2%) on a DKK 46m BSI decline, margin +134bps to 73.7% (Q3 74.5%), as online migration cut dealer commission DKK 21m (-7.8%). _(structural)_
- Fortress balance sheet: cash + securities DKK 2,231m (~$330m), equity ratio 58.8%, no borrowings disclosed; 9M CFO DKK 1,860m (~$275m) funded DKK 1,094m (~$162m) of distributions and DKK 127m (~$19m) of capex with room to spare. _(structural)_
- Portfolio simplification: conditional sale of Elite Gaming removes 5% of BSI that earns a 4% margin, exits the land-based slot regime and sharpens the group into lottery plus digital licensed games. _(structural)_
- Q4 lottery tailwind already in hand: Eurojackpot rolled to the DKK 750m (~$111m) cap by 6 October and stayed there until won on 13 October — the first max-pool run since May — ahead of the Christmas Lotto/Quick season. _(one-off)_
- Klasselotteriet EBIT +6.6% to DKK 146m (~$22m) at a 71.2% margin (+598bps) despite BSI -2.4%, showing the operating leverage of the subscription lottery base. _(mixed)_

**Lowlights**

- Q3 momentum reversed: derived Q3 BSI -1.4% to DKK 1,255m (~$186m), EBIT -1.3%, net profit -4.2% to DKK 503m (~$74m), after H1 had printed +1.5% / +4.6%; the 9M growth is entirely a Q2 jackpot/World Cup artefact. _(mixed)_
- Lottery top line is in the hands of the pool cycle: Danske Lotteri Spil Q3 BSI -5.9% to DKK 717m (~$106m) on fewer Eurojackpot high pools, smaller Lotto pools and falling in-store Quick sales; FY26 DLS BSI is tracking -1% to -2%. _(one-off)_
- Retail distribution is eroding fast: dealers 3,464, -295 (-7.8%) YoY and -158 in Q3 alone; the Quick scratchcard decline is the first visible casualty. _(structural)_
- Likely share loss in online casino: DLiS +7.5% total growth in Q3 against a Danish online-casino market growing 12.5–22% per month (DGA, secondary) and new entrants (Stake); no handle, margin, actives or ARPU disclosure to diagnose it. _(watch)_
- Gross-margin dilution is structural: gaming duties +3.7% to 12.3% of BSI and other gaming-related (platform/content) costs +16.6% to 6.2% of BSI as the mix shifts to 28%-duty licensed games; gross profit from games margin -67bps (9M) / -185bps (Q3). _(structural)_
- Financial items swung DKK 43m QoQ (Q3 -DKK 17m vs Q2 +DKK 26m) on securities mark-to-market, and 9M financial items are DKK 11m worse YoY — non-operating noise that will keep moving quarterly net profit. _(one-off)_
- Thin disclosure and no cost programme: no product split of licensed BSI, no marketing figure, no customer KPIs, no quantified savings target; staff costs -4.5% while FTEs +1.7% and capitalised development additions rose to DKK 122m (~$18m) from DKK 99m. _(governance)_

## 8. Forecast & Outlook

Guidance is unchanged for the third time this year: FY26 BSI of DKK 5,200–5,300m (~$769–784m) and net profit of DKK 1,950–2,050m (~$288–303m), as first set with the FY25 annual report on 13 March 2026 — the only edit is the explicit statement that it excludes the conditional Elite Gaming sale. No division drove a change because there was none; what moved inside the year is mix — Danske Licens Spil is running ahead (BSI +6.6%, EBIT +24%) while Danske Lotteri Spil is running behind (BSI -2.1%, Q3 -5.9%) on the jackpot cycle, and opex (-DKK 55m) is bridging the gap. Mechanically, 9M actuals leave an implied Q4 of BSI DKK 1,359–1,459m versus DKK 1,338m in Q4-25 (+1.6% to +9.0%) and net profit DKK 403–503m versus DKK 484m (-16.7% to +3.9%); the profit range midpoint therefore embeds a Q4 decline, which looks conservative after October's Eurojackpot max-pool run.

- **FY26 BSI guidance (old → new):** DKK 5,200–5,300m (~$769–784m) (Unchanged; now stated ex-Elite Gaming sale. 9M actual DKK 3,841m → implied Q4 DKK 1,359–1,459m vs Q4-25 DKK 1,338m)
- **FY26 net profit guidance (old → new):** DKK 1,950–2,050m (~$288–303m) (Unchanged. 9M actual DKK 1,547m → implied Q4 DKK 403–503m vs Q4-25 DKK 484m)
- **Division driving the year:** DLiS +DKK 77m BSI / +DKK 45m EBIT vs DLS -DKK 46m BSI / -DKK 4m EBIT (Mix shift to licensed games (+180bps to 32.2% of BSI); opex -DKK 55m fills the lottery hole)
- **Our FY26 estimate (not guidance):** BSI DKK 5,250–5,290m (~$777–783m); net profit DKK 2,000–2,040m (~$296–302m) (Upper half of both ranges; Q4 BSI DKK 1,410–1,450m (+5% to +8%), Q4 net profit DKK 455–495m)

**Revenue:** we model Q4-26 BSI of DKK 1,410–1,450m (~$209–214m), +5% to +8% YoY (estimate): Danske Lotteri Spil roughly flat to +3% at DKK 770–795m on the October Eurojackpot max pool (DKK 750m cap reached 6 Oct, won 13 Oct) and Christmas products against a tough Q4-25 comp; Danske Licens Spil +5–8% to DKK 445–455m on casino/poker with no tournament tailwind; Klasselotteriet and Elite Gaming c.DKK 70m each (Elite Gaming assumed still consolidated). That lands FY26 BSI at DKK 5,250–5,290m, +1.8% to +2.6% on FY25's DKK 5,158m. **Margin:** group EBIT margin should hold around 52% for the year (Q4 seasonally similar to Q3; marketing is being held down and D&A keeps rolling off), with gross-profit margin drifting lower as licensed mix rises — we see FY26 EBIT of roughly DKK 2,700–2,740m (estimate). Net profit of DKK 2,000–2,040m assumes financial items near zero in Q4 and a 22% tax rate; a repeat of Q3's securities loss would shave c.DKK 15–20m. **Leverage:** none — net cash of c.DKK 2.2bn at 30 September will be partly distributed; the pattern (DKK 1,094m paid in Q2, nothing in Q3) suggests the balance of the FY25 profit and an on-account FY26 distribution fall in Q4/Q1, so expect year-end cash plus securities around DKK 1.5–2.0bn with no debt. **Swing factors:** (1) jackpot rollovers in Lotto/Eurojackpot through December — each max-pool week is worth tens of millions of BSI; (2) sports results in a tournament-free Q4 (the Danish betting market swung -43% to +65% month-to-month in Q3); (3) timing of the Elite Gaming completion and any disposal gain/loss, which sits outside guidance; (4) securities mark-to-market; (5) the FY27 guidance and 2027 advertising legislation at the March 2027 annual report, where we would expect another 'stable' range (BSI c.DKK 5.0–5.2bn ex-Elite Gaming, net profit c.DKK 1.95–2.05bn) rather than growth.

**What to watch next quarter:**

- Q4 lottery pool cycle: number of Eurojackpot draws at or near the DKK 750m cap and Lotto/Vikinglotto rollovers through December versus the Q4-25 comp (DKK 149m Danish Eurojackpot win on 2 Dec 2025; record Christmas Lotto/Quick).
- Danish Gambling Authority monthly data (Oct–Dec 2026): whether online-casino market growth stays in the mid/high-teens while DLiS grows high single digits — the share-loss question.
- Elite Gaming completion: Competition and Consumer Authority clearance, the deconsolidation date, and any gain/loss booked outside guidance.
- Retail dealer count at year-end (3,464 at 30 Sep, -7.8% YoY) and the trajectory of in-store Quick scratchcard sales.
- FY26 distribution to udlodningsmidler and FY27 guidance in the March 2027 annual report, including how the 2027 advertising legislation (successor to Spilpakke 1 / lapsed bill L 127) is reflected.
- Cost quality: staff costs vs FTE growth, capitalised development additions (DKK 122m 9M-26 vs DKK 99m), and whether marketing is held down into a competitive Q4.
- Financial items: securities portfolio (DKK 832m) mark-to-market after a DKK 43m QoQ swing in Q3.
