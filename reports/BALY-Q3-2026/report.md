# Bally's Corporation (BALY) — Q3 2026 Analyst Report
** · Reporting currency: USD (tables in $ millions unless stated; Bally's Intralot figures in EUR with ~USD at 1.10) · Results released 16 Nov 2026 (full results, 8-K/10-Q; preliminary revenue/EBITDAR released 9 Nov 2026)**
**Market reaction:** Preliminary print (9 Nov, after close): shares -4% to $11.47 on 10 Nov (YTD -31%). Full results and 10-Q with the covenant breach/going-concern language (16 Nov, after close): -11.7% to $9.55 on 17 Nov, taking the one-week fall to ~23%; Truist cut its PT to $9 (Hold). +11.5% to $10.65 on 18 Nov after the $560m second-lien/GLPI financing commitments. 52-week range $8.44–19.45; market cap ~$0.5–0.6bn.

> Top line fine, balance sheet not. Q3-26 revenue of $807.6m (+21.7% YoY) was in line with consensus, but Segment Adj. EBITDAR of $179.6m (+2.2% YoY, margin -425bps to 22.2%) missed the Street by ~5%, the GAAP net loss widened to $117.0m (-$1.47/share), and the 10-Q disclosed a breached revolver minimum-liquidity covenant at 30 Sep (waived 1 Oct), a "probable" repeat breach at 31 Dec and an unresolved going-concern qualification. The 17 Nov $560m second-lien/GLPI commitment buys runway, not a fix: on our estimates annualised post-rent EBITDA (~$456m) does not cover annualised interest (~$505m), so the equity remains an option on refinancing and asset sales rather than on operations.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Revenue in line, EBITDAR light.** Q3-26 consolidated revenue $807.6m (+21.7% YoY, +1.9% QoQ) vs TipRanks consensus ~$807.9m. Sum-of-segments Adj. EBITDAR $179.6m (+2.2% YoY, -4.2% QoQ); Truist flagged a 2% miss vs its own estimate and ~5% vs Street, attributing the shortfall to Casinos & Resorts and Intralot B2B. Group EBITDAR margin 22.2%, -425bps YoY and -143bps QoQ.
- **Growth is acquired, not organic.** Bally's Intralot B2B + B2C delivered $334.0m, 41% of group revenue; B2B alone added ~$71m YoY from the Intralot consolidation (Oct 2025) and B2C includes Turkey sports betting that was not in the Q3-25 base. Strip those plus Sam's Town Shreveport (closed Aug 2026) and underlying growth is low-double-digit at best [our estimate]; Casinos & Resorts grew 3.2% largely on landside moves and M&A.
- **The UK duty is the margin story.** The RGD step-up (21%→40% from 1 Apr 2026) cost ~$39m of B2C Segment EBITDAR in Q3 (≈15pts of B2C revenue), in line with Q2. Management says ~55% was offset YoY through top-line growth, marketing efficiency and cost control — down from the 'close to 65%' claimed at Q2. B2C EBITDAR fell 10.9% to $77.0m (margin 29.9%, -1,130bps YoY) despite revenue +22.8%.
- **GAAP loss widened on D&A and G&A.** Loss from operations -$69.9m vs +$1.0m: D&A jumped 70% to $133.4m (vs ~$95m/qtr H1 run-rate — not explained in the release [unverified cause]) and G&A +17.7% to $344.1m. Net interest expense +19.3% to $126.3m. Other non-operating income swung to +$85.4m (from -$42.6m) and flattered the pre-tax line; composition not disclosed in the release. Net loss $117.0m (attributable $91.0m; -$1.47/share vs -$1.70). 9M net loss $441.8m (-$6.55/share).
- **Going concern is unresolved — and the covenant was actually breached.** The 10-Q discloses Bally's failed the Revolving Credit Facility minimum-liquidity requirement as of 30 Sep 2026; lenders granted a conditional waiver on 1 Oct, but management says another breach at 31 Dec is probable absent a further waiver/amendment. The RCF ($498.3m drawn) is reclassified as current (ASC 470-10-55); management concluded its plans 'do not alleviate substantial doubt' even after including the Bronx financing. No default exists under the term loan or senior notes, and Intralot's debt is ring-fenced.
- **$560m lifeline, 17 Nov.** Commitment letter from affiliates of existing term-loan lenders for $500m net proceeds of new Second Lien Term Loans (for repayment of certain debt — we assume the RCF — and general corporate purposes), plus $60m additional GLPI capital for Chicago (total GLPI commitment $1.0bn; subject to regulatory approval). These are commitments, not closed deals; the company says it is 'confident' it can meet cash-flow requirements for the coming year. Terms (rate, OID, maturity) undisclosed.
- **Cash burn and debt build.** 9M-26 net cash used in operating activities -$399.6m (working-capital outflow -$269.3m); capex + capitalised software $156.2m; NY licence fee $503.1m and $115m golf-course contingent payment, funded by $685m Lincoln sale-leaseback proceeds and net borrowing. Cash $497.3m (Dec-25: $798.4m). Gross carrying debt rose $336m QoQ to $4.84bn ($5.24bn face): RCF drawings +$195m to $498.3m and a new $207.8m 2026 Intralot British term loan.
- **Leverage and coverage (our estimates).** Net debt (carrying) $4.35bn ≈ 6.0x annualised 9M Segment EBITDAR ($728m); ~9.2x on a lease-adjusted basis (debt + $2.38bn lease liabilities − cash). Annualised triple-net rent ~$272m (9M cash rent $204.0m, +59% YoY post-Lincoln) leaves post-rent EBITDA ~$456m against annualised net interest ~$505m — coverage <1.0x. Truist cites $7.2bn total debt vs a ~$0.5bn market cap.
- **UK online is the one structural positive.** UK online revenue +12.2% in constant currency (Q2 +11.6%, Q1 +10.5%), with Bally's Intralot reporting record NGR for a third straight quarter and share gains as smaller operators cut marketing. Spain +18.9% cc to a record €13.3m (~$14.6m). CBRE's read (via NEXT.io): scale lets Bally's hold bonusing longer than sub-scale rivals as the market consolidates.
- **Intralot B2B is under-earning.** Revenue $76.6m (-3.7% QoQ), Segment EBITDAR $12.2m (-44% QoQ; margin 15.9% vs 27.6% in Q2), including ~$11m of negative IFRS→US GAAP adjustments (Q2: ~$7m). At the Athens-listed level, legacy B2B 9M revenue -8.1% to €191m (~$210m) with US lottery -11% to €119.6m (~$132m) on post-jackpot normalisation and lower equipment sales; ex-BII adj. EBITDA -13.4% 9M. New contracts (OLG Ontario, Victoria EGM monitoring, Chile, Hellenic Lotteries) are in the investment phase.
- **North America Interactive: growth, not yet profit.** Revenue $62.7m (+25.6% YoY, -5.2% QoQ), Segment EBITDAR -$3.2m (+$2.8m YoY) but a reversal from +$3.0m in Q2 as NFL-season promotional spend returned. Annualised run-rate ~$251m; Bally Bet live in 16 North American jurisdictions. No handle, hold, actives or ARPU disclosed.
- **Casinos & Resorts is flat-lining.** Revenue $408.6m (+3.2%) on Baton Rouge (landside Dec-25), Marquette (landside Feb-26), Chicago temporary and Sam's Town Shreveport ($30m, ~750 slots/14 tables); Segment EBITDAR flat at $107.8m, margin 26.4% (-87bps YoY, -96bps QoQ). 9M EBITDAR $313.6m vs $314.5m pro forma 2025 — zero growth across a 21-property, 11-state portfolio (17,700 slots, 630 tables, 3,950 rooms).
- **Development pipeline keeps slipping and keeps calling for capital.** Chicago permanent casino now 'spring 2027' (Q2: 'early 2027'; original: Sept 2026) after a 12-month temporary-licence extension; non-gaming construction paused in Aug after the City legalised VGTs (~12% of the project under review); $1.34bn minimum capital commitment reaffirmed. Bronx ($4.0bn, open by 2030): $560m WhiteHawk term loan closed 1 Oct ($400m funded, $160m delayed-draw) funds pre-construction only. Las Vegas ($1.19bn, adjacent to the A's stadium): little progress; GLPI's $200–250m is conditional and the A's are contingency-planning.
- **The Star (Australia).** AUSTRAC penalty of A$260m (~US$178.5m) for historic AML failures; Star Sydney licence suspension extended to 30 Jun 2027. Management says Star returned to positive EBITDA in September, Sydney + Gold Coast revenue grew YoY for the first time since quarterly reporting began, and FY27 should be the first positive operating-cash-flow year (pre one-offs) in over two years.
- **evoke slips to 2027.** Bally's Intralot's takeover of evoke (0.537 BYLOT shares per evoke share; ~£243.1m equity value [USD n/a]; ~£180m pre-tax cost and capex savings targeted by end of year two post-close) has both shareholder approvals and several competition clearances, but remaining gaming approvals and the Gibraltar court sanction are now expected by Q1-27. Bally's Intralot Q3: revenue €286.1m (~$315m), adj. EBITDA €88.9m (~$98m), +5% QoQ.
- **Corporate cost take-out is real.** Corporate & Other Segment EBITDAR -$14.1m vs -$18.0m (Q3-25) and -$38.2m 9M vs -$53.2m pro forma — a ~$15m YTD saving, partly reallocation of shared-service costs into C&R (~$1.6m/qtr flagged at Q2).
- **Reporting hygiene is deteriorating.** Third consecutive 12b-25 late 10-Q; the 9 Nov preliminary release contained only revenue and Segment EBITDAR, with the net loss, covenant breach and going-concern language only surfacing in the 16 Nov 10-Q. No earnings call was held; management commentary is confined to the release.
- **Share reaction confirms it is a credit story.** -4% to $11.47 on 10 Nov (prelim), -11.7% to $9.55 on 17 Nov (full 10-Q; -23% on the week), +11.5% to $10.65 on 18 Nov on the $560m commitments. Truist cut its PT to $9 (Hold) on the EBITDA miss; TipRanks cites a $12 target elsewhere. 52-week range $8.44–19.45.
- **Thesis read-through.** Operationally: one structural winner (UK iGaming), one break-even digital business (NAI), one ex-growth cash cow burdened by rent (C&R) and one under-earning lottery supplier (Intralot B2B). Financially: FCF-negative through at least 2027 given Chicago/Bronx capex, interest and rent, so the equity value hinges on the second-lien close, asset monetisation (management lists asset sales and an equity sale among options), and GLPI's continued willingness to fund.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — Casinos & Resorts (21 US casinos, 11 states + Newcastle UK)

*Revenue +3.2% on landside moves and Sam's Town; EBITDAR flat at $107.8m with margin -87bps — the portfolio is ex-growth once rent is deducted.*

### Revenue and EBITDAR

**Revenue rose 3.2% to $408.6m but EBITDAR was flat at $107.8m with margin 26.4%, -87bps YoY; the portfolio is ex-growth once rent is deducted.**

- Revenue +3.2% to $408.6m (+1.9% QoQ), driven by Baton Rouge (landside since December 2025), Marquette (landside since February 2026), Chicago temporary casino and Sam's Town Shreveport.
- Sam's Town Shreveport acquired from Boyd for $30m, closed 1–3 August, adding roughly 750 slots and 14 tables next to Bally's Shreveport.
- Segment Adjusted EBITDAR 'relatively flat' at $107.8m (-0.2% YoY, -1.7% QoQ); margin 26.4%, 87bps below Q3-25 and 96bps below Q2-26.
- Nine months: revenue $1,189.3m (+2.5% vs pro forma 2025 including Queen); EBITDAR $313.6m vs $314.5m pro forma, no profit growth from the enlarged estate.

### KPIs and margin drivers

**No operating KPIs or property-level disclosure were updated, so margin slippage drivers are inferred; Truist flagged C&R as a source of the ~5% consensus EBITDAR miss.**

- No same-store revenue, rated visitation (Q2 was +4.3%), slot handle or table drop disclosed this quarter.
- At Q2 management flagged elevated competition in Atlantic City and East St. Louis and a ~$1.6m/quarter shared-service cost reallocation from Corporate; both presumably persisted.
- Truist singled out Casinos & Resorts (alongside B2B) for the ~5% consensus EBITDAR miss, consistent with a flat regional-gaming peer set.
- The Chicago temporary facility will cannibalise itself once the permanent property opens.

### Rent burden

**Group triple-net lease cash payments were $204.0m for nine months (+59% YoY); C&R post-rent EBITDA was only ~$110m for nine months on our estimate.**

- Rent rose after the February Lincoln sale-leaseback to GLPI for $700m, the Chicago master lease at $20m initial rent plus 8.5% on GLPI development advances, and the Queen leases.
- Nearly all rent sits against this segment: ~$110m post-rent EBITDA in nine months (~$40m a quarter) [estimate], before maintenance capex.
- GLPI advances to Chicago now up to $1.0bn after the 17 Nov $60m top-up; each adds rent at 8.5%, compressing post-rent earnings until the permanent casino ramps.

### Chicago and Las Vegas pipeline

**Chicago permanent casino (minimum $1.34bn commitment) now guided to open spring 2027, versus 'early 2027' at Q2 and the original September 2026.**

- Management says it will 'meet and likely exceed' the $1.34bn commitment; Illinois granted a 12-month extension to the temporary licence.
- Non-gaming construction paused in August after the City Council legalised video gaming terminals; around 12% of the project under re-evaluation, though casino, event centre, required F&B and 100 hotel rooms are on schedule.
- The 17 Nov amendment to the GLPI development agreement authorises additional developments phased through 2027.
- Las Vegas ($1.19bn Tropicana site beside the A's stadium) remains in 'advanced negotiations with potential partners'; GLPI's $200–250m is conditional on commercial items settled with the A's, who are contingency-planning.
- No segment guidance was given.

### Our read

**A stable but structurally low-growth regional portfolio whose reported EBITDAR flatters the economics because the real estate has been progressively sold.**

- The segment cannot self-fund Chicago, Las Vegas and the Bronx.
- It is a rent-paying operator whose landlord GLPI is also its largest development financier, aligning GLPI with completion but not necessarily with Bally's equity.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — Bally's Intralot B2C (International Interactive: UK, Spain, Turkey)

*UK online +12.2% cc and record NGR, but the 40% RGD took ~$39m: EBITDAR -10.9% to $77.0m (margin 29.9%, -1,130bps) and the offset ratio slipped to 55% from ~65%.*

### Revenue growth

**Revenue grew 22.8% to $257.5m with UK online +12.2% constant currency and record NGR; Spain +18.9% cc to a record €13.3m.**

- Revenue +22.8% to $257.5m (+5.7% QoQ), driven by 'continued strength in our U.K. operations' and inclusion of Intralot's legacy Turkey B2C sports-betting business from Q4-25.
- UK online +12.2% YoY cc, accelerating from 11.6% in Q2 and 10.5% in Q1, 'despite lower marketing investment'; UK NGR at an all-time high for a third sequential quarter; nine-month UK revenue +11.4%.
- Spain delivered 18.9% cc growth to a record €13.3m (~$14.6m) on a larger active player base.
- UK is roughly 64% of BII revenue, so this is a Gamesys iGaming and bingo story (Jackpotjoy, Virgin Games, Monopoly Casino); no handle or margin metrics apply or are disclosed.

> “continued strength in our U.K. operations” — Management

### RGD impact on profitability

**EBITDAR fell 10.9% to $77.0m, margin 29.9% (-1,130bps), as the 40% RGD took ~$39m; the offset ratio slipped to 55% from ~65%.**

- Margin 29.9% vs 41.2% a year ago, but up 331bps sequentially from the 26.6% Q2 trough, the first quarter under the new duty.
- RGD rise from 21% to 40% (effective 1 April 2026) cost approximately $39m, ~15 percentage points of segment revenue; at Intralot level €34m (~$37m) for the quarter and ~€68m (~$75m) for nine months.
- Management says approximately 55% was offset through top-line growth, marketing efficiency and cost management; at Q2 the ratio was 'close to 65%' with marketing cuts yet to begin, suggesting a shifted base or timing in Q2 offset.
- Nine-month EBITDAR $228.8m is 0.5% below $230.1m of 9M-25 despite revenue +25%, the cleanest measure of the duty's cost; includes ~$2m negative IFRS-to-US GAAP lease adjustments (Q2: ~$5m).

> “on track against the margin commitments previously disclosed to investors” — Management

### Athens-listed figures

**BII revenue was €201.6m (~$222m) in Q3, +4% QoQ; Bally's Intralot group adjusted EBITDA €88.9m (~$98m) versus €84.6m in Q2.**

- BII nine-month revenue €579.2m (~$637m); legacy Intralot B2C (Turkey) +7.1% to €60.1m (~$66m) over nine months.
- Group revenue €286.1m (~$315m), +249% YoY on consolidation and +3.6% QoQ; adjusted EBITDA €88.9m vs €29.9m a year ago; nine-month adjusted EBITDA €273.6m (~$301m).
- B2C + B2B Segment EBITDAR of $89.2m plus ~$13m IFRS-to-GAAP adjustments broadly reconciles to the Intralot figure at Q3 average rates [our reconciliation].

### Consolidation and evoke

**Management is leaning on UK consolidation; the evoke acquisition completion has slipped to Q1-27, pushing back the ~£180m synergy timetable.**

- At Q2 management conceded UK consolidation had 'not moved quite as quickly as we originally expected' but expects smaller operators under pressure post-World Cup and into the autumn tax season.
- CBRE's Colin Mansfield told NEXT.io that Bally's 'went into the tax increases from a position of strength' and is gaining share as sub-scale players retreat.
- evoke deal: 0.537 new Bally's Intralot shares per evoke share, ~£243.1m equity value, ~£180m targeted pre-tax cost and capex savings by end of year two; shareholder approvals and several competition clearances obtained.
- Remaining gaming-regulatory approvals and Gibraltar court sanction pushed completion to Q1-27; evoke flagged 'material uncertainties' about going concern; Reeves ruled out an immediate break-up.
- No numeric segment guidance; implicit commitment is to hold margin trajectory while absorbing a full year of 40% duty from Q2-27.

### Our read

**UK organic growth is genuine and better than peers, but the duty hit is permanent and absorbing evoke will dilute segment quality before synergies arrive in 2028–29.**

- Lower marketing with accelerating revenue is a strong cohort signal.
- The segment now earns ~30% margins on a structurally higher tax base.
- evoke is a lower-margin, sportsbook-heavy, William Hill retail estate.

## 7. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- UK online revenue +12.2% cc, accelerating for a third straight quarter (10.5% → 11.6% → 12.2%) with record NGR and lower marketing — evidence of share gain under the 40% RGD as sub-scale operators retreat; Spain +18.9% to a record €13.3m (~$14.6m). _(structural)_
- North America Interactive revenue +25.6% to $62.7m with EBITDAR loss narrowed $2.8m YoY to -$3.2m; ~$251m annualised run-rate and 9M EBITDAR -$7.3m vs -$10.1m pro forma — the segment is close to break-even for the year. _(early)_
- Corporate & Other costs cut to -$14.1m from -$18.0m (Q3-25); 9M -$38.2m vs -$53.2m pro forma, ~$15m YTD saving (part reallocation). _(structural)_
- Liquidity bridge assembled: $560m Bronx term loan closed 1 Oct ($400m funded, $160m DDTL) and, on 17 Nov, commitments for $500m net second-lien term loans plus $60m extra GLPI capital (Chicago commitment to $1.0bn). Shares +11.5% on the news. _(one-off)_
- Bally's Intralot adjusted EBITDA €88.9m (~$98m), +5% QoQ and +3.6% revenue QoQ; the UK duty offset (55%) and record UK/Spain revenue show the Gamesys engine is still compounding. _(mixed)_
- The Star returned to positive EBITDA in September; Sydney + Gold Coast revenue grew YoY for the first time since quarterly reporting began; FY27 guided as first positive operating-cash-flow year in >2 years. AUSTRAC exposure now quantified at A$260m (~US$178.5m). _(early)_

**Lowlights**

- Going concern maintained and the covenant was actually tripped: minimum-liquidity requirement under the RCF failed at 30 Sep, waived 1 Oct; management calls a further breach at 31 Dec 'probable' absent waiver/amendment; RCF ($498.3m) reclassified current; 'management's plans do not alleviate substantial doubt' even including the Bronx loan. _(governance)_
- Segment EBITDAR $179.6m missed Street by ~5% (Truist) despite in-line revenue; group margin 22.2%, -425bps YoY / -143bps QoQ. Weakness in C&R and Intralot B2B. _(mixed)_
- B2C EBITDAR -10.9% to $77.0m (margin -1,130bps to 29.9%); ~$39m RGD hit only 55% offset vs 'close to 65%' claimed at Q2; 9M B2C EBITDAR flat ($228.8m vs $230.1m) on +25% revenue. _(structural)_
- Cash burn: 9M operating cash flow -$399.6m (working capital -$269m); FCF (calc.) -$555.8m; gross debt +$336m QoQ ($195m RCF draw + $208m new Intralot UK term loan) while cash was flat at ~$497m. Post-rent EBITDA ~$456m annualised vs ~$505m interest — coverage <1x (est.). _(structural)_
- Casinos & Resorts EBITDAR flat at $107.8m with margin -87bps; 9M EBITDAR $313.6m vs $314.5m pro forma — no growth from 21 properties while triple-net rent is up 59% to $204m YTD. _(structural)_
- Intralot B2B EBITDAR -44% QoQ to $12.2m (margin 15.9%) incl. ~$11m GAAP adjustments; US lottery revenue -11% 9M; ex-BII Intralot adj. EBITDA -13.4%. The lottery leg of the merger thesis is under-earning while new contracts absorb investment. _(mixed)_
- Execution slippage across the pipeline: Chicago permanent now 'spring 2027' (was 'early 2027'; original Sep-26) with non-gaming construction paused post-VGT legalisation; evoke completion pushed to Q1-27; Las Vegas stalled with GLPI funding conditional and the A's contingency-planning. Third consecutive late 10-Q and no earnings call. _(governance)_
- P&L opacity: D&A +70% YoY to $133.4m (vs ~$95m/qtr in H1) is unexplained in the release, and a +$85.4m swing in other non-operating income flattered pre-tax; neither is reconciled in the 8-K. _(watch)_

## 8. Forecast & Outlook

Bally's issues no numerical guidance, so the 'bridge' is the set of qualitative markers management moved between Q2 (14 Aug) and Q3 (16 Nov). All four moved the wrong way or sideways: the Chicago permanent opening slipped from 'early 2027' to 'spring 2027'; evoke completion slipped from 2026 to Q1-27; the share of the ~$39m/quarter UK RGD hit being offset fell from 'close to 65%' to 'approximately 55%' (both driven by the Bally's Intralot B2C segment); and the liquidity position moved from a projected covenant shortfall to an actual 30 Sep breach, waived, with another 'probable' at 31 Dec. The offsetting change is on financing: the $560m Bronx term loan closed (1 Oct) and $560m of further commitments ($500m second-lien net proceeds + $60m GLPI) were announced on 17 Nov, which the company says gives it confidence in meeting cash-flow requirements for the coming year. North America Interactive's 'pacing toward positive EBITDAR' is unchanged.

- **Chicago permanent opening:** Spring 2027 (from 'early 2027' at Q2 (original Sep-2026); non-gaming scope ~12% under review post-VGT)
- **UK RGD offset ratio (B2C):** ~55% of ~$39m/qtr (from 'close to 65%' at Q2; 9M B2C EBITDAR flat YoY)
- **evoke completion:** Q1-2027 (from 2026; ~£180m synergy clock starts later)
- **Liquidity / covenants:** $560m closed (Bronx) + $560m committed (2L + GLPI) (vs 30 Sep min-liquidity breach (waived 1 Oct); 31 Dec breach 'probable' pre-financing; going concern unchanged)
- **Chicago permanent opening:** Spring 2027 (from 'early 2027' at Q2 (original Sep-2026); non-gaming scope ~12% under review post-VGT)

**Q4-26 (our estimates):** revenue ~$800–820m — C&R ~$395–405m (seasonally softer regional quarter, first full quarter of Sam's Town), B2C ~$255–265m (UK +11–13% cc, Turkey), B2B ~$75–80m, NAI ~$65–70m (NFL/NBA seasonality, Q4 is typically the strongest digital quarter). Segment EBITDAR ~$170–190m: C&R ~$100–105m, B2C ~$75–80m (a full quarter of marketing cuts vs. the ~$39m duty drag), B2B ~$12–18m, NAI around break-even, Corporate ~-$13m. That implies **FY26 revenue ~$3.16–3.18bn and Segment EBITDAR ~$715–735m**, i.e. roughly flat-to-up low-single-digits on a pro forma basis, with a group margin of ~22.5–23% versus ~26% pro forma in 2025. **Below EBITDAR**, annualised triple-net rent of ~$270m and net interest of ~$500m+ (rising once the second-lien is drawn — we assume a double-digit all-in coupon [assumption]) leave post-rent EBITDA short of interest, so GAAP losses of $100m+ per quarter continue and **FY26 net loss is likely to exceed $550m** before any impairment. **Leverage direction: up.** Net debt (carrying) was $4.35bn at 30 Sep; the second-lien is partly a refinancing of the RCF but Chicago (balance of the $1.34bn+), Bronx pre-construction and Las Vegas will all consume cash in 2027, so we expect net debt/EBITDAR to drift above 6x on our basis and lease-adjusted leverage to stay around 9x unless assets are sold. **Swing factors for 2027:** (1) the Chicago permanent ramp (positive, but with 8.5% rent on GLPI's $1.0bn advances); (2) a full year of 40% RGD lapping from Q2-27 — UK growth of 11–13% should allow B2C EBITDAR to return to modest YoY growth from Q2-27; (3) evoke consolidation into Bally's Intralot from Q1-27 (revenue-accretive, margin-dilutive, synergies back-end loaded); (4) US lottery jackpot cycle for B2B; (5) any Star recapitalisation or AUSTRAC payment timing. The investment case remains a credit case: watch whether the second-lien actually closes on the committed terms, whether the 31 Dec liquidity test is met or re-waived, and whether management executes the 'asset monetization' and 'equity sale' options it lists in the 10-Q.

**What to watch next quarter:**

- Closing of the $500m second-lien term loans and the $60m GLPI top-up (regulatory approval): terms, coupon, OID, maturity and any equity-linked features; use of proceeds vs the $498.3m RCF.
