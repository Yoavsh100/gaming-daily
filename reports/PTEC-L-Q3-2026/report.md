# Playtech plc (PTEC.L) — Q3 2026 Analyst Report
**LSE · Reporting currency: EUR (tables in € millions; USD equivalents at EUR/USD 1.12, ECB fix 10 Nov 2026) · Results released 10 Nov 2026 (trading update covering 1 Jul–31 Oct 2026; H1-26 results published 10 Sep 2026)**
**Market reaction:** Opened +2–4% (intraday high 385p, +4.5% vs the 368.4p prior close) on the upgrade, then faded as the 2027 caveats were digested: 363.8p (−1.2%) at 10:39, 366.7p (−0.5%) at 11:46, and a close of c.364.8p (c.−1.0%; derived from the 11 Nov close of 369.4p, +1.26%). The shares drifted to 351.6p by 17 Nov (−4.6% vs pre-update; 52-week range 225.5–456p; market cap c.£1.0bn). Peel Hunt reiterated Buy/690p.

> An 11% upgrade to FY26 adj. EBITDA (>€300m / ~$336m, 7.5% above the €279m consensus) that the market refused to pay for, because management simultaneously told it 2026 is the peak: PMR wallet share at Hard Rock Bet erodes in 2027, Brazil is dark, and the UK's 40% RGD annualises. The beat is high quality (cash-generative, Americas-led, cost-disciplined) but its durability — one product, one partner, one state — is the entire debate, and FY27 consensus (€266m) already sits 11% below the new 2026 floor.

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Guidance raised to >€300m (~$336m) for FY26 adj. EBITDA**, up from the ">€270m" (~$302m) given with the interims on 10 Sep and 7.5% above the company-compiled consensus of €279m (~$312m; range €271–285m across 7 analysts). Versus FY25A €197.0m (~$221m) this implies +52% YoY and lands at the top of the €250–300m medium-term range set in early 2025 — management now talks of working "towards the top-end" of that range.
- **The upgrade is a B2B Americas story and nothing else.** The RNS attributes the beat to (i) Past Motor Racing (PMR) games at Hard Rock Bet in Florida, where H2 revenue "continued to perform strongly" rather than normalising as guided in July/September, and (ii) Mexico and Colombia post-World Cup tailwinds. No change is flagged for Europe, the UK or B2C; the headwinds there are being absorbed, not avoided.
- **Implied H2-26 adj. EBITDA is >€137.5m (~$154m)**, vs >€107.5m (~$120m) implied by the old guide — a c.€30m (~$34m) uplift concentrated in Q3/Oct. That is +30% vs H2-25's derived €105.4m (~$118m) but still −15% HoH vs H1-26's €162.5m, so the "H2 lower than H1" framing from July stands; it is simply less low.
- **No numbers were disclosed.** The update is qualitative (no revenue, handle, actives or regional split) and Playtech reports half-yearly, so the financial tables below rest on H1-26 (10 Sep), derived H2-25 and FY25 actuals. The Q3 'result' is the guidance change and the 2027 language.
- **H1-26 base: revenue €425.1m (~$476m) +10%** (+13% excluding the €10.0m Caliente additional B2B services fee in H1-25); **adj. EBITDA €162.5m (~$182m) +77%**, group margin 38.2% (+1,450bps); adj. EBITDA from operations €128.3m (~$144m) at a 30.2% margin vs 18.6%. Reported EBITDA €86.8m (~$97m) vs €12.9m; reported PAT €98.1m (~$110m) vs a €78.1m loss; adjusted PAT €95.0m (~$106m) +472%; adjusted diluted EPS 33.1c (~$0.37) vs 5.4c.
- **B2B did the work:** revenue €394.8m (~$442m) +14% reported / +17% underlying; regulated B2B €328.4m (~$368m) +16% / +21% underlying and now 83% of B2B (81%); B2B adj. EBITDA €128.1m (~$143m) +75% with margin 32.4% (+1,130bps YoY, +1,240bps HoH). Costs fell 3% to €266.7m (~$299m) with >€20m (~$22m) of annual run-rate cost removed — the only 'cost programme' disclosed; there is no formal multi-year transformation target.
- **Regional H1-26 scorecard:** US & Canada €56.9m (~$64m) +161% (+176% cc), predominantly PMR; LatAm €99.9m (~$112m) +14% reported / +29% underlying (Colombia more than doubled); Europe ex-UK €104.5m (~$117m) +2% (+10% ex H1-25 hardware sale; Poland, Spain); UK €59.0m (~$66m) −8% (−5% cc); RoW €8.1m (~$9m) +23% (South Africa); unregulated €66.4m (~$74m) +2%.
- **2027 is explicitly flagged as weaker, on three fronts:** (1) third-party PMR suppliers are being introduced at Hard Rock Bet and are "now expected to have a more material impact on Playtech's wallet share into 2027" (trade press names IGT and Light & Wonder); (2) Brazil's MP 1.394 shutdown — Playtech absorbs a three-month 2026 hit but warns of a "more significant" 2027 impact if not lifted; (3) the first full year of UK Remote Gaming Duty at 40% (from 21%, April 2026) plus higher taxes in other European markets. FY27 consensus €266m (~$298m) is −11% vs the FY26 floor and management did not push back on it.
- **Brazil detail:** Provisional Measure 1.394/2026 (signed 25 Sep 2026) halted deposits immediately, forced licensed sites offline on 6 Oct and extinguished all 85 federal authorisations on 25 Oct. Congress's first 60-day window runs to 23 Nov (auto-extends 60 days); 99 amendments were filed and an STF challenge (ADI 7721) is pending. Playtech's 'major strategic partnership' in Brazil, which H1 guided to sign in late 2026 and which it was spending against, is unmentioned in the update — read that as paused. Existing exposure is GaleraBet; the three-month hit being 'absorbable' suggests single-digit €m.
- **UK detail:** RGD 21%→40% from April 2026; a new 25% remote betting GBD arrives April 2027 (Nov-25 Budget). UK B2B revenue −8% in H1 was only partly tax — one operator insourced self-service betting terminals and another renegotiated terms. Sun Bingo (B2C, RGD-taxed online bingo) is also in scope.
- **Concentration is the structural risk:** one product (PMR), one partner (Hard Rock Digital), one state (Florida) drove the upgrade; a single customer was 14.1% of H1 continuing revenue (15.1% H1-25). Neither Playtech nor Hard Rock discloses PMR handle, revenue or actives, so wallet-share loss cannot be modelled from the outside. The HRD equity stake was revalued to €246.7m (~$276m) at Jun-26 from €178.8m (~$200m) at Dec-25 (c.3x cost in three years) — a real asset, but marked by a third-party valuer off HRD's own projections.
- **Investment income is 21% of adj. EBITDA and is equity-accounted:** €34.2m (~$38m) +73%, of which the 30.8% Caliente Interactive stake contributed €30.1m (~$34m) share of income and HRD €4.4m (~$5m) dividends. Net cash dividends received from Caliente were €35.6m (~$40m, nil in H1-25, gross of WHT) — these flow into FCF, not adj. EBITDA. H2-25 investment income was a derived €42.0m (~$47m), so H1-26 was actually −19% HoH; the line is lumpy and Caliente's World Cup marketing spend sits inside it.
- **Cash and capital return:** FCF €101.0m (~$113m) in H1 vs €6.6m, already above the €70–100m (~$78–112m) medium-term annual FCF target, of which €37.6m (~$42m) was dividends received. Net cash €39.2m (~$44m) at Jun-26 vs €28.5m (~$32m) Dec-25 after a €24.6m (~$28m) buyback (1.8% of ISC); c.10% of ISC repurchased for c.€100m (~$112m) since Sep 2025; €225m (~$252m) RCF undrawn; the 2026 bond was fully repaid in 2025. Caveat: net cash rose only €10.7m against €76.4m of FCF-less-buyback — c.€66m (~$74m) of below-FCF outflows (Snaitech-linked option dividend-equivalents/bonus, tax, leases) [composition unverified].
- **B2C is a rounding error, deliberately:** revenue €32.0m (~$36m) −22% as HAPPYBET (Germany) winds down; adj. EBITDA €0.2m vs −€1.5m (Sun Bingo & other +€1.5m, HAPPYBET c.−€1.3m derived). FY25 B2C adj. EBITDA was −€6.2m, so the drag is largely gone.
- **Product/SaaS progress is real but second-order to PMR:** SaaS revenue €69.0m (~$77m) +20%, 17% of B2B; Live revenue +8% (+12% in regulated markets) on c.480 tables across 20 studios with Live costs +1% to €70.1m (~$79m); US Live +c.25% with >60 tables in three studios; Playtech Protect on 41 brands in 16 jurisdictions (+13 in H1); Lucky Box Baccarat (a PMR-driven baccarat variant) launched at Hard Rock Bet in October.
- **Share price told the story:** +4.5% at the open (385p high vs 368.4p prior close) on the upgrade, faded to −0.5%/−1.2% by late morning and closed c.364.8p (c.−1%, derived); 351.6p by 17 Nov (−4.6% vs pre-update). Peel Hunt (Buy, 690p TP; note titled 'No brakes on NASCAR') raised FY26 by 6% to €300m but left FY27 unchanged — the sell-side agrees 2026 is a peak year.
- **Management/governance:** CEO Mor Weizer, CFO Chris McGinnis; announcement signed off by IR director Rohan Chitale. No M&A signalled (H1: 'well-positioned to invest' but no immediate plan). No 2027 guidance given; the next hard datapoint is FY26 results (c. late March 2027) where we expect 2027 guidance and a capital-return decision (buyback renewal vs ordinary dividend).
- **Thesis read-through:** the equity now trades on 2027 trough EBITDA (our est. €255–275m / ~$286–308m) with SOTP support from net cash, the €247m HRD mark and the 30.8% Caliente stake. The bull case needs PMR share loss to be gradual, Brazil to reopen in H1-27 and Mexico/Colombia to keep compounding; the bear case is that a non-recurring first-mover windfall is being capitalised as run-rate. We lean to the view that FY26 lands €305–310m (Playtech's guidance has been conservative: FY25 beat initial consensus by ~20%, H1-26 beat its own July guide by ~5%) and FY27 is flat-to-down 10–15%.

## 3. Segment deep-dive — B2B (core; c.93% of revenue)

*Americas-led upgrade on a 32% margin base — but the PMR engine behind it is explicitly flagged to lose share in 2027, and the UK is going the other way.*

### Revenue and margin

**H1-26 B2B revenue €394.8m (~$442m), +14% reported, +17% underlying; adj. EBITDA +75% to €128.1m on a 32.4% margin.**

- Underlying growth strips the €10.0m Caliente additional B2B services fee in H1-25 before the revised agreement took effect 31 March 2025.
- Revenue 16% above derived H2-25 base of €340.7m (~$382m).
- Regulated revenue €328.4m (~$368m), +16% reported, +21% underlying; regulated share of B2B 83% from 81%. Unregulated €66.4m (~$74m), +2%.
- B2B adjusted costs fell 3% to €266.7m (~$299m) despite Americas and Brazil investment; management cites more than €20m (~$22m) annual run-rate cost removed.
- Margin expanded 1,130bps YoY and 1,240bps HoH vs 20.0% in H2-25; highest since before the Caliente restructuring and the base for the >€300m guide.

> “driven by continued strong performance in the Americas” — Management

### US & Canada / PMR

**US & Canada revenue €56.9m (~$64m), +161% reported, +176% constant currency, driven by PMR with Hard Rock Bet; share loss flagged for 2027.**

- July and September guidance expected PMR to 'normalise at a more sustainable level' in H2; Q3 update says PMR 'continued to perform strongly in H2', the ~€30m swing.
- Third-party PMR suppliers expected to have a more material impact on wallet share into 2027; Gambling Insider reports IGT and Light & Wonder equivalents arriving.
- No PMR handle, revenue or actives disclosed; US & Canada annualises at >€115m on H1. A one-third wallet-share loss plausibly removes €25–35m (~$28–39m) of 2027 EBITDA [estimate].
- US business reached profitability earlier than planned; US Live revenue grew c.25% with more than 60 tables across three studios.
- HRD stake marked up to €246.7m (~$276m) from €178.8m at Dec-25, roughly three times cost; a single customer was 14.1% of H1 continuing revenue (15.1%).

> “the introduction of other third-party PMR suppliers is underway and is now expected to have a more material impact on Playtech's wallet share into 2027” — Management

### Latin America and Brazil

**Latin America revenue €99.9m (~$112m), +14% reported, +29% underlying on Mexico and Colombia; Brazil shutdown makes the 2027 contribution binary.**

- Colombia revenue more than doubled; Mexico and Colombia 'benefitted from tailwinds after the Men's FIFA World Cup'.
- MP 1.394/2026 banned fixed-odds betting and online casino on 25 September, forced sites offline 6 October and extinguished all 85 federal authorisations 25 October.
- Three-month 2026 adj. EBITDA impact absorbed inside the >€300m guide; 'a more significant impact expected in 2027 should the existing market shutdown not be lifted'.
- The 'major new strategic partnership' expected to sign in late 2026 is not mentioned in the update; we read it as paused.
- Congress's first 60-day window closes 23 November (automatic 60-day extension), 99 amendments filed, several restoring licences; ADI 7721 sits with the STF.

### Europe, UK, Rest of World

**Europe ex-UK €104.5m (~$117m), +2% reported, +10% ex-hardware; UK €59.0m (~$66m) fell 8% on RGD rising to 40%.**

- Poland and Spain named growth markets; Q3 update warns of 'increased taxation across other European markets' as a 2027 headwind, jurisdictions unnamed.
- UK decline −5% constant currency, also reflecting one operator insourcing self-service betting terminals and contractual changes at another.
- Full year of 40% RGD hits in 2027 and 25% remote betting GBD arrives April 2027; revenue-share fees mean operator tax pass-through lands directly on this line.
- Rest of World grew 23% to €8.1m (~$9m) on South Africa (Hollywoodbets, Betway, Tsogo Sun Gaming, new customer LottoStar).

### Product lines

**SaaS revenue rose 20% to €69.0m (~$77m), 17% of B2B; underlying franchise improving but 2026 earnings set by one contract.**

- Cost-based revenue €35.7m (~$40m).
- Live revenue grew 8% (12% in regulated markets) across c.480 tables in 20 studios; Live costs rose only 1% to €70.1m (~$79m).
- Playtech Protect added 13 brands in H1 to reach 41 across 16 jurisdictions.
- None of these lines moves the 2027 needle the way PMR does.

### Segment outlook

**H2 adj. EBITDA guided below H1; we model FY26 B2B adj. EBITDA €230–240m and 2027 flat-to-down 10–15% [estimates].**

- September reasons for lower H2: normalising HRD revenue, continued Brazil investment, full half-year of UK RGD; Q3 update raises the floor, not the shape.
- H2-26 B2B revenue c.€380–390m (~$426–437m), adj. EBITDA c.€100–110m (~$112–123m) at 26–28% margin [estimates].
- FY26 B2B revenue c.€775–785m (~$868–879m), adj. EBITDA c.€230–240m (~$258–269m).
- 2027 bridge: PMR share loss −€25–35m, UK RGD and customer changes −€6–10m, Brazil −€5–10m if shut all year plus foregone partnership upside.
- Offsets: Mexico/Colombia/US Live/Canada growth +€15–25m and further cost actions.

## 4. Segment deep-dive — B2C (Sun Bingo & Other; HAPPYBET in wind-down)

*Non-core and now roughly break-even — the wind-down of HAPPYBET has removed the loss, but Sun Bingo is in scope for the 40% RGD.*

### Revenue and EBITDA

**B2C revenue fell 22% to €32.0m (~$36m) on HAPPYBET wind-down; adj. EBITDA turned positive at €0.2m vs €1.5m loss.**

- Revenue c.15% below derived H2-25 figure of €37.5m (~$42m); decline reflects HAPPYBET Germany wind-down, not Sun Bingo weakness.
- Austria closed in H2-24; sale process for Germany started early 2025.
- Sun Bingo & Other contributed €1.5m (~$2m, vs €0.8m), implying HAPPYBET lost c.€1.3m (vs c.€2.3m) [derived].
- FY25 B2C loss €6.2m (HAPPYBET revenue €12.2m, adj. EBITDA −€6.3m); most of the drag already engineered out.

### Sun Bingo and RGD

**Sun Bingo falls under UK RGD rising to 40% from April 2026; the €1.5m contribution should come under pressure rather than grow.**

- Operated under long-term contract with News UK; gross win from online bingo and casino.
- RGD hits directly in H2-26 and for a full year in 2027.

### Outlook

**No B2C guidance given; we model FY26 B2C adj. EBITDA roughly break-even to −€1m (~−$1m) [estimate].**

- Division is immaterial to the >€300m guide.
- Whether Playtech exits Sun Bingo at contract end to become a pure B2B supplier remains open and was not addressed.

## 6. Highlights & Lowlights

**Highlights**

- Second guidance upgrade of 2026: FY26 adj. EBITDA now >€300m (~$336m) vs >€270m in September and €279m consensus; implies +52% YoY and the top of the €250–300m medium-term range a year early. Playtech's guidance has a track record of conservatism (FY25 beat initial consensus by ~20%; H1-26 beat its own July floor by ~5%). _(mixed)_
- B2B margin reset is structural: 32.4% in H1-26 (+1,130bps YoY, +1,240bps HoH) on costs −3% to €266.7m (~$299m) with >€20m (~$22m) of run-rate cost removed and Live costs +1% against Live revenue +8%. Even if PMR fades, the cost base has been rebased. _(structural)_
- Regulated B2B growth of +21% underlying (83% of B2B) is broad across the Americas — US & Canada +161% (€56.9m / ~$64m), LatAm +29% underlying (€99.9m / ~$112m), Colombia >2x, RoW +23% — and the US business turned profitable ahead of plan. _(structural)_
- Cash generation has step-changed: H1 FCF €101.0m (~$113m) vs €6.6m, already above the €70–100m (~$78–112m) full-year medium-term target; Caliente paid €35.6m (~$40m) of real cash dividends. Net cash €39.2m (~$44m), €225m (~$252m) RCF undrawn, no bonds outstanding. _(mixed)_
- Capital return discipline: c.10% of issued share capital repurchased for c.€100m (~$112m) since Sep-25 (€24.6m / 1.8% in H1-26) on top of the €1.8bn (~$2.0bn; €5.73/share) Snaitech special dividend in June 2025; management explicitly ties the balance sheet to 'further capital returns'. _(structural)_
- The HRD stake was marked to €246.7m (~$276m), +38% in six months and c.3x cost; with the 30.8% Caliente stake and net cash, the SOTP floor under a 2027 earnings trough is tangible. _(mixed)_
- SaaS revenue +20% to €69.0m (~$77m), 17% of B2B; Playtech Protect on 41 brands/16 jurisdictions; Lucky Box Baccarat extends PMR beyond slots — the product pipeline that would need to replace first-mover PMR economics is at least visible. _(early)_

**Lowlights**

- Management has effectively called 2026 the peak: 'growing competition from third-party PMR suppliers in the US' is 'now expected to have a more material impact on Playtech's wallet share into 2027'. The upgrade is one product, one partner (Hard Rock Digital), one state (Florida) — and no PMR handle, revenue or actives are disclosed by either side, so the decay rate cannot be modelled externally. _(structural)_
- FY27 consensus of €266m (~$298m) sits 11% below the new FY26 floor and management chose not to challenge it; Peel Hunt lifted FY26 by 6% but left FY27 untouched. Our own FY27 estimate is €255–275m (~$286–308m). _(watch)_
- Brazil is a binary 2027 risk: MP 1.394 shut the regulated market on 6 Oct and extinguished all 85 licences on 25 Oct; the 'major strategic partnership' guided to sign in late 2026 — against which Playtech was already spending — goes unmentioned in the update. Three months of 2026 impact is 'absorbed'; a full year is 'more significant'. Congress deadline 23 Nov (+60 days); STF case pending. _(watch)_
- UK is in structural decline for a revenue-share supplier: H1 revenue −8% (−5% cc) to €59.0m (~$66m) on RGD 21%→40% (full-year effect only in 2027), SSBT insourcing by one customer and renegotiated terms at another; the 25% remote betting GBD lands April 2027 and 'increased taxation across other European markets' is flagged without quantification. _(structural)_
- Earnings quality: 21% of H1 adj. EBITDA (€34.2m / ~$38m) is equity-accounted income from a 30.8% stake Playtech does not control, down 19% HoH; a single customer is 14.1% of revenue; the HRD mark is set off HRD's own projections. Group 'adj. EBITDA' and 'EBITDA from operations' (30.2% margin) are very different numbers. _(mixed)_
- Cash conversion below the FCF line is opaque: FCF €101.0m less the €24.6m buyback should have added c.€76m to net cash, yet net cash rose only €10.7m — c.€66m (~$74m) of other outflows (Snaitech-linked option dividend-equivalents/bonus, tax, leases) that are excluded from the company's FCF definition [composition unverified]. _(governance)_
- Disclosure is thin for an 'inside information' RNS: no Q3 revenue, no regional data, no H2 split, no 2027 guidance, and no update on the Brazil partnership or buyback renewal. Investors are asked to take a >€300m number on trust until late March 2027. _(governance)_

## 7. Forecast & Outlook

Guidance moved from '>€270m' (~$302m; set at the 10 Sep interims, itself ahead of the 9 Jul trading update's H1 floor of >€155m) to '>€300m' (~$336m) for FY26 adj. EBITDA, versus a €279m (~$312m) consensus (range €271–285m, 7 analysts). The entire uplift is B2B Americas: PMR revenue at Hard Rock Bet did not normalise in Q3/Oct as guided, and Mexico and Colombia delivered the post-World Cup acquisition payback. Nothing improved in Europe, the UK or B2C; the three H2 headwinds flagged in September (HRD normalisation, Brazil investment, a full half of UK RGD) are still there, plus a new one — the Brazil shutdown from 6 Oct — which is being absorbed inside the number. Arithmetically, implied H2-26 goes from >€107.5m to >€137.5m (~$154m): +30% vs H2-25's €105.4m but −15% vs H1-26's €162.5m.

- **FY26 adj. EBITDA guide — old (10 Sep):** >€270m (~$302m) (H1-26A €162.5m + implied H2 >€107.5m)
- **B2B Americas outperformance (PMR at Hard Rock Bet; Mexico/Colombia post-World Cup):** +c.€30m (~$34m) (est.; entirely in H2)
- **Brazil 3-month shutdown + Brazil investment + full-half UK RGD:** absorbed (not quantified by company)
- **FY26 adj. EBITDA guide — new (10 Nov):** >€300m (~$336m) (+11% vs old; +7.5% vs cons.; +52% vs FY25A €197.0m)

**FY26: we model adj. EBITDA of €305–310m (~$342–347m)** — the 'over €300m' language plus Playtech's habit of beating its own floors by low-to-mid single digits — on group revenue of c.€830–850m (~$930–952m, +9–11%; H2-26 c.€405–425m with B2B +12–15% YoY, B2C c.€30m) and a group adj. EBITDA margin of c.36% (FY25: 26%). Segment split [estimates]: B2B c.€230–240m, investment income c.€65–75m, B2C c.nil. FCF should comfortably exceed €150m (~$168m) for the year given H1's €101m, though H2 will be softer on Caliente dividend timing and Brazil spend; **net cash should build towards €80–120m (~$90–134m) by December absent a new buyback**, and we expect the buyback to be renewed with FY26 results in late March 2027 — the balance sheet (no bonds, €225m undrawn RCF) is under-levered for a 30%+ margin B2B business. **FY27 is where the stock is priced: our range is €255–275m (~$286–308m), bracketing the €266m consensus**, built as PMR wallet-share loss −€25–35m, full-year UK RGD/GBD and customer changes −€6–10m, Brazil −€5–10m if dark all year (and no partnership upside), offset by Mexico/Colombia/US Live/Canada growth +€15–25m and further cost take-out; that is −10% to −17% on our FY26 estimate, with a group margin falling back towards 31–33%. Swing factors, in order of size: (1) how fast IGT/L&W PMR content takes share at Hard Rock Bet — a 20% share loss keeps FY27 near €275m, a 50% loss drags it below €250m; (2) whether Brazil's Congress amends or lets MP 1.394 lapse by Q1-27, which would restore the strategic partnership and GaleraBet; (3) whether PMR can be exported beyond Florida (compact-specific, so we assume no in 2027); (4) Caliente's dividend cadence. The next hard datapoint is FY26 results in late March 2027; we would not expect a Q4 trading statement to add numbers.

**What to watch next quarter:**

- Brazil: Congress's first 60-day window on MP 1.394 closes 23 Nov 2026 (auto-extends to late Jan); amendment text restoring licences; STF ruling on ADI 7721; any RNS on the 'major strategic partnership' that was due to sign in late 2026.
- Florida PMR: launch timing and Hard Rock Bet lobby placement of IGT and Light & Wonder PMR titles; any disclosure of Playtech's wallet share or PMR run-rate at the FY26 results; Hard Rock's appetite to take PMR to its other eight sportsbook states (compact-dependent).
- UK: full-year 40% RGD pass-through in revenue-share fees, the 25% remote betting GBD from April 2027, further SSBT/platform insourcing by large UK customers, and any gaming-machine tax changes flagged on the H1 call; Sun Bingo's H2 contribution.
- Investment income: H2 Caliente share of income vs H1's €30.1m, size and timing of the next Caliente dividend, HRD's December fair-value mark and any HRD liquidity event (IPO/secondary).
- Capital allocation at FY26 results (c. late Mar 2027): buyback renewal size, any move to an ordinary dividend, net cash reconciliation (the c.€66m of below-FCF outflows in H1), and whether M&A appetite changes as net cash builds.
- Other European tax moves named by management as 2027 headwinds — jurisdictions and quantum — plus Poland/Spain growth sustainability in Europe ex-UK.
