Q3 2026 · results · analyst report

Bally's Intralot

INLOT · Released 10 Nov 2026 (preliminary Q3/9M announcement, Euronext Athens) / 20 Nov 2026 (full 9M 2026 press release, slides and call) · Currency EUR (tables in € millions; ~USD equivalents at EUR/USD 1.10) · Data confidence: MEDIUM

Market reaction: 20 Nov 2026 (full 9M release and call): Bally's Intralot (Euronext Athens: INLOT) -1.9% to ~€1.065 (~$1.17) per Investing.com, which printed the level with a '$' sign we believe is a currency mislabel of the ATHEX euro quote. Reaction to the 10 Nov preliminary announcement not sourced [unverified].

The one-line verdict

Operationally a clean quarter inside a stretched balance sheet: UK online NGR grew 12.2% cc through the first full quarters of 40% RGD, group AEBITDA edged up 5% QoQ to €88.9m (~$98m) at a 31.1% margin, and the legacy lottery business stabilised QoQ. But pro-forma net leverage climbed to 4.33x (vs 3.5x at Dec-25 and a 4.75x covenant) because the LTM denominator is shrinking, the evoke close has slipped into 2027, and the group still reported a €23.8m (~$26m) Q3 net loss after €40.1m (~$44m) of finance costs and €13.7m (~$15m) of deal fees. Deleveraging now depends on Q4 seasonality and a US jackpot cycle, neither of which management controls.

1Executive summary

  • Headline: revenue €286.1m (~$315m), +249% YoY and +3.6% QoQ; AEBITDA €88.9m (~$98m), +197% YoY and +5.1% QoQ. The YoY optics are meaningless (Bally's International Interactive, 'BII', was only consolidated from Q4-25); the relevant read is sequential: AEBITDA +€4.3m (~$5m) QoQ with margin +50bps to 31.1%. There is no published sell-side consensus for INLOT; the parent Bally's Corp printed Q3 revenue of $807.6m vs a $807.85m estimate (in line).
  • 9M 2026: revenue €830.3m (~$913m) vs €263.9m; AEBITDA €273.6m (~$301m) vs €90.1m; margin 33.0% vs 34.2%. On a pro-forma basis (as if BII owned throughout) management disclosed 9M AEBITDA down ~15% YoY — i.e. the combined group lost roughly €48m (~$53m) of EBITDA YoY, essentially the UK duty hit plus US lottery weakness.
  • No formal numeric guidance exists and none was introduced. Bally's Intralot gives qualitative direction only: continued UK online growth, a stronger H2 in US lottery and Turkey (H1 call), and evoke completion now 'in 2027' (Earnings+More cites January 2027). The 'guidance bridge' for this name is therefore the LTM pro-forma AEBITDA that drives the leverage covenant: €430.8m (~$474m) at Dec-25 → €376.7m (~$414m) at Sep-26.
  • The division that drove the quarter was BII (B2C online), +4.0% QoQ to €201.6m (~$222m), 70% of group revenue. UK online revenue rose 3.8% QoQ (+€6.8m) and 12.2% YoY in constant currency, a third straight quarter of acceleration (10.5% → 11.6% → 12.2%) and a third consecutive all-time-high quarterly NGR — achieved on lower marketing spend. Spain hit a record €13.3m (~$15m), +18.9% YoY on a larger active base.
  • UK Remote Gaming Duty (21% → 40% from 1 Apr 2026) cost ~€34m (~$37m) of gross AEBITDA in Q3, in line with Q2; 9M gross hit ~€68m (~$75m). Management says ~55% of the Q3 hit was offset YoY (Q2: 65%) via top-line growth, marketing efficiency and cost control. Note the metric is loosely defined: the 9M components disclosed (gross-profit growth €26.2m + fixed-cost actions €6.8m = €33.0m) equal ~49% of €68m, consistent with iGB's 'nearly half' rather than 55-65%. Gaming-tax expense in the P&L was €76.8m (~$84m) in Q3 and €192.2m (~$211m) for 9M.
  • Legacy Intralot (B2B lottery + Bilyoner/Argentina) revenue was €84.5m (~$93m) in Q3, +2.7% QoQ and +3.2% YoY vs the €81.9m Q3-25 standalone base; AEBITDA €26.0m (~$29m), +6.3% QoQ but -13% YoY (Q3-25: €29.9m). The QoQ improvement was Bilyoner (+€4.7m AEBITDA) and Argentina; B2B went the other way.
  • B2B lottery remains the soft spot: 9M revenue €191.0m (~$210m), -8.1% reported / -6.2% cc. The US, the largest market, fell 11% cc to €119.6m (~$132m) against a prior year flattered by record jackpots and on lower equipment sales; the rest of the B2B portfolio grew 3.6% cc. Management keeps framing this as jackpot cyclicality and seasonality; the equipment-sales shortfall is less cyclical.
  • Legacy B2C (Bilyoner Turkey + Argentina) 9M revenue €60.1m (~$66m), +7.1%. Bilyoner grew 20.6% in local currency but only 3.4% in euros to €45.8m (~$50m) — TRY depreciation ate most of it — with amounts wagered +28.4% vs a market +17.8% (share gain). Q3 Bilyoner revenue rebounded 25.8% QoQ (+€3.3m) after a seasonal Q2 drop of 21.8%. Argentina grew 21.1% to €14.3m (~$16m) for 9M.
  • Below AEBITDA the P&L is heavy: Q3 D&A €45.2m (~$50m), net finance costs €40.1m (~$44m), transaction fees €13.7m (~$15m) → pre-tax loss €14.3m, net loss after tax €23.8m (~$26m), NIATMI €(26.6)m. 9M: finance costs €109.7m (~$121m, vs €20.5m), D&A €130.7m, deal fees €27.7m (~$30m), net loss €36.4m (~$40m) vs €1.1m profit. Deal fees are 'one-off' only if the evoke deal stops generating them, which it will not until closing.
  • Cash generation is the better story: 9M operating cash flow €206.6m (~$227m) vs €79.1m; Q3 OCF €73.2m (~$81m) vs €14.7m. But 9M capex was €133.3m (~$147m), of which €85m (~$94m) was the 15-year Victoria (Australia) EGM-monitoring licence premium paid in Q2; underlying Q3 capex was ~€16m (~$17m, derived). OCF is stated before interest paid (IFRS financing classification), so after ~€100m+ of cash interest the 9M free cash flow is at best marginally positive.
  • Leverage is the issue: adjusted net debt €1,629.9m (~$1.79bn), +€11m QoQ and +€136m YTD; pro-forma adjusted net leverage 4.33x vs 4.05x at Jun-26 and 3.5x at Dec-25/Mar-26. Management stressed the QoQ move 'is arithmetic, not cash' — LTM PF AEBITDA fell to €376.7m from €399.9m — but the YTD net-debt increase is real: €85m Australia licence, €19.5m transaction/bond-issuance costs, €30.0m dividends and ~€21m of H1 buybacks. Funded debt rose to €1,882.0m (~$2.07bn) from €1,690.5m at Dec-25; total debt incl. leases ~€1.94bn (~$2.13bn).
  • Covenant headroom is thin: the Intralot notes/RCF carry a total net leverage ceiling of 4.75x (per Bally's Corp 10-Q disclosure). On our arithmetic, Q4-25 pro-forma AEBITDA was ~€103m (LTM €376.7m less 9M €273.6m); unless Q4-26 beats that pre-duty comparable, LTM AEBITDA falls further and Dec-26 leverage lands ~4.4-4.6x on flat-to-higher net debt (our estimate, see Forecast).
  • Liquidity was rebuilt: €472.4m (~$520m) at 30 Sep vs €287.3m at 30 Jun, comprising €312.4m (~$344m) cash incl. restricted and a €160m (~$176m) undrawn RCF. The driver was the £261.78m (~$340m) senior secured term facility signed 27 Jul, with £157.1m (~$204m; net £150.0m) drawn 30 Jul (matures Jul-2029, SONIA + 5.25-5.50%, bullet) and £104.7m undrawn. Cash and equivalents rose to €297.2m from €177.1m.
  • evoke (William Hill/888) all-share acquisition — 0.537 new INLOT shares per evoke share, ~£243.1m (~$316m) equity value, 77% premium to the 17 Apr undisturbed price — approved by evoke shareholders on 18 Aug (99.63%) but completion now pushed to 2027. The UKGC has confirmed evoke's licences continue on completion; remaining gating items are other gambling-regulator approvals and the Gibraltar court hearing. Management reiterated ~£180m (~$234m) of pre-tax cost and capex savings by the end of year two post-completion; the delay pushes that timetable out. Until closing the two groups are 'separate, independent and competing'.
  • Parent-level overhang: Bally's Corp (NYSE: BALY) owns ~58% of Bally's Intralot and reports it as two segments — Bally's Intralot B2C revenue $257.5m (+22.8%) with Adj. EBITDAR $77.0m vs $86.4m (-10.9%), and B2B $76.6m vs $5.5m — combined $333.9m (+55%), EBITDAR $89.1m (-3%). Bally's Corp has filed late 10-Qs for all three 2026 quarters, operates under a conditional RCF covenant waiver with a minimum-liquidity test (GamblingInsider reported on 17 Nov that the Q3 10-Q shows this requirement was missed — not verified by us against the filing), and held no Q3 call. The parent's funding needs are a structural risk to minority holders of INLOT.
  • Capital allocation is mixed-signal: €30.0m (~$33m) of dividends and ~€21m (~$23m) of buybacks were paid in 9M while leverage rose above 4x; since 16 Jun a total-return equity swap with Deutsche Bank has seen DB buying INLOT shares weekly through November (synthetic buyback; terms not disclosed in sources read). For a 4.3x-levered group with an acquisition pending, this is not what a deleveraging story looks like.
  • B2B pipeline is a genuine offset to US weakness: 2026 wins include OLG (Ontario) selecting Intralot Canada as its lottery technology provider (Jun), Polla Chilena (Chile, Apr), the 15-year Victoria EGM-monitoring licence (Apr), BCLC shared services (Apr), the Hellenic Lotteries renewal with Allwyn Hellas (May) and the Premier Lotteries Ireland extension (Jul). None is yet material to revenue; OLG conversion will be the swing factor for 2027-28 B2B.
  • Pro-forma LTM: revenue €1,101.7m (~$1.21bn), AEBITDA €376.7m (~$414m), 34.2% margin. The LTM margin is above the 33.0% 9M margin only because Q4-25 was pre-duty; the true run-rate margin for a 40%-RGD UK book is the 31% printed in Q2/Q3.
  • Thesis read-through: the UK scale argument is holding — share gains as sub-scale rivals cut promotion (CBRE: 'scale is key') are visible in three quarters of accelerating cc growth at lower marketing cost. But the equity is a levered option on (i) Q4 UK seasonality, (ii) a US jackpot cycle, (iii) evoke closing and the £180m synergy case being delivered on a target that itself was flagged as a going-concern risk. Shares -1.9% to ~€1.065 (~$1.17) on the 20 Nov full release (Investing.com; reaction on the 10 Nov preliminary not sourced).

3Segment deep-dive: B2C – Bally's International Interactive (BII: UK, Spain, other online)

UK online +12.2% cc on lower marketing, record NGR for a third quarter; ~€34m/qtr RGD hit ~55% offset; segment margin stuck at ~31% post-duty vs ~39% pre-duty.

Segment scale

BII, the former Gamesys business acquired from Bally's Corp in October 2025, is now the group: €201.6m (~$222m) of Q3 revenue, 70% of the total.

  • Revenue up 4.0% quarter-on-quarter from ~€193.8m (~$213m).
  • UK is roughly 92% of the segment and ~65% of group revenue.
  • Brands: Jackpotjoy, Virgin Games, Monopoly Casino, Bally's-branded casino.

UK online

UK online rose 12.2% YoY in constant currency and 3.8% QoQ, a third successive acceleration and third consecutive record quarterly NGR, on lower marketing.

  • QoQ +€6.8m, implying ~€185.7m (~$204m) in the quarter on our arithmetic.
  • Acceleration from 10.5% in Q1 and 11.6% in Q2.
  • Management attributed growth to organic growth and the installed player base rather than acquisition; marketing was lower than prior quarter and prior year.
  • No actives, ARPU or marketing as a percentage of revenue disclosed — a disclosure gap for a business whose equity case rests on UK customer economics.

Spain and other

Spain delivered another record at €13.3m (~$15m), +18.9% YoY in constant currency and roughly +7% QoQ, on more actives.

  • Q2 was ~€12.4m after +15.1% YoY.
  • Remaining ~€2-3m of BII revenue (other markets) is immaterial.
  • Bally's Corp cross-check: 'Bally's Intralot B2C' revenue $257.5m, +22.8% YoY (includes Bilyoner and Argentina).
  • Segment Adjusted EBITDAR fell to $77.0m from $86.4m (-10.9%) — the cleanest statement of what 40% RGD did to the UK book.

Margin and duty

Derived BII AEBITDA of ~€62.9m (~$69m) in Q3, a ~31.2% margin, versus 39.5% in Q1 before the RGD step-up from 21% to 40%.

  • Q2 was ~€60.1m / 31.0%; Q1 ~€72.6m / 39.5%; 9M €195.6m (~$215m) at 33.8%, corroborated by Earnings+More.
  • Gross negative AEBITDA impact of RGD ~€34m (~$37m) in Q3, in line with Q2, ~€68m (~$75m) year-to-date.
  • Management says ~55% of the Q3 hit was offset YoY (Q2: 65%, comprising €11.3m operating-cost optimisation and €10.1m UK/Spain top-line growth).
  • 9M disclosure of €26.2m gross-profit growth plus €6.8m fixed-cost actions (€33.0m, ~49% of €68m) does not reconcile to 55-65%; we use the 9M components.
  • No separate cost-transformation programme with a named target; offsets are run-rate marketing and overhead actions.

Structural read

UK scale is working as the bull case predicted, but the post-duty margin of ~31% will not recover to 35-39% without pricing that would test growth.

  • Sub-scale operators are pulling back promotion; first RGD receipts more than doubled to ~£590m per NEXT.io.
  • Bally's is taking share at lower cost per pound of NGR.
  • Q2-27 is when the duty laps; until then the right KPI is QoQ NGR.

Guidance and evoke

Segment guidance is qualitative only — continued UK growth, Spain records — with the evoke combination slated for 2027 roughly doubling the UK B2C footprint.

  • evoke brings William Hill, 888 and Mr Green.
  • Combination would bring the ~£180m (~$234m) two-year cost/capex synergy target into scope.

4Segment deep-dive: Legacy Intralot – B2B lottery technology (US, RoW) and legacy B2C (Bilyoner Turkey, Argentina)

Stabilised QoQ (revenue +2.7%, AEBITDA +6.3% to €26.0m) on Bilyoner's seasonal rebound, but AEBITDA still -13% YoY as US lottery (-11% cc 9M) laps record jackpots and equipment sales.

Perimeter results

Legacy Intralot stabilised QoQ with revenue €84.5m (~$93m) +2.7% and AEBITDA €26.0m (~$29m) +6.3%, but AEBITDA is still 13% below a year ago.

  • Revenue 3.2% above the €81.9m standalone Q3-25 base; AEBITDA versus €29.9m a year ago; margin 30.8% against 36.5%.
  • Nine months: revenue €251.1m (~$276m) (-4.9%), AEBITDA €78.0m (~$86m) (-13.4%).
  • Management led with the sequential improvement; the YoY decline, the more honest comparison, was not emphasised.
“led by Bilyoner and Argentina”Management

B2B lottery

B2B lottery is the drag: nine-month revenue €191.0m (~$210m), -8.1% reported and -6.2% constant currency, with the US down 11% cc.

  • US, the largest market at €119.6m (~$132m), laps record Powerball/Mega Millions jackpots and non-recurring equipment sales.
  • Rest of portfolio (Greece, Australia, Ireland, Canada, LatAm, Africa) grew 3.6% cc.
  • Management pointed in September to a stronger H2; Q3 did not obviously deliver, and B2B AEBITDA fell by several million euros QoQ on our arithmetic.
  • Bally's Corp 'Bally's Intralot B2B' Q3 revenue $76.6m, implied EBITDAR roughly $12m ($89.1m combined less $77.0m B2C) — thin for a business carrying the Intralot bond structure.
“softness and seasonality”Management

Legacy B2C

Legacy B2C is the bright spot at €60.1m (~$66m) for nine months, +7.1%, led by Bilyoner's 20.6% local-currency growth and share gains.

  • Bilyoner grew only 3.4% in euros to €45.8m (~$50m) as lira depreciation absorbs most growth; amounts wagered +28.4% versus market +17.8%.
  • Seasonal around the Süper Lig: Q2 revenue -21.8% QoQ (-€3.6m) with €3.2m AEBITDA drag; Q3 +25.8% QoQ (+€3.3m, to ~€16.1m (~$18m)) with AEBITDA +€4.7m (~$5m).
  • Argentina added €0.9m QoQ and is up 21.1% to €14.3m (~$16m) for nine months.
  • No actives, margins or hold data disclosed for either market.

Contract pipeline

The contract pipeline is genuinely better than a year ago and is the medium-term offset to US weakness, but none is yet in the numbers.

  • OLG (Ontario) selected Intralot Canada as lottery technology provider in June; Polla Chilena de Beneficencia new contract in April.
  • 15-year Victoria EGM-monitoring licence secured in April, with an €85m (~$94m) premium that inflated Q2 capex and net debt.
  • BCLC shared services (April), Hellenic Lotteries renewal with Allwyn Hellas (May), Premier Lotteries Ireland extension (July).

Guidance

No explicit segment guidance; we expect B2B revenue to track the jackpot cycle and OLG/Chile hardware timing, with Bilyoner Q4 the more predictable lift.

  • September call framed H2 as stronger in US lottery and Turkey.
  • Q3 call split qualitative commentary between 'the BII business and the lotteries business' without numbers.
  • Bilyoner Q4 (football season) is the more predictable earnings lift.

5Highlights & lowlights

▲ Highlights

  • UK online NGR +12.2% YoY cc in Q3, accelerating for a third quarter (10.5% → 11.6% → 12.2%) and a third straight all-time high, achieved on lower marketing — the scale/share-gain thesis under 40% RGD is being evidenced, not asserted. structural
  • Group AEBITDA +5.1% QoQ to €88.9m (~$98m) with margin +50bps to 31.1%, despite a flat ~€34m (~$37m) quarterly duty burden; the post-duty margin floor appears to be ~31%. structural
  • Spain at a record €13.3m (~$15m), +18.9% YoY on a larger active base — small, but a second regulated market compounding at high-teens. structural
  • Liquidity rebuilt to €472.4m (~$520m) from €287.3m via the £261.78m (~$340m) 2029 term facility (£104.7m still undrawn); operating cash flow of €73.2m (~$81m) in Q3 and €206.6m (~$227m) for 9M is real cash before interest. one-off
  • Bilyoner rebounded 25.8% QoQ in revenue and added €4.7m (~$5m) of AEBITDA, with wagers growing 28.4% vs a market at 17.8%; legacy Intralot AEBITDA +6.3% QoQ. mixed
  • B2B pipeline refreshed (OLG Ontario, Polla Chilena, Victoria 15-year monitoring, BCLC, Hellenic Lotteries, PLI Ireland) — not in the numbers yet, but the first positive contract momentum in several years. early

▼ Lowlights

  • Pro-forma adjusted net leverage 4.33x vs 4.05x at Jun-26 and 3.5x at Dec-25, against a 4.75x total net leverage covenant; LTM PF AEBITDA has fallen €54m (~$59m) in nine months to €376.7m (~$414m) and will fall further in Q4 unless the quarter beats the ~€103m pre-duty Q4-25 comparable (our arithmetic). structural
  • Net loss after tax €23.8m (~$26m) in Q3 and €36.4m (~$40m) for 9M; net finance costs €109.7m (~$121m) YTD and €27.7m (~$30m) of transaction fees — the capital structure consumes ~40% of AEBITDA before any deleveraging. structural
  • US lottery revenue -11% cc for 9M (€119.6m / ~$132m) on jackpot and equipment comps; legacy Intralot AEBITDA -13% YoY in Q3 and -13.4% for 9M. The 'stronger H2' promised in September is not yet visible. mixed
  • evoke completion slipped to 2027 (Gibraltar court, further regulator approvals), deferring the ~£180m (~$234m) synergy clock and extending the period in which deal fees accrue and the target — which flagged going-concern uncertainty tied to Bally's intentions — operates independently. watch
  • Parent Bally's Corp (58% holder) has filed three late 10-Qs in 2026, operates under a covenant waiver with a minimum-liquidity test (reported missed per GamblingInsider, unverified by us), and held no Q3 call; the parent's funding needs are a governance and capital-allocation overhang on INLOT minorities. governance
  • Disclosure is thin for a B2C-led group: no actives, ARPU, marketing % of revenue, hold or segment EBITDA by quarter; the duty 'offset' metric moves between 49% (9M components), 55% (Q3) and 65% (Q2) without a consistent definition. governance
  • €30m (~$33m) of dividends, ~€21m (~$23m) of H1 buybacks and an ongoing Deutsche Bank total-return-swap purchase programme while leverage rose through 4x — capital allocation is at odds with the deleveraging the covenant requires. watch

6Forecast & outlook

Guidance bridge. Bally's Intralot does not publish numeric group guidance, so there is no 'old vs new' to reconcile; the operative forward-looking numbers are the LTM pro-forma AEBITDA that sets covenant leverage and management's qualitative steer (continued UK online growth; stronger H2 in US lottery and Turkey; evoke closing in 2027, Earnings+More cites January). The division driving the deterioration in the leverage denominator is BII (UK duty, ~€34m/qtr gross, roughly half recovered), with legacy B2B (US lottery) the secondary drag; the division driving the sequential improvement is also BII (UK NGR +3.8% QoQ) plus Bilyoner's seasonal recovery. Independent view. Q4-26e (our estimate): revenue €290-300m (~$319-330m), AEBITDA €88-95m (~$97-105m), margin ~30-32%. The UK bingo/casino book is seasonally strongest in Q4 and the Süper Lig lifts Bilyoner, but the duty is now fully in the base and the US needs a jackpot run to lap a strong Q4-25. That puts FY26e revenue at ~€1.12-1.13bn (~$1.23-1.24bn) and AEBITDA at ~€362-369m (~$398-406m), a ~32% margin — below the €376.7m LTM print, because Q4-25 PF AEBITDA (~€103m) was pre-duty. Leverage therefore rises again at Dec-26: on flat net debt of ~€1.63bn we get ~4.4-4.5x; with the October bond coupon and any further DB share purchases pushing net debt to ~€1.66bn, ~4.5-4.6x — inside the 4.75x ceiling but with headroom of only ~€10-20m of AEBITDA. The direction only reverses in H2-27 when the duty laps (Q2-27), OLG/Chile revenue starts, and — if it closes — evoke's EBITDA enters the denominator; but evoke brings its own debt and a 'material uncertainty' balance sheet, so pro-forma leverage is more likely to rise than fall at completion before the £180m synergies land in 2028-29. Net: revenue up, margin flat at ~31%, leverage up through year-end; the equity is a call on execution in 2027, not on 2026 numbers.

What to watch next quarter

  • Q4 UK NGR growth (cc) vs the ~€34m quarterly duty: anything below ~10% cc suggests the share-gain tailwind is fading before the Q2-27 lap.
  • Dec-26 pro-forma leverage vs the 4.75x covenant: watch Q4 AEBITDA against the ~€103m Q4-25 PF comparable and any further Deutsche Bank TRS purchases or dividend declarations that add to net debt.
  • evoke closing timetable: remaining gambling-regulator approvals and the Gibraltar court hearing (company: 2027; E+M: January 2027), plus evoke's own Q4 trading and going-concern language.
  • US lottery: jackpot cycle, equipment-sales timing and OLG (Ontario) contract conversion into 2027 revenue; legacy B2B AEBITDA needs to stop falling YoY.
  • Bilyoner: Q4 football-season revenue and TRY/EUR — 20.6% local-currency growth became 3.4% in euros for 9M.
  • Parent Bally's Corp: 10-Q timeliness, RCF covenant waiver/minimum-liquidity compliance, going-concern language and any upstreaming or asset moves that affect INLOT minorities.
  • UK regulation: Gambling Act review implementation (affordability checks, bonus restrictions) and whether a second-round RGD or stake-limit change appears in the 2027 fiscal calendar.
  • Disclosure: whether the FY25/FY26 annual report finally gives actives, ARPU and marketing % of revenue for the UK book — without them the 'marketing efficiency' claim cannot be audited.
