H1 FY27 · results · analyst report

Konami Group

9766.T · Released 29 Oct 2026 · Currency JPY (tables in ¥ millions; USD equivalents in parentheses at USD/JPY 157.83, 29 Oct 2026 close) · Data confidence: HIGH

Market reaction: Shares rose 13.8% to ¥26,110 (~$165) on Friday 30 Oct 2026 per Investing.com (Nikkei and Gaming Intelligence cite ~+15% at the close), against a Nikkei 225 that fell 2–3% on AI-capex and BoJ concerns; the stock has since extended to ~¥29,180 (~$185) by mid-November, with JPMorgan (¥35,100), CLSA (¥33,600), Macquarie (¥30,000) and Nomura (¥27,000) raising targets post-print.

The one-line verdict

A clean beat on every line and the first mid-year guidance upgrade in 15 years: derived Q2 operating profit of ¥66.0bn (~$418m) was ~32% above the ~¥50bn market expectation and FY3/27 OP guidance moved from ¥143bn to ¥193bn (~$1.22bn), with 100% of the raise coming from Digital Entertainment (eFootball's World Cup window plus Yu-Gi-Oh! card demand). The debate now is durability, not delivery: management's own H2 implies DE business profit falling 25% from H1, Gaming & Systems guidance was left stale, and the equity's ~26x forward multiple rests on one live-service franchise holding a tournament-year cohort into a quadrennial comp.

3Segment deep-dive: Digital Entertainment (mobile, console/PC, Yu-Gi-Oh! card game, esports)

Q2 revenue +50% and a 44% margin on eFootball's tournament window and Yu-Gi-Oh!; the whole group upgrade sits here, and so does the FY3/28 comp risk.

H1 revenue

H1 revenue ¥243.5bn (~$1.54bn), up 44.3%; Q2 ¥143.4bn (~$909m), up 50.3% YoY and 43.3% QoQ, the division's largest quarter.

  • Segment was 80% of group revenue in the period.
  • eFootball campaigns commemorating the June–July 2026 World Cup, then the August major update and new-season campaign they say has been performing well.
  • Mobile baseball titles (PROFESSIONAL BASEBALL SPIRITS A, JIKKYOU PAWAFURU PUROYAKYU, eBaseball MLB PRO SPIRIT) where various measures were "received positively".
  • Yu-Gi-Oh!: MASTER DUEL campaign for passing 100 million cumulative downloads; OCG special-edition collectible products themed on past anime protagonists.
“received positively”Management

New releases, mix

New releases were secondary to the live-service base; no MAU, ARPU, paying-user, title-level revenue or mobile/console/card split is disclosed.

  • METAL GEAR SOLID: MASTER COLLECTION Vol.2 (August, first port of MGS4), SILENT HILL: Townfall (September), eBaseball PRO SPIRIT 2026 (July, 200+ territories), Suikoden STAR LEAP (mobile, Japan, August).
  • Group service & other revenue rose 42.0% to ¥223.2bn (~$1.41bn); product sales rose 25.3% to ¥84.8bn (~$537m).
  • Consistent with in-game spend rather than packaged software doing the heavy lifting; Yu-Gi-Oh! physical product the main contributor on the product side.

Profit and margin

H1 business profit rose 75.1% to ¥107.5bn (~$681m), a 44.1% margin versus 36.4% (+780bps); Q2 ¥63.6bn (~$403m), 44.3% margin.

  • Q2 margin +920bps YoY, +50bps QoQ. After ¥1.5bn other expenses (¥0.5bn last year), operating profit ¥106.0bn (~$672m), +74.2%, a 44% margin.
  • ¥46.1bn incremental business profit on ¥74.7bn incremental revenue, roughly 62%, is what live-service monetisation on an installed base looks like.
  • Group gross margin moved to 54.8% from 49.4%; SG&A fell to 18.4% of revenue from 20.4% without any announced cost programme.
  • Margin is hostage to eFootball's spend curve: leverage runs in reverse if tournament-year cohorts churn.
  • Segment is now ~93% of segment-level business profit before corporate costs.

Geographic split

Split is by selling entity: Japan-booked revenue rose 58.3% to ¥251.7bn (~$1.59bn) while US fell 13.2% and Europe fell 38.6%.

  • Japan carries global eFootball and Yu-Gi-Oh! digital sales. US ¥34.7bn (~$220m), Europe ¥10.3bn (~$65m); Q2 down 23.7% and 41.7% respectively.
  • Overseas declines are roll-off of METAL GEAR SOLID Δ (August 2025) and SILENT HILL f (September 2025), both over a million units at launch, sold through overseas subsidiaries.
  • Not evidence of weak overseas live-service demand, but the premium console pipeline is thinner in FY3/27 and growth is almost entirely in-game.
  • Asia/Oceania rose 24.3% to ¥11.3bn (~$71m), helped by Gaming & Systems' Australian business as much as by DE.

Esports

Esports activity was unusually visible; retention tools rather than revenue lines, but the mechanism by which management hopes to hold the tournament cohort.

  • eFootball was a medal title at the 20th Asian Games in Japan.
  • Yu-Gi-Oh! World Championship 2026 finals for all four divisions were held in Tokyo.

FY3/27 guidance

Guidance raised to ¥450bn revenue/¥188bn business profit (~$2.85bn/~$1.19bn) from ¥372bn/¥138bn; operating profit ¥181bn (~$1.15bn) from ¥131bn.

  • Implies a 40% OP margin for the year versus 35% in the initial plan.
  • Implied H2 revenue ¥206.5bn (~$1.31bn; −15% vs H1, +2% vs H2 FY3/26); business profit ¥80.5bn (~$510m; −25% vs H1, 39% margin).
  • That is ~¥40bn a quarter against the ¥63.6bn just printed.
  • Investing.com reports management told the briefing the plan assumes a slowdown in H2 DE profit and they regard it as conservative; we agree.
  • Our estimate: H2 DE business profit of ¥88–92bn looks more plausible than ¥80.5bn unless eFootball spend reverts fully to pre-tournament levels within two quarters.

H2 slate, FY3/28 risk

H2 slate centres on Castlevania, Yu-Gi-Oh! TAG FORCE GX and eFootball Championship 2027; the harder question is FY3/28 when the quadrennial comp rolls.

  • Castlevania: Belmont's Curse (October, 40th-anniversary title); Yu-Gi-Oh! TAG FORCE GX (February 2027, remake with online multiplayer and ~200 added cards); continued OCG product cadence.
  • Suikoden STAR LEAP global rollout; eFootball Championship 2027 with expanded finals field after 45.21m entrants last time; September season update designed to prevent spend reversion.
  • Rev. NOiR, Dice Strikers (soft-launched in Canada and Spain) and Rhapsody in Scarlet are 2027/TBD.
  • No equivalent catalyst to the tournament in the disclosed pipeline for FY3/28.

4Segment deep-dive: Arcade Game (video, music, medal, prize/redemption; separate segment since Q3 FY3/26)

Demon Slayer card-dispensing title lifted Q2 revenue 25% and the margin to 29%; H2 is loaded with cabinet launches and guidance was not raised.

Revenue and drivers

H1 revenue grew 17.4% to ¥10.4bn (~$66m); Q2 ¥6.0bn (~$38m), up 24.9% YoY and 40.2% on Q1's seasonally thin ¥4.3bn.

  • Standalone since the December 2025 quarter after Konami Arcade Games Co. (established 2 June 2025) absorbed Konami Amusement's arcade business on 1 October 2025.
  • Driver was Demon Slayer: Nichirin Battle Slash, launched July as first arcade title on the IP, with machines dispensing collectible character cards.
  • Second card series (Mugen Train Arc) followed in September and is described as well received.
  • beatmania IIDX 34 ZINRAI, the Shohei Ohtani-fronted PAWAFURU PUROYAKYU KAIMAKU MEDAL SERIES! KIWAMI NITOURYU medal game and North American start of TREASURE ROAD rounded out the half.
  • Domestic arcade market characterised as performing steadily on the back of the moderate economic recovery.

Profit and margin

Business profit rose 50.1% to ¥2.7bn (~$17m), a 25.8% margin versus 20.1%; Q2 ¥1.7bn (~$11m), a 28.8% margin.

  • Q2 margin +420bps YoY, +730bps QoQ.
  • Card-dispensing formats carry a consumable revenue stream structurally higher-margin than cabinet sales, explaining most of the mix-driven lift.
  • No segment opex or unit disclosure to decompose further; other income/expenses nil, so business profit equals operating profit.

Guidance and H2 pipeline

FY3/27 guidance unchanged at ¥29.0bn revenue (~$184m) and ¥7.5bn business profit (~$48m, 26% margin); H1 only 36% of both, H2-weighted by design.

  • November AMUSEMENT EXPO 2026 showcases jubeat U'nit on first new jubeat cabinet in 18 years (touch panel above screen, synced visual effects; Japan, Asia, North America, then global).
  • Momotaro Dentetsu 2: Anata no Machi no Medal Game (East and West Japan maps); Anima Lotta: Anima to Shiawase no Okurimono for the series' 15th anniversary.
  • North America pipeline: BLAZING ENCOUNTER (October 2026), GHOSTBUSTERS and the HOLO CARD STATION card-printing game.
  • BEMANI PRO LEAGUE Season 6 regular stage begins in November across beatmania IIDX, SOUND VOLTEX and DanceDanceRevolution.

H2 delivery risk

Implied H2 of ~¥18.6bn revenue (~$118m) and ~¥4.8bn business profit (~$30m) requires new cabinets to ship on schedule into year-end operator budgets.

  • Compares with ¥17.6bn revenue and ~¥5.0bn business profit in H2 FY3/26.
  • Segment is 3% of group revenue and 2% of segment profit, so it does not move the group.
  • It is the one place where guidance is not obviously conservative and where a cabinet slip would show up as a miss.

5Segment deep-dive: Gaming & Systems (Konami Gaming Inc., Las Vegas; Konami Australia)

Revenue +41% and profit +154% on the Solstice 49C refresh, SYNKROS cruise/property wins and BOMBERMAN; margin still only 9.6% and the FY guide was left untouched, implying an H2 revenue decline we don't believe.

Revenue and cabinets

H1 revenue grew 41.3% to ¥25.1bn (~$159m); Q2 ¥12.7bn (~$81m), up 24.1% YoY and 2.7% on Q1, after Q1's exceptional +64.9%.

  • North American and Australian markets described as stable; new casino openings and replacement demand create placements amid competitor launches, a market-share story rather than market growth.
  • Solstice 49C curved 49-inch UHD cabinet, receiving high praise, drove gaming-machine unit sales in both quarters.
  • Lineup broadened with Solstice 27 (27-inch monitors, launched Q2) and 75-inch Solstice SuperNova shown at Global Gaming Expo 2026 for Q3 launch.
  • BOMBERMAN series, Gaming Intelligence's North America land-based game of the year, led North American sales; Dragon Rush and Bull Rush Stampede perform in Australia, Fortune Hearts next.

Systems and specialty markets

SYNKROS and specialty markets hold the recurring-revenue optionality, with more than 150,000 slot machines connected worldwide, but recurring mix cannot be verified.

  • SYNKROS extended to California and Arizona properties in Q2 (Pennsylvania and Nevada in Q1) and Carnival Corporation cruise ships; further cruise installations and property selections in hand.
  • Specialty Markets expanded Class II installations in Texas and Washington; VLTs and historical horse racing machines next; iGaming widening content distribution across online platforms.
  • No disclosure of units shipped, ASP, installed base, participation/recurring share, ship-share or gaming-operations yield, so price cannot be separated from volume.
  • +41% revenue against a flat market implies meaningful ship-share gain; absence of recurring-revenue disclosure is a real gap for a segment whose peers are valued on it.

Profit and margin

Business profit rose 154.4% to ¥2.4bn (~$15m), a 9.6% margin versus 5.3%; Q2 ¥1.6bn (~$10m), 12.5% margin (+160bps YoY, +580bps QoQ).

  • Q1 FY3/26 was a ¥166m loss.
  • Absolute margin remains low for a slot supplier; R&D and fixed costs of Las Vegas manufacturing and Australian operations are not broken out.
  • Segment is 8% of group revenue but only 2% of segment business profit, so the group investment case does not turn on it.
  • Provides a regulated-market revenue stream uncorrelated with eFootball; yen translation (USD/JPY ~158 at period-end) is a tailwind that reverses if the BoJ's hawkish turn sticks.

Guidance

FY3/27 guidance left at ¥46.0bn revenue (~$291m) and ¥6.5bn business profit (~$41m, 14% margin) despite H1 already 55% of the revenue guide.

  • Implied H2 revenue ¥20.9bn (~$132m) would be −17.5% YoY against H2 FY3/26's ¥25.3bn and −17% versus H1, yet implied H2 business profit ¥4.1bn (~$26m) is a 19.6% margin.
  • Most likely the guide was simply not refreshed (Tanshin frames the revision as DE-only), or they expect Solstice 49C replacement demand to fade as SuperNova ramps.
  • Group inventories rose ¥4.1bn to ¥23.0bn (~$146m) in the half, consistent with cabinet build ahead of Q3 launches rather than a demand problem.
  • Our estimate: FY revenue nearer ¥50bn and business profit ¥5.5–6.0bn; a Q3 refresh of the segment guide would be the natural moment to concede it.

6Segment deep-dive: Sports (Konami Sports Club, Pilates Mirror, Personal 30, outsourced facilities)

Revenue +3% on new-format rollouts (Pilates Mirror to 116 studios) but Q2 profit fell 13% on energy and price inflation; H1 OP is flattered by ¥0.5bn of other income.

Revenue and new formats

H1 revenue grew 3.3% to ¥25.7bn (~$163m); Q2 ¥13.0bn (~$82m), up 1.4% YoY and 2.3% on Q1, driven by new formats not legacy clubs.

  • Pilates Mirror added 31 studios (16 Q1, 15 Q2) to reach 116, including first sites in Hokkaido and Chiba; seven more scheduled to take it to 123.
  • Personal 30 opened two sites in Setagaya and a sixth at Yutenji in October.
  • Outsourced management began in six municipalities from April (Yuki, Hachioji, Shizuoka, Hyogo Prefecture, Higashihiroshima, Fukuoka); outsourced school swimming instruction expanding nationally on school demand.
  • Clubs ran UNITED FEEL event and introduced Training Partner in July; Undo Juku and Golf Academy's 13th Konami Sports Jr. Cup; KONAMI J.B.STAR won 2026 World Hip Hop Dance Championship.

Profit and margin

H1 business profit rose 8.9% to ¥1.9bn (~$12m), a 7.5% margin versus 7.1%, but Q2 business profit fell 13.4% to ¥1.2bn (~$7m).

  • Q2 margin 9.0% versus 10.5%; management cites continued goods price increases and soaring energy costs, against a market expanding on health consciousness and format diversification.
  • Segment operating profit ¥2.4bn (~$15m), up 49.2%, is flattered by ¥0.5bn other income in Q1 (versus ¥0.1bn other expense last year) and is not underlying.

Guidance

FY3/27 guidance unchanged at ¥52.0bn revenue (~$329m) and ¥4.0bn business profit (~$25m, 8% margin); H1 is 49% and 48% of those.

  • Delivery requires H2 business profit of ~¥2.1bn (~$13m), roughly in line with H2 FY3/26's run-rate, while the Q2 exit margin was falling.
  • Membership counts, yield per member, churn and Pilates Mirror unit economics are not disclosed, so accretion of the 31-studio rollout at maturity cannot be judged.

Capital drag, pachinko

At 8% of group revenue and under 2% of segment business profit, Sports is a return-on-capital drag kept for brand and social reasons.

  • Sits on a large share of the group's ¥188bn property, plant and equipment; nothing in this print changes that.
  • New-format pivot (Pilates, 30-minute gyms, outsourced municipal facilities, school swimming) at least moves mix toward lower-capital, higher-frequency revenue.
  • Pachinko/pachislot, now in Others, is the smaller problem child: revenue −19.2% to ¥4.6bn (~$29m), business profit −44.8% to ¥0.8bn (~$5m).
  • Unchanged ¥9.0bn/¥1.5bn FY guide implies an H2 rebound we would not underwrite.

7Highlights & lowlights

▲ Highlights

  • Operating leverage of the live-service model is now visible in the group P&L. Gross margin 54.8% (+540bps), SG&A 18.4% of revenue (−200bps), group BP margin 36.4% (+750bps); DE BP margin 44.1% (+780bps), Q2 44.3%. Incremental BP/revenue ~57% at group, ~62% in DE. No restructuring, no one-offs — it is mix (in-game and card revenue +42% vs product +25%). structural
  • Guidance credibility reset. The first H1-stage upgrade since FY3/12, taking OP to ¥193bn (~$1.22bn, +35%) and net profit to ¥135bn (~$855m) — ~6% above the pre-results QUICK consensus of ¥127.5bn — from a management that guided FY3/27 ~10% below consensus in May. Even so, the implied H2 (OP ¥81.7bn, DE BP ¥80.5bn, a −¥6.2bn other-expense cushion) leaves room; management reportedly called the plan conservative. one-off
  • eFootball + Yu-Gi-Oh! are proving durable franchises, not just a tournament spike. Q2 DE revenue +50.3% was built on the World Cup window but also on the August major update, a new-season campaign management says is performing well, MASTER DUEL's 100m-download milestone and strong special-edition OCG product; >1bn eFootball downloads and 45.21m esports entrants give a large funnel to re-monetise. mixed
  • Gaming & Systems is finally growing at scale. H1 revenue +41.3% to ¥25.1bn (~$159m), BP +154% to ¥2.4bn (~$15m), Q2 margin 12.5% (+580bps QoQ). Solstice 49C driving units, Solstice 27 launched, SuperNova (75") in Q3; BOMBERMAN the #1 content line in North America; SYNKROS >150,000 connected machines incl. Carnival's fleet; Class II in Texas/Washington with VLT/HHR next. Stale FY guide (¥46bn) is upside, not risk. structural
  • Cash generation and balance sheet. OCF ¥85.2bn (~$540m) +62.5%, capex halved to ¥16.2bn (~$103m), FCF (OCF−capex) ¥69.0bn (~$437m) vs ¥14.7bn; cash ¥373.6bn (~$2.37bn), net cash ¥333.7bn (~$2.11bn), equity ratio 76.6%. No impairments in H1. structural
  • Dividend +33% to ¥299 (~$1.89), interim ¥112 (~$0.71). Mechanical under the ≥30% payout policy, but it converts the upgrade into cash for holders immediately (payable 19 Nov) and signals confidence in the H2 number. structural
  • Arcade margin expansion on the Demon Slayer card format. Q2 revenue +24.9%, BP +46.0%, margin 28.8% (+420bps); jubeat's first new cabinet in 18 years and three further launches in H2. Small, but a second IP-driven consumables engine in the group. early

▼ Lowlights

  • Single-franchise, tournament-timed concentration. 100% of the ¥78bn revenue / ¥50bn BP upgrade is DE; DE is 79% of revenue and ~93% of segment profit. eFootball's World Cup window will not recur until 2030; the implied H2 DE BP of ~¥40bn/quarter vs ¥63.6bn in Q2 is management's own admission of normalisation. FY3/28 faces a quadrennial comp with no disclosed catalyst of similar scale. watch
  • Overseas-booked revenue fell sharply and the console slate is thin. US −13.2% (Q2 −23.7%), Europe −38.6% (Q2 −41.7%) as MGS Δ / SILENT HILL f rolled off; MASTER COLLECTION Vol.2 and Townfall are lower-ticket. Castlevania: Belmont's Curse (Oct) and TAG FORCE GX (Feb) are the only dated H2 premium releases; Rev. NOiR and Rhapsody in Scarlet are undated. structural
  • Disclosure is thin for a company now valued on live-service quality. No MAU, ARPU, paying users, title revenue, mobile/console/card split, GS units/ASP/recurring share, or Sports membership; no English earnings call transcript. The Street cannot verify cohort retention post-tournament from the filings — it can only watch third-party app data. governance
  • Gaming & Systems guidance left stale and margin still sub-scale. Unchanged ¥46bn/¥6.5bn implies H2 revenue −17.5% YoY at a 19.6% margin — internally inconsistent. H1 margin 9.6% remains well below global slot peers; the segment is 8% of revenue, 2% of profit. watch
  • Sports profit slipped in Q2 and Others shrank. Sports Q2 BP −13.4% to ¥1.2bn (~$7m), margin 9.0% (−150bps) on energy and price inflation; H1 OP flattered by ¥0.5bn other income. Others (pachinko/pachislot) revenue −19.2%, BP −44.8%, with an unchanged FY guide implying an H2 rebound. mixed
  • Working capital build and an H2 other-expense cushion. Receivables +¥15.6bn to ¥75.6bn (~$479m) and inventories +¥4.1bn to ¥23.0bn (~$146m) absorbed ~¥22bn; the FY other-income/expense guide of −¥7.0bn vs −¥0.8bn actual implies −¥6.2bn of H2 impairment/disposal charges that are not explained. watch
  • Capital allocation remains passive. Net cash of ¥333.7bn (~$2.11bn) is 53% of equity, the equity ratio is 76.6%, treasury purchases were ¥3m and the payout is pinned at 30%. A ¥20bn bond matures within 12 months. There is no buyback, no stated ROE target in this release, and Sports continues to tie up a large share of ¥188bn of PP&E. governance

8Forecast & outlook

Guidance bridge. Konami guided FY3/27 (year to 31 March 2027) in May at revenue ¥505bn / business profit ¥150bn / OP ¥143bn / net profit ¥101bn (EPS ¥745.08), below consensus at the time. With H1 at ¥308.0bn / ¥112.1bn / ¥111.3bn / ¥79.2bn, the 29 Oct revision took revenue to ¥583bn (~$3.69bn, +18.1% YoY), business profit to ¥200bn (~$1.27bn), OP to ¥193bn (~$1.22bn, +42.0% YoY), PBT to ¥193bn and net profit to ¥135bn (~$855m, +35.0%; EPS ¥995.90, ~$6.31). Independent view. Our FY3/27 estimate sits 4–6% above the new guide: revenue ~¥595bn (~$3.77bn), OP ~¥200–205bn (~$1.27–1.30bn), net profit ~¥140–143bn (~$890–905m), EPS ~¥1,035–1,055. The arithmetic: Q3 FY3/26 was ~¥128.2bn revenue / ~¥37.5bn OP (derived from the rounded 9M deck); with the September eFootball season live, Castlevania: Belmont's Curse (Oct), continuing Yu-Gi-Oh! OCG product, the Solstice SuperNova launch and the arcade cabinet cycle, we model Q3 FY3/27 at ~¥145bn revenue (~$919m) and ~¥48bn OP (~$304m), +13%/+28% YoY, and H2 OP of ~¥90–95bn versus the ¥81.7bn implied — i.e. DE business profit nearer ¥88–92bn than ¥80.5bn, and we do not assume the full −¥6.2bn H2 other-expense cushion is used. Margins: FY OP margin ~34% (guide 33.1%), with H2 ~32–33% versus H1's 36.1% as eFootball spend normalises off the tournament peak; DE at ~41–42% for the year. Leverage/cash: net cash should rise from ¥333.7bn toward ~¥360–370bn (~$2.3bn) by March 2027 after the ¥15.2bn interim dividend (Dec), higher H2 capex (~¥20bn), H2 tax payments and the receivables unwind; the ¥20bn bond maturing within 12 months is a rounding error against cash and is more likely repaid than refinanced. Swing factors: (1) eFootball post-tournament retention — each ¥10bn of H2 DE revenue is ~¥6bn of OP at the observed drop-through; (2) Yu-Gi-Oh! OCG product cadence and MASTER DUEL events; (3) yen — USD/JPY ~158 at period-end; a 5% stronger yen takes ~¥1bn off GS revenue and trims overseas DE; (4) Solstice SuperNova timing and whether the GS guide is refreshed in Q3; (5) the unexplained −¥6.2bn H2 other-expense line. The bigger valuation question is FY3/28: on a quadrennial comp with no disclosed catalyst of World Cup scale, we would not extrapolate 40%+ DE margins; a flat-to-down DE profit year is the base case the ~26x forward multiple is already contesting.

What to watch next quarter

  • eFootball monthly revenue trajectory post-tournament (third-party app-store data) and the September season update's retention — the single variable behind ~¥50bn of guidance.
  • Q3 FY3/27 print (late Jan 2027): DE business profit vs the ~¥40bn/quarter implied by guidance — anything above ¥50bn means the FY guide is beaten by >¥10bn.
  • Gaming & Systems: Solstice SuperNova shipments, SYNKROS cruise/property wins, Class II/VLT/HHR placements, and whether the ¥46bn/¥6.5bn guide is refreshed; any disclosure of units, ASP or recurring revenue share.
  • Working capital: unwind of +¥15.6bn receivables and +¥4.1bn inventories in Q3 OCF; H2 capex versus the ¥16.2bn H1 run-rate.
  • Capital allocation: treatment of the ¥20bn bond maturing within 12 months, any buyback against ¥334bn net cash, and whether the 30% payout floor moves.
  • Sports exit margin (Q2 9.0%, −150bps) under energy/price inflation and Pilates Mirror unit economics at 123 studios; Others (pachinko/pachislot) H2 rebound assumption.
  • FX and rates: BoJ hawkishness (30 Oct) and USD/JPY vs ~158 — translation of GS and overseas DE revenue, and the −¥1.8bn FX hit in OCI already taken in H1.
